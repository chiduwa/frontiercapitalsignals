Nyasa Times reports that Malawi's Minister of Mining, Thoko Tembo, has opened a gold trading centre in Karonga, making it the country’s sixth such facility. This initiative is part of government efforts to bring the gold sector into the formal economy. The centre was established under the Export Development Fund (EDF), a Reserve Bank of Malawi program introduced in 2021 to create an organized gold marketing structure.

This development signifies a clear policy direction towards formalizing and regulating Malawi's gold sector. For investors, the expansion of official trading centres could lead to increased transparency and reduced operational risks previously associated with illegal mining. It also suggests potential for more structured investment opportunities within the gold supply chain, as the government aims to enhance organized marketing.

Investors should monitor the effectiveness of these trading centres in curbing illicit gold activities and their impact on formal gold exports. Future government initiatives supporting the Export Development Fund and the expansion of formal gold marketing infrastructure across other regions will be crucial indicators of sustained commitment to this sector's formalization.
