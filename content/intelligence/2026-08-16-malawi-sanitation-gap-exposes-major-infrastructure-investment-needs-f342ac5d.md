Malawi's Deputy Minister of Health and Sanitation, Charles Chilambula, recently disclosed that despite public claims, a significant portion of the population still lacks basic sanitation facilities and practices open defecation. This concerning reality was revealed at the official launch of the Presidential Compact on Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH).

This critical infrastructure gap presents substantial investment opportunities in public health, construction, and WASH-related services. The government's commitment, highlighted by the new Presidential Compact, indicates potential for Public Private Partnerships and tenders in water treatment, sewage systems, and sanitation product manufacturing. Improved public health is also foundational for broader economic stability.

Investors should closely monitor the implementation details and funding allocations for the Presidential WASH Compact. Watch for specific tenders, policy incentives for private sector participation, and progress reports on sanitation coverage improvement. Companies specializing in water infrastructure, waste management, and sustainable sanitation solutions are advised to track these developments proactively.
