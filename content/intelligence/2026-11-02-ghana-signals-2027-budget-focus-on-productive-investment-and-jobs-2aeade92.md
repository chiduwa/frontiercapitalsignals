MyJoyOnline Business reports that Ghana's Deputy Finance Minister, Thomas Nyarko Ampem, stated the government intends to use the 2027 Budget to convert economic stability into job creation, productive investment, and improved living standards.

This statement signals a forward-looking policy emphasis on tangible economic outcomes for Ghana. For international investors, a government-led push to boost productive investment could create new opportunities across various sectors. The focus on job creation and living standards may suggest supportive policy measures or incentives for industries that align with these national objectives.

Investors should monitor future governmental communications regarding the 2027 Budget for specific policy frameworks, sector-targeted allocations, and proposed investment initiatives. Details on how "economic stability" will be measured and "productive investment" defined will be crucial in identifying concrete areas for engagement and understanding the scope of potential opportunities.
