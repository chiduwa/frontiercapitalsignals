Premium Times reports that Olam Agri has broken ground on a new Integrated Feed Mill in Delta State, Nigeria. This facility is strategically located to serve customers across Nigeria's South-South and South-East regions. The report indicates this move deepens investment in Nigeria's agric sector.

This development signals Olam Agri's commitment to meeting the growing demand for quality animal feed in these key regions. By bringing production closer to poultry farmers, aquaculture operators, and livestock producers, the new mill could enhance supply chain efficiency and reduce logistical costs for customers. Investors may see this as a positive indicator for further growth in Nigeria's agricultural value chain, particularly in animal protein production.
