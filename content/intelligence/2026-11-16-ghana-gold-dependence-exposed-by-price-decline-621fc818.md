The High Street Journal reports that bullion has fallen to a 13-month low. This decline is attributed to expectations of higher US interest rates, which are strengthening the dollar.

This development raises concerns for Ghana's export earnings. It exposes the country's growing dependence on gold for foreign exchange, according to The High Street Journal. For investors, this indicates potential pressure on Ghana's external accounts and macroeconomic stability, given gold's role as a key revenue source.

Investors should monitor the trajectory of global gold prices and US monetary policy decisions. Continued gold price weakness could impact Ghana's foreign exchange reserves and its ability to service dollar-denominated obligations, potentially affecting asset valuations within the country.
