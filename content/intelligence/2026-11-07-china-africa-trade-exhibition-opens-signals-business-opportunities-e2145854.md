The Independent (Uganda) reports that the 2026 China-Africa Trade Commodity Exhibition opened on November 6 in Yiwu, China. This three-day event gathered 186 companies from 15 countries to showcase products and explore new business opportunities between China and Africa.

For investors in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda, this exhibition signifies continued interest in expanding trade and business links with China. It suggests potential for new market access, supply chain diversification, and export opportunities for companies operating in these African markets, particularly within the commodity trade sector.

Investors should monitor future outcomes from such exhibitions, watching for any new trade agreements, partnerships, or investment commitments that may emerge.
