Nile Post reports that Uganda is initiating the development of a National Electricity Master Plan. This strategic government move aims to expand power access across the country.

This policy initiative indicates a commitment to scaling up the national electricity grid and increasing connectivity. Such efforts typically open avenues for investment in power generation, transmission, distribution, and associated infrastructure projects within Uganda.

Investors should monitor for further announcements detailing the plan's specific objectives, timelines, and any identified project areas or potential financing structures, which could signal concrete investment opportunities.
