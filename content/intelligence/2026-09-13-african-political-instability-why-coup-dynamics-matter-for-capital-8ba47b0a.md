Kamissa Camara, Mali's youngest foreign minister, experienced firsthand the overthrow of her government by a coup. She is now directly engaging with military officers responsible for coups in Mali and Burkina Faso, seeking to understand the underlying drivers of these power seizures. This research offers a unique, insider perspective on the dynamics of political instability in the Sahel region.

For investors, this work underscores the persistent and significant political instability risks in parts of West Africa. Military interventions disrupt policy continuity, legal frameworks, and long-term investment planning. Understanding the motivations and triggers behind such events is crucial for assessing country risk and developing robust investment strategies in potentially volatile environments.

We should monitor the findings from this type of in-depth analysis for insights into potential future stability trends or triggers of unrest. Pay close attention to governance shifts and civil-military relations in affected nations and neighboring countries. These political dynamics directly influence the regulatory landscape and overall business environment for foreign capital.
