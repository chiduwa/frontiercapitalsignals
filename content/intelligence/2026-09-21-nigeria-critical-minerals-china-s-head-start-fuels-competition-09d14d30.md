The US is now actively laying diplomatic groundwork for access to Nigeria's critical minerals. However, Chinese firms have already secured over a billion dollars in investments and established a substantial operational head start on the ground. This sets the stage for intensified competition.

This dynamic significantly elevates Nigeria's strategic importance in the global critical minerals supply chain. Investors should recognize the immediate implications for logistics, infrastructure, and ancillary services supporting mineral extraction. The competitive landscape can also drive up asset valuations for existing local players or potential joint ventures.

Monitor the Nigerian government's policy responses to this increased foreign interest, particularly concerning new licensing rounds or partnership frameworks. Watch for specific US-backed initiatives or projects emerging, and assess opportunities to partner with local entities positioned to benefit from this global rivalry.
