Capital FM Business reports that Kenya is strategically positioning its geothermal resources to drive industrial investment. A proposed Sh103.2 billion fertilizer manufacturing plant in Olkaria is highlighted as a key effort in this strategy to attract industrial capital.

This initiative signals a clear policy direction to utilize domestic energy for large-scale industrial projects. For investors, this indicates potential opportunities in supporting infrastructure, related industries, and the agricultural inputs sector if the proposed plant proceeds. It also suggests a government commitment to resource-backed industrialization.

Investors should monitor developments surrounding the proposed Sh103.2 billion fertilizer plant in Olkaria. Tracking its progress will offer insight into Kenya's effectiveness in converting its geothermal potential into tangible industrial investments and the broader trajectory of its industrialization strategy.
