Sahara Power Group, Africa's largest privately held power company, has broken ground on a new $12 million, 12MW power plant in Lagos. This significant investment is targeted at strengthening the Lagos grid and is projected to become operational in the first quarter of 2027.

This development signals robust private sector confidence in Nigeria's power sector, a critical area for economic growth. Improving power supply in Lagos, the country's commercial hub, directly benefits businesses, reduces operational costs, and enhances industrial productivity. For investors, this indicates increasing opportunities within Nigeria's infrastructure and energy value chain, including potential for partnerships or related service providers.
