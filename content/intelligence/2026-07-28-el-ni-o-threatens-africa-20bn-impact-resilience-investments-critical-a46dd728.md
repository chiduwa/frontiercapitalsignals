El Niño is rapidly strengthening, posing a significant threat to African economies. The African Development Bank estimates potential losses between $10 billion and $20 billion due to severe drought, floods, and storms.

This directly impacts sectors like agriculture and power, crucial for regional stability and growth. Supply chain disruptions are likely, affecting commodity prices and operational costs for businesses. Investment in climate resilience infrastructure and sustainable energy solutions will become critical.

Monitor specific country-level weather forecasts and government responses, particularly in our target markets. Track AfDB and other development finance institution announcements regarding adaptation funding. Watch for shifts in agricultural commodity futures and energy sector policies.
