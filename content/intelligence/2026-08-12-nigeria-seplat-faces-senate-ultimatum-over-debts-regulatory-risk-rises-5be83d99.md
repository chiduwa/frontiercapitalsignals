The Nigerian Senate Public Accounts Committee has given Seplat Energy, Network E&P, and other oil companies a 48-hour ultimatum. This follows a NEITI report highlighting outstanding royalty and gas flare debts, with Dubri Oil specifically defending a $3.025 million liability.

This signals heightened regulatory scrutiny on operational compliance and financial obligations within Nigeria's oil and gas sector. Unresolved issues could lead to fines, operational disruptions, or reputational damage for involved companies like Seplat, potentially impacting their stock performance and future exploration approvals. It underlines rising governance risks.

Monitor the companies' responses to the ultimatum and any subsequent actions by the Senate PAC or other regulatory bodies. Watch for payment resolutions, negotiation outcomes, or potential sanctions. This will indicate the government's resolve in enforcing compliance and the broader risk landscape for energy investments in Nigeria.
