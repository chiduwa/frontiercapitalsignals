TechCabal reports that a recent startup competition in Abidjan showcased Ivorian startups with practical solutions, such as AI for livestock monitoring without internet access and drones for reducing agricultural inputs. While a woman-led startup won the top prize, few women had the opportunity to make their case to the jury.

This event signals strong innovation in Ivorian tech and agriculture, demonstrating solutions with clear field results. The success of a woman-led startup winning despite broader underrepresentation suggests significant untapped potential in diverse founder pipelines, which could offer attractive investment avenues.

Investors should monitor the Ivorian startup ecosystem for continued innovation in practical, agriculture-focused technologies. It will be important to observe any future efforts to address the observed gender imbalance in pitching opportunities, as increasing diversity could open new and promising investment channels.
