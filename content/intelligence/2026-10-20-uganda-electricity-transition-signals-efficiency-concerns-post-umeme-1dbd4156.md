The Independent (Uganda) reports an apparent overnight collapse in electricity service delivery in Uganda. This follows the transition from UMEME, a private company, to the state-owned Uganda Electricity Company Limited (UEDCL).

This shift is significant as UMEME operated under a commercial model where efficiency was crucial for its survival. The reported decline in service delivery under UEDCL raises questions about future operational standards and the commercial viability focus in Uganda's power sector. This could impact businesses relying on stable electricity and broader economic productivity.

Investors should closely monitor UEDCL's operational performance and its approach to efficiency and service delivery. The long-term implications for private sector participation and the reliability of Uganda's power infrastructure will depend on UEDCL's ability to restore and maintain service standards.
