Bandits killed eight and abducted eight travelers on the Gusau-Funtua highway in Zamfara State. Police in Katsina and Kebbi states concurrently reported operations against suspected bandits and electricity cable vandals.

This attack underscores persistent insecurity impacting critical transit routes in Nigeria's North-West. Such incidents raise operational costs for logistics-dependent sectors, deter new investment, and increase risk premiums for businesses relying on regional supply chains.

Investors should monitor the effectiveness of ongoing security operations and the government's strategy to secure major highways. Continued instability could impact agricultural output, mining operations, and overall regional economic activity, demanding heightened risk assessment for related investments.
