Nigeria's Dangote Petroleum Refinery has dominated European jet fuel imports for two consecutive months, displacing US refiners. This leadership shift stems from geopolitical disruptions compelling Europe to diversify its energy supply chains.

This represents a structural realignment in global energy markets, significantly boosting Nigeria's strategic position in refining. For investors, it underscores Dangote's operational strength and substantial revenue potential, while also offering critical diversification for European energy buyers. The recent IPO context adds a key valuation consideration.

Our focus now is on the sustainability of Dangote's market share against evolving US and global competition. We also need to track the long-term performance of its publicly traded entities and broader implications for Nigeria's energy export strategy and regional downstream investments.
