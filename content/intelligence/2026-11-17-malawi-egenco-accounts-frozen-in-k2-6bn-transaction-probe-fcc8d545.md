Malawi24 reports that the government has frozen accounts linked to a K2.6 billion transaction between the Electricity Generation Company (Egenco) and M & M Global Law Consultants. Secretary to the Treasury, Cliff Chiunda, confirmed this action, stating that an investigation into the matter has also been instituted.

This development signals increased government scrutiny over large financial transactions involving state-owned enterprises, particularly in the energy sector. Investors should note potential implications for governance standards and regulatory enforcement in Malawi. Such actions could affect deal certainty and perceived risk for public-private partnerships.

The ongoing investigation will clarify the circumstances surrounding the Egenco legal fee and the government's stance on financial oversight. Investors should monitor outcomes for insights into Malawi's commitment to transparency and its impact on the operational environment for businesses.
