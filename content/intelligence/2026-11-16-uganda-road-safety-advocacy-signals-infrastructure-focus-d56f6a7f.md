The Independent (Uganda) reports that Kampala Lord Mayor Ronald Balimwezo recounted a road crash where he lost a leg, inspiring his political advocacy for safer roads in Uganda. Separately, the Professional Drivers Network is outlining measures to address rising traffic deaths in the country.
