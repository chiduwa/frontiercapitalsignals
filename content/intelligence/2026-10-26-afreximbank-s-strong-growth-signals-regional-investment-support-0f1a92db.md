MyJoyOnline Business reports Afreximbank achieved a 24% net income growth, reaching US$813.5 million. The bank's assets and contingencies now exceed US$54.1 billion. Denys Denya, Afreximbank's Senior Executive Vice President, commented that this performance highlights the bank's strong and sustainable delivery while fulfilling its development mandate across Africa and the Caribbean.

This growth demonstrates Afreximbank's effectiveness in its target markets. Its expanded lending portfolio, robust profitability, and strong capital position indicate the growing relevance of its interventions, which is positive for regional economic activity and investment opportunities.

Afreximbank stated its ongoing commitment to providing financing, trade facilitation, and strategic investments. These efforts are aimed at strengthening regional value chains, supporting industrialisation, and accelerating economic transformation amidst global uncertainties. Investors should monitor how these commitments translate into specific project financing and support in markets like Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda.
