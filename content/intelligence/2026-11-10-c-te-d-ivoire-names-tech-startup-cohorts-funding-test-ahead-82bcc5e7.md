TechCabal reports that Abidjan has named the first cohorts for its Ivoire Tech Next 15 and Ivoire Tech Scale Up programs. TechCabal states that the selection of these cohorts is credible.

This initiative signals governmental intent to support the tech and startup ecosystem in Côte d'Ivoire, a notable development for the broader African tech landscape. While not directly in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, or Uganda, similar government-backed startup programs across the continent can influence investor sentiment and regional competition for tech talent and funding.

Investors should monitor how the program progresses, specifically regarding the money allocated, the implementation of procurement rules, and the effectiveness of its scorecard. TechCabal indicates these factors will determine the success of Abidjan's plan to back tech startups.
