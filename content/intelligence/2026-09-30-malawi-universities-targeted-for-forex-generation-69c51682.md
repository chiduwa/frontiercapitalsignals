Malawi's Education Minister Bright Msaka publicly challenged universities to earn foreign exchange through their services, emulating Zambia's approach. This directive emerged during a public sector reform commitment signing, underscoring the government's urgency in addressing persistent forex shortages.

This move highlights Malawi's critical need for foreign currency and a proactive government stance to mobilize it beyond traditional exports. For investors, it indicates potential for new service sector opportunities, particularly in higher education, research, and vocational training, perhaps via international student recruitment or institutional partnerships. A successful initiative could also contribute to greater forex stability, mitigating currency risk for broader investments.
