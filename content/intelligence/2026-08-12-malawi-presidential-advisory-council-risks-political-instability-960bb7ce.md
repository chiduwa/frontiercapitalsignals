Malawi faces a new political dynamic with a proposal for a Presidential Advisory Council composed of former presidents. While framed as a national unity initiative, critics immediately flagged it as a strategic move. The concern is it could establish a parallel power center, potentially enabling specific former leaders to maintain relevance and influence ahead of the 2030 elections.

For investors, this proposal introduces significant political uncertainty. It risks fragmenting executive power and diverting the administration’s focus from economic policy and reforms. A potential power struggle among political elites signals reduced predictability in governance and could delay critical project approvals or policy implementation, impacting market confidence.

Monitor the progress of this council proposal and the political factions supporting or opposing it. Observe how the current administration navigates this internal challenge and if the proposal gains traction. Sustained political infighting or a perceived weakening of executive authority will be a key indicator for potential shifts in Malawi's investment landscape.
