MyJoyOnline Business reports that Professor Irene Egyir, an agricultural economist, urged the government to significantly increase investment in commercial agriculture and rural infrastructure for the 2027 Budget. She emphasized that commercial farmers require reliable roads, electricity, water, and affordable credit to expand their operations. Prof. Egyir also advocated for government spending to address the entire agricultural value chain, from production to processing, input supply, and distribution.

This call signals a potential future focus for government policy and spending in Ghana's agriculture sector. Investors should note that increased public investment in rural infrastructure, such as roads and utilities, could create opportunities for businesses involved in construction, energy, and water services. Additionally, an emphasis on affordable credit and value chain support could benefit financial institutions, agricultural input suppliers, processors, and distributors.

Investors should monitor future government budget statements for the 2027 fiscal year for concrete policy decisions and funding allocations related to rural infrastructure and commercial agriculture. The specific initiatives and their implementation will indicate the extent of these potential opportunities.
