MyJoyOnline Business reports that the Chief Executive Officer of the Minerals Income Investment Fund (MIIF), Justina Nelson, accompanied by executive and senior management, visited the Zijin Golden Ridge Mine. The delegation's purpose was to gain insight into mining operations and learn about Zijin's activities, investments, and economic contributions to Ghana. MIIF expressed gratitude for the hosting and the opportunity provided.

This engagement by MIIF, a key state-backed fund in Ghana's minerals sector, is significant for investors.
