Africa Briefing reports that Ethiopia has become Africa's fifth-largest economy, overtaking Kenya and Morocco. This follows revised national accounts, which project its GDP to reach $210.7 billion in 2025/26.

This re-evaluation suggests a significantly larger market size than previously understood. For investors, a larger GDP could lead to improved debt ratios, potentially enhancing sovereign creditworthiness without requiring a reduction in existing liabilities. This shift could alter perceptions of risk and opportunity in the region.

Investors should monitor how these revised economic figures translate into actual policy decisions and market sentiment. While a larger GDP could improve debt ratios, Africa Briefing does not detail how quickly this might materialize or its specific impact on investment conditions. Further information on debt management strategies and economic reforms linked to this growth will be key.
