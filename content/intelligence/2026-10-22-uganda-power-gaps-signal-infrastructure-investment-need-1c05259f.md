Nile Post reports that Uganda Electricity Distribution Company Limited (UEDCL) attributes persistent power blackouts to ageing infrastructure, overloaded lines, and years of limited investment.

This situation highlights significant operational challenges within Uganda's power distribution sector, following the utility’s takeover of electricity distribution in April 2025. Persistent blackouts could impact industrial productivity, consumer confidence, and overall economic stability. For investors, this signals potential for infrastructure development projects, upgrades in power transmission and distribution, or opportunities in alternative energy solutions.

Investors should monitor UEDCL's strategies for addressing these infrastructure deficits and improving service reliability. Consumer demands for improved service could pressure the utility to accelerate investment in modernization and expansion.
