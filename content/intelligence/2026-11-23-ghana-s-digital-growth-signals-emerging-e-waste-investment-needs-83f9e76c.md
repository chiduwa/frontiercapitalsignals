The High Street Journal reports that Ghana is facing increasing pressure to strengthen its collection and safe disposal of electronic waste. This comes as investment in digital infrastructure, artificial intelligence, and telecommunications accelerates across the country.

For investors, this situation highlights a growing market gap in e-waste management services and infrastructure. The imperative to address electronic waste, driven by Ghana's rapid digital expansion, could lead to new regulatory frameworks, government incentives, or private sector demand for innovative disposal and recycling solutions. This presents potential risks for businesses generating e-waste if not managed, but also clear opportunities for those in waste management, recycling technologies, or environmental services.

Investors should closely monitor upcoming policy developments and governmental initiatives aimed at improving e-waste collection and safe disposal. Watch for potential tenders, public-private partnerships, or private sector innovations emerging to address these identified challenges in Ghana's waste management landscape.
