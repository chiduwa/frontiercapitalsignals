Nyasa Times reports that the Lilongwe University of Agriculture and Natural Resources (LUANAR) held its 2025/26 Vice-Chancellor's Excellence Awards on November 20, 2026. The university recognized top-performing students, researchers, innovators, and academic departments. Achievements celebrated included research, innovation, academic excellence, and resource mobilization. Awards honored exceptional contributions across student academic performance, innovation, and resource (implying resource mobilization).

This initiative signals a focus on human capital development and innovation within Malawi's agriculture and natural resources sectors.
