Premium Times reports that FIFA President Gianni Infantino stated Nigeria is currently unprepared to host the World Cup. He cited significant infrastructure shortcomings, specifically stadiums, airports, and transportation networks. However, he indicated a future bid remains a possibility for Nigeria if these issues are addressed.

This signals a clear potential future focus or intention by the government to address critical infrastructure gaps. Such an ambition, tied to a major international event like the World Cup, could drive substantial public and private investment into these sectors, creating opportunities for construction, logistics, and related service providers.

Investors should monitor any policy statements or budget allocations related to infrastructure development in Nigeria, particularly concerning stadiums, airports, and transportation networks. The viability of future large-scale project tenders in these areas may be linked to such national aspirations.
