The Standard reports Kenya aims to achieve 100 percent green energy by 2030. Currently, the country generates approximately 91 percent of its electricity from renewable sources, with thermal generation accounting for the remaining share.

This stated policy objective underscores a continued commitment to renewable energy expansion and decarbonization within Kenya's power sector. For investors, this signals potential opportunities in the development of new renewable energy projects, associated infrastructure, and technologies. The existing high base of renewable energy generation suggests a supportive environment for further green investments.

Fund managers should monitor upcoming policy announcements and regulatory frameworks designed to facilitate reaching the 100 percent target. Watch for details on specific project tenders, financing incentives, or public-private partnership opportunities in the renewable energy space to identify concrete investment entry points.
