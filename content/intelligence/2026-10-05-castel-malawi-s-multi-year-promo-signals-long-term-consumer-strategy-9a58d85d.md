Castel Malawi initiated a substantial multi-year consumer promotion, “Tikwere Under the Crown,” offering high-value prizes including a Nissan Navara and motorbikes. This campaign encourages purchases of specific beer brands and is set to run until January 2027.

This aggressive, multi-year campaign signals Castel's strong commitment to the Malawian market and confidence in long-term consumer purchasing power. It suggests a strategic move to boost volume, enhance brand loyalty, and potentially capture greater market share in the competitive beverage sector.

Investors should monitor Castel's sales volumes and market share performance, alongside broader trends in Malawian consumer spending. Watch for any competitive responses from other beverage players and assess the promotion's impact on Castel's financial results over the coming quarters.
