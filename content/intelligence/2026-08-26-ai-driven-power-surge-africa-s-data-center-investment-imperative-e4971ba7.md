Global data center rack density has sharply escalated, jumping from 16 kW to 27 kW in just one year. This unprecedented increase, identified by AFCOM's latest report, is primarily driven by surging AI workloads. Operators project continued significant growth in demand for high-density computing.

This trend presents a critical investment signal for African markets. As global tech giants expand into or require services from data centers in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, and Uganda, the demand for reliable, high-capacity, and cooled power infrastructure will skyrocket. The imperative for resilient energy solutions, including renewables and grid upgrades, alongside advanced cooling technologies, creates lucrative opportunities for infrastructure developers and energy providers.

Investors should monitor government initiatives supporting energy infrastructure and data center development in the region. Look for partnerships between local power utilities and international data center operators. Track progress in grid modernization and the adoption of scalable, sustainable energy sources to meet this evolving, high-power demand.
