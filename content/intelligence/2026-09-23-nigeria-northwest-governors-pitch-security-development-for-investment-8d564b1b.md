Northwest Nigerian governors presented their 2026-2030 Peace, Security and Development Framework to international development institutions and investors in New York, on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly. This move aims to secure critical funding for ambitious regional plans.

This initiative directly addresses a primary concern for investors in Northwest Nigeria, security. A concerted, government-led effort to de-risk the region through planned security and development interventions can unlock significant long-term investment opportunities in sectors like infrastructure and agriculture. Proactive engagement with global capital is a positive signal.

Monitor the follow-up on these discussions for concrete funding commitments and the unveiling of specific project pipelines. We will also track tangible progress on security improvements on the ground, which will be crucial for validating the framework's effectiveness and investor confidence.
