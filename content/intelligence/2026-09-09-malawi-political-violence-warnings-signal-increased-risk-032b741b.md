Mathews Mtumbuka, a senior member of Malawi's UTM party, issued a strong warning against political violence, stating "NO to death." This follows alleged threats by Deputy Minister of Homeland Security Norman Chisale targeting UTM leaders, captured in a circulating social media video. Chisale reportedly threatened to "whip" or "deal with" them.

This incident signals increasing political tension and potential instability ahead of future elections or political shifts. Investor confidence is directly linked to political stability and the rule of law. Threats of violence, especially from government officials, create uncertainty, deter foreign direct investment, and can impact market sentiment and asset valuations in the short to medium term.

Monitor government's official response to Chisale's alleged threats and any disciplinary actions taken. Watch for further statements from political parties and civil society regarding violence. The broader political climate, particularly how institutions address such inflammatory rhetoric, will indicate Malawi's commitment to democratic processes and stability, crucial for long-term investment viability.
