TechCabal reports that South African fintech companies are broadening their services beyond traditional payments. Firms like Yoco, Optasia, and Paymenow are now engaging in merchant financing, leveraging transaction data and digital platforms to provide services to both consumers and small businesses.

This development signals a significant evolution within the fintech sector, highlighting new opportunities for revenue generation and market expansion beyond core payment processing. For investors in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda, this trend suggests a potential for similar diversification by local fintechs or new market entry points for regional players. It underscores the increasing sophistication in using data and digital infrastructure to serve previously underserved consumer and small business segments.

Investors should closely observe if fintechs in other African markets, including our focus countries, begin to adopt similar strategies of expanding into merchant financing. Monitoring the success and scalability of these new offerings in South Africa could provide valuable insights into their viability across comparable emerging markets. This expansion reflects the growing maturity of the regional fintech sector.
