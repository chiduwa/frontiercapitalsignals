Malawi's High Court discharged an injunction, removing Yeremia Chihana as the suspended CEO of Blantyre Water Board (BWB). Attorney General Frank Mbeta successfully argued for the discharge, noting that BWB was operating “like a headless organisation.” This decision immediately vacates Chihana's position.

This development signals increased government assertiveness in addressing governance and operational stability within state-owned enterprises (SOEs) in Malawi. For investors, the AG's direct intervention highlights a commitment to accountability that could improve long-term institutional health, but also points to current operational fragility in key public utilities. It suggests a potential shift towards more rigorous oversight.

Investors should monitor the appointment of new leadership at BWB and any subsequent operational or structural reforms. Watch for further government actions targeting governance issues across other Malawian SOEs. This case could set a precedent for how the government addresses leadership and management challenges in critical infrastructure.
