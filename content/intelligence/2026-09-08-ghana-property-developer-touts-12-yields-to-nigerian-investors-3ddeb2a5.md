Devtraco Group, a leading real estate developer in Ghana, is actively pitching to Nigerian investors. The company is promising average rental yields of 12% and strong investment security for properties located in Accra, Ghana. This outreach represents a direct push to attract regional capital into Ghana's property market.

This development is significant for investors seeking diversification and potentially higher returns within the African continent. For Nigerian investors, it presents an opportunity to hedge against local market volatility and currency depreciation, while tapping into what is being positioned as a stable and high-yielding real estate segment in a neighboring economy. It underscores a growing trend of intra-African capital flow.

Moving forward, we should monitor the actual investor uptake from Nigeria and assess if the promised yields are consistently met. It is also crucial to observe any regulatory shifts affecting cross-border property ownership or capital repatriation between Ghana and Nigeria. Watch for similar initiatives from other Ghanaian developers, indicating broader market confidence.
