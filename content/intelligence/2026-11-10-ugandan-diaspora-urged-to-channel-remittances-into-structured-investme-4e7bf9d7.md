The Independent (Uganda) reports that Ugandans residing and working in Nairobi are being encouraged to transition from sending remittances to embracing structured investments. This initiative aims to enable them to build wealth in Uganda while simultaneously growing their businesses across borders.

This development suggests a potential increase in formal investment channels and capital flows between Kenya and Uganda. It highlights a strategic effort to direct funds towards productive, wealth-generating assets within Uganda, which could foster economic growth and strengthen cross-border commerce. For international investors, it signals a growing emphasis on structured financial participation over traditional informal transfers.
