Nyasa Times reports that projects under Malawi's reformed Constituency Development Fund (CDF) have been launched in Thyolo Central. This event offers a glimpse into the government's broader vision for decentralisation across the country.

Officials argue that this new CDF model aims to shift decision-making power closer to communities while strengthening accountability for public resources. For international investors, this policy shift could influence how public funds are allocated and managed at the local level, potentially altering the landscape for infrastructure, development, and other community-focused projects.

Investors should monitor the broader implementation of this reformed fund and other decentralisation initiatives within Malawi. The effectiveness of the new model in improving resource accountability and local project delivery will be a key indicator of its long-term impact on the business environment and potential investment opportunities.
