MyJoyOnline Business reports the Ecobank JoyBusiness SME Fair 2026, a three-day exhibition at Ghud Park, Accra Mall, entered its final day today, Sunday, November 15. The event featured over 80 Ghanaian brands from sectors like food and beverages, fashion, beauty, agriculture, technology, finance, household products, and real estate. Attendees explored local products, met business owners, and accessed special deals.

This fair highlights continued activity and growth in Ghana's local business and SME landscape. The broad industry representation, from technology to real estate, points to diverse opportunities for partnership, acquisition, or supply chain integration. Such platforms offer valuable insights into local consumer demand and the expansion potential of domestic brands.

Investors should observe the frequency and scale of future SME exhibitions. These events serve as key indicators of entrepreneurial vitality and market trends across various sectors in Ghana. They also provide direct avenues for engaging with promising local businesses.
