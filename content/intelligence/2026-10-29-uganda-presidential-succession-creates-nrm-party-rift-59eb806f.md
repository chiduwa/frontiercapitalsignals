Africa Briefing reports that Uganda's President Museveni has initiated a debate over his succession, revealing internal divisions within his ruling NRM party. These divisions center on the growing political ambitions of his son, army chief General Muhoozi. Senior officials are reportedly divided over his son’s ambitions, and an adviser alleges threats for opposing Muhoozi.

Political succession and stability are key factors for investor confidence. The reported rift within the ruling party and the involvement of the army chief could signal potential political uncertainty in Uganda. Such developments can impact long-term policy predictability and the broader business environment, which investors monitor closely.

Investors should watch for further developments regarding the succession debate and the reported divisions within the NRM party. Any escalation of internal disputes or allegations of threats could indicate increasing political risk. Observing how these internal party dynamics evolve will be crucial for assessing Uganda's political stability.
