The Independent (Uganda) reports that a local company in Benin is giving new purpose to used cooking oil, which was previously discarded. This company, located at Ouedo-Kpossidja, north of Cotonou, is transforming the waste oil into useful products like soap and biofuel.

This development in Benin showcases a viable circular economy business model in Africa. For investors focused on Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda, it signals potential opportunities in waste valorization and sustainable manufacturing. The successful repurposing of used cooking oil into consumer and energy products suggests a market for enterprises that address waste management challenges while creating economic value.

Investors should monitor the potential for replication of such waste-to-product initiatives across the wider region. Watch for local regulatory environments in target markets that could support or hinder similar ventures. Also, observe the market acceptance and scalability of products like biofuel and soap derived from used cooking oil, as success in Benin could indicate broader regional market viability.
