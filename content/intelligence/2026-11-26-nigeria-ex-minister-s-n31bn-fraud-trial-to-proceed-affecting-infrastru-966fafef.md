Premium Times reports that a Nigerian judge has dismissed an application from former Minister Saleh Mamman, who sought to quash a nine-count fraud and forgery charge. This decision allows his trial to proceed regarding the alleged diversion of N31.07 billion. These funds were reportedly earmarked for the Mambilla and Zungeru hydroelectric power projects.

This development highlights ongoing governance and transparency risks within Nigeria's public infrastructure sector. For investors considering energy and infrastructure projects in the country, the case underscores potential challenges related to project funding allocation and execution integrity. The alleged diversion of substantial funds from critical power projects could affect investor confidence in the reliability of public sector partnerships.

Investors should monitor the progression of Mr. Mamman's trial for further insights into accountability mechanisms for public funds and project management. The outcome may provide clearer signals on the government's commitment to tackling corruption in large-scale infrastructure initiatives, which could influence future investment sentiment in these vital sectors.
