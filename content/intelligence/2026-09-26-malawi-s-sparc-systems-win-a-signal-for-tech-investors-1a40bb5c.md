Sparc Systems Limited, a Malawi-based company, has been named ICT Company of the Year at the prestigious TICON Africa Excellence Awards 2026. This significant pan-African recognition, presented in Livingstone, Zambia, firmly establishes Sparc Systems as a leading technology provider within the continent.

This win matters to investors. It signals a strong, regionally competitive tech player operating out of Malawi, a market often overlooked but demonstrating growing digital maturity. Sparc Systems' proven capabilities present a compelling case for strategic partnerships or direct equity investment into an established African tech asset with clear growth potential.
