Elektron Energy is preparing to switch on a 40-megawatt power plant in Victoria Island, Lagos. This new facility provides a significant, dedicated power option for businesses located in one of Nigeria's primary commercial districts.

Reliable power remains a key constraint for Nigerian businesses. This capacity directly addresses operational inefficiencies, potentially enhancing productivity, lowering costs, and making Victoria Island more attractive for further investment. It underscores ongoing private sector commitment to vital infrastructure development.

Monitor the plant's operational performance, service uptake, and pricing models for businesses. Watch for similar embedded power projects emerging in other strategic commercial zones across Lagos and other major cities, alongside any supporting regulatory frameworks.
