The Standard reports that Kenya is seeking private investment to expand its electricity transmission network. This initiative comes as rising demand, existing infrastructure constraints, and the cost of new power lines are putting pressure on the national grid.

For investors, this signals a policy shift towards private sector involvement in critical national infrastructure. It highlights a clear need within Kenya's energy sector, driven by both growing demand and the financial burden of expansion. Opportunities could emerge for firms specializing in power transmission, construction, and related financial instruments.

We will monitor further announcements regarding the scope and mechanisms for private sector participation. Details on specific projects, investment frameworks, and government incentives will be key to understanding the actionable opportunities in Kenya's power infrastructure.
