Africa Briefing reports that Ghana has launched two port projects in Takoradi valued at $235.41 million. These projects include a new ship-repair facility and an expanded cargo terminal, specifically Berth 19, which will enhance mineral cargo capacity.

This development signals a strategic investment in Ghana's maritime infrastructure, aiming to capture more business from the Gulf of Guinea. The new shipyard targets the regional ship-repair market, while Berth 19's expansion is set to increase mineral cargo throughput. These initiatives could enhance trade flows and logistics efficiency, potentially benefiting sectors involved in mineral exports and maritime services.

Investors should monitor the progress of these projects and their impact on trade volumes through Takoradi port. Keep an eye on new business attracted from the Gulf of Guinea and any increases in mineral cargo throughput, which could indicate related opportunities in logistics, shipping, or mining support services.
