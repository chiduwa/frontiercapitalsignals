Nigeria's Federal Government has commenced national mobilization for the second cohort of its N2.5 billion Student Venture Capital Grant (S-VCG). This program provides critical early-stage capital to student entrepreneurs across tertiary institutions, aiming to stimulate youth-led business creation.

This initiative signals strong government commitment to nurturing domestic innovation and a robust pipeline of investable early-stage ventures. It effectively de-risks initial startup phases for student-led businesses, creating potential co-investment or follow-on opportunities for private capital funds in key sectors.

Investors should track the types of ventures funded and their early market traction. Success in this second cohort could attract further foreign direct investment into Nigeria's burgeoning startup ecosystem, potentially leading to more substantial funding rounds and strategic partnerships for these enterprises.
