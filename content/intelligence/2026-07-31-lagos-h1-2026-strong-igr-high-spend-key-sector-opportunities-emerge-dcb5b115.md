Lagos State generated N1.69 trillion in the first half of 2026, with 69% sourced from its strong Internally Generated Revenue. The state also expended over 91% of this income, indicating rapid deployment of funds.

This robust IGR performance highlights Lagos's economic self-reliance and significant growth capacity, positioning it as a compelling sub-national investment hub. High expenditure, however, signals potential fiscal tightness or a focus on operational costs, creating specific opportunities in underspending sectors like health and education where capital needs remain high.
