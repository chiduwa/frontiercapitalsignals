The Independent (Uganda) reports that the Ugandan government has launched a new National Energy Compact. This compact aims to achieve 85% electricity access and 50% access to modern cooking solutions nationwide by 2030. Key objectives include expanding energy infrastructure, attracting private investment, and addressing existing weaknesses in the country’s electricity supply and distribution systems.

This initiative indicates a significant government commitment to scaling up Uganda's energy sector. The explicit mention of attracting private investment suggests potential opportunities across power generation, transmission, distribution, and clean cooking solution providers. It highlights a policy-driven push for growth in a critical sector.

Investors should monitor how the government plans to implement the compact, particularly the specific mechanisms and incentives designed to attract private capital. Details regarding upcoming infrastructure projects and any related regulatory reforms will be crucial next steps to watch.
