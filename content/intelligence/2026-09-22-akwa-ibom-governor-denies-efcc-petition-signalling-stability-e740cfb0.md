Akwa Ibom State Governor Umo Eno has denied reports claiming he petitioned the Economic and Financial Crime Commission (EFCC) against his predecessor, Udom Emmanuel, concerning property ownership in Lagos. The current administration has distanced itself from allegations of involving the anti-graft agency in an inter-governmental dispute.

Political disputes, particularly those involving high-profile figures and anti-graft agencies like the EFCC, introduce significant political risk and uncertainty. Governor Eno's swift denial aims to stabilize the political narrative and reassure investors by avoiding an appearance of governance instability or internal friction, which investors typically avoid.

Investors should monitor any further reports or official statements regarding the alleged property dispute or the broader relationship between Governor Eno and ex-Governor Emmanuel. Any resurgence of such allegations or visible political infighting could erode confidence in the state's governance and investment climate.
