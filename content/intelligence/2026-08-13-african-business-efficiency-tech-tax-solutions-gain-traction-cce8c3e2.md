Deloitte Africa and Refuel have formed a strategic alliance to streamline businesses' claims for SARS diesel rebates. This partnership combines Deloitte's tax and advisory expertise with Refuel's fuel tracking technology, creating a practical "tech plus tax" solution for sectors like mining and agriculture.

For investors, this collaboration signals an increasing professionalization of tax and regulatory compliance services across the African continent. Such solutions reduce operational burdens, improve cash flow, and enhance efficiency for businesses navigating complex tax regimes. It makes companies in sectors benefiting from rebates more attractive by effectively monetizing available incentives, freeing up capital for growth.

We will monitor the broader adoption of this "tech plus tax" model beyond South Africa and its specific application to other tax incentives or rebates in key African markets. Observe how competing advisory firms or local tech providers respond. Look for tangible improvements in financial performance among companies leveraging such efficiency-driven solutions.
