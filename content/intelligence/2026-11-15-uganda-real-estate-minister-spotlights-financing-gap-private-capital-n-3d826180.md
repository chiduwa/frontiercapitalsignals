The Independent (Uganda) reports the real estate sector faces stiff demand, with current supply far below capacity, primarily due to financing challenges. Finance Minister Henry Musasizi indicated that this situation requires private investment and financing affordable for households and developers, also suggesting mortgage refinancing could aid lenders.

This signals a significant investment opportunity in Uganda's real estate market, where fundamental demand outstrips supply due to capital constraints. The minister's comments suggest government recognition of the financing gap and a potential policy inclination towards attracting private capital and improving liquidity, possibly through mortgage refinancing, which could benefit lenders and developers.

Investors should monitor any future government actions or policy pronouncements aimed at facilitating private investment or enhancing access to affordable financing in the real estate sector. The development and adoption of real estate investment trusts (REITs) in Uganda will also be an important trend to watch.
