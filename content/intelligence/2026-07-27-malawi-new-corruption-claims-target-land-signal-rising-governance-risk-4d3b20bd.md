Human rights activist Sylvester Namiwa alleges corruption has significantly worsened under Malawi's ruling Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) government, surpassing previous levels. He specifically points to fresh claims of senior officials unlawfully snapping up prime land.

These allegations directly impact investor confidence, signaling increased governance risk and potential land tenure insecurity. Unchecked corruption can raise operational costs, complicate asset acquisition, and introduce significant regulatory uncertainty for both existing and prospective investments in Malawi.

Investors should closely monitor the government's response to these serious allegations and any subsequent investigations. Watch for public sentiment, civil society actions, and how these claims may influence the political landscape and future land administration policies in Malawi.
