East Africa is projected to lead continental growth in 2025. The African Development Bank Group estimates real GDP growth accelerating significantly to 6.6 percent, up from 4.3 percent in 2024. This confirms its status as the continent’s fastest-growing region.

This accelerated growth rate signals robust economic tailwinds for region-focused portfolios. Strong GDP performance typically correlates with improved consumer spending, business expansion, and investment opportunities in key sectors. While some individual economies lag, the regional aggregate points to a generally favorable investment climate.

Monitor the $119 billion annual financing gap identified by the AfDB. How this gap is addressed, whether through foreign direct investment, domestic capital mobilization, or multilateral support, will dictate the sustainability and quality of this growth. Also, track individual country performance within the region, as not all economies are participating equally.
