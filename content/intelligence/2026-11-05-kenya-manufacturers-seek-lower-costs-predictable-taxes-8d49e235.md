Capital FM Business reports that Kenyan manufacturers have urged the government to reduce the cost of doing business. Their key demands include lower electricity prices and predictable tax policies. The report also mentions the removal of unspecified multiple burdens.

This push from manufacturers signals ongoing challenges to profitability and operational efficiency for businesses in Kenya. High input costs, particularly for electricity, combined with unpredictable tax environments, can deter new investment and hinder expansion plans across various sectors. For international investors, this highlights potential headwinds affecting local production and supply chains.

Investors should closely watch for any government announcements or policy changes in response to these appeals. Movement towards lowering energy costs or establishing clearer tax frameworks could significantly improve the operating environment. Conversely, inaction might indicate continued structural challenges for industrial growth in Kenya.
