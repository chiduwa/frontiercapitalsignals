Africa Briefing reports the global rare-earth magnet market is forecast to reach $39.23 billion by 2034. The non-China supply segment is projected to grow 10.85 percent annually. This report indicates that African mining projects are advancing, suggesting potential for increased regional production.

This significant market growth, driven by demand from carmakers and wind-energy companies, creates potential for African rare-earth producers. Africa Briefing notes that African producers could find new buyers, with permanent magnet sales forecast to reach $39.23 billion by 2034. The progression of African mining projects signals movement toward meeting this demand.

A key challenge identified by Africa Briefing is refining capacity, which remains a major obstacle for African rare-earth producers. Investors should monitor developments in refining infrastructure and potential partnerships that could address this bottleneck to unlock the full market potential.
