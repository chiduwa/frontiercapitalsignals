The Standard reports that Afreximbank has disbursed a Sh126 billion ($975 million) financing facility. This funding is intended to part-fund the management buyout of Oryx Energies SA, which serves as the holding company for Addax Energy SA.

This substantial capital deployment by a key regional financial institution highlights robust M&A activity within Africa's energy sector. For investors, it signals continued interest and available financing for large-scale energy deals, potentially pointing to consolidation or strategic restructuring trends. Afreximbank's involvement underscores confidence in the sector's long-term prospects.

Investors should closely monitor the implications of this buyout on the broader African energy market. Watch for further details on Oryx Energies SA's strategic direction post-acquisition and any ripple effects on regional energy supply chains or infrastructure development. This deal could set a precedent for future M&A in the energy sector, indicating potential avenues for both direct investment and growth through partnerships.
