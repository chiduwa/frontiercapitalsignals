Premium Times reports that the Nigerian government has launched a USSD credit service. As of October 31, CREDICORP reported that 353,561 Nigerians had accessed credit through this program, with over N61.40 billion disbursed. This service is designed to cover household needs and solar solutions for Nigerians.

This development signifies a significant government-backed initiative to expand financial inclusion and consumer lending in Nigeria. The substantial number of beneficiaries and disbursed amount indicate considerable demand for accessible credit. For investors, this could point to opportunities within consumer finance, fintech platforms, and sectors benefiting from increased household purchasing power, particularly in renewable energy given the explicit mention of solar.

Investors should monitor the program's expansion, particularly the types of household needs being met and the uptake of solar financing. The sustainability and recovery rates of the disbursed credit will be key indicators of the program's long-term impact and potential for further private sector involvement in similar credit schemes.
