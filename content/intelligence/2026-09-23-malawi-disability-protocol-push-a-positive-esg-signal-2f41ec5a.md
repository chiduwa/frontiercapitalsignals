Disability advocates, including Sightsavers and FEDOMA, are pressing Malawi to domesticate the African Disability Protocol (ADP). This push aims to strengthen protections against discrimination and exclusion for women with disabilities, following a recent strategy workshop in Blantyre.

Domestication of the ADP would signal Malawi's commitment to social inclusion and human rights, enhancing its Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) profile. This move suggests an improving regulatory environment that prioritizes social equity, potentially fostering a more stable and predictable operating landscape for businesses. Inclusive policies also expand the accessible talent pool and consumer base, benefiting long-term economic growth.

Investors should monitor the Malawian government's response to this advocacy and the timeline for ADP domestication. Observe legislative developments and any related policy shifts that demonstrate progress on social inclusion. Successful implementation could indicate a deepening commitment to a fair and productive society, a positive long-term indicator for sustained investment.
