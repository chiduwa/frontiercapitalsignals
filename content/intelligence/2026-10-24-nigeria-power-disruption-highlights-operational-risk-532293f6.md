Premium Times reports that residents in Makurdi, Nigeria, have experienced a prolonged electricity disconnection. This outage is linked to an attack on JED staff, leading to complaints about heat, business losses, and difficulties charging devices.

This incident highlights operational risks within Nigeria's power sector and the broader impact of insecurity on essential services. Unplanned disruptions, even if triggered by external events, can significantly impact local businesses through lost revenue and increased operational costs. For investors considering or operating in regions dependent on grid power, the reliability of local infrastructure and the security environment surrounding utility operations are critical factors influencing market stability and potential returns.

Investors should monitor the duration of such disconnections and the measures utility providers and local authorities implement to prevent similar occurrences and ensure service continuity. The ability to mitigate these risks will be key to investor confidence in the broader Nigerian market, particularly in sectors sensitive to stable power supply.
