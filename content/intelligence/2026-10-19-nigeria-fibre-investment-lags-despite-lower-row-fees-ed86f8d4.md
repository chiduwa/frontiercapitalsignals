TechCabal reports that Nigeria's fibre deployment figures demonstrate that reduced Right of Way fees have not been sufficient to attract investment. The lowered fees alone have also failed to close the country's connectivity gaps.

This signals to investors that policy adjustments, such as reduced administrative fees, may not be the primary impediment to infrastructure development in Nigeria. The market's inability to attract investment despite these concessions suggests deeper, systemic challenges are at play, potentially hindering returns and growth for infrastructure projects.

Investors should watch for further insights into the specific non-fee related barriers to fibre infrastructure investment in Nigeria. Understanding these underlying issues is crucial for evaluating future opportunities and risks, as isolated policy incentives appear insufficient to drive the desired investment outcomes.
