Niger has signed a $1.9 billion deal with Canadian firm Zimar to construct an oil refinery in Dosso. However, Zimar's reported lack of verifiable track record is raising serious credibility questions, even internally within the Nigerien government.

This situation underscores the substantial governance and due diligence risks inherent in large-scale African infrastructure projects. Investors monitoring regional energy development must factor in the potential for project delays, financial irregularities, or outright failure when partner credibility is compromised.

We need to watch for further official statements from Niger on Zimar's financial backing and vetting process. Any international financial institution response or new disclosures about Zimar's capacity will be critical indicators for both this project and the broader investment climate for similar ventures.
