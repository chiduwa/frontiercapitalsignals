Kenya's courts are increasingly scrutinizing President Ruto's core economic agenda, including major infrastructure projects and privatization plans. A recent Safaricom ruling specifically disrupted a crucial financing strategy, highlighting judicial challenges to the administration's executive authority and constitutional guardrails.

This growing judicial assertiveness creates significant regulatory uncertainty for investors. It introduces potential delays and legal risks to government-backed initiatives and public-private partnerships, potentially impacting project timelines and financial viability. Investors must now carefully factor in these evolving legal dynamics.

Moving forward, watch for the Ruto administration's strategic responses to these judicial setbacks. Pay close attention to future court decisions on key privatization efforts and flagship projects. Assess the government's capacity to adapt its economic program while navigating these constitutional constraints.
