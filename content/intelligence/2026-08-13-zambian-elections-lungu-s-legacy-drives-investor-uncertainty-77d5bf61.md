Zambia faces critical national elections on August 13, overshadowed by a rising cost of living, significant governance challenges, and broader economic concerns. The enduring legacy of former President Edgar Lungu is poised to heavily influence voter sentiment in this devoutly religious nation, indicating a complex political landscape.

For investors, this election presents heightened political risk and potential for policy shifts. The influence of Lungu's past governance on the current electorate means any new administration, or even a re-elected one, must navigate expectations tied to previous policies and their economic outcomes. This uncertainty could impact market stability, regulatory environments, and investor confidence in key sectors.

Moving forward, closely monitor the election results and the immediate market reaction. Key indicators will be the new government's stance on economic reforms, its approach to tackling the cost of living crisis, and how it addresses or distances itself from the 'ghost' of past governance issues. Clear policy direction will be crucial for reassuring capital markets.
