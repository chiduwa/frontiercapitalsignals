TechCabal reports that Onafriq has ceased operations for Baxi, its agent banking business in Nigeria, after five years. This venture was a significant investment by Onafriq into Nigeria's offline payments economy.

Baxi's core strategy involved establishing a large network of agents to deliver financial services to customers primarily using cash. The shutdown of this operation suggests potential challenges in executing or sustaining such models, despite the substantial reliance on cash within the target market. Investors should consider the viability and scalability of agent-led financial inclusion initiatives in similar economies.

Going forward, investors should observe how other companies operating in Nigeria's offline payments sector adapt their strategies. This event could indicate broader shifts in the approach to serving cash-heavy populations with financial services and may influence investment decisions regarding agent-based financial service delivery across the region.
