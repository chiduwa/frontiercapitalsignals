A Lilongwe man reportedly shot a bar owner after being asked to pay a K116,000 beer debt. Romario Lolenzo, 31, allegedly reacted violently when confronted by the establishment's owner, Fauzia Issa, regarding the outstanding amount. Police have confirmed the incident.

This extreme response, though an isolated criminal act, signals potential operational risks for businesses in Malawi, particularly concerning debt enforcement and the security environment.
