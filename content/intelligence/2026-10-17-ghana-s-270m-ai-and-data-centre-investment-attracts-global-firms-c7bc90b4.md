MyJoyOnline Business reports that President John Dramani Mahama announced a $250 million investment in April 2026 for a world-class AI Computing Centre in Ghana. An additional $20 million will fund the rollout of the country's National AI Strategy. International data centre operators, including Digital Realty, Africa Data Centres, PAIX, and Equinix, are noted to be staking claims in the market.

This substantial investment signals Ghana's strategic commitment to advancing its artificial intelligence and digital infrastructure capabilities. The engagement of prominent international data centre operators underscores increasing foreign investor interest in Ghana's emerging digital economy, potentially opening doors for related technology and service sector investments.

Investors should closely track the execution of the AI Computing Centre project and the National AI Strategy. Monitoring infrastructure development timelines, evolving AI regulatory landscapes, and the emergence of further international tech partnerships will be crucial. These developments could significantly influence Ghana's appeal as a regional technology hub.
