Premium Times reports Nigeria's Power Minister, Mr. Tegbe, has apologized to Nigerians for recent electricity outages. He also promised an improved electricity supply in the future. The report indicates that President Bola Tinubu shares these concerns regarding unreliable electricity supply, and the government is doing everything possible to address the challenges.

This development signals a clear government acknowledgment of infrastructure deficiencies in Nigeria's power sector.
