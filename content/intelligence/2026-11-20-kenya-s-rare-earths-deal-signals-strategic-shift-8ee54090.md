Africa Briefing reports that Kenya has chosen a US-led Frontier consortium to develop its Mrima Hill rare earths and niobium deposit. This decision provides US companies with a potential source of strategically important minerals.

This development is significant for investors, as Washington seeks to reduce China’s grip on these critical resources. The move signals increased strategic interest and potential investment in Kenya’s mining sector, aligning with broader geopolitical objectives.

However, there are factors to watch closely. Africa Briefing notes that two rival bidders are threatening procurement challenges. Furthermore, issues regarding local refining and community consent for the project remain unresolved. These points introduce uncertainty and potential delays for the project’s progression.
