Malawi's parliamentary inquiry into the Saulos Chilima aircraft crash faces scrutiny. A key witness, Ephraim Duma, claimed the Dornier 228-202 hovered just two meters above ground. Aviation experts immediately dismissed this testimony as scientifically improbable.

This incident highlights potential issues with transparency and the integrity of official processes within Malawi. For investors, the credibility of such high-profile investigations directly reflects the robustness of the country's governance and regulatory environment. Disregard for scientific evidence in official inquiries can erode confidence and increase perceived political and operational risks.

Monitor how this inquiry proceeds, specifically if it pivots towards evidence-based conclusions. The outcome will signal the government's commitment to objective truth and the rule of law. A failure to deliver a credible, scientific explanation could indicate persistent institutional fragilities, affecting investor sentiment and long-term stability.
