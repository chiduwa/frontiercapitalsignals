Capital FM Business reports that Kenya is proposing a new policy aimed at attracting investment in its blue economy. This policy includes tax breaks and other incentives. The proposed policy targets sectors such as fisheries, ports, and shipping, alongside other blue economy activities.

This development signals potential new opportunities for local and foreign investors. The proposed incentives could significantly enhance the attractiveness and profitability of ventures within Kenya's blue economy sectors. Investors should note the government's clear intent to stimulate growth in these areas.

Investors should closely monitor the progression of this proposed policy. Key details to watch for include the finalization of the policy, the specific nature and duration of the tax breaks, and other incentives, which remain to be fully defined.
