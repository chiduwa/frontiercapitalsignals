Malawi's Local Government Minister, Ben Phiri, has affirmed that the K5 billion Constituency Development Fund (CDF) will remain untouched, rejecting calls for cuts. Phiri confirmed development planning is now fully devolved to local councils, shifting resource control from central government to the constituencies.

This decision confirms consistent government commitment to decentralized development, directly funding local infrastructure and service projects. The K5 billion allocation presents immediate opportunities for businesses in construction, supply chains, and related services operating at the local level, signaling stable demand for regional projects.

Investors should closely monitor the transparency and efficiency of CDF project implementation by local councils. Watch for specific tenders, procurement announcements, and the impact on local economic activity. The government's continued ability to fund and oversee these decentralized initiatives will be key.
