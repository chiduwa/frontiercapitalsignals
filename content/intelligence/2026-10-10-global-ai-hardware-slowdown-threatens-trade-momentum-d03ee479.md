Premium Times reports UNCTAD warned that a potential slowdown in demand for AI hardware could remove a major source of momentum from global merchandise trade. The organization also highlighted that while the AI boom has lifted trade, it has simultaneously raised financial risks.

This global outlook signals potential headwinds for international trade, which could impact markets across Africa, including Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda. A reduction in global merchandise trade momentum could affect demand for exports from these countries and influence overall economic stability and investor confidence. This creates uncertainty for economies linked to global supply chains and trade flows.

Investors should monitor global economic indicators, particularly those related to technology demand and international trade volumes. The direct impact on specific sectors or countries within the region is not detailed, but a general slowdown in global trade momentum is a factor to consider for strategic planning.
