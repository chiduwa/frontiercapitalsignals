Capital FM Business reports that KPC Plc-owned Morendat National Polytechnic is scaling up its training program for certified pipeline welders. This expansion is designed to address the growing demand for skilled labor within Kenya's energy sector.

This development is significant as it points to potential investment opportunities within Kenya's energy and supporting infrastructure sectors. The increased demand for skilled technicians is directly attributed to major ongoing projects, specifically Dangote's Lamu Refinery and the South Lokichar oil developments. Such skill development initiatives suggest sustained project activity and a focus on local capacity building, which can enhance project efficiency and reduce reliance on external expertise.

Investors should closely track the progress and timelines of the Dangote's Lamu Refinery and South Lokichar oil developments. Additionally, observe if other training institutions begin offering similar specialized programs. This broader trend would further confirm sustained growth in the energy sector and potentially reveal more opportunities in associated services and local supply chains.
