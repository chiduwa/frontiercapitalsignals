Kenya faced 11.12 billion cyber threats in the year to June 2026, a 29% increase from the previous year. DDoS attacks saw the sharpest rise, doubling by 114.3%. These figures, from the Communications Authority of Kenya, highlight a rapidly escalating digital threat landscape.

This surge significantly impacts investors. Businesses operating in Kenya face increased operational risks, potential service disruptions, and higher costs for robust cybersecurity measures. Conversely, it creates substantial opportunities for tech investors specializing in cybersecurity solutions, resilient digital infrastructure, and data protection services, driven by urgent corporate and governmental demand.

Investors should watch for enhanced regulatory responses from the CA, including potential new compliance mandates. Monitor government and corporate spending allocations for cybersecurity initiatives. Also, observe which specific sectors, such as finance and telecommunications, become early adopters of advanced security technologies.
