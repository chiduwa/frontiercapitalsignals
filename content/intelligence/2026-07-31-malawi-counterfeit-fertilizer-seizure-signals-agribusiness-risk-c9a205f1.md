Malawian authorities seized over 600 bags of counterfeit fertilizer, packaged under the ETG brand. This prompted a government warning against fake agricultural inputs, with the Ministry of Agriculture expressing concern over potential poor harvests and farmer losses.

This incident underscores significant supply chain and regulatory risks within Malawi's agricultural sector. Investors in agribusiness, particularly those involved in input distribution or off-take agreements, face increased exposure to fraud, reputational damage, and potential default on farmer loans due to poor yields.

Monitor government response to combat counterfeiting, including enforcement and subsidy program integrity. Observe ETG's response and any broader industry initiatives to secure supply chains. This event will likely influence farmer purchasing behavior and crop output estimates for the upcoming season.
