Capital FM Business reports that Ruto has ordered research into Gikomba fires and economic sabotage in Kenya. This research is intended to inform future policies and interventions.

These upcoming policies and interventions aim to protect the lives, livelihoods, and investments of traders operating in the informal economy. This sector is significant, accounting for over 80% of employment in Kenya. For international investors, initiatives to stabilize or formalize such a large segment of the economy could reduce operational risks or reveal new market opportunities. The precise nature and timing of these interventions, however, are yet to be determined.

Investors should closely monitor the outcomes of this research for specific policy recommendations. Any future announcements regarding interventions in the informal economy, especially those pertaining to investment protection or regulatory changes, will be key signals for potential market impacts and adjustments.
