Africa Briefing reports that BRICS researchers are advocating for a joint payments network. The goal is to make trade less dependent on the US dollar, with researchers prioritizing this over the more challenging ambition of a common BRICS currency.

This development could be significant for African markets, as Africa Briefing indicates it could lead to more trade settlement options. For investors, this signals potential shifts in international trade dynamics and could impact the cost and ease of cross-border transactions for businesses operating in the region.

Investors should monitor the progress in building this BRICS payments system. The critical next step will be to observe how these new trade settlement options materialize for countries in Africa and their potential impact on regional trade flows and financial infrastructure.
