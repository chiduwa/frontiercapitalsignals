The US Congress has officially renewed the African Growth and Opportunity Act, extending its provisions until 2028. This bipartisan move, with the House following the Senate's lead, ensures continued duty-free access for eligible African goods to the American market for another five years.

This extension significantly de-risks trade and investment decisions for companies operating in our focus markets, including Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda. It provides critical predictability, stabilizing supply chains and enhancing the competitive advantage of African exporters. Investors should view this as a clear signal of sustained US commitment to fostering trade, encouraging capital deployment into manufacturing, textiles, and agricultural processing geared for export.

Going forward, we will be monitoring how individual countries in our portfolio leverage this extended window. Look for targeted government incentives, infrastructure improvements, and private sector initiatives aimed at maximizing AGOA benefits. Key indicators will include new foreign direct investment, export diversification, and progress toward enhancing local value chains beyond 2028's horizon.
