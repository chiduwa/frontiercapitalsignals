The High Street Journal reports that Ghana will host GEXPO 2026 from November 18–22 at Black Star Square. The event is set to feature a dedicated Export School and a series of seminars.

This initiative aims to equip Ghanaian Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) to enter and expand global markets. For investors, this could signal a strategic push to boost Ghana's export capacity and competitiveness. Improved export capabilities may open new avenues for investment in export-oriented sectors and supporting logistics or services.

Investors should monitor the outcomes of the GEXPO 2026 Export School and seminars, particularly their effectiveness in helping SMEs access and grow in international markets. Observing the types of sectors and products that benefit most from this program could indicate emerging investment opportunities.
