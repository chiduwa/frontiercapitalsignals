MyJoyOnline Business reports that the Ghana National Chamber of Commerce and Industry (GNCCI) has urged the government to deepen its engagement with the private sector. This call is specifically in the context of the government's New Economy Agenda.

The GNCCI insists that businesses must play a central role in shaping economic reforms, job creation, and growth. This highlights the private sector's desire for greater influence on economic policy. Increased private sector input could lead to more business-friendly regulations and policies, potentially fostering a more conducive investment environment in Ghana.

Investors should monitor the government's response to this call for deeper private sector engagement. Observing whether the New Economy Agenda incorporates specific feedback or proposals from the business community will be key. This could indicate the government's openness to private sector partnerships and its commitment to improving the business climate.
