Capital FM Business reports that Kenya's outgoing Data Commissioner has warned the country risks losing leverage over its data. This concern stems from Kenya's reliance on foreign technology infrastructure, which the Commissioner stated leaves the country struggling.

This situation signals potential shifts in Kenya's regulatory landscape for digital infrastructure and data management. Investors should recognize the growing emphasis on national data sovereignty and control. A public warning about struggling leverage could precede policy initiatives to build local capacity or reduce dependence on foreign technology providers.

Investors should monitor future policy developments or regulatory changes in Kenya concerning data storage and digital infrastructure. Watch for any government initiatives aimed at strengthening domestic control or introducing new requirements for technology firms operating within the country. Such measures could create new market opportunities or challenges for existing investments.
