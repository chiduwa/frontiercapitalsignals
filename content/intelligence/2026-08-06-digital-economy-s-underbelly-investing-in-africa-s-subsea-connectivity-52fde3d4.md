Digital services, from online payments to advanced AI queries, are not instantaneous or weightless. They fundamentally depend on a complex physical system comprising fibre networks, internet exchanges, data centres, and, critically, a vast network of submarine cables that form the backbone of global connectivity.

This essential infrastructure is not exclusive to South Africa, but a foundational requirement for all developing digital economies across the continent. Investors should recognize the escalating demand for robust and resilient connectivity, driven by rapid internet penetration and digital transformation in markets like Nigeria, Kenya, and Ghana. Stable access to these undersea networks directly impacts business continuity, data sovereignty, and the growth trajectory of local tech ecosystems.

Monitor new cable landing projects, data centre expansions, and regulatory policies that support digital infrastructure development in key African markets. Pay close attention to public-private partnerships aimed at enhancing last-mile connectivity and regional data exchange, signaling long-term growth potential in this vital sector.
