Africa Briefing reports Rio Tinto has opened two lithium facilities in Argentina following a combined investment of about $1.4bn. These facilities are expected to add 25,000 tonnes of annual production capacity once fully operational. The report also notes that Ghana's Ewoyaa project recently changed investment hands.

This global expansion, particularly Rio Tinto's increased capacity, indicates growing demand and supply in the lithium market. For investors in African markets, the change in investment hands for Ghana's Ewoyaa project suggests active deal-making and evolving opportunities within the continent's critical minerals sector. Africa Briefing further highlights Zimbabwe's processed lithium exports reaching $190m, underscoring the regional economic significance of lithium.

Investors should monitor how new global lithium production capacity affects market dynamics and pricing. More locally, tracking developments and plans of the new investors in Ghana's Ewoyaa project will be crucial. The continued growth in African lithium exports, as evidenced by Zimbabwe's figures, suggests sustained potential for related investments.
