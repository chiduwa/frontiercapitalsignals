Kenya experienced a substantial increase in cyber threats, with events reaching 11.12 billion in the year to June 2026. This marks a 29% rise from the previous year's figures, according to the Communications Authority of Kenya. Notably, Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks more than doubled, driving a significant portion of this surge.

This trend signals heightened operational risk for investors in Kenya's digitally-reliant sectors, including finance, e-commerce, and various tech-enabled services. Companies may face increased costs related to compliance, data protection, and business continuity, potentially impacting profitability and valuations. Conversely, the escalating threat landscape presents a compelling opportunity for investors in cybersecurity solutions and infrastructure resilience firms.

Investors should monitor both government and private sector responses to fortify digital infrastructure and implement advanced security protocols. Regulatory frameworks designed to enhance cyber resilience will also be crucial. This evolving environment will differentiate companies demonstrating robust security postures and spotlight the growing market for innovative cybersecurity ventures.
