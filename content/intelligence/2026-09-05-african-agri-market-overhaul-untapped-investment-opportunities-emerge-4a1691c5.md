A recent panel on agricultural transformation in Kigali warned that viable farming businesses across Africa depend on fixing inefficient agricultural markets. Sara Mbago-Bhunu of IFAD emphasized the critical need for governments and development partners to prioritize market-centric solutions for agricultural goods.

This highlights significant untapped potential within the agricultural value chain across our target markets, including Malawi, Kenya, and Uganda. Inefficient markets suppress productivity and consumer access, but crucially, present clear opportunities for investment in logistics, storage, processing, and tech-enabled market access solutions. Savvy investors can position themselves to facilitate this necessary overhaul and capture substantial returns.

Next, watch for concrete government policy shifts and development partner initiatives focusing on market infrastructure and regulatory reforms. Look for new public-private partnerships or funding announcements targeting cold chain logistics, digital marketplaces, and agro-processing facilities. These actions will signal tangible progress towards unlocking the sector's immense growth potential.
