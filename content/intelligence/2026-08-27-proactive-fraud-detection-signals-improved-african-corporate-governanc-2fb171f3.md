SAS and FACTS Consulting are pushing for organizations to adopt continuous monitoring for procurement fraud, shifting from reactive investigations to proactive detection at the point of suspicious activity. This call follows a recent client session in Johannesburg, highlighting the urgency of preventing financial losses before they escalate.

This development matters to investors as procurement fraud significantly erodes profitability and shareholder value. Increased adoption of real-time fraud prevention systems signals a maturing corporate governance landscape across African markets. Companies implementing such solutions can demonstrate improved financial integrity, lower operational risks, and potentially attract higher valuations from discerning international investors.

Investors should monitor the uptake of continuous monitoring technologies, especially among listed companies and large enterprises in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda, and Malawi. Watch for new regulatory guidelines or industry best practices emphasizing proactive fraud management. Track the financial performance of early adopters, looking for tangible improvements in cash flow and reduced operational losses.
