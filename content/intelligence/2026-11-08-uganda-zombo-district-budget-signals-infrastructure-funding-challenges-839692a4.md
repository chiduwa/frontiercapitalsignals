Nile Post reports Zombo District's proposed Shs37.7 billion budget for FY2027/28 allocates nearly two-thirds to salaries. This raises questions about the funds available for infrastructure and service delivery projects within the district, as the government pursues its Tenfold Growth Strategy.

This budget structure could impact investment opportunities in public works and service provision within the district. Investors targeting Uganda's local government projects or those reliant on public infrastructure development may face slower project pipelines or reduced demand. The focus on salaries may also signal broader fiscal pressures at the local level, potentially affecting the pace of the government's Tenfold Growth Strategy.

Investors should monitor how other districts structure their budgets and the central government's response to these local fiscal challenges. The practical implementation of the Tenfold Growth Strategy, especially regarding resource allocation for infrastructure versus recurrent expenditures, will be a key indicator for project development and execution risks.
