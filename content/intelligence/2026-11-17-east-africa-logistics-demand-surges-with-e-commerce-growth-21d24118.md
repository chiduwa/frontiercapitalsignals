The Standard reports that East African businesses are increasingly turning to outsourced logistics. This trend is a direct result of the growth in online commerce, which is driving up demand for transportation services across the entire supply chain.

This development signals a significant expansion in the logistics and transportation sector within East Africa. For investors, the increased demand for comprehensive supply chain services presents opportunities in logistics infrastructure, technology, and specialized service providers. The reported growth of online commerce is a key catalyst for this expanding market.

Moving forward, investors should track the sustained growth of online commerce within the region and how this translates into specific needs across the supply chain. Monitoring the capacity and technological adoption of existing logistics providers will help identify areas ripe for new investment or strategic partnerships.
