This development complicates stability in the Sahel, a region already grappling with significant security challenges. Geopolitical realignments, particularly involving external actors like Ukraine and the US, introduce new layers of risk and uncertainty. Investors should assess potential ripple effects on regional stability, supply chains, and political risk across West Africa.
