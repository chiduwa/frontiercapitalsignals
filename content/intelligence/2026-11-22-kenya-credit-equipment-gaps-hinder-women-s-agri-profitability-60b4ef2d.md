Capital FM Business reports that women-led agribusinesses in Kenya face limited access to affordable credit and agricultural equipment. This situation constrains their profitability, even though women farmers are generating higher revenue per hectare.

This dynamic highlights significant untapped potential within Kenya's agricultural sector. Investors in financial services or agricultural technology may find opportunities in addressing these credit and equipment gaps. Enabling these businesses could unlock greater overall sector productivity and returns, given their reported efficiency in revenue per hectare.

Monitor for policy initiatives or private sector solutions aimed at improving financial inclusion and equipment access for women in agriculture. Developments in rural credit facilities or innovative equipment-sharing models could indicate promising avenues for investment or partnership.
