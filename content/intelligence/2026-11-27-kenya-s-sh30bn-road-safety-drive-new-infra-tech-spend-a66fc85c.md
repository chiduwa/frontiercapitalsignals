The Standard reports that Kenya has unveiled a Sh30 billion plan aimed at curbing road crashes. Transport CS Davis Chirchir stated this five-year road safety action plan will cover infrastructure safety, intelligent enforcement, and motorcycle safety.

This significant government spending commitment presents potential opportunities for infrastructure development firms, technology providers for enforcement solutions, and businesses in the motorcycle safety sector. The plan indicates a structured approach to a persistent national issue, which could improve business operating environments and logistics over time, particularly for transport-reliant industries.

Investors should monitor the specific tenders and project rollouts under the infrastructure, intelligent enforcement, and motorcycle safety components. Details on funding mechanisms and implementation timelines will be crucial for identifying actionable investment entry points and assessing the scale of opportunities.
