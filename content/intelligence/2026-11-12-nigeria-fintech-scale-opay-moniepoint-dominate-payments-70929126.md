TechCabal reports that OPay and Moniepoint, both holding microfinance banking licenses, have achieved significant scale in Nigeria's payments market. OPay has established itself in consumer payments, notably for transactions like Bolt driver payments. Moniepoint has carved a niche in merchant processing, frequently seen as terminals at local store checkouts.

This signals the continued growth and specialization within Nigeria's fintech sector. The strong market presence of these licensed fintechs suggests robust payment infrastructure development and adoption. Investors considering the Nigerian market may see these developments as indicators of sector maturity and potential for further financial inclusion and digital transaction growth.

Future developments to monitor include how these established players sustain their scale and potentially expand their offerings or geographical reach within Nigeria. Observing any new regulatory shifts impacting microfinance banking licenses or payment market operations would also be key.
