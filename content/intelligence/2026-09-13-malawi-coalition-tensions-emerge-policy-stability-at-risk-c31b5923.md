Malawi's Second Vice-President, Enock Chihana, is pushing the governing DPP to honor the "Blue Alliance Manifesto" pre-election pact. Details of this agreement, made before last year's general election between the DPP and AFORD, are anticipated to become public soon.

This signals potential political instability within Malawi's ruling coalition. Disagreements over pre-election pacts can disrupt governance, delay policy implementation, and create uncertainty for businesses and investors. Such internal friction may impact the government's focus on economic reforms and investment promotion.

Investors should monitor the disclosure of the Blue Alliance Manifesto's terms and the DPP's response. Watch for any cabinet reshuffles or political realignments, as these developments will indicate the extent of governance stability and potential shifts in policy priorities.
