Africa Briefing reports the Africa Finance Corporation (AFC) is targeting a 2027 financial close for the 830km Zambia-Lobito Railway. Construction contractors have been shortlisted for the rail link, and prospective customers have shown interest in transporting approximately 2 million tonnes of freight annually.

This development indicates solid progress for a major cross-border infrastructure initiative, which could substantially improve regional trade routes and logistics efficiency. The early demand for significant freight capacity suggests potential for strong utilization and economic impact once the line is operational. Investors should see this as a positive signal for regional development.

Investors should closely watch for further announcements regarding the finalization of contractor selection and the securing of full financing. The successful achievement of the 2027 financial close will be a critical milestone, signaling the project's firm trajectory and potential for related investment opportunities.
