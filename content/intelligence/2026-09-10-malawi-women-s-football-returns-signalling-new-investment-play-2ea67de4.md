Malawi's National Bank of Malawi (NBM) Women’s Premiership is set to resume after a nine-month pause. The Football Association of Malawi confirmed the 2026/27 season kickoff, explicitly targeting preparation for the global stage like the World Cup.

This signals renewed corporate confidence, with NBM actively backing a growing consumer segment. A resurgent league boosts the local entertainment economy, offering opportunities in media rights, event management, sports retail, and hospitality. It also reflects a positive social trajectory, attracting investors seeking markets with stable growth and increasing discretionary spending.
