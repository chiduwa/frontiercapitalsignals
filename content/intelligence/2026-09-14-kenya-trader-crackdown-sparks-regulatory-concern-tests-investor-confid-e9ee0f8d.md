Kenya's President Ruto has issued a 90-day ultimatum, mandating foreign traders obtain permits or leave. This directive has prompted widespread efforts for regularization across the sub-region and is challenging Kenya's long-standing reputation as an East African business haven and a symbol of Pan-African integration.

For investors, this move introduces significant regulatory uncertainty and potential operational hurdles, especially for businesses relying on cross-border talent or regional supply chains. It signals a more protectionist stance, which could deter foreign direct investment and complicate workforce planning for international enterprises.

Investors should closely track the enforcement of this ultimatum and any subsequent policy clarifications regarding foreign participation in local markets. Monitor its impact on regional trade dynamics, specific sector disruptions, and overall investor sentiment towards Kenya's business environment.
