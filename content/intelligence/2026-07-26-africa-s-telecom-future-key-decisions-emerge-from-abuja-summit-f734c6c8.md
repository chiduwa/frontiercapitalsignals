African ICT Ministers are meeting in Abuja, Nigeria, for the African Telecommunications Union (ATU) CPL-26 conference, July 23-24, 2026. The session will decide the future direction and policies of the ATU, setting regional standards for telecommunications and digital economies.

This conference is critical for regional ICT policy harmonization, spectrum allocation, and digital infrastructure strategies. Investors in telecommunications, digital services, and related infrastructure will see directly how future regulatory frameworks and investment landscapes could shift across multiple African markets. Decisions here can accelerate or hinder digital economy growth.

Monitor the outcomes of CPL-26 closely for specific policy directives, new agreements, or leadership changes within the ATU. These announcements will signal where regional ICT investment opportunities are likely to emerge or intensify, particularly in areas like 5G deployment, data centers, and cross-border digital services.
