TechCabal reports that data centres and cloud platforms are distinct, despite being frequently used interchangeably. The Central Bank of Nigeria mandates that all payment data must reside on local infrastructure by 2027.

This regulatory requirement makes developing local cloud platforms upon Nigeria's expanding data centre foundation critical. The demand for localized cloud services for payment data suggests potential growth and investment opportunities within Nigeria's tech infrastructure sector.

Investors should monitor developments in establishing these distinct local cloud platforms and the expansion of underlying data centre infrastructure in Nigeria, particularly given the approaching 2027 deadline for payment data localization.
