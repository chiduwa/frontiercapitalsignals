Malawi's Public Affairs Committee (PAC) has strongly condemned recent activist remarks suggesting First Vice President Jane Ansah could assume the presidency within two months. PAC labeled these claims dangerous and unconstitutional, emphasizing adherence to established succession protocols.

Such comments, even if refuted, create unnecessary political noise and can heighten perceptions of instability. Investors prioritize constitutional adherence and predictable political transitions, as any deviation or even suggestion of it introduces uncertainty, potentially impacting market confidence and policy continuity.

Monitor public discourse for similar unconstitutional claims and the official responses from political institutions and the electoral body. Continued strong condemnation and clear adherence to the constitutional framework will be crucial for maintaining investor confidence ahead of any future electoral or succession processes.
