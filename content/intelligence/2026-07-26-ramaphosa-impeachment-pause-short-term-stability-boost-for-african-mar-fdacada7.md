South Africa's Western Cape High Court has temporarily halted public impeachment hearings against President Cyril Ramaphosa. This grants Ramaphosa breathing space to challenge the Phala Phala report, central to the scandal, before judges hear his case.

For investors, this pause offers immediate relief from political uncertainty, supporting near-term market stability. Ramaphosa's administration has been a beacon of relative stability and reform, so a reduction in immediate political pressure is positive for policy continuity and broader investor sentiment across the continent.

The immediate focus shifts to the court's ruling on Ramaphosa's report challenge. Parliamentary preparations will continue, with the Phala Phala report facing further review in September. These developments will dictate the medium-term political risk outlook.
