Suspended Blantyre Water Board CEO Yeremia Chihana alleges his removal is tied to his resistance against powerful interests and insistence on proper procedures for a multi-million-dollar World Bank project, not misconduct. This marks his second suspension in five months, pointing to deep-seated conflicts.

This situation flags significant governance and transparency risks within Malawi's infrastructure sector. For international investors, such disputes signal potential project delays, increased operational costs, and an unpredictable regulatory environment, eroding confidence in project execution and capital deployment.

Watch for official investigations into Chihana's claims and the World Bank's response regarding the project's integrity. How the Malawian government addresses these allegations will indicate its commitment to combating corruption and safeguarding foreign-backed development initiatives.
