Malawi24 reports a suspected attempt to steal ESCOM electricity cables in Mzuzu, Malawi. A 39-year-old scrap dealer was seriously injured after being electrocuted and trapped inside a transformer cage. The incident occurred on Thursday, November 5, 2026, in Chibanja, where the individual was allegedly attempting to cut an electrical cable.

This event highlights persistent challenges with infrastructure security and asset integrity for utility companies like ESCOM in Malawi. Cable theft contributes to operational losses, increased maintenance costs, and potential service disruptions for electricity providers. For investors in the energy or infrastructure sectors, such incidents signal ongoing security risks that can impact profitability and reliability of essential services.

Investors should monitor ESCOM's response to such incidents, including any announced measures to enhance infrastructure protection and deter theft. The broader implications for power sector stability and the financial health of utility providers in Malawi, and potentially other regional markets facing similar issues, remain key considerations.
