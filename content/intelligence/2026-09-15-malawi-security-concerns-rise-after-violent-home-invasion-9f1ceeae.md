A violent home invasion in Lilongwe, Malawi, saw an accountant and his wife severely attacked with machetes. Thieves stole K6.7 million in valuables, including a firearm and laptops. Police have since arrested one suspect, recovering some items.

This incident highlights escalating personal security risks, particularly for high-net-worth individuals and professionals in urban centers. Such violent crime raises operational costs for expatriate security, impacts talent retention, and erodes investor confidence in the broader security landscape and rule of law.

Monitor police progress in apprehending remaining suspects and recovering assets. Observe government efforts to address urban crime rates and enhance public safety.
