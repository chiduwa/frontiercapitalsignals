Dangote Cement's Circular Economy Champions Team, alongside the E-waste Relief Foundation, has urged broader industry collaboration to address Nigeria's increasing e-waste challenge. This initiative signals a strategic focus on sustainable practices and resource recovery within a major industrial player.

For investors, this engagement positions Dangote Cement at the forefront of Nigeria's nascent circular economy. It indicates a proactive approach to ESG, potential for new revenue streams through recycling infrastructure, and risk mitigation against future environmental regulations. It also highlights an underserved market for waste management solutions across the region.

Monitor specific government policies or incentives for e-waste management and recycling, and watch for further partnerships involving Dangote or other major industrial players.
