A state-backed Romanian nuclear energy company is moving to claim approximately one-third of the uranium stock currently held at Niamey airport. This significant stockpile remains at the center of an ongoing dispute between the Nigerien government and French mining giant Orano.

This development signals a clear shift in resource control and commercial partnerships within Niger's critical mining sector. It highlights increasing competition for strategic African mineral assets, particularly post-coup, and indicates that the new Nigerien leadership is actively seeking alternative partners beyond traditional European ties. Investors should note the evolving landscape for resource agreements and the potential for new entrants.

Monitor how Niger resolves its dispute with Orano and the specifics of any deal with Romania or other new partners. Watch for details on new mining concessions, export logistics, and any broader implications for other resource-rich nations in the region seeking to renegotiate or diversify their agreements. This could set a precedent for future resource nationalism across the Sahel.
