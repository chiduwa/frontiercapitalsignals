The Africa Report indicates that AFIS 2026 will bring together over 1,250 financial leaders, including central bank governors, top bankers, and development finance executives, in Luanda on 3-4 November. These leaders will convene to discuss how African capital can drive continental growth, addressing the gap between profits and investment.

This high-level assembly of financial decision-makers is significant for investors. The discussion on profits versus investment suggests a strategic re-evaluation within the financial sector that could influence capital allocation and investment priorities across the continent, including in our target markets. Potential policy shifts or frameworks affecting capital flows could emerge.

Investors should watch for any overarching themes or intentions expressed by central bank governors and development finance executives during this gathering. While immediate policy changes are not specified, the collective focus on leveraging African capital could shape the long-term investment landscape and regulatory direction.
