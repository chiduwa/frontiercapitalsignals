TechCabal reports MTN Nigeria's fintech revenue has not returned to pre-suspension levels. This follows the temporary halt of its airtime and data credit service, which occurred between April and June 2026. Separately, the company is migrating its mobile money customers to a new platform.

The stalled recovery in fintech revenue suggests ongoing challenges for MTN Nigeria's financial services segment, a key growth area for many African telecom operators. The concurrent migration to a new platform could introduce further operational complexities or, conversely, may be part of a strategy to improve service and revenue in the future. Investors should consider the impact of service interruptions on financial performance and customer retention.

Investors should watch for further updates on the performance of MTN Nigeria's fintech revenue, particularly how the new mobile money platform impacts customer adoption and transaction volumes. The success of this platform migration will be key to determining the long-term trajectory of the company's financial services growth.
