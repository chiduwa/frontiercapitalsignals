Frogfoot Networks, Vox, and Hypa Fibre secured a significant capital injection, valuing the South African fibre trio at R14.4 billion. This deal provides fresh equity and leverage, enabling Frogfoot to target 360,000 new fibre connections yearly, with a focus on underserved communities.

This South African deal signals strong private sector confidence in African digital infrastructure, particularly last-mile fibre expansion into lower-income segments. It demonstrates the viable commercial case for addressing connectivity gaps across the continent, attracting significant capital for high-growth, essential services.

Investors should track Frogfoot's deployment pace and subscriber uptake in these new market segments. We will monitor for similar large-scale capital injections targeting fibre expansion in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda, indicating a broader continental trend towards robust digital infrastructure investment.
