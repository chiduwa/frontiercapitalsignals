Nyasa Times reports that CTS Courier Services has expanded its network in Lilongwe, Malawi, by opening two new branches. These new outlets are located in Area 25 Dzeza and along Bypass Road. This expansion brings CTS's total network to 36 branches across three cities, and the opening was attended by Lilongwe City Mayor Peter Alex Banda.

For investors, this expansion signals CTS's continued growth and potentially increased capacity within Malawi's logistics sector. The addition of new branches in key areas of Lilongwe suggests rising demand for courier services or a strategic move by CTS to capture greater market share. The presence of the Lilongwe City Mayor could indicate a supportive local environment for business expansion.

Investors should monitor CTS's operational performance and any further expansion plans, as well as the broader growth trajectory of Malawi's logistics and delivery sector. Understanding the specific drivers behind this expansion, such as evolving consumer patterns or increased business activity, would provide further insight into the market opportunity.
