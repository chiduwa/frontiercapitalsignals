The Independent (Uganda) reports that Kampala Lord Mayor Ronald Balimwezo Nsubuga has issued a warning to contractors undertaking road construction and rehabilitation projects in the city. The Mayor cautioned against installing substandard streetlights, noting that some installations fail within three months despite contractual maintenance obligations. Contractors assigned to road construction are also required to install and maintain these streetlights.

This development highlights potential issues with public infrastructure quality and contract enforcement within Uganda's capital. For investors in the infrastructure sector, particularly those involved in urban road construction and related services, this signals increased scrutiny and potential pressure for higher quality standards and stricter adherence to maintenance clauses. It also points to operational risks for existing contractors and due diligence considerations for new entrants.

Investors should monitor how Kampala city authorities enforce these warnings and whether new measures are introduced to improve oversight of infrastructure projects. Observe any impact on tender processes or contract awards, and how contractors respond to calls for improved quality and maintenance.
