Former US President Donald Trump announced a 1,055km highway linking southern Morocco to Dakhla in Western Sahara will carry his name. While Rabat has not formally confirmed the naming, the ambitious project elevates a major transport corridor and the unresolved Western Sahara dispute into the diplomatic spotlight.

This development signals long-term infrastructure investment and regional integration efforts, particularly in a geopolitically sensitive area. Investors should note potential for ancillary industries like logistics, construction, and resource extraction, driven by enhanced connectivity and Morocco's strategic push for economic development in its southern provinces.

Monitor formal confirmations from Rabat regarding the highway's name and funding mechanisms. Watch for tender announcements, project timelines, and any shifts in international diplomatic recognition of Morocco's claims over Western Sahara, as these will directly impact project certainty and broader regional stability.
