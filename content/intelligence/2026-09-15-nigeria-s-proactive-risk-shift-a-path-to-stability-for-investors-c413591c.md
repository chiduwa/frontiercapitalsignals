A recent article highlights Nigeria's critical need to move beyond reactive crisis management. It advocates for establishing a clear hierarchy of risks and aligning national capabilities and spending to prevent crises rather than just responding to them.

This shift is crucial for investors. A proactive approach to national risk, from economic volatility to security challenges, implies greater policy predictability and operational stability. It suggests a potential for more efficient resource allocation and reduced disruption, fostering a more secure and attractive investment climate.

Investors should monitor for concrete policy actions that reflect this strategic shift. Watch for government initiatives, budget reallocations, or new frameworks aimed at proactive risk assessment and prevention. Evidence of implementation, beyond rhetoric, will signal genuine progress towards a more stable operating environment.
