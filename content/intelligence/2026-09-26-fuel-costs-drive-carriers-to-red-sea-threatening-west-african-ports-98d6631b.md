Houthis maintain control over the Bab el-Mandeb strait, creating persistent security risks. Despite this, soaring fuel prices are now compelling major shipping carriers to reconsider this shorter, albeit dangerous, route to save operational costs, bypassing longer routes around Africa.

This shift significantly jeopardizes the investment thesis for deepwater port infrastructure and associated logistics in West Africa, which had anticipated increased traffic from extended shipping routes. While West African hubs face reduced throughput, select deepwater ports and Djibouti, strategically positioned for the Red Sea corridor, are poised for increased activity and potential valuation boosts.

Investors should closely monitor real-time shipping route declarations and global bunker fuel price trends. Evaluate the utilization rates and expansion plans of West African deepwater ports against those in East Africa, particularly Djibouti. Assess which specific "handful of deepwater hubs" are truly gaining traction, providing clarity on future maritime trade flows.
