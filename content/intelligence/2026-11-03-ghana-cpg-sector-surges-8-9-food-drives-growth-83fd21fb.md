MyJoyOnline Business reports that Ghanaian consumer packaged goods (CPG) saw an 8.9% increase in the first half of 2026. This growth was notably driven by food categories, which expanded their share of CPG volume from 32.2% to 34.2%. Specific categories leading this recovery included edible oil, tomato paste, milk, noodles, and food seasonings.

This performance indicates robust consumer demand within Ghana's CPG market, especially for essential food items. Investors may find opportunities in companies involved in the production, distribution, or retail of these identified high-growth food products. The increase in food's share of CPG volume suggests a prioritization of these goods by consumers, potentially indicating resilient demand even in broader economic shifts.

Investors should closely monitor the sustained growth of these specific food categories and the overall Ghanaian CPG market. Further investigation into companies best positioned to capitalize on these trends, including their market penetration and supply chain efficiencies, would be beneficial. Observing broader consumer spending trends beyond food CPG will also be key to understanding market direction.
