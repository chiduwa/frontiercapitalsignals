The World Bank just warned that Malawi's considerable critical mineral mining potential is severely hindered by its chronic electricity shortage. Their report, "From Potential to Prosperity," underscores that this mineral wealth will stay largely untapped without urgent power sector reform.

This matters because Malawi's critical minerals are vital for the global energy transition, representing a significant long-term resource play. However, the severe power deficit creates a substantial bottleneck, implying that returns on mining investments are contingent on effective power sector solutions.

Going forward, we need to track tangible progress on power sector reforms, including specific government policies, infrastructure project announcements, and financing commitments. These steps will be key indicators of when Malawi's mining sector becomes genuinely accessible for substantial capital deployment.
