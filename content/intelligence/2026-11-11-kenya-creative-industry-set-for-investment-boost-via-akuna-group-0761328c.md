Africa Briefing reports that Kenya, along with Sierra Leone, has signed Statements of Intent with British actor and entrepreneur Sir Idris Elba's Akuna Group. This initiative aims to help their creative industries attract investment. A Google-backed filmmaking program is cited as an early example of Akuna's work.

For investors, this signifies a potential for increased financing, training, and international market access within Kenya's creative sectors. The involvement of Akuna Group, specifically a Google-backed program, suggests a focus on developing industry infrastructure and talent, potentially opening new avenues for private equity or venture capital in areas like film, music, and digital content.

Investors should monitor the specific programs and funding mechanisms that emerge from these Statements of Intent. Details on how better financing will be facilitated and the scale of international market access will be key to identifying tangible investment opportunities in Kenya's evolving creative economy.
