The world's largest commodity traders, Glencore and Mercuria, are intensifying their competition in the critical minerals sector. Historically focused on oil and gas, these giants are now actively battling for control of mining assets, exemplified by recent actions in Guinea.

This strategic pivot by major players signals aggressive capital deployment into the critical minerals value chain. It confirms strong long-term demand projections and validates the investment thesis for African jurisdictions rich in these resources. Expect increased M&A activity and infrastructure development around these assets. This creates opportunities for ancillary services, logistics, and power solutions in resource-rich nations like Ghana or Uganda.

Monitor further expansion strategies of these commodity houses into other African critical mineral hubs. Observe how local governments in our focus regions, particularly those with significant mineral deposits, adapt their regulatory frameworks and incentives to attract or manage this intensified foreign investment. Pay attention to supply chain localization efforts and potential for partnerships with local enterprises.
