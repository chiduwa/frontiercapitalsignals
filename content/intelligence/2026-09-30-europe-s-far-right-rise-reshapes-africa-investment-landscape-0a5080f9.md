Europe's far-right parties are gaining power, advocating for stricter borders, mass deportations, and economic nationalism. This shift marks a significant political reorientation across the continent.

This ideological pivot extends beyond migration. Investors should anticipate potential reductions in foreign aid, stricter trade terms, and altered climate finance commitments from Europe to African nations. These changes could impact market access, project funding, and macroeconomic stability across the continent.

Monitor European election results closely, particularly their impact on specific bilateral and multilateral agreements with African countries. Observe policy changes in trade pacts, development assistance budgets, and climate adaptation funding mechanisms. Local policy responses from African governments will also be crucial.
