The High Street Journal reports that three banks in Ghana are currently seeking non-interest licences. This initiative is part of Ghana's broader strategy to tap into the US$10.5 trillion global non-interest finance market, with a target set for 2030.

This development suggests potential for expanded business financing and new investment opportunities within Ghana. The move could also unlock funding for productive sectors, offering investors diversified avenues for capital allocation and growth.

Investors should closely watch the approval process for these licences and any subsequent policy developments. Observing the speed of market adoption and the specific sectors that benefit from this new financing will be crucial for assessing impact.
