The Standard reports that Kenyan businesses are urged to align their funding choices with their specific needs. This involves evaluating the repayment pressure associated with debt financing against the costs of ownership and control tied to equity. The emphasis is on matching capital sources to operational requirements.

For international investors, this highlights a nuanced approach to capital structuring within Kenya's business landscape. Understanding how local businesses perceive and balance debt versus equity can inform investment strategies, particularly for those considering direct equity investments or providing debt facilities. It suggests a growing sophistication in financial decision-making among Kenyan enterprises.

Investors should monitor how Kenyan businesses adapt their capital structures in response to these considerations. Future developments might include shifts in preferred financing methods, potentially creating opportunities for tailored investment products or partnerships that address specific business funding needs.
