Despite a loss on the field, Haiya, Bibles in hand, orchestrated jubilant scenes infused with gospel music and national pride, showcasing a leader deeply connected to Malawian cultural values.

This public display from a prominent national figure signals a strong alignment with Malawi's conservative, faith-based societal fabric.
