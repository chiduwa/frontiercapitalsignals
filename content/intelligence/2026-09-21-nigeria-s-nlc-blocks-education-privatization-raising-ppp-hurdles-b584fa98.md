Nigeria's Labour Congress (NLC) has publicly opposed the proposed concession of King's College and other Unity Colleges. The NLC advocates for continued government ownership, emphasizing the need for robust public funding and effective management of these institutions.

This NLC stance signals potential political and labor headwinds for public-private partnership (PPP) initiatives in Nigeria, particularly those involving public assets. Investors evaluating opportunities in education, infrastructure concessions, or broader privatization efforts should note this strong union opposition, which could delay or alter deal structures.

Watch for the government's reaction to the NLC's strong position and the future of the Unity Colleges' concession plans. Monitor developments in other planned privatization or asset commercialization projects, as this opposition could indicate broader challenges in executing such initiatives across the economy.
