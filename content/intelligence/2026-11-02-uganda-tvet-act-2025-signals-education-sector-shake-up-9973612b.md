The Independent (Uganda) reports that parents and students have been urged to exercise caution before enrolling in or paying tuition for university diploma and certificate Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) programmes. This warning follows the introduction of stricter regulations under the TVET Act, 2025.

These new regulations threaten the validity of qualifications awarded through these programmes. For investors, this indicates a significant policy shift aimed at standardizing vocational training in Uganda. Businesses involved in private education, human capital development, or those relying on certified skilled labor, may face challenges related to credential recognition or discover opportunities with compliant, accredited institutions.

Investors should closely monitor the specific provisions and implementation of the TVET Act, 2025. Observing enrollment trends in university-based TVET programmes and the emergence of institutions that meet the new regulatory standards will be crucial next steps.
