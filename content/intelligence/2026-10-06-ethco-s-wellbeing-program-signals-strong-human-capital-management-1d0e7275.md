Ethanol Company Limited (EthCo) has initiated a peer counselling program in Malawi to provide first-line mental health support for its employees. Six staff members have been trained and certified by St John of God Hospitaller Services, establishing an accessible internal support system for workplace and personal well-being challenges.

This move is significant for investors, signaling strong human capital management and a commitment to employee welfare. Such programs enhance operational resilience by potentially reducing absenteeism, improving productivity, and fostering staff retention. It indicates a proactive leadership approach to mitigating human resource risks, which contributes to long-term business stability and shareholder value.

Investors should monitor the program's impact on EthCo's key operational metrics, specifically employee retention rates and productivity trends. Further, observe if this initiative spurs similar human capital investments among other industrial players in Malawi or the broader region, indicating a growing focus on workforce well-being as a strategic advantage.
