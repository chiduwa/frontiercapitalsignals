South Africa's e-commerce market is experiencing significant growth, projected to exceed R130 billion this year with 20% annual expansion. Online sales now account for 8-10% of total national retail, handling over 100 million annual shipments. Despite this robust turnover and market penetration, the sector grapples with underlying profitability challenges.

This dynamic in South Africa is a crucial signal for investors in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda. While African e-commerce markets show immense potential, the SA experience highlights that top-line growth does not automatically translate to sustainable profit. Investors must scrutinize unit economics, customer acquisition costs, logistics efficiencies, and competitive pressures more closely in nascent markets.

Monitor how South African e-commerce players address the profit squeeze. Look for strategies around operational efficiencies, diversification of revenue streams, and potential market consolidation. Observe whether these challenges ripple into other high-growth African markets, guiding investment thesis towards models prioritizing profitability alongside scale.
