The High Street Journal reports that the Chamber of Agribusiness Ghana has urged Parliament to scrutinise eight critical issues within the 2027 agricultural budget. The chamber warns that unclear funding, cuts to irrigation, inadequate climate preparedness, and trade risks pose threats.

These identified gaps could undermine agricultural transformation in Ghana. For investors with exposure to Ghana's agricultural sector, or considering it, these budget concerns suggest potential headwinds related to policy support, infrastructure development, and resilience. Unclear funding and irrigation cuts, if implemented, could directly impact operational costs and yields for agribusinesses.

Investors should monitor the parliamentary scrutiny of the 2027 agricultural budget. The key factors to watch include how Parliament addresses the Chamber's concerns regarding funding clarity, irrigation provisions, climate preparedness measures, and trade risk mitigation. The outcome of this scrutiny could signal the government's commitment to supporting the sector.
