Phillips Consulting formally presented the Personnel Audit and Skills Gap Analysis (PASGA) report to the Nigerian Federal Civil Service. This report establishes an evidence base to foster a more capable and future-ready public workforce.

For investors, this signals a serious government commitment to improving institutional capacity and efficiency. A more competent civil service can streamline regulatory processes, improve policy implementation, and enhance the overall ease of doing business in Nigeria, reducing operational friction for foreign direct investment.

We need to watch for the immediate implementation of PASGA's recommendations. Observe specific policy reforms, training initiatives, and any measurable improvements in service delivery or bureaucratic efficiency. Genuine follow-through will be key to unlocking tangible investment benefits.
