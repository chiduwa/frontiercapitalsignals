Dubai has launched a Kenyan Business Council. This new body creates a formal platform for companies from both markets to pursue partnerships, exchange market intelligence, and identify commercial opportunities across fast-growing sectors.

This initiative signals a strategic push to accelerate bilateral trade and investment flows into Kenya. With significant non-oil trade already established, the council will streamline market access and reduce friction for cross-border capital. Investors should anticipate increased deal flow and enhanced market transparency, driven by intensified UAE engagement.

Monitor the specific sectors and projects highlighted by the council. Look for initial partnership announcements and any policy adjustments in Kenya designed to capitalize on this deepened engagement. Pay close attention to how this translates into tangible improvements in trade volume and foreign direct investment figures.
