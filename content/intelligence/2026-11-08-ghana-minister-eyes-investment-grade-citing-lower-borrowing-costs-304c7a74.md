MyJoyOnline Business reports that Ghana's Finance Minister, Dr. Cassiel Ato Forson, anticipates a "New Economy" will propel the country towards achieving investment grade status. This reflects a key economic ambition articulated by the Minister.

The Minister's rationale is that attaining investment grade will lower Ghana's borrowing costs. Furthermore, it aims to assist Ghanaian businesses in securing long-term financing. For international investors, this signal could imply a potentially improved sovereign risk profile for Ghana, which might enhance the appeal of its debt instruments and the operational environment for businesses seeking capital.

Investors should observe concrete policy developments and economic reforms that could facilitate this projected shift. The report does not detail the specific mechanisms of the "New Economy" or provide a timeframe for reaching investment grade, indicating that this is currently a strategic objective rather than an immediate certainty.
