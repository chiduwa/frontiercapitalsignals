Gunfire and explosions were heard overnight in Niamey, Niger's capital, specifically around a military base near the international airport. This indicates a significant security incident or a direct challenge to the current government.

While Niger is not a core Frontier Capital Signals market, instability there creates a volatile security precedent for the wider Sahel and West Africa. Investors should factor in heightened regional geopolitical risk, potentially impacting supply chains, security costs, and broader investor sentiment for neighboring states, especially those with similar political fragilities or resource interests.

Watch for official statements from Nigerien authorities and ECOWAS, assessing the nature of the events, whether it was a coup attempt, and the immediate security response. Also, track any cross-border implications or shifts in regional security postures that could affect investment climates in West African markets.
