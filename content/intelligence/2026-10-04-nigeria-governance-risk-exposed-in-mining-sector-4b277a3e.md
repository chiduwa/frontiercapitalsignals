Taraba State Governor Kefas publicly accused government and security officials of actively aiding illegal mining operations within the state. This allegation was made during a recent meeting with artisanal and small-scale miners in Jalingo.

This direct accusation from a sitting governor signals significant governance and rule of law challenges, particularly within Nigeria's mining sector. Such complicity heightens operational risks, undermines legitimate investments, and exacerbates regulatory uncertainty for licensed miners, impacting ESG considerations.

Investors should monitor the state and federal government's response. Watch for concrete actions, including investigations, arrests, or policy reforms aimed at curbing official corruption and illegal mining activities. The effectiveness of these measures will indicate commitment to improving the investment climate and securing mining assets.
