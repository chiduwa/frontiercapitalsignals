Nyasa Times reports a fierce row has erupted in Malawi after the Non-Governmental Organisations Regulatory Authority (NGORA) moved to deregister the Human Rights Defenders Coalition (HRDC). Veteran activist Redson Munlo insists that NGORA has no legal authority to strike the influential pressure group off the map.

This regulatory action against a prominent civil society organization, coupled with Nyasa Times reporting that donor fears are growing, suggests potential challenges to governance stability and the regulatory environment in Malawi. Such disputes can erode investor confidence and potentially affect broader economic stability by influencing international aid and perceptions of the rule of law.

Investors should monitor the outcome of NGORA's attempt to deregister the HRDC and any further reactions from civil society or international donors. The resolution of this dispute could signal the direction of regulatory oversight and civil liberties, impacting the overall investment climate in Malawi.
