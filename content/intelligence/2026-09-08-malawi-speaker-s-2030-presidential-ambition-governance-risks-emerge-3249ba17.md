Malawi's Speaker, Sameer Suleman, is being positioned for a presidential bid in 2030, according to a prominent local news outlet. This comes amidst ongoing efforts to investigate public sector corruption, which are currently stalled by a court order affecting parliamentary committee formations.

Early political positioning for the 2030 elections signals potential shifts in Malawi's governance landscape. The continued struggle with public sector corruption, underscored by legal delays in parliamentary oversight, highlights persistent institutional risks for investors requiring regulatory predictability and transparency.
