Malawi's Minister of Information and Communications Technology, Shadric Namalomba, reports that the new Nzika digital ID system faces significant adoption challenges. Only 33% of the population owns a smartphone, and just 12.5% possess basic internet usage skills, creating a major barrier to widespread rollout.

This data underscores a substantial digital divide, indicating a pressing need for investment in digital infrastructure, affordable smartphones, and digital literacy programs. For investors, this signals overlooked opportunities in telecommunications, fintech, and hardware distribution, especially in last-mile connectivity solutions.

Monitor government strategies to address these gaps, including policy incentives for mobile network operators or public-private partnerships. Progress on digital inclusion will be key for broader economic development and for realizing the potential of Malawi's digital transformation initiatives.
