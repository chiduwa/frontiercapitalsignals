The Independent (Uganda) reports that the weakening of the Uganda shilling against the US dollar is hindering Kiruddu National Referral Hospital’s ability to procure essential medical equipment. This currency depreciation has led suppliers to revise quotations upwards, creating funding gaps and forcing the hospital to reconsider projects already included in its approved budget. Exchange-rate fluctuations are cited as a cause for these increased difficulties.

This situation signals broader challenges for Uganda’s economy, particularly for sectors reliant on imports. Investors should note the direct impact of currency volatility on public sector procurement, potentially affecting project execution and budgeting across various government initiatives. It also highlights the risk of rising operational costs for businesses importing goods or services into Uganda.

Given that exchange-rate fluctuations have reportedly increased these procurement issues, investors should continue to monitor the performance of the Ugandan shilling against major currencies. Sustained depreciation could exacerbate funding shortfalls for public and private entities dependent on foreign supplies, while any stabilization or appreciation might ease these pressures.
