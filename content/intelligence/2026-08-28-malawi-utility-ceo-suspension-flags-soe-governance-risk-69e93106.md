Blantyre Water Board (BWB) has suspended its CEO, Yeremia Chihana, effective immediately, citing alleged misconduct. The suspension follows a board resolution, executed under his employment contract's specific clause.

This event signals potential governance vulnerabilities within Malawi's state-owned enterprises, particularly critical infrastructure providers like BWB. Investor confidence relies on transparent, stable management, and leadership changes under misconduct allegations can introduce uncertainty regarding operational continuity, future project execution, and regulatory oversight.

Monitor the ongoing investigation's findings and the appointment of an interim or permanent CEO. Assess any subsequent impact on BWB's operational stability, financial health, and planned infrastructure developments. This situation may prompt broader government action on SOE governance.
