Malawi's Congress Party (MCP) is in a significant internal succession dispute following an election loss. This isn't just typical post-defeat squabbling; the situation has deeper implications potentially linked to President Chakwera and the country's constitutional framework.

This internal party instability signals elevated policy uncertainty and governance risk for investors. A contentious power struggle can disrupt legislative priorities, impact regulatory predictability, and shift economic policy direction, directly influencing investment confidence and operational stability.

Monitor the resolution of the MCP's leadership question and its impact on President Chakwera's political authority. Watch for any broader constitutional challenges or shifts in government policy stemming from this dynamic, especially regarding economic reforms and regulatory consistency.
