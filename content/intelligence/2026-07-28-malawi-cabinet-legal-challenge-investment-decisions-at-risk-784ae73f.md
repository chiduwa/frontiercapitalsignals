A prominent constitutional lawyer in Malawi has issued a stark warning, asserting that President Peter Mutharika broke the law by having the Second Vice President chair a Cabinet meeting while the First Vice President was available. This legal challenge suggests that any decisions made during that meeting could be invalidated by the courts, creating immediate uncertainty.

For investors, this development signals increased regulatory and political risk. Potential invalidation of Cabinet decisions introduces significant uncertainty regarding policy stability, contractual agreements, and government commitments. It highlights a potentially fragile legal and political environment that could directly impact operational security and investment returns.

We are closely monitoring the government's official response and any formal legal proceedings that follow. The specific nature of decisions challenged, particularly those affecting key economic sectors or foreign direct investment, will be critical. Watch for broader implications on political stability and the rule of law in Malawi, as this could set a precedent for future governance challenges.
