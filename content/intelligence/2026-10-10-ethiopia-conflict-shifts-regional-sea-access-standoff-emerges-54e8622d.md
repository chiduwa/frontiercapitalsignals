The Africa Report reports that the Tigray insurgency is withering, with federal forces entering Shire and resuming flights to Mekelle. This follows defections and retreating rebels, which have left a power vacuum in northern Ethiopia.

For international investors, this development signals a potential shift in the security landscape of northern Ethiopia. While specific to Ethiopia, a resolution or de-escalation of such conflicts can positively influence broader regional stability perceptions across Africa. The reported escalating diplomatic standoff over sea access also raises geopolitical stakes, which could impact regional trade dynamics and investor confidence.

Investors should monitor the evolving situation concerning the diplomatic standoff over sea access. Its resolution or further escalation could have wider implications for maritime trade routes and regional economic cooperation, potentially influencing long-term investment strategies across East Africa.
