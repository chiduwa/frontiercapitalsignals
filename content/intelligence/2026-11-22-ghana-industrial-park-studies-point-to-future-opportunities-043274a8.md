The High Street Journal reports that UNIDO is offering feasibility support for Ghana's proposed Afienya Industrial Park. This support concerns proposed studies for the project.

These proposed studies are expected to guide infrastructure planning for the project. They are also expected to inform investment decisions and contribute to the long-term sustainability of the industrial park. This signals potential future clarity on investment avenues within the park.

Investors should watch for outcomes from these studies, as they are intended to direct future planning and investment. The current information does not specify when these studies will conclude or what the specific findings might be.
