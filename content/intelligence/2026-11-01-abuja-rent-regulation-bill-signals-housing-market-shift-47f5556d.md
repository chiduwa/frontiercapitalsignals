Premium Times reports on a proposed law in Abuja targeting house rent regulation. The bill seeks to prohibit compulsory payments exceeding 12 months and establish a dedicated rent regulation authority.

This proposed legislation introduces new regulatory oversight to the Abuja housing market. For real estate investors, the prohibition on upfront payments beyond 12 months could impact cash flow predictability and tenancy agreements. The establishment of a new authority suggests a formalization of rental market governance, potentially influencing operating costs and investment attractiveness.

Investors should monitor the progress of this bill and further details regarding the scope and powers of the proposed rent regulation authority. Understanding the specifics of its implementation will be key to assessing its full impact on rental yields and property investment strategies in Abuja.
