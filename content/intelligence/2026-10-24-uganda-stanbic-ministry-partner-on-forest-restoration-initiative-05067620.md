Nile Post reports that Uganda's Environment Ministry and Stanbic have launched the Manwa Forest Restoration Campaign. This initiative at the Manwa Local Forest Reserve aims to restore degraded land, protect newly planted trees, and mobilize communities to conserve forests. These forests are vital for supporting agriculture, water resources, and biodiversity.

This collaboration between a government ministry and a major financial institution signals an increasing focus on environmental sustainability and corporate social responsibility within Uganda. For investors, Stanbic's involvement in a project directly supporting agriculture and water resources highlights the growing emphasis on ESG factors and climate resilience.
