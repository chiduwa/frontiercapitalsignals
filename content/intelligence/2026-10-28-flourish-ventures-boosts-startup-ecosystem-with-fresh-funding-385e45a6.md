TechCabal reports that Flourish Ventures has invested an additional $6 million into Madica’s startup program. This funding means each participating startup will continue to receive up to $200,000 in investment.

This investment signifies ongoing confidence from a venture capital firm in the African startup ecosystem. The program also provides startups with 18 months of mentorship, executive coaching, and investor-readiness support. For investors, this suggests a pipeline of potentially better-prepared and supported companies emerging from the program.

Investors should monitor the types of startups Madica supports and their subsequent performance. The continued funding and comprehensive support package indicate a commitment to developing scalable businesses, which could eventually present future investment opportunities.
