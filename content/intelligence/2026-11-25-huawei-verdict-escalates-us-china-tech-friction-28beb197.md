The Standard reports a US jury found tech giant Huawei guilty of stealing trade secrets and lying to regulators regarding its activities in Iran. Following this verdict, Beijing lashed out at the US, accusing it of "suppressing" Chinese companies.

This development escalates the ongoing friction between the US and China, particularly concerning technology and international business practices. For investors in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda, heightened US-China tensions could influence technology access, infrastructure development, and supply chain stability, as many local markets rely on Chinese technology and investment.

Investors should monitor how these tensions evolve and if they lead to broader trade or investment restrictions impacting Chinese companies operating in Africa. Any further US actions or Chinese retaliations could affect technology procurement, project financing, and the competitive landscape within these emerging markets.
