Aliko Dangote is preparing to launch what could be Africa's largest IPO, targeting ₦2.15 trillion by selling 4.1 billion shares in his flagship refinery. This strategic move aims to broaden ownership in a critical industrial asset.

For international investors, this represents a unique entry point into substantial energy infrastructure with reverberations extending across the continent, not just Nigeria. The refinery is positioned to fundamentally reshape regional petroleum markets, offering potential for long-term growth linked to Africa's increasing energy demands.

We will closely monitor the definitive IPO timeline, valuation specifics, and accessibility for foreign capital. Key indicators to track include the offering's market reception, initial trading performance, and the refinery's operational impact on regional fuel supply dynamics and pricing stability.
