Geregu Power Plc recently disbursed N6 billion, settling overdue coupon and principal payments on its bonds. While this resolves immediate defaults, market sentiment suggests it was a costly move, dubbed a "pyrrhic victory."

This matters because missed payments erode investor confidence in corporate debt and highlight potential liquidity issues within the Nigerian power sector. Even with the settlement, the underlying financial strain and operational challenges that led to the default likely persist, posing continued risk for bondholders and equity investors.

Investors must monitor Geregu's cash flow generation, particularly its ability to service future debt obligations without distress. Watch for details on how this N6 billion was funded and any strategic shifts to improve operational efficiency and revenue stability in Nigeria's complex power market.
