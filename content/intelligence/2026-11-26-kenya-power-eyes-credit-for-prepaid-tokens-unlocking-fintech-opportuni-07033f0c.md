Capital FM Business reports that Kenya Power is seeking a financial services provider. This firm would offer short-term credit to prepaid electricity customers, enabling them to purchase tokens. This move is aimed at giving consumers more flexibility.

This development signals a new market opportunity for financial services firms in Kenya, particularly those specializing in micro-credit or digital lending solutions. For Kenya Power, enabling prepaid customers to access credit for tokens could stabilize or increase electricity consumption, potentially improving revenue streams. Investors in the financial sector should note the potential for new partnerships and expanded service offerings.

Investors should monitor the outcome of Kenya Power's search, specifically which financial institutions or fintech companies secure this partnership. The success and adoption rate of this credit facility by consumers will indicate the viability and scale of this new market segment. It could also provide a blueprint for similar credit-based utility payment solutions in other African markets.
