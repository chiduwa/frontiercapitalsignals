Nkhoma Synod Women's Guild in Malawi has acquired land to construct rental houses. This marks a strategic shift from traditional church fundraising, with rental income now intended to finance the women's ministry work directly.

This development signals a nascent but significant trend: non-profit organizations exploring real estate as a sustainable income stream. For investors, it indicates growing local demand for property-based assets and a potential new avenue for capital deployment or partnerships in development projects within Malawi's social sector.
