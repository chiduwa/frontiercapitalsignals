MyJoyOnline Business reports that Ghana's Finance Minister, Dr Cassiel Ato Forson, urged local and international businesses to invest in the country. He invited them to seize opportunities under the New Economy programme, addressing the Invest Ghana Summit in Accra.

MyJoyOnline Business indicates the minister outlined planned public investment equivalent to US$10 billion over four years. This initiative aims to support production, business expansion, and job creation, signaling potential government backing for private sector growth and foreign direct investment.

Investors should monitor further details on the New Economy programme and how the outlined public investment will be deployed. Specific sector allocations and implementation timelines could provide clearer paths for engagement in Ghana's productive economy.
