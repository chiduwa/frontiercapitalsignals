Nigeria’s crucial oil and gas sector faces a severe technical skills deficit. Despite offering high salaries and attractive benefits, companies are now forced to bring back retired personnel to fill critical roles.

This severe skills gap signals direct operational risks and potential cost escalations for oil and gas ventures in Nigeria. It underscores a critical human capital challenge impacting production reliability and long-term sector growth.

Watch for government-led training reforms and industry investments in vocational programs. The emergence of specialized human capital development firms or tech solutions targeting the energy sector could represent new investment opportunities.
