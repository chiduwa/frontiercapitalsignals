The Independent (Uganda) reports the National Information Technology Authority-Uganda (NITA-U) is confident that Information and Communication Technology (ICT) infrastructure at Akii-Bua Olympic Stadium in Lira City is nearing completion. NITA-U expects these installations will meet Confederation of African Football (CAF) requirements for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON).

This development signals progress in Uganda's major infrastructure projects, particularly those tied to hosting significant continental events.
