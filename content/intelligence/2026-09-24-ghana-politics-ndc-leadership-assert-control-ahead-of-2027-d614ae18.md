Ghana's National Democratic Congress (NDC) national leader, Dickson, asserted the party leadership's exclusive right to form its 2027 presidential campaign council. This public statement addresses an internal conflict, specifically a face-off with the OK Movement, a support group, over its attempts to establish the council.

This internal friction within a major political party signals potential for pre-election instability and a less cohesive opposition. A stable political landscape is crucial for investor confidence, especially leading up to a national election. Unresolved internal power dynamics could translate into policy uncertainty or electoral unpredictability.

Monitor the NDC's ability to unify its factions and present a coherent front. Watch for any further public disputes or resolutions concerning the OK Movement or similar support groups. The cohesiveness of major parties directly impacts perceived political risk and Ghana's broader investment climate as 2027 approaches.
