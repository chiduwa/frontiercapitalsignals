Blantyre Water Board (BWB) swiftly replaced its recently fired CEO, Yeremiah Chihana, with Henry Bakuwa. However, Bakuwa's appointment immediately drew scrutiny due to his documented history of controversy, effectively moving the utility from one leadership scandal to another.

This incident highlights significant governance vulnerabilities within a critical Malawian state-owned enterprise. The rapid, seemingly ill-vetted appointment of a controversial figure signals weak oversight and raises concerns about management integrity and the potential for operational instability. Such issues erode investor confidence in parastatal dealings and the broader regulatory environment.

Monitor public and government reactions to Bakuwa's appointment and any potential reversal or further investigation. Investors should also assess the broader implications for governance standards across Malawi's public sector, particularly concerning due diligence for key leadership roles in utilities and other state entities.
