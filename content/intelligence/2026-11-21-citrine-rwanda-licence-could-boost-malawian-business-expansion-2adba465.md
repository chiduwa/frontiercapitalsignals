Nyasa Times reports Citrine Investment Bank Rwanda Plc received its investment banking licence in Kigali on November 17, 2026. This marks the first licensed operation for Citrine Financial Group Holdings Plc.

This development could signal new financial avenues for Malawian businesses seeking expansion, according to co-founder Benedicto Bena Nkhoma. It suggests increased cross-border financial activity and potential for capital flows, addressing a practical challenge for Malawian businesses looking to grow regionally.

Investors should monitor how this new licence translates into tangible support and opportunities for Malawian businesses. Details on specific sectors or types of expansion plans that Citrine intends to facilitate, or the actual uptake by Malawian firms, are not yet clear from this report.
