Africa Briefing reports that Cote d'Ivoire's total trade more than doubled to $57 billion in 2025, up from $26 billion seven years earlier. This increase is detailed in a new Afreximbank report. Afreximbank forecasts the nation's economic growth at 6.2 percent in 2027.

The significant trade expansion and projected economic growth highlight Cote d'Ivoire as a dynamic market. Africa Briefing indicates oil and gas expansion is a key driver for this growth, mitigating pressures in the cocoa market. This suggests diversified investment opportunities, particularly in energy-related sectors, beyond traditional agricultural exports.

Investors should observe the continued trajectory of oil and gas expansion and its impact on broader economic stability and growth. The sustained resilience in economic growth forecasts, despite identified cocoa market pressures, will be a key indicator for evaluating long-term investment viability in Cote d'Ivoire.
