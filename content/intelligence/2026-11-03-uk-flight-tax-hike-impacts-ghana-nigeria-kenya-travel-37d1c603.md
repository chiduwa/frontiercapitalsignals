Africa Briefing reports that Britain will increase departure taxes on flights to several African destinations from April 2028. UK Air Passenger Duty (APD) on economy journeys to Ghana, Nigeria, and Kenya will reach £108.60. Business-class duty to South Africa is set to rise to £269.36.

This policy change represents a rise in the cost of travel between the UK and key African markets, specifically Ghana, Nigeria, and Kenya. For international investors, this could marginally impact business travel expenses and potentially tourism flows, depending on the price elasticity of demand for air travel. The long lead time until April 2028 provides time for market adjustments.

Investors should monitor any potential responses from the affected African nations or industry bodies regarding these increased taxes. Observing long-term travel trends and the broader economic impact on sectors reliant on UK inbound tourism or business travel in Ghana, Nigeria, and Kenya will be important.
