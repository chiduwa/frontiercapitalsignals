Nile Post reports that the Uganda Development Corporation (UDC) and Bukona have commissioned an ethanol blending plant located in Tororo. This marks a new operational asset in Uganda's energy infrastructure.

This development is highlighted as boosting Uganda's energy security. For investors, it signals a nascent market for biofuels and domestic energy solutions, indicating potential government backing for such initiatives aimed at diversifying the energy mix and reducing reliance on traditional fuels. It could open avenues for investment in the agricultural supply chain for ethanol production.
