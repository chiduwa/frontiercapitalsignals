Malawi's NGO Regulatory Authority (NGORA) has launched an aggressive campaign against the Human Rights Defenders Coalition (HRDC) over alleged non-compliance with NGO law. Peculiarly, NGORA issued its summons to Gift Trapence, the coalition's former chairperson, completely bypassing its current leader.

This unusual and targeted regulatory action against a prominent civil society organization signals a potentially unpredictable operating environment. Such tactics raise concerns about the rule of law and government overreach. For international investors, this implies heightened political risk and uncertainty regarding regulatory enforcement, directly impacting investment security and long-term stability.

Investors should closely watch how this case develops and if NGORA clarifies its rationale for targeting a former leader. Monitor the broader regulatory landscape and government-civil society relations. Any escalation or similar actions against other entities could indicate a trend of tightening civic space and increased operational challenges across sectors.
