Premium Times reports Rivers United secured a three-year front-of-shirt sponsorship deal with Goka Sports and Casino, valued at $350,000 or N490m. This agreement provides a commercial boost for the Port Harcourt club as it pursues the NPFL title.

This development highlights the growing commercialization of Nigerian sports, signaling potential opportunities for advertising and consumer-facing ventures. The deal's size and the involvement of a sports and casino firm suggest increasing activity in the leisure and entertainment sector, pointing to potentially rising disposable income or targeted consumer spending.
