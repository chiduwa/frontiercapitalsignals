A recent report by the American NGO Center for Advanced Defense Studies confirms significant air logistics support for Russia's 'Africa Corps' operations in Mali, Burkina Faso, and Niger. Seven airlines, including Russian and African state-owned entities, are reportedly involved in supplying these bases.

This development significantly elevates geopolitical risk across West Africa. Involvement of African state-owned airlines in these operations creates potential exposure to Western sanctions, which could disrupt the region's aviation sector and broader trade. Investors should factor in increased security concerns and regulatory scrutiny impacting logistics and cross-border operations.

Monitor closely for official responses from Western governments, including potential targeted sanctions against implicated airlines or individuals. Track any shifts in regional security dynamics and the broader implications for supply chains and infrastructure investments in neighboring countries like Ghana and Nigeria.
