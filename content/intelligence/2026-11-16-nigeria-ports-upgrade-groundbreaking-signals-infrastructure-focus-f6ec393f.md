BusinessDay reports that Nigeria's federal government announced the groundbreaking for upgrades at the Apapa and Tin Can Island ports. These ports are noted as ageing, and the upgrade has been long-awaited.

This development signals a potential commitment to improving critical trade infrastructure in Nigeria. For investors, enhanced port efficiency at Apapa and Tin Can Island could reduce logistics costs and improve supply chain reliability for businesses operating in or trading with Nigeria. While details on the scope and timeline are not provided, the start of upgrades suggests a move towards addressing long-standing operational bottlenecks.

Investors should monitor further announcements regarding the upgrade's specific funding, phases, and expected completion. Clarity on the project's details, including how it addresses existing port congestion and efficiency challenges, will be key to assessing its full impact on trade and investment flows.
