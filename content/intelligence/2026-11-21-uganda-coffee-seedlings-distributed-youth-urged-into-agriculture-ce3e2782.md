Nile Post reports that National Youth Council Chairperson Daniel Ongom urged young people in Greater Luweero to pursue commercial agriculture as a path to economic independence. The report also indicates 20,000 coffee seedlings are being distributed to encourage youth engagement in coffee farming.

This initiative signals local leadership support for agricultural development, specifically coffee cultivation, in Greater Luweero. The distribution of a substantial number of seedlings could lead to increased regional coffee production capacity over time. This may indicate potential opportunities across the coffee value chain, including agricultural inputs, processing, or export.
