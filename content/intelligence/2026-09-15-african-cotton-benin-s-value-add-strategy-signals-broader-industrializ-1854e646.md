Benin is strategically enhancing its cotton value chain, moving beyond raw fibre exports to domestic garment manufacturing. This initiative aims to create local factory jobs and retain greater economic value within the country, offering new opportunities for farmers and women.

This strategic shift in Benin signals a broader continental trend towards industrialization and value addition in primary sectors. It underscores government commitment to local content and economic diversification, creating opportunities for investors in textile manufacturing, logistics, and related industrial infrastructure across West and East African markets.

Investors should monitor the success metrics of Benin's strategy, particularly export growth and job creation. Watch for similar value-addition policies in our target markets, focusing on government incentives for local manufacturing, skills development programs, and intra-African trade policies that support finished goods.
