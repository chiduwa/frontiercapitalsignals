Enhanced MRA efficiency signals a more predictable operating environment for investors. Consistent revenue collection improves government fiscal stability, indirectly benefiting both local and international enterprises through better public services and streamlined trade processes. This reduces friction for businesses.

Monitor MRA's reported revenue collection figures and measurable improvements in customs processing times. Track broader government budget allocations for digital infrastructure across other critical agencies. These indicators will confirm sustained ease of doing business.
