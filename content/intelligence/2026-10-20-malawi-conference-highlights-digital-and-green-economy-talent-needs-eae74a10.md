Nyasa Times reports the Thom Frank Mpinganjira (TFM) Centre of Excellence donated K10 million to the Institute of People Management Malawi (IPMM) for its 2026 Annual Lakeshore Conference. The conference is scheduled for October 21 to 23, 2026, in Mangochi.

This conference will address the theme ‘Future-Ready: Building Talent for the Malawian Digital and Green Economy’. This focus suggests that human capital development in these sectors is a strategic priority for Malawi. Investors could find opportunities in sectors supporting digital infrastructure, green technologies, or professional training that aligns with these national talent needs.

Monitoring the outcomes and specific initiatives arising from this 2026 conference, particularly any policy recommendations or private sector partnerships, could provide further insight. The involvement of the IPMM indicates a focus on actionable talent strategies.
