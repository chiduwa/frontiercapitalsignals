Malawi24 reports that the Electricity Supply Corporation of Malawi (ESCOM) is preparing a nationwide disconnection exercise targeting postpaid customers with unpaid bills. This initiative is scheduled from November 30 to December 11, 2026, and threatens to exacerbate existing power challenges for homes and businesses already grappling with blackouts.

This development could increase power supply instability in Malawi, potentially impacting operational costs and productivity for businesses reliant on consistent electricity. Investors in sectors sensitive to infrastructure reliability or consumer purchasing power should note the risk of increased operational disruption and potential economic slowdown due to widespread disconnections.

Investors should monitor the scope and enforcement of ESCOM's disconnection exercise, particularly its impact on business operations and consumer sentiment. Observe any official statements regarding ESCOM's financial health or government interventions to address the underlying power supply issues and debt collection challenges.
