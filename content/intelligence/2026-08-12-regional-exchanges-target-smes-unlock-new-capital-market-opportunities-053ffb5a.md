The Botswana Stock Exchange is actively pushing for greater regional cooperation and private sector education to encourage SME listings. Their goal is to connect more small and medium enterprises with long-term capital through public markets. This initiative was highlighted during discussions with Malawian business journalists.

This development signals a growing regional commitment to deepening capital markets across Africa. Increased SME listings mean a broader pool of investable companies and enhanced liquidity, which is crucial for fund managers seeking diversified growth opportunities beyond traditional large caps. It also suggests improving regulatory frameworks and investor awareness.

Monitor tangible initiatives emerging from this call for collaboration, particularly within Malawi and neighboring countries. Look for new policy statements, joint ventures between exchanges, or specific educational programs targeting SMEs. Progress on these fronts will indicate the viability of new listing pipelines and the overall health of regional capital markets.
