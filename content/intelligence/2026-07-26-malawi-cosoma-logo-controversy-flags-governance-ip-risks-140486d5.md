Malawi's Copyright Society (COSOMA) faces renewed public backlash over its visually "cheap" logo. Critics argue the design fails to reflect the organization's financial stature, linking it to ongoing controversies surrounding the disbursement of royalties to creatives. This signals a perceived lack of professionalism and accountability within the body.

This public discontent highlights broader governance and transparency risks within a key institution overseeing Malawi's creative sector. Investor confidence in intellectual property protection and the regulatory environment could erode if a significant rights management body is seen as mismanaged or opaque, potentially stifling growth in media, entertainment, and tech.

Monitor COSOMA's response to these criticisms and any government intervention or investigations into its operations. Look for signs of reform in IP rights management and regulatory oversight.
