The Independent (Uganda) reports that Afreximbank has extended a €30-million revolving short-term line of credit facility to dfcu Bank in Uganda. This marks Afreximbank's first-ever extension of such a facility to dfcu Bank.

This transaction aims to strengthen Afreximbank’s support for the Ugandan economy. For investors, this indicates increased liquidity and potential for growth within Uganda's financial sector, particularly for dfcu Bank, which could leverage these funds for various economic activities.

Investors should monitor how dfcu Bank deploys this significant credit line. Observing its impact on economic sectors benefiting from the facility will provide insight into the effectiveness of such international financial support in Uganda.
