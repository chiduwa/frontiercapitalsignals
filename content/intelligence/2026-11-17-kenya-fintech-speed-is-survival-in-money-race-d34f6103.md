Capital FM Business reports that for financial transactions in Kenya, speed is not a luxury but a matter of survival. The report highlights that entities like traders needing payment to restock and parents sending money rely on swift processes. Slow fintechs risk falling behind in this environment.

This indicates a critical market driver for the financial sector in Kenya. Investors should note that operational efficiency and rapid transaction processing are essential competitive advantages. Companies that fail to deliver speed may struggle to gain or retain market share, impacting profitability and growth prospects.

Investors should monitor the transaction speed and efficiency of fintech platforms and financial service providers operating in Kenya. Opportunities may lie with firms that consistently demonstrate superior speed and reliability in their payment solutions.
