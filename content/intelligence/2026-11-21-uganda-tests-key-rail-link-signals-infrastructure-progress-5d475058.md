Nile Post reports that Uganda Railways is conducting tests for its Kampala-Jinja passenger train service. This assessment focuses on operational readiness and the passenger experience. The service is expected to launch on December 5, with the journey anticipated to last two to three hours.

This development represents tangible progress in Uganda's transport infrastructure. An operational passenger rail link between Kampala and Jinja could improve connectivity and reduce travel times in a key economic corridor. While the report focuses on passenger services, the successful activation of this route generally signals broader investment in crucial logistics.

Investors should observe the launch and initial operational stability of this new service. Continued government commitment to railway infrastructure, potentially including freight capacity or further route extensions, would indicate expanding opportunities in logistics and related sectors.
