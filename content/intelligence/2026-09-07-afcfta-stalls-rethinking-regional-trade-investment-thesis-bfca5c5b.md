Despite significant institutional progress, the African Continental Free Trade Area is not delivering expected results. Border closures and non-tariff barriers persist, highlighting a gap between policy and practical implementation. Accountability for these delays remains unclear, hindering the vision of a seamless continental market.

This persistent stalling directly impacts businesses banking on expanded regional market access and reduced trade costs. Investors must anticipate continued logistical challenges, higher import-export frictions, and slower cross-border supply chain integration across AfCFTA signatory nations, affecting growth projections and regional expansion strategies.

Monitor specific countries, especially Ghana, Kenya, and Nigeria, for any concrete steps towards border harmonization or tariff reductions. Watch for shifts in political will and enforcement mechanisms. Also, assess the impact on local market competitiveness as external trade might remain more accessible than intra-African trade for the foreseeable future.
