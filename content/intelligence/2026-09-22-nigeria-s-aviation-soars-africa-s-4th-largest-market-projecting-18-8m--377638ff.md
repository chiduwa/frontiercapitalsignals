Nigeria's aviation market has rapidly grown to become the fourth largest in Africa. The FAAN Managing Director projects the market will handle 18.8 million domestic and international passengers by 2025, a significant indicator of robust expansion and increasing air travel demand across the country.

This growth presents a compelling signal for investors, reflecting increasing economic activity, a rising middle class, and expanding business connectivity within Nigeria. It highlights substantial opportunities in aviation infrastructure, airport services, logistics, tourism, and the broader hospitality sectors, underpinning the market's potential for sustained demand and profitability.

Investors should closely monitor progress on planned airport expansions, potential private sector participation in aviation infrastructure projects, and any new regulatory reforms. Tracking actual passenger numbers against the 2025 projection will be key, alongside observing performance trends across domestic and international carriers.
