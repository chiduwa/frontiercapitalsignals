BusinessDay reports the Federal Government will on Wednesday begin comprehensive modernization and upgrade of the Lagos Port Complex, Apapa, and the Tin-Can ports.

This significant infrastructure initiative aims to boost cargo handling. For investors, improved port efficiency could enhance supply chain reliability, reduce operational costs for import and export businesses, and potentially stimulate trade volumes in Nigeria.

Investors should closely watch the commencement and initial phases of this modernization effort, assessing its progress towards the stated objective of boosting cargo handling at these critical ports.
