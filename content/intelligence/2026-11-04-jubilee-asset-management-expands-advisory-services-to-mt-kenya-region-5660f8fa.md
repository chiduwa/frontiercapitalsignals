Capital FM Business reports that Jubilee Asset Management Limited has opened a new financial advisory centre. This centre is located in Meru and aims to expand the company's services into the Mt. Kenya and Upper Eastern regions.

This expansion signals a potential for increased penetration of financial advisory services into regional markets beyond Kenya's main urban hubs. For investors, it indicates growth opportunities within the asset management sector and suggests a possible increase in overall financial literacy and investment activity in these areas.

Investors should monitor the pace of adoption of these services in the newly targeted regions. It will be important to observe if this move prompts other financial institutions to consider similar regional expansions, potentially signaling a broader trend in the market.
