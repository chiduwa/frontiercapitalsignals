BusinessDay reports that according to Okwuosa, energy security must be prioritized over digital inclusion in Nigeria and other African countries. The statement highlights that these nations cannot assume renewables will soon replace fossil fuels and therefore need to secure reliable energy sources.

For investors, this signals a potential policy direction favoring conventional energy infrastructure and fossil fuel development over an immediate, aggressive shift to renewables. Companies involved in fossil fuel exploration, extraction, and traditional power generation in Nigeria and across the continent may find a more favorable operating environment or sustained demand for their services.

Investors should closely watch for concrete policy decisions, regulatory changes, and infrastructure project announcements that align with this stated priority. Specific government commitments to fossil fuel-based projects or extended timelines for renewable transitions will be key indicators of this long-term energy strategy.
