The recent "lowest solar tariff in Africa" was not the market signal many anticipated. Initial perceptions of a booming solar market or significant cost reductions are likely premature. Instead, this figure points to a flawed tender process, where only one developer remained standing.

This situation highlights a critical due diligence point. Exceptionally low tariffs can indicate market instability or a lack of robust competition, not necessarily an optimal investment environment. For investors, it signals potential project execution risks, unsustainable pricing, or underlying regulatory issues masked by an attractive headline number.

Moving forward, closely monitor the specifics of these "record-low" deals. Assess the transparency of tender processes, the diversity of participating developers, and the long-term financial viability of such projects. This pattern bears watching across new energy tenders in the region, particularly in our target markets.
