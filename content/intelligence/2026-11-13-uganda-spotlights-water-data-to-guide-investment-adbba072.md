Nile Post reports that the Uganda Bureau of Statistics (UBOS) will use African Statistics Week to focus on current gaps in water safety data. This initiative is geared towards promoting stronger statistical systems.

For investors, this signals a potential push for improved data transparency and availability within Uganda's water and sanitation sector. Stronger statistical systems, as promoted by UBOS, are intended to guide investment, enhance service delivery, and bolster climate preparedness. This could lead to clearer opportunities or reduced uncertainty for capital deployment in related infrastructure and services.

Investors should monitor how UBOS's focus translates into concrete data initiatives or policy changes. The development and implementation of these stronger statistical systems, and their impact on informing and facilitating private sector involvement in water and sanitation projects, will be important to watch.
