Nigerian police tear-gassed protesting pensioners in Lagos who were demanding unpaid post-retirement entitlements. This incident drew strong condemnation from civil rights activists Falana and Sowore.

This event signals potential fiscal strain within government finances, impacting its ability to meet long-term obligations. Such public unrest and heavy-handed responses can erode investor confidence by highlighting governance challenges and increasing political risk.

Investors should monitor the government's strategy for resolving pension arrears and broader public sector financial commitments. Watch for any escalation of social unrest or policy responses indicating improved fiscal management and accountability.
