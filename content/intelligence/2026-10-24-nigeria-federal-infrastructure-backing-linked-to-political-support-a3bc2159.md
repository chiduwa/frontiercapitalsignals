Premium Times reports that Plateau State Governor Caleb Mutfwang urged indigenes of Plateau State living in Kaduna to support President Bola Tinubu's re-election. The governor reportedly cited federal backing for infrastructure projects in Plateau as a reason for this political endorsement.

This event signals a potential correlation between political alignment and federal project allocation within Nigeria. For investors, the mention of "federal backing for infrastructure projects" could indicate areas of potential government spending and opportunities in sectors like construction or public works, particularly in Plateau State. It highlights how political considerations may influence the distribution of federal resources, a key factor when assessing regional investment attractiveness.
