The High Street Journal reports that Seth Terkper is advocating for Ghana to accelerate local mineral processing. He argues this move is crucial for increasing mining revenues and industrializing the economy. His reasoning highlights value addition, stronger export financing, and reduced gold smuggling as key benefits.

This potential policy shift could significantly impact Ghana's mining sector, making it more attractive for investors focused on value chain integration. Companies involved in mineral processing, technology for value addition, or export finance could see new opportunities. Reduced gold smuggling could also improve transparency and official revenue streams, benefiting legitimate operators and the broader economy.

Investors should monitor any legislative or regulatory developments stemming from Terkper's push. Observing government initiatives to support local processing, new financing mechanisms, or measures to combat smuggling will be important for understanding potential changes to the investment landscape in Ghana's mining and industrial sectors.
