Nigeria's digital economy promise remains largely unfulfilled, held back by significant fibre deployment delays and persistent poor network quality. This marks a critical shortfall three years into Minister Bosun Tijani's tenure, impacting millions.

This creates a substantial bottleneck for growth in Nigeria's fintech, e-commerce, and broader digital service sectors. Investors should note both the immense opportunity in core digital infrastructure, and the operational hurdles for digital service providers needing reliable connectivity to scale.

Monitor government policy actions and accelerated public private partnerships specifically targeting fibre rollout and network infrastructure upgrades. Key indicators will be regulatory incentives for providers and concrete timelines for project completion, signaling a potential shift.
