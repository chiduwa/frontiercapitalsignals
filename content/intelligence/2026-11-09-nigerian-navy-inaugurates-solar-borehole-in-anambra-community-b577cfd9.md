Premium Times reports the Nigerian Navy inaugurated a solar-powered borehole in an Anambra community. This project was carried out under the Navy’s Civil-Military Cooperation initiative and the Chief of Naval Staff’s Special Intervention Quick Impact Project Scheme. The initiative was facilitated in honor of Rear Admiral Ifeanyi Okpala, who is from the community.

This event indicates a government-led focus on critical infrastructure development at the community level, specifically incorporating solar technology for sustainable solutions.
