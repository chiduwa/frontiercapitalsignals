TechCabal reports that the Nigerian government launched the 3 Million Technical Talent (3MTT) program in 2023. This initiative aims to train three million Nigerians by 2027. The program seeks to build a workforce for the country’s digital economy, address technical skills gaps, and position Nigeria as a global supplier of technology talent, connecting trainees to local and international opportunities.

This policy signifies the Nigerian government's commitment to developing its digital economy and human capital. For investors, it suggests a potentially growing pool of skilled tech talent, which could reduce operational costs and improve the quality of local tech workforces. Businesses in the tech sector, or those relying on digital skills, may find a more robust talent pipeline emerging over the next few years.

Investors should monitor the progress of the 3MTT program in achieving its training targets and its success in connecting participants to job opportunities. The actual absorption rate of these trained talents into the workforce will be key. Further government initiatives supporting digital infrastructure and broader tech sector growth would also be important signals.
