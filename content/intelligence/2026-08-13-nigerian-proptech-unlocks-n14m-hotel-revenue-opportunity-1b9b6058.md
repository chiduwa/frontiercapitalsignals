A Nigerian property technology startup has uncovered a N14m opportunity by addressing hidden revenue leaks within the country's hotel and serviced apartment sector. This bootstrapped company is tackling a significant operational pain point for hoteliers, offering solutions to reclaim lost income.

This signals a significant, addressable market for operational efficiency in Nigeria's hospitality sector. Investors should note the potential for high-ROI tech solutions that directly improve hotel profitability by tackling existing revenue losses, rather than only creating new demand. This type of PropTech offers strong scalability within a growing market.

Investors should closely monitor the startup's adoption rates and verifiable revenue recovery figures across various Nigerian hotels. Watch for market traction, potential for scaling across major urban centers, and any signals of expansion into broader West African hospitality markets. Also, observe competitive responses validating this market segment.
