The High Street Journal reports that the Chamber of Agribusiness Ghana has urged Parliament to postpone approval of the GH¢30 billion 2027 agricultural allocation. The Chamber is demanding that the Finance Ministry itemise expenditure for the allocation before parliamentary approval.

This development signals potential delays in the disbursement of significant agricultural funding in Ghana. Investors in the sector should note the Chamber's concerns about seven unfunded sectors, declining real funding levels, and unaddressed El Niño climate risks. These issues, if not resolved, could impact the sector's long-term growth and resilience, affecting returns on agricultural investments.

Investors should closely monitor how Parliament responds to the Chamber's request and whether the Finance Ministry provides the demanded expenditure breakdown. Any shifts in the allocation's structure or resolution of the identified funding gaps and climate risks will be critical to understand future policy direction and potential investment opportunities or challenges in Ghana's agriculture sector.
