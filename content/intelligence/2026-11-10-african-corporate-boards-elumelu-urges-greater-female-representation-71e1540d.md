BusinessDay reports that Tony Elumelu, chairman of Heirs Holdings, has urged African businesses to accelerate the appointment of women to senior corporate positions. This statement from a prominent continental business leader signals a growing emphasis on gender diversity within the region's corporate power structures.

For investors, this call suggests an evolving landscape for corporate governance and environmental, social, and governance (ESG) considerations across African markets. Companies that proactively respond to such calls by enhancing female representation in senior roles may differentiate themselves, potentially improving their corporate image and long-term sustainability appeal.

Investors should monitor whether this advocacy translates into measurable actions by African businesses. Observe any subsequent discussions from industry associations or other influential figures regarding diversity initiatives, as well as any emerging metrics on female leadership representation across the continent.
