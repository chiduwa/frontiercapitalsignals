South Africa has intensified its crackdown on undocumented foreign nationals. Enforcement operations by police and immigration officials are increasing, leading to more arrests and thorough immigration status checks for individuals interacting with the criminal justice system.

This escalation directly impacts regional economies, particularly Malawi, by disrupting critical remittance flows. It also raises concerns about potential labor supply shifts and increased social pressure in migrant-sending countries if a significant number of individuals return or face difficulties working abroad.

Investors should monitor the scale of repatriations and any subsequent changes in remittance patterns to Malawi. Also, track potential social stability implications in Malawi and the Malawian government's policy responses to support returning citizens or address labor market adjustments.
