Ethiopian forces are currently battling Tigray rebels in the Afar region, with widespread reports of an internet blackout across northern Ethiopia. This marks a significant escalation in the country's protracted conflict, highlighting a deteriorating security situation.

This resurgence of hostilities and the accompanying communication disruption heighten regional instability, impacting overall investor sentiment towards East Africa. Such events underscore critical political and operational risks, particularly concerning connectivity, supply chain reliability, and the potential for broader humanitarian crises affecting regional economies.

Investors should closely monitor the conflict's trajectory, especially any spillover effects into neighboring states. Watch for communication restoration efforts and the broader impact on humanitarian access, regional economic stability, and the overall perception of political risk across the East African market.
