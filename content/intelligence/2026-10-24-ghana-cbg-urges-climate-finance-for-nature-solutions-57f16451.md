MyJoyOnline Business reports that Consolidated Bank Ghana (CBG) has highlighted limited access to finance, grants, and donor support as key obstacles to scaling nature-based solutions in Ghana and across Africa. Mrs. Eva Ackuaku, CBG's Head of Sustainable Financing and Impact Department, noted that commercial viability concerns often prevent these projects from securing conventional bank financing. She presented these views at the Africa Climate Dialogue 2026.

This matters for investors as it signals a growing recognition from a major Ghanaian bank of the untapped potential and existing financing gaps in nature-based solutions. The call for increased financial support, an enabling policy environment, and stronger collaboration among financial institutions and development partners could pave the way for new investment opportunities in this emerging sector.

Investors should monitor policy developments and collaborative efforts among financial institutions and development partners in Ghana and across Africa. Any progress in creating a more conducive policy environment or new financing mechanisms could reduce the perceived risks and improve the commercial viability of nature-based projects, potentially opening up new avenues for private capital.
