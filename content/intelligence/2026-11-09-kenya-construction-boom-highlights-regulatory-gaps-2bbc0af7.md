The Standard reports Kenya's construction sector is experiencing a boom, evidenced by rising project registrations. This growth, however, is leading to scrutiny over building safety and professional oversight. Gaps in compliance and regulation are being exposed by the increasing number of projects.

For investors, this signals potential increased regulatory risk and potentially higher operational costs in Kenya's construction sector. Heightened scrutiny could lead to stricter enforcement, new compliance requirements, or delays in project approvals. While the sector is growing, these developments suggest a need for thorough due diligence on regulatory adherence.

Investors should monitor how Kenyan authorities respond to these exposed gaps. Watch for any proposed policy changes, new regulatory frameworks, or increased enforcement actions concerning building safety and professional standards. Such developments could reshape the investment landscape for construction and real estate in Kenya.
