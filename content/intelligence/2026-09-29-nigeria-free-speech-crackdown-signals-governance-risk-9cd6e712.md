Nigerian police arrested a university lecturer following a social media post critical of a Kogi state road project. The arrest, made in Ilorin, reportedly stemmed from a petition by the state security adviser alleging false claims.

This incident highlights potential political risk and challenges to free expression within the Nigerian operating environment. Such actions, particularly when related to public infrastructure projects and state criticism, can signal broader governance concerns, regulatory unpredictability, and a lack of transparency for foreign investors. A predictable legal and social environment is crucial for long-term capital deployment.

Monitor the lecturer's legal proceedings and any official statements from Kogi State or the federal government. Investors should also observe broader trends in civil liberties and government responses to public criticism across Nigerian states, as these indicate shifts in the political risk landscape.
