Premium Times reports that Nigerian lawmakers are proposing road concession and toll gates as a strategy to address the country's infrastructure deficit. This comes amid concerns that the current approach, relying on annual budgetary allocations, has not adequately supported the necessary rehabilitation and maintenance of the extensive federal road network.

This policy proposal could signal a shift towards greater private sector involvement in Nigeria's infrastructure development. For investors, potential road concessions and the introduction of toll gates could create new avenues for public-private partnerships, offering long-term investment opportunities in a critical sector. The move aims to secure more consistent funding for road infrastructure.

Investors should monitor the progress of these proposals within the legislative body. Key developments to watch include the specific details of any proposed concession frameworks, potential government incentives, and the overall legislative commitment to attracting private capital for road projects. The implementation timeline and regulatory environment will be crucial for assessing viability.
