TechCabal reports that Luminate CEO Melanie Hui warned at the Build 2026 Conference in Abuja that Nigeria experiences significant annual losses due to its reliance on foreign cloud services. She emphasized that for Africa to prosper in the global AI economy, it must establish genuine digital sovereignty and ownership of its infrastructure.

This sentiment signals a strategic focus on localizing digital infrastructure, particularly cloud services, across the continent. Investors should note the potential for increased demand and policy support for ventures that build or operate data centers, cloud platforms, and related digital infrastructure within Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda. The stated financial losses underscore a clear economic incentive for governments to back such initiatives.

Investors should monitor statements from regional bodies and national governments regarding digital sovereignty policies, local content requirements for cloud services, and incentives for domestic infrastructure development. Signs of concrete policy shifts or public-private partnerships aimed at reducing foreign cloud dependency would indicate actionable opportunities in the tech infrastructure sector.
