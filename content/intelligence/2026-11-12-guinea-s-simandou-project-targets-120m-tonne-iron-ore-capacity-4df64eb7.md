Africa Briefing reports that Guinea's Simandou iron ore project is preparing its railway and port network. This infrastructure aims to handle 120 million tonnes annually by the end of 2027. Rio Tinto expects full SimFer production in 2028.

This development signals a significant increase in Guinea's potential export capacity for a critical global commodity. Investors should note the scale of this project, which Africa Briefing suggests could reshape Guinea's economy and global supply dynamics. The timeline provides a clear horizon for infrastructure readiness and production ramp-up.

Moving forward, investors should monitor progress on the infrastructure development towards the 2027 capacity target and the readiness of the mines supplying it. Further details on the operational ramp-up for SimFer production in 2028 will also be crucial to assess the project's overall impact.
