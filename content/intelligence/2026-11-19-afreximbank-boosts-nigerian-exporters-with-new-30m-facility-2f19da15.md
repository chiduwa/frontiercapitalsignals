Africa Briefing reports that Afreximbank has agreed to provide a $30 million facility to the Nigerian Export-Import Bank (NEXIM). This funding aims to assist smaller businesses in Nigeria with financing production, fulfilling export orders, and reaching customers in new markets across Africa and globally. Specifically, $20 million is designated for revolving working capital.

This initiative signifies enhanced financial support for Nigeria's export sector, including creative firms and other eligible exporters. For investors, this could highlight potential growth in businesses benefiting from improved working capital access and expanded market reach. It suggests an environment focused on fostering non-oil export capabilities.

Fund managers should track the deployment of this facility and its resulting impact on export volumes and market diversification for Nigerian small and medium-sized enterprises. Monitoring which sectors show the most significant growth due to this financing could pinpoint specific investment opportunities. Further government or institutional efforts to boost export financing will also be key.
