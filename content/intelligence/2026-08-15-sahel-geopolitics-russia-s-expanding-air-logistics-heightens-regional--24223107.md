An American NGO report confirms Russia's 'Africa Corps' air logistics network. Seven airlines, including state-owned Russian and African carriers, are supplying bases in Mali, Burkina Faso, and Niger. This solidifies Russia's expanding military footprint in the Sahel.

This development signals intensifying geopolitical competition and heightened regional instability. For investors in Ghana and Nigeria, it raises concerns about security spillover, potential disruption to trade corridors, and a re-evaluation of Western security partnerships. Geopolitical shifts could influence foreign direct investment appetite.
