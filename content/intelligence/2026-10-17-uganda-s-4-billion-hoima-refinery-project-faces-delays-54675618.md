The Independent (Uganda) reports that the US$4 billion oil refinery planned for Kabaale in Hoima District is targeting a Final Investment Decision (FID) in February 2027. This refinery is projected to process 60,000 barrels of crude oil per day.

For investors, the targeted FID in 2027 indicates a long development timeline for this significant infrastructure project. The report highlights outstanding key agreements, engineering work, and arrangements for transporting heavy equipment, signaling potential hurdles or further delays in reaching this milestone.

Monitoring progress on these key outstanding agreements and the crucial engineering work will be important. The status of arrangements for transporting heavy equipment will also provide insight into the project's overall advancement towards its targeted FID.
