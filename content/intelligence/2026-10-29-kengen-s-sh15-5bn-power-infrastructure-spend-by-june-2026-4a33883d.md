Capital FM Business reports that KenGen spent Sh15.5 billion on power infrastructure in the year to June 2026. This represents a 14.3 percent increase from the previous year. The report indicates this spending is tied to KenGen, an electricity generator, increasing its operations.

This significant capital expenditure by a key player like KenGen highlights ongoing investment and growth within Kenya's power generation and infrastructure sector. The reported 14.3 percent year-on-year increase suggests a robust expansion strategy, potentially leading to increased capacity or efficiency. For investors, this indicates a commitment to long-term sector development.

What specific areas of infrastructure KenGen is targeting with this increased capital is not detailed in the report. Investors should watch for further announcements regarding the nature of these projects and their anticipated impact on generation capacity and energy supply in Kenya.
