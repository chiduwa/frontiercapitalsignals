The High Street Journal reports on a legal analysis regarding GoldBod's proposed regularisation for small-scale mining in Ghana. This proposal is argued to potentially conflict with Ghanaian law, which mandates licenses before mining can commence and criminalises unlicensed operations.

This development suggests potential regulatory instability or reform in Ghana's mining sector. Investors in the sector, particularly those with exposure to small-scale operations, should note the reported tension between proposed changes and established legal frameworks. This could impact the legality and operational risk of mining activities.

Investors should monitor the progression of GoldBod's proposed regularisation and any official responses or clarifications from Ghanaian legal authorities concerning its compatibility with current mining statutes.
