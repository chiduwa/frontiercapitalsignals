Prominent Nigerian lawyer Femi Falana has called for an immediate ban on petrol importation. He urges the government to redirect crude oil to domestic refiners, asserting this would reduce import dependence and ease hardship from subsidy removal.

This proposal signals potential policy shifts favoring local refining, a boost for the domestic energy sector. Investors should consider opportunities in refinery upgrades, logistics, and associated petroleum product distribution networks. Reduced forex pressure from imports could also stabilize the Naira, improving overall market sentiment.

Monitor government pronouncements regarding this proposal and timelines for policy implementation. Key indicators include progress on local refinery capacity expansion, particularly the operationalization of the Dangote refinery and rehabilitation efforts on state-owned facilities. Watch for any legislative support or resistance to a ban.
