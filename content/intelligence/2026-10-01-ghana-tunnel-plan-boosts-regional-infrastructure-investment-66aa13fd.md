Ghana is advancing plans for a significant 2.7km tunnel through the Akuapem range. This tunnel is a key component of its Abidjan-Lagos Corridor Highway section, a major regional infrastructure project. The new highway will substantially bypass the existing N1 route.

This project signals a firm commitment to regional trade and logistics infrastructure development. Investors should see opportunities in construction, engineering services, and potentially logistics and real estate along the new corridor. Improved connectivity will reduce transit times and costs, boosting economic activity and potentially raising land values in key areas.

Monitor progress on financing agreements and the finalization of detailed designs. Identify specific tenders for construction and engineering firms. Also, track the timeline for project commencement and any associated policy frameworks that could create further investment incentives, such as special economic zones along the corridor.
