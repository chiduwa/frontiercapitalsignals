Kenya's annual inflation rebounded in July, reversing June's decline. This acceleration was primarily driven by persistently high transport and food prices, keeping headline inflation elevated.

This resurgence signals potential for continued monetary tightening by the Central Bank of Kenya. Higher interest rates will increase borrowing costs for businesses and consumers, impacting corporate profitability, capital expenditure plans, and overall economic growth projections. Investors should anticipate pressure on equity valuations and assess fixed income opportunities carefully.

We will closely monitor upcoming inflation data, particularly the trends in food and fuel costs. Watch for any further policy signals from the CBK and government interventions aimed at mitigating living costs. Sector-specific impacts, especially on consumer-facing businesses, warrant close attention.
