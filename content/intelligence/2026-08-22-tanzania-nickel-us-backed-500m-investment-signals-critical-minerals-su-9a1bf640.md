A US government-backed consortium is reportedly considering a $500 million investment in Tanzania's Kabanga nickel project. This potential deal for strategic nickel, copper, and cobalt assets reflects Washington's accelerated push to secure critical mineral supply chains in Africa.

This development signals significant government confidence and de-risking for critical mineral projects on the continent. For investors, it highlights a robust pipeline of strategic capital entering the African mining sector, potentially enhancing project viability and asset valuations across related critical mineral plays.

Monitor the formalization of this investment and any new US government-backed initiatives targeting critical minerals in other African nations. We expect increased strategic interest and FDI flow into similar projects, particularly those involving nickel, copper, cobalt, and lithium across East and Southern Africa.
