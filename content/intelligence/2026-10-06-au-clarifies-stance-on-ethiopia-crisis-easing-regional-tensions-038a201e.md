The African Union Commission clarified it never intended to blame Egypt for Ethiopia's deepening internal crisis. This followed implied accusations, which Cairo promptly rejected, asserting no role in Ethiopia's internal turmoil.

This diplomatic de-escalation, while specific to Egypt and Ethiopia, is a positive signal for regional stability. Intertwined Horn tensions often create uncertainty, impacting investor sentiment across East Africa. Clear diplomatic efforts to manage these disputes reduce perceived geopolitical risk, especially in the wider East African bloc.

Monitor ongoing diplomatic engagements between AU member states, particularly regarding regional security and water resource management, like the Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam. The speed and transparency of these resolutions will indicate the region's commitment to mitigating interconnected geopolitical risks.
