The Independent (Uganda) reports that Uganda is using the World Travel Market (WTM) London to promote itself. The country is marketing itself as a destination for both football fans and general holidaymakers. This initiative aims to showcase Uganda's tourism attractions and expanding sports tourism opportunities at a leading international travel trade exhibition.

This promotional push indicates Uganda's strategic focus on growing its tourism sector, with a specific emphasis on sports tourism. For investors, this could highlight potential growth areas in hospitality, travel services, and infrastructure that supports sports events and related activities within Uganda. Increased international visibility for Uganda's tourism could lead to higher demand and new business ventures.

Investors should monitor the effectiveness of this WTM London presence and any subsequent government or private sector initiatives. Observing further investments in sports facilities, accommodation, or specialized travel packages designed to attract football fans and tourists will be key indicators of sustained commitment and potential market expansion.
