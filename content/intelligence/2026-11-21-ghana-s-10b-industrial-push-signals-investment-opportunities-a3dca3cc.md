MyJoyOnline Business reports that Simon Madjie, CEO of the Ghana Investment Promotion Authority (GIPA), announced the government's New Economy agenda. This agenda aims to accelerate industrialisation, attract investment, and strengthen Ghana's productive capacity. A 2027-2030 program, valued at US$10 billion, is part of this initiative, intending to transform the economy into a production-led, industrialised, and export-driven one through strategic investments.

This initiative signals a clear government focus on industrial transformation and investment attraction, backed by a substantial US$10 billion program. The shift towards a production-led, industrialised, and export-driven economy could create new opportunities across various sectors for international investors. The stated goal of attracting investment directly aligns with investor interests, suggesting potential for favourable policies or incentives to follow.

Investors should monitor specific details regarding the "strategic investments" and the sectors targeted by the US$10 billion program, as these are not specified in the current report. Further announcements from GIPA or relevant government ministries regarding policy implementation and sector priorities will be crucial for identifying actionable investment opportunities in Ghana.
