Nyasa Times reports that National Bank of Malawi (NBM) plc has awarded 15 scholarships to students at the Malawi University of Science and Technology (MUST). These awards are part of the bank's Mo Excellence programme, marking a step up in its investment in higher education. The NBM chief executive, Harold Jiya, stated the bank intends to fund 103 students, though further details were not provided.

This initiative signals NBM plc's increased commitment to human capital development and corporate social responsibility in Malawi's education sector. For investors, such actions by a major financial institution suggest a long-term view of contributing to a skilled workforce, which can improve the overall business operating environment. It also highlights local banks' growing engagement in community-focused programs beyond their core financial services.

Investors should monitor NBM plc's progress on its stated intention to fund a larger number of students, as the full scope of this program remains unclear from the report. Observe if other Malawian financial institutions follow suit with similar education-focused investments, potentially indicating a broader trend of private sector support for higher education in the country.
