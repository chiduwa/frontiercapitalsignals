Africa Briefing reports that Ghana is working to secure a greater share of its gold wealth. This involves the government raising royalties and preparing revisions to the laws governing mining.

These government actions are challenging Newmont's confidence, with its chief executive calling for investment certainty. For investors, the higher royalties are increasing mining costs in Ghana, which impacts operational profitability for companies in the sector.

Investors should monitor the specific changes being prepared for Ghana's mining laws. The outcome of these policy revisions will be crucial in determining the future investment climate and operational framework for gold mining in the country.
