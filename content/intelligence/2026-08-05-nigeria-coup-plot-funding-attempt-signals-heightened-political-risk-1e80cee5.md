Premium Times reported on a suspect in Nigeria's alleged 2025 coup plot. The individual detailed alleged efforts to recruit financiers for the plot, suggesting a serious attempt to destabilize the government through financially backed means.

This revelation underscores persistent political risk in Nigeria, particularly the potential for extra-constitutional changes. Such instability directly impacts investor confidence, asset valuations, and project viability, increasing the risk premium for capital deployment.

Watch ongoing investigations and judicial proceedings closely. Key indicators include government responses, the robustness of democratic institutions, and public sentiment. These will signal the plot's true depth and the government's capacity to maintain stability.
