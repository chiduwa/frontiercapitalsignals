BusinessDay reports that Nigeria's Vice President proposed a peer review framework aimed at accelerating the implementation of the 2023 Electricity Act. The Vice President urged states to leverage this Act to end their reliance on the federal government for power. The proposal also includes a focus on consumers' protection.

This policy initiative signals a potential decentralization of power sector development. For investors, this could open up new opportunities at the state level for power generation, distribution, and related infrastructure projects. The push to reduce federal dependence might streamline state-led energy initiatives, potentially improving the ease of doing business in local power markets. A strong emphasis on consumer protection could also contribute to a more stable and predictable market environment.

Investors should monitor the adoption and operationalization of this peer review framework by state governments. Observe which states proactively take advantage of the 2023 Electricity Act to develop their own power sector initiatives. Also, watch for specific policy or regulatory changes at the state level that clarify investment guidelines and enforce consumer protection measures.
