Nigeria's power sector overhaul, led by Joseph Tegbe, has been characterized by 'modest achievements' in its first 100 days. This assessment indicates early, incremental progress rather than a swift transformation in this vital infrastructure area.

This signals continued long-term investment opportunities in Nigeria's power infrastructure, albeit with an understanding of the slow pace of reform. Modest but consistent improvements can build a more stable operating environment, attractive to patient capital willing to navigate inherent challenges.

Investors should monitor specific policy rollouts, measurable upticks in generation or distribution efficiency, and further clarity on private sector engagement. Sustained government commitment, even if gradual, remains key to realizing the sector’s significant potential.
