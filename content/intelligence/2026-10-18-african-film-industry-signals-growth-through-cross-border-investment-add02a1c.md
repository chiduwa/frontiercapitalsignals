The Independent (Uganda) reports that African filmmakers and industry leaders are advocating for stronger cross-border partnerships, investment, and business structures. This initiative aims to help the continent's film industry reach wider audiences and become more commercially sustainable.

This development signals a clear intent from industry stakeholders to enhance the commercial viability and market reach of African cinema. For investors, it highlights a sector actively seeking capital and strategic alliances to overcome fragmentation and achieve scale. There could be opportunities in financing new productions, distribution channels, or technology supporting cross-border collaboration.

Investors should monitor specific proposals for new business structures and any policy developments designed to facilitate cross-border film projects. Observing where investment is directed and what types of partnerships emerge will be key to identifying actionable opportunities within the evolving African film landscape.
