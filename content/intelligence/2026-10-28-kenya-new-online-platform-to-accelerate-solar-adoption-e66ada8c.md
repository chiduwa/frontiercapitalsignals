The Standard reports that Kenya's solar market is expanding. An online marketplace is set to offer solar inverters and battery storage systems. These products will be available to households, businesses, and installers who are seeking equipment.

This development signals increased accessibility and potentially greater adoption of solar energy solutions in Kenya. For investors, it points to a maturing market with new distribution channels emerging to meet existing demand from various consumer segments. Opportunities may arise in logistics, e-commerce infrastructure, or direct investment in firms leveraging this new marketplace.

Investors should monitor the uptake and scale of this online marketplace. Key indicators include the volume of sales, the range of products offered, and its impact on the broader solar equipment supply chain. Further insights into the specific firms behind this initiative could also reveal new investment pathways.
