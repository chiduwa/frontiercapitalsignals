Premium Times reports that the PEER Foundation has urged the President not to treat the Ibom Deep Seaport as a reward for electoral support. The foundation asserts that Akwa Ibom's contribution to national revenue provides its people a legitimate claim to federal infrastructure investment.

This development is significant for investors, as it highlights potential political considerations in the allocation and progression of major infrastructure projects. The debate over whether the Ibom Deep Seaport's development is based on economic merit or political motives could influence investor confidence in the predictability of project execution.

Investors should monitor the government's response and any subsequent decisions regarding the Ibom Deep Seaport. The criteria ultimately applied for this federal investment will offer insight into the broader approach to infrastructure development and resource distribution within key economic regions.
