Houthi activity is escalating in Bab el-Mandeb. Despite the heightened risk, soaring fuel costs are now incentivizing carriers to reconsider using this shorter route, directly threatening the momentum and investment in West Africa's burgeoning mega-ship port developments.

This shift poses a significant headwind for existing and planned infrastructure investments in West African deepwater ports and associated logistics. Capital allocated to build capacity and capture mega-ship traffic could face reduced utilization and diminished returns as shipping volumes potentially divert. Djibouti, along with a select few other strategic deepwater hubs, will likely capture the redirected traffic.
