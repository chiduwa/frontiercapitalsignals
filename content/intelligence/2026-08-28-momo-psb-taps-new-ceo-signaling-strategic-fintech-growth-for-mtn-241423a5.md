MoMo Payment Service Bank, MTN Nigeria's fintech arm licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria, announced Bode Abifarin as its new Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer. The appointment is effective September 1, 2026, marking a significant leadership transition for the digital financial services provider.

This strategic leadership change, with its distant effective date, suggests deliberate long-term planning for MoMo PSB's operational expansion and market penetration. Abifarin's vision will be crucial in steering MTN's fintech growth in Nigeria's rapidly evolving and competitive mobile money landscape. It underscores MTN's broader commitment to scaling financial services beyond core telecom offerings.

Investors should monitor forthcoming statements regarding Abifarin's strategic priorities and growth objectives as her start date approaches. Her executive experience and specific plans will determine how MoMo PSB innovates, adapts to regulatory shifts, and competes effectively against rival fintechs and traditional banks in Nigeria. Also, watch for any interim leadership announcements.
