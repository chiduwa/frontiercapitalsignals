Capital FM Business reports that President William Ruto has challenged opposition leaders over their interpretation of a World Bank report. This report raises concerns about corruption and procurement weaknesses.

This matters for investors as a World Bank report highlighting corruption and procurement weaknesses could signal elevated operational risks and potentially impact the cost and transparency of public sector projects. Such concerns can influence investor confidence and project viability, especially in sectors reliant on government contracts or regulatory oversight.

Investors should monitor any further details emerging from the World Bank report's findings or government responses to address the raised concerns. Developments in procurement reforms or anti-corruption initiatives will be key indicators for assessing the investment climate.
