Malawi's Dzaleka Refugee Camp faces severe humanitarian distress. Refugee leaders report a sharp decline in aid, leading to widespread hunger and desperation among families. Conditions have deteriorated significantly, with women reportedly resorting to extreme measures.

This situation signals systemic stress on Malawi's social fabric and potentially strained government capacity. Such escalating desperation can trigger social unrest, disrupt local economies, and impact broader stability metrics. Investors should evaluate potential reputational risks or operational disruptions for projects operating near affected areas or reliant on local labor pools.

Monitor government and donor responses to the crisis closely. Watch for any increases in social unrest or crime rates in affected regions, as these will indicate escalating instability. Pay attention to how this humanitarian situation impacts Malawi's overall risk perception and investment climate in the short to medium term.
