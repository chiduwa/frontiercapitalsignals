TechCabal reports that OPay's COO, Dotun Daniel Adekunle, argues Nigeria's most durable companies are built to survive its worst days. He notes that Nigeria's technology market faces problems like unreliable infrastructure and a volatile economy. Adekunle suggests that "stubbornness is a business model in Africa."

This perspective highlights a crucial operational reality for investors in Nigeria's technology sector and potentially other African markets. It indicates that successful ventures require resilience and strategies accounting for significant market volatility and infrastructural deficits. Investors should evaluate business models for their adaptability and robustness against adverse conditions rather than just growth potential.

Investors should monitor how companies in Nigeria's tech market implement strategies to mitigate risks from unreliable infrastructure and economic volatility. Observing the operational approaches of "durable" companies could provide insights into sustainable investment models in challenging environments.
