Capital FM Business reports that the Kenya Electricity Transmission Company (KETRACO) has secured three ISO certifications, covering quality management, information security, and occupational health and safety. The report also notes KETRACO's expansion of coverage to 38 sites.

For investors, these certifications signal KETRACO's commitment to higher operational standards and risk mitigation within Kenya's crucial electricity transmission network. Coupled with the reported expansion of coverage, this suggests a growing and more robust infrastructure, potentially enhancing confidence in the stability and reliability of the country's energy sector and related projects.

Investors should observe how these certifications and network expansion translate into observable improvements in KETRACO's project execution, network efficiency, or overall financial performance. Future reports on operational stability and new infrastructure developments would be key indicators to monitor.
