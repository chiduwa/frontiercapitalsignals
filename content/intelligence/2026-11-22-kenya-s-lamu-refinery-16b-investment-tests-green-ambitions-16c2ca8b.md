Africa Briefing reports that Kenya's $16bn Lamu refinery project is testing the country's green ambitions, aiming to strengthen energy security and industrial capacity. This development comes as COP31 concludes, highlighting Africa's familiar argument for necessary space, finance, and technology in climate justice discussions.

For investors, this significant capital project signals opportunities in large-scale infrastructure and the energy sector. However, Africa Briefing notes that land disputes and employment promises raise questions about who truly benefits. The project's long-term success also depends on developing skills, integrating local industries, and adapting to the evolving energy transition.

Going forward, investors should monitor how Kenya navigates its climate justice position alongside this major fossil fuel-related investment. Key areas to watch include the resolution of local disputes, progress on skill development and local industrial integration, and the project's demonstrated adaptability to global energy transition demands.
