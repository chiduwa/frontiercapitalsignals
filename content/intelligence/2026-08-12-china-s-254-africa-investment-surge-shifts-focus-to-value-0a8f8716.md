This substantial capital influx signals robust economic activity and validates the long-term growth prospects in these key sectors across the continent. For Frontier Capital investors, this creates significant potential for co-investment opportunities, increased liquidity, and enhanced valuations in markets like Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda.
