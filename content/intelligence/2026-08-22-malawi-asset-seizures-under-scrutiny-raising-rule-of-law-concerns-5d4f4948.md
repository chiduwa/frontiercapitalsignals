Malawi's Human Rights Consultative Committee (HRCC) has raised concerns about 'selective justice' in the country's asset seizure and property forfeiture laws. The HRCC warns these allegations risk eroding public trust in state efforts to recover stolen assets, announcing plans to submit a Reform Charter for review.

This development is crucial for investors. Perceptions of selective justice undermine the rule of law and regulatory predictability, critical factors for foreign direct investment. An unpredictable legal environment, particularly regarding asset ownership and forfeiture, increases operational risk and can deter capital.

Investors should closely monitor the HRCC's formal submission of its Reform Charter and the government's response. The nature of any legislative reforms, or the lack thereof, will signal Malawi's commitment to transparent and equitable application of justice, directly impacting investor confidence.
