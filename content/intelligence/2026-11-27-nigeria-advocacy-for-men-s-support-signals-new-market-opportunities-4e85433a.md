This signals a potential emerging market for services and platforms addressing men's well-being and development in Nigeria. Investors might consider opportunities in areas like vocational training, educational guidance, mental health support services, or community-based initiatives focused on men's empowerment. The call for "greater investment" suggests a recognized need that could attract both public and private sector funding.
