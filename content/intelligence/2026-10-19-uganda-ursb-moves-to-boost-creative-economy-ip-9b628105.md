Nile Post reports that the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB) is spearheading an initiative aimed at strengthening copyright protection within the country. This effort is also intended to foster the growth of Uganda's creative economy.

For international investors, enhanced copyright protection could signal a more secure operating environment for businesses reliant on intellectual property in Uganda. This may improve the attractiveness of sectors such as media, entertainment, software, and other creative industries by potentially reducing risks associated with intellectual property infringement.

Specific details regarding the proposed measures, their implementation timeline, or the anticipated impact on existing businesses are not clear from the report.
