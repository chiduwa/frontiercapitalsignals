The High Street Journal reports that the Bank of Ghana will introduce new Heritage Series cedi notes on December 1, 2026. These new notes will circulate alongside existing banknotes, which will remain legal tender.

This move is positioned by the Bank of Ghana as a gradual transition aimed at avoiding disruption to payments or public confidence. For investors, this signals the central bank's intent to manage currency updates carefully, minimizing potential short-term volatility in transaction systems. The continuation of existing notes as legal tender should prevent immediate market shocks related to currency exchange or acceptance.

Investors should observe the public's reception of the new notes and any communications from the Bank of Ghana leading up to and after the December 2026 introduction. The key will be whether the transition remains as smooth as intended, particularly in retail and cross-border transactions.
