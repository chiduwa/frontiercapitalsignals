BusinessDay reports that the Pan Niger Delta Forum (PANDEF) held a stakeholders meeting where they called on presidential candidates to declare their commitment to environmental justice for the oil region. The forum also expressed concern over the "permanent 13% derivation allocation for decades".

This development is significant for investors in Nigeria's energy sector, particularly those involved in oil and gas exploration, production, and related infrastructure. Demands for environmental justice could translate into future regulatory changes, potentially increasing operational costs or requiring new compliance measures. Discussions around the derivation allocation highlight ongoing regional sensitivities concerning resource revenue distribution, which could influence government policy.

Investors should monitor how presidential candidates respond to these calls for environmental justice and any proposed changes to resource allocation formulas. Future policy pronouncements, especially regarding environmental regulations in the Niger Delta and the distribution of oil revenues, will be key indicators for the long-term operating environment and stability in the region.
