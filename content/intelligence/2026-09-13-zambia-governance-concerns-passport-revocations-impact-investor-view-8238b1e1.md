Zambia's government has revoked the passports of six prominent critics, including a former diplomat and an academic, citing national security concerns. This move by President Hichilema's administration intensifies scrutiny over political freedoms, coming after an August election already facing criticism.

Such actions against critics introduce uncertainty and raise red flags about governance stability and respect for the rule of law. A deteriorating political freedom index often correlates with increased investment risk, as it can signal unpredictable policy shifts and reduced judicial independence, impacting business predictability and capital security.

Investors should monitor the broader government response to dissent and civil society reactions. Observe international community statements and any changes in Zambia's democratic governance indicators. Further tightening of political space could signal higher sovereign risk, warranting a re-evaluation of long-term investment strategies.
