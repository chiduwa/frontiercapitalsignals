Premium Times reports that a public figure has stated that Tinubu failed Nigerians on economy and security. This assessment highlights that insecurity prevents millions of children from attending school, while dangerous roads and the rising cost of living continue to affect Nigerians.

These reported issues signal persistent operational challenges and economic headwinds for businesses in Nigeria. Insecurity impacts human capital development and disrupts logistics, while dangerous roads further impede supply chains and increase transport costs. The rising cost of living suggests pressure on consumer demand and potential for increased business operating expenses.

Investors should monitor developments related to Nigeria's security situation and government efforts to address infrastructure deficiencies and inflation. The stability of the business environment and consumer purchasing power will depend on improvements in these critical areas.
