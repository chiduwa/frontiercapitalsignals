President Mutharika, one year into his second term, received positive independent press assessments. While opinions varied on specific achievements, both reports agreed he has significantly steadied Malawi. Nyasa Times notably scored him 7.6/10, citing progress in free secondary education and a reformed Affordable Inputs Programme.

This verdict signals crucial political stability and policy continuity. A steady hand at the top reduces political risk, fostering a more predictable operating environment for businesses and attracting long-term capital. Improvements in education and agricultural programs suggest focused social spending and potential for broad-based economic benefits, which can underpin market growth.
