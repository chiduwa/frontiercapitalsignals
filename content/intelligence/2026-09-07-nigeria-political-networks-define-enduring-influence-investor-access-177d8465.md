Florence Ajimobi successfully transformed her late husband's political network into sustained influence within the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC). Conversely, Betty Anyanwu-Akeredolu's estrangement from the ruling party exposed the limits of inherited power without direct political alignment.

This dynamic underscores the critical role of entrenched political networks and party loyalty in Nigeria. Investment decisions must account for the durability of influence, which often hinges on strategic alignment with dominant political structures rather than mere familial ties. Stability and access remain tied to these established power channels.

Monitor future power transitions and succession planning within Nigeria's political landscape. Observe how new figures establish or integrate into established networks, as continued reliance on inherited, unaligned power will likely face similar challenges. This dictates future policy predictability and influence channels.
