Landlords and tenants across the region commonly use WhatsApp for daily communications, from rent reminders to maintenance requests. However, the legal validity of critical notices, such as evictions, delivered through this platform remains uncertain, creating a grey area for property management.

This regulatory ambiguity significantly impacts property investors. Unclear legal standing for eviction notices increases operational risk, potentially prolonging tenant disputes, delaying property repossession, and impacting rental income streams. It can lead to higher legal costs and depress property valuations by adding uncertainty to asset management.

Investors should monitor legislative developments and court rulings that clarify the legal status of digital communication in landlord-tenant law. Any formalization or clarification will directly influence property market risk premiums and asset liquidity across the continent.
