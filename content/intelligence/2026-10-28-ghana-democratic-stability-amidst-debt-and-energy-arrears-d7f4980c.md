The High Street Journal reports that Ghana ranks 1st in West Africa and 6th continentally in an RMB report, yet it also sits in the "Basin" quadrant. The report indicates that while Ghana benefits from strong democratic governance, offering a degree of stability, underlying economic vulnerabilities persist.

For investors, this presents a nuanced picture. The Journal highlights that Ghana's strong democratic governance is a positive factor, potentially fostering a predictable operating environment. However, this stability is reportedly undermined by lingering debt default liabilities and significant energy sector arrears, which the report states erode economic resilience.

Investors should closely observe how Ghana addresses these contrasting forces. The presence of strong democratic governance against the backdrop of persistent debt and energy liabilities creates a situation where the long-term economic trajectory is subject to how these core issues are managed.
