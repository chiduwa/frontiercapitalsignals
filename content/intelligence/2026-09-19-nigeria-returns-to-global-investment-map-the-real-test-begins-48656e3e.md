J.P. Morgan and FTSE Russell are actively repositioning Nigerian assets for global investors. This officially marks Nigeria's return to the international investment radar.

This development significantly broadens the market for Nigerian debt and equities, signaling renewed confidence from major financial institutions. It presents a critical window for international capital deployment into a market with substantial growth potential.

The real test now lies in Nigeria's ability to convert this renewed attention into sustained capital inflows and tangible economic progress. Watch for consistent policy execution and improvements in business conditions to solidify long-term investor trust.
