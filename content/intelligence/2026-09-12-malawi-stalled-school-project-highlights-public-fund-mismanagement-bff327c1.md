A primary school project near Mitundu, Malawi, has been abandoned mid-construction for years, with classroom blocks and facilities unfinished. Local reports indicate funds were 'chewed', pointing to severe mismanagement or corruption as the root cause of the prolonged stall.

This incident underscores significant public sector governance and execution risks in Malawi. For investors, it highlights potential challenges in public-private partnerships, infrastructure projects, and any ventures reliant on government funding or efficient regulatory oversight. Such delays increase project costs and diminish returns.

Monitor any official investigations into these stalled projects and observe government efforts to enhance public financial management and project oversight. Investor confidence will hinge on tangible reforms and accountability measures, especially given potential impacts on future aid and development funding.
