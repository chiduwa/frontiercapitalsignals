Nyasa Times reports Malawi is losing approximately $100 million annually due to gold smuggling. This information comes from the state-backed Malawi Mining Investment Company (Mamico). Mamico's chief executive, Leonard Kalindekafe, noted that this leakage harms the economy and deprives Malawians of their wealth.

For investors, this highlights efforts to formalize and capture value in Malawi's mining sector. Mamico is establishing trading centres to directly purchase gold from small-scale miners, an initiative that could reduce illicit trade and increase transparency. This move could signal a more stable and regulated environment for gold production.

Investors should monitor the effectiveness and rollout of these new trading centres. The success of Mamico's initiative in curtailing smuggling and formalizing the small-scale mining sector will be key. This development may also indicate broader governmental intent to improve oversight and revenue collection in natural resource extraction.
