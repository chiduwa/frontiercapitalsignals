Nile Post reports that Uganda Airlines has launched a new direct connection between Entebbe and Kigali. This new route is expected to deepen regional integration between Uganda and Rwanda.

According to the State Minister for Works and Transport, this development is set to expand business opportunities and strengthen economic ties between the two neighboring countries.
