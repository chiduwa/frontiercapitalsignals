Malawi24 reports that a man in Dedza was convicted for offensive communication under Section 87 of the Electronic Transactions law. The conviction followed his guilty plea for posting an offensive comment on TikTok about a deceased police officer. He faces three months in prison if he fails to pay a K200,000 fine.

This event highlights the enforcement of Malawi's Electronic Transactions law regarding online content. For investors, particularly those in the tech sector, social media, or any business with an online presence, this signals a regulatory environment where digital communication is actively monitored and legal consequences for perceived offensive content are enforced. It points to potential legal and reputational risks associated with online interactions in the market.

Investors should monitor further applications and interpretations of Malawi's Electronic Transactions law, especially Section 87. Observing how frequently and broadly these regulations are applied will provide insights into the operating risks for digital platforms and online businesses within the country.
