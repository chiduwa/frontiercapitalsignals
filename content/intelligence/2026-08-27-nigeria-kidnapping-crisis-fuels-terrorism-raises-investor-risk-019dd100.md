SBM Intelligence reports over 7,800 kidnappings in Nigeria over the past year, with N7.78 billion paid in ransom. Boko Haram reportedly secured over 90% of these proceeds, highlighting a significant funding stream for the terrorist group.

This data underscores a severe and persistent security risk for businesses operating in Nigeria. Kidnapping incidents raise operational costs, disrupt supply chains, and erode both local and international investor confidence. Critically, these ransom payments directly fuel a primary destabilizing force, perpetuating a cycle of insecurity that impacts long-term economic stability and talent retention.

Investors must monitor the Nigerian government's evolving strategy to counter these security threats and disrupt terror financing. Watch for signs of improved law enforcement capabilities, community engagement programs, and any shifts in the operational footprint or targets of Boko Haram. The sustained impact on FDI and local consumption will be a key indicator.
