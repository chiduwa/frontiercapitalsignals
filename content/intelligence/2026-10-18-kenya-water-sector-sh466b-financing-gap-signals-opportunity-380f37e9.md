The Standard reports that Co-op Bank received a Water Finance Award. This recognition comes as the water sector in the country faces significant challenges. Specifically, the sector is grappling with a financing shortfall of approximately Sh466 billion and losing nearly half of its treated water supply.

These reported figures highlight both substantial needs and potential opportunities for international investors. The large financing gap suggests demand for capital in water infrastructure, while the high loss rate indicates a need for technology and solutions to improve efficiency and reduce waste. Such opportunities could span public-private partnerships, infrastructure development, or technology provision aimed at conservation and better supply management.

Investors should monitor how the country plans to address this reported Sh466 billion financing shortfall and implement strategies to mitigate the significant losses in treated water supply. Any policy changes or new initiatives targeting these areas could signal specific entry points for capital.
