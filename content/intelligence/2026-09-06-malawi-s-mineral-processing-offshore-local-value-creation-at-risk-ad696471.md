Malawi is directing approximately 1 trillion Kwacha worth of its precious stones to Poland for processing. This decision means that the critical value addition phase for these minerals will occur overseas, not within Malawi, despite domestic efforts to train mining specialists.

For investors, this signals a continued challenge in local beneficiation within Malawi's mining sector. It limits opportunities for direct investment in processing infrastructure and downstream industries, reducing local economic multipliers and job creation. This also implies a policy or capacity gap preventing in-country value capture.

We will monitor any shifts in government policy regarding local content for mining and future investment in domestic processing facilities. Watch for private sector partnerships aiming to establish local beneficiation plants and track the employment outcomes for mining graduates.
