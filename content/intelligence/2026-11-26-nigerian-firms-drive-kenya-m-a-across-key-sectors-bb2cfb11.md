TechCabal reports that Nigerian companies are increasingly choosing Kenya for expansion. This trend is notable across sectors including energy, banking, and capital markets. For instance, Dangote acquired a pipeline capable of distributing fuel beyond Kenya, while Moniepoint purchased a bank that serves small businesses.

This signals a strategic shift by Nigerian firms towards M&A in Kenya, driven by the desire to quickly establish market presence. They are acquiring existing infrastructure and operations, suggesting a preference for speed over greenfield development. For investors, this highlights Kenya's attractiveness as a regional hub with ready-made assets that can be leveraged for broader market reach.

Investors should observe if this trend of Nigerian companies acquiring established assets in Kenya intensifies or expands to other sectors. Monitoring the performance and integration of these acquired entities will provide insights into the long-term viability and success of this expansion strategy. This could indicate further opportunities for M&A or partnership in the region.
