Capital FM Business reports that Zimele Asset Management has launched a new education savings plan in Kenya. This plan enables parents and guardians to set aside funds for school fees through flexible contributions.

This development suggests an expansion in the range of investment products available in Kenya's financial sector. The introduction of a specialized education savings plan highlights a perceived market demand for long-term savings vehicles, which could attract new funds into asset management. This move may also indicate increasing competition or specialization within the Kenyan financial services industry.

Investors should monitor the adoption rates of this new plan and observe whether other asset management firms follow suit with similar offerings. The success of such products could offer insights into consumer savings patterns and the evolving landscape of investment opportunities in Kenya.
