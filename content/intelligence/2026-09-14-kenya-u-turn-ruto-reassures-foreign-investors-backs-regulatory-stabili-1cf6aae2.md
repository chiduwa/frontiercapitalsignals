Kenyan President William Ruto has reversed course on a recent crackdown targeting foreign small traders. After initially triggering concerns, he now assures lawful foreign businesses and employees they are welcome, providing a 90-day window to regularize operations.

This policy reversal is a critical signal for international investors. The initial crackdown generated significant uncertainty regarding Kenya's regulatory environment and its commitment to regional trade blocs like the EAC. Ruto's clarification now suggests a more predictable and stable operational landscape, mitigating perceived risks.

Monitor the practical implementation of the 90-day regularization period and the consistency of enforcement. Watch for any subsequent policy statements or actions that could further define Kenya's stance on foreign business. This will indicate the true long-term stability for international investment.
