Capital FM Business reports that KenGen is seeking to attract investment into renewable energy and industrial projects. This effort is tied to a conference scheduled for November 24 to 27 at its Olkaria Geothermal location.

This initiative signals potential opportunities for investors interested in Kenya's renewable energy and industrial sectors. The conference serves as a direct channel for engaging with KenGen's project pipeline, offering insight into specific investment needs.

Investors should monitor outcomes from the November conference, including any specific project announcements or partnership opportunities. Details on the nature and scale of the industrial projects, alongside renewable energy initiatives, will be key.
