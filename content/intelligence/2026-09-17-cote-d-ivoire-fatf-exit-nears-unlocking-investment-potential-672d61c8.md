Cote d’Ivoire is on the verge of removal from the FATF financial crime watchlist. International regulators confirm substantial strengthening in bank supervision, corporate transparency, and anti-money laundering enforcement. A formal delisting decision is expected at the October plenary.

This development significantly eases investment friction. Delisting will improve the perception of risk, enhance access to global financial services, and attract more foreign direct investment. It signals a more transparent and compliant operating environment for international capital.

Investors should closely watch the October FATF plenary for official confirmation. Track immediate shifts in market sentiment and capital flows into Cote d’Ivoire. This could also signal broader regulatory improvements and investor confidence returning to other francophone West African markets.
