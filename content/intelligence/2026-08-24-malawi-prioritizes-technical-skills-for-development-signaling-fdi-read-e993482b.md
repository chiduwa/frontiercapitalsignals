Former Minister Moses Kunkuyu has urged technical students in Malawi to apply their skills for national development. This call highlights a concerted effort within the government and private sector to leverage and integrate local technical expertise.

This advocacy signals Malawi's commitment to human capital development and local content utilization. For international investors, a skilled local workforce can reduce operational costs, enhance project sustainability, and facilitate knowledge transfer, particularly in sectors requiring specific technical expertise such as infrastructure, manufacturing, or renewable energy. It suggests an improving environment for sourcing talent domestically.

Investors should monitor upcoming government initiatives or private sector partnerships focused on vocational training, technical education funding, and local employment incentives. Observe specific projects or industries that are actively recruiting and deploying these technical skills, as these could present early investment entry points or partnership opportunities.
