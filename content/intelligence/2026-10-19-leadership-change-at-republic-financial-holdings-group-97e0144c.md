MyJoyOnline Business reports that Republic Financial Holdings Limited (RFHL) and Republic Bank Limited (RBL) have announced leadership changes. Richard S. Sammy exited the Group, effective October 14, 2026, after 18 years. Karen Yip Chuck has taken over leadership of the Group. This announcement was made in a statement issued from Port of Spain, Trinidad, on October 15, 2026.

Changes in senior leadership at a significant financial group like RFHL, which has operations in markets such as Ghana, can be material for investors.
