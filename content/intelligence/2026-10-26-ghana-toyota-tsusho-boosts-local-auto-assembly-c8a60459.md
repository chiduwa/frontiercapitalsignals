MyJoyOnline Business reports that Toyota Tsusho Manufacturing Ghana has launched the ninth-generation Hilux and the new Land Cruiser FJ. This follows fresh investment in its local assembly operations, as stated by the company.

This investment is expected to support Toyota Tsusho Manufacturing Ghana's market expansion and contribute to the development of Ghana's automotive industry. For investors, this indicates potential for growth within the Ghanaian automotive sector and could signal opportunities in related supply chains and services.

Investors should monitor the impact of this local assembly investment on the company's market share and the overall growth of the automotive industry. The extent to which this contributes to broader industry development, as suggested by Toyota Tsusho Manufacturing Ghana's Head of Sales Operations, Andrew Lamptey, remains to be observed.
