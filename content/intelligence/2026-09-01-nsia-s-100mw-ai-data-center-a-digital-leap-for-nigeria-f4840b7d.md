The Nigeria Sovereign Investment Authority (NSIA) plans to develop a 100-megawatt hyperscale AI data center. This significant technology investment marks a critical push from Nigeria's sovereign wealth fund into advanced digital infrastructure, aiming to power the nation's burgeoning digital economy.

This initiative signals a strong government-backed commitment to closing Nigeria's digital infrastructure gap, particularly in high-demand areas like AI and cloud computing. For investors, this opens significant avenues in supporting technology services, data center operations, and energy solutions crucial for powering such an ambitious project. It positions Nigeria as a potential frontrunner for digital transformation and innovation in the West African region, enhancing its appeal for tech-focused capital.
