Premium Times reports that Makinde unveiled his "RESET" agenda, promising reforms in education, security, and the economy. Key components of this agenda include specific promises of tax breaks and an end to militancy. Makinde also accused the federal government of worsening the burden on Nigerians through its tax and economic policies.

For investors, the promise of tax breaks could signal a potentially more favorable operating environment, reducing costs and improving profitability for businesses in sectors that might benefit. An end to militancy, if successfully implemented, would significantly enhance security and stability, which are critical factors for business continuity, supply chain reliability, and overall investment confidence.

Investors should monitor for specific details regarding the proposed tax breaks and the concrete strategies for achieving economic reforms and an end to militancy under the "RESET" agenda. The feasibility and timeline of these promises, particularly concerning their potential impact on the broader economic landscape and security situation, will be crucial to watch.
