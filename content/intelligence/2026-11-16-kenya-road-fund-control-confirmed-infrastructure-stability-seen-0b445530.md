Capital FM Business reports that Kenya’s national road agencies will retain control of allocations from the Road Maintenance Levy Fund (RMLF). This follows a decision by the Court of Appeal, which upheld the existing legal framework governing these funds.

This ruling clarifies the institutional management of substantial infrastructure funding in Kenya. For investors in the infrastructure sector, particularly those engaged in road construction and maintenance, this confirmation of centralized control provides greater certainty. It suggests a more stable and predictable operational environment for tendering and project execution related to road networks.

Investors should monitor how this reaffirmed control translates into consistent and transparent planning for road maintenance projects. The continuity of the established funding framework could support sustained investment in Kenya's road infrastructure, but specific details on future project pipelines remain to be seen.
