The High Street Journal reports that African artificial intelligence startups are seeking guarantees to compete for government contracts. Local technology companies are reportedly facing significant barriers in accessing procurement opportunities. They also lack the computing resources needed to develop and scale AI products.

This situation presents a dual signal for investors in the region's tech sector. The existence of these barriers suggests a challenging operating environment for local AI firms. However, the push for guarantees could indicate a forthcoming policy shift to open up government contracts, potentially creating substantial new market opportunities for well-positioned tech companies or those providing necessary computing infrastructure.

Investors should monitor policy discussions around these guarantees and any initiatives to address the identified barriers. Watch for changes in government procurement regulations or investments in shared computing resources. The resolution of these issues will be key to unlocking the full potential of AI development and government contract markets in Africa.
