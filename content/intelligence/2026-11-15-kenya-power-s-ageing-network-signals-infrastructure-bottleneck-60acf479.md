The Standard reports that officials from Kenya Power acknowledge that Naivasha's electricity distribution network is ageing. This network is currently unable to cope with the demands of rapid population growth in the area.

This situation highlights potential infrastructure challenges within Kenya's energy sector. An ageing power grid unable to support population growth can hinder economic development and impact businesses relying on stable electricity. For investors, this signals potential risks to industrial operations and urban expansion, as well as possible opportunities in infrastructure upgrades.

Investors should observe how Kenya Power plans to address its ageing infrastructure and increasing demand. Any initiatives to modernise the distribution network, particularly in fast-growing regions, could indicate future capital expenditure and potential for private sector participation in power infrastructure development.
