A fertiliser truck in Mzuzu, Malawi, crashed after individuals reportedly seized control of the vehicle during a road rage incident. The lorry, carrying essential agricultural supplies, flipped into a ditch, leading to one arrest.

This incident underscores persistent last-mile supply chain vulnerabilities in Malawi, especially for critical agricultural inputs. Such unpredictable events introduce significant operational risks, increasing logistics costs and potential delays for commodity-dependent investments. It also signals underlying public order and road safety challenges impacting business continuity.

Monitor local authorities' response to road security and enforcement against such public order disruptions. Investors should reassess logistics partners' risk management capabilities and factor in exposure to supply chain volatility, particularly in regions with reported security and governance gaps.
