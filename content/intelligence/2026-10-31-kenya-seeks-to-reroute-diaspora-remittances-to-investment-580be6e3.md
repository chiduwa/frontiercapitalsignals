Capital FM Business reports that Kenya is encouraging its citizens abroad to shift their remittance use. The government notes that a significant majority, 80 percent, of current diaspora remittances are directed towards consumption, rather than investment.

This policy signal indicates Kenya's intent to channel its substantial diaspora funds towards domestic economic development and capital formation. For international investors, it suggests a potential push for greater local capital participation across various sectors. This could either complement or potentially compete with foreign direct investment, depending on specific government initiatives.

Investors should monitor for any specific government incentives or policy changes designed to encourage this diaspora investment. Clarity on the sectors targeted for these funds, and how such efforts might influence the broader investment landscape and capital availability, will be key to watch.
