BusinessDay reports that Pan-Atlantic University recently held its 26th inaugural lecture. During this event, it was communicated that fossil fuels are expected to remain a critical component of the global energy mix.

This perspective signals a potential continued significant role for fossil fuels, even amidst the ongoing energy transition. For investors in the region, this suggests that assets and companies linked to fossil fuel production and infrastructure may retain their strategic importance and investment viability for longer than some projections might indicate. This view could also influence the pace and nature of energy policy development.

Investors should monitor subsequent expert opinions, policy discussions, and government stances on energy development in Nigeria and other African markets. Future developments related to the long-term role of fossil fuels in national energy strategies will be key indicators.
