Zambian President Hakainde Hichilema, of the United Party for National Development (UPND), has secured a second term after tallying all votes. He garnered 2,965,326 votes, significantly ahead of his closest rival Brian Mundubile, as confirmed by the Electoral Commission of Zambia.

This outcome provides critical policy continuity for investors. Hichilema's first term focused on economic stabilization, debt restructuring, and improving the business environment. His re-election reduces political risk, suggesting sustained commitment to fiscal discipline, attracting foreign direct investment, and advancing key economic reforms across vital sectors like mining and agriculture.

Investors should monitor the new government's cabinet appointments and the swift implementation of promised reforms, particularly debt management strategies and new mining policies. Observe ongoing efforts to enhance the ease of doing business and any new incentives for foreign capital, which will indicate the pace of Zambia's economic trajectory.
