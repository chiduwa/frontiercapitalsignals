The Independent (Uganda) reports that the government has lifted the suspension on silverfish (mukene) fishing in Lake Albert. This decision allows fishers and dealers in the Bunyoro sub-region to resume their activities, though under new stringent regulations.

This development is relevant for investors monitoring economic activity in Uganda, particularly within the fisheries sector and its associated supply chains. The resumption of fishing signals a return to potential stability and renewed output for an important local industry, which was previously impacted by the government's suspension.

Investors should monitor the implementation and enforcement of these new stringent regulations. The precise nature of these rules and their potential impact on operational costs and yields for mukene fishing businesses are key factors to watch.
