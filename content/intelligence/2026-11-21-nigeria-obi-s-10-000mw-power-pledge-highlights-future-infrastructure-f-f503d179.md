Premium Times reports that Peter Obi set targets for power, healthcare, and education in Onitsha, notably pledging 10,000MW for the power sector. Separately, a federal judge refused Atiku Abubakar and the ADC's request to amend their challenge to Bola Tinubu’s eligibility. In another ruling, the court ordered the ADC to restore Omar Suleiman’s Adamawa nomination. Opposition parties also moved their summit to November 30.

Obi's pledges, particularly the significant 10,000MW power target, indicate potential long-term investment opportunities in Nigeria's infrastructure and energy sectors. While these are currently aspirational, they highlight key development priorities that could drive future policy and spending should his political influence grow. The reported court rulings and political summit provide a glimpse into Nigeria's evolving political landscape, which is a factor in overall investment stability.

Investors should monitor the political landscape for shifts in influence among key figures and any detailed policy proposals related to these sector targets. Clarity on funding mechanisms, timelines, and the feasibility of such ambitious infrastructure projects will be important to watch. The outcome of political alignments and future election cycles will significantly shape the potential for these initiatives.
