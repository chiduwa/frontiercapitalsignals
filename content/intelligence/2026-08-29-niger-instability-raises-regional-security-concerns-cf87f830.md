Niger's capital experienced heavy gunshots and explosions on Saturday, lasting approximately two hours. The attack specifically targeted the airport and the security perimeter surrounding the presidency.

This incident in Niger, a nation bordering Nigeria, elevates regional political risk perceptions. Investors assess such events for potential spillover effects on broader West African stability, influencing capital allocation decisions and risk premiums for regional assets.

Monitor the official response from Nigerien authorities and any immediate impact on regional security alliances like ECOWAS. Investors should also watch for changes in travel advisories or security assessments that might affect operations or project valuations in neighboring countries.
