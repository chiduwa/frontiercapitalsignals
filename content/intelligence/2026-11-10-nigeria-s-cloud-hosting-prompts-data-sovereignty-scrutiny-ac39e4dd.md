TechCabal reports that technical checks of Nigeria's postcode.gov.ng's public domain name system (DNS) records indicated Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure in the United States. This finding highlighted that a critical public digital asset might be hosted abroad.

This discovery raises questions for investors, particularly as Nigeria is reinforcing its push for data sovereignty. The hosting of a government's critical digital asset outside the country could influence future regulatory frameworks and procurement policies for cloud services and digital infrastructure within Nigeria. Companies providing or relying on cloud services for public sector contracts should take note.

Investors should monitor how Nigeria's government addresses the perceived conflict between its data sovereignty objectives and the current hosting arrangement. Future policy decisions regarding local data hosting requirements for public digital assets will be key indicators for the tech and infrastructure sectors.
