A private foundation, Apostle Hulda, has committed K20 million to fully fund a student's five-year Biological Sciences degree at the University of Malawi. This significant scholarship covers tuition, accommodation, and living expenses for a student from Lilongwe.

This substantial private investment in local higher education signals strengthening human capital development in Malawi. It points to growing philanthropic capacity and a potential future increase in skilled labor, which are positive indicators for long term economic stability and investment attractiveness. A more educated workforce reduces operational risks for foreign investors.
