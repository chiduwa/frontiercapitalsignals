Trevor Manuel, South Africa’s former Finance Minister, publicly stated the nation's democracy requires significant repair work. He discussed the political landscape and economic challenges facing the continent's largest economy with The Africa Report.

This assessment from a highly respected figure underscores growing concerns about governance stability and institutional strength in a key African market. For international investors, it signals potential political headwinds and policy uncertainty that could impact long-term returns, influencing broader regional sentiment regarding investment risk across Sub-Saharan Africa.

Investors should monitor South Africa's upcoming elections and subsequent policy shifts closely. Observe how other African nations, particularly those facing similar governance challenges, address calls for reform. Pay close attention to any shifts in investor sentiment regarding political risk premiums across the continent.
