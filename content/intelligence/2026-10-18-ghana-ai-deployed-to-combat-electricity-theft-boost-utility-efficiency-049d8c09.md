MyJoyOnline Business reports that Hubtel disclosed working with the Electricity Company of Ghana (ECG) to use artificial intelligence (AI). This AI deployment helps identify customers suspected of stealing electricity.

This initiative suggests a move towards technology-driven solutions for operational challenges within Ghana's utility sector. For investors, this indicates potential for improved revenue assurance and reduced losses for power distributors like ECG, which could lead to a more stable investment environment. It also highlights the growing adoption of AI by local tech companies to address critical infrastructure issues.

Investors should monitor the broader adoption of AI and other smart technologies by utilities in Ghana and across the region. The success and scaling of such collaborations, and any resulting improvements in utility financial health, will be key indicators. Further partnerships between local tech firms and essential service providers could signal new growth avenues.
