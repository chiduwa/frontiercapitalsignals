The Africa Report reports that Oando, a Nigerian energy group, has been built over three decades, growing from a battered tanker to billion-dollar acquisitions. Its chief, Wale Tinubu, now faces the challenge of translating control of Nigeria's oil assets into sustainable, lasting profits. The company also has long-term ambitions to eventually move into deepwater operations.

This development signals a strategic shift for Oando, moving from asset accumulation to a focus on profitability and operational efficiency. For investors, this indicates a potential for improved financial performance and value creation within a significant player in Nigeria's energy sector. The emphasis on lasting profits suggests a more mature phase of growth for the company.

Investors should monitor Oando's execution on its stated goal of generating lasting profits from its current oil assets. The potential future move into deepwater operations also suggests long-term growth ambitions and will require close observation for specific plans and capital allocation.
