This investment, alongside accelerated development by Australian miners, marks a decisive new phase for Namibia's uranium industry amidst a global nuclear energy revival.

This reinforces Africa's growing strategic value in global energy supply chains, particularly for critical minerals. Chinese capital's aggressive pursuit of resource assets across the continent signals a broader trend of significant, geopolitically driven investment.
