The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC), under chairman Olanipekun Olukoyede, has successfully recovered $60 million as part of its ongoing investigation into Nestoil. This represents a significant breakthrough in a high-profile case.

This substantial recovery underscores Nigeria's renewed commitment to combating financial crime and prosecuting illicit financial flows. For international investors, such decisive action by the EFCC can enhance confidence in the regulatory environment, signaling a more level playing field and reduced operational risk over time. It suggests a more serious approach to accountability.

Investors should monitor the progress of the Nestoil investigation and subsequent prosecutions, including any further asset recoveries. Also, observe if this robust enforcement becomes a sustained trend across other sectors and high-profile cases, which would further solidify Nigeria's improving governance perception.
