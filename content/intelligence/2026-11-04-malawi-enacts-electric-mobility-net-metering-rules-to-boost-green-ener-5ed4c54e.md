Nyasa Times reports that Malawi has unveiled its initial regulatory framework for electric mobility and new net-metering rules. The Ministry of Energy and the Malawi Energy Regulatory Authority (MERA) launched these regulations.

This move aims to accelerate the adoption of clean energy technologies and reduce Malawi's reliance on conventional fuel sources. Investors may find opportunities emerging in electric vehicle infrastructure, renewable energy solutions, and related services as the market adapts.

Stakeholders should watch for the practical implementation of these regulations and MERA's enforcement. The pace and scale of clean energy and electric mobility growth in Malawi will be shaped by how these rules foster investment and private sector involvement.
