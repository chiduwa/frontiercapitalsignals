The Standard reports that the government is adopting technology to reduce road maintenance costs. This technology is anticipated to improve how the government determines road conditions, estimates the remaining lifespan of pavements, and prioritizes maintenance interventions.

For investors, this policy shift suggests potential opportunities in the technology sector, particularly for firms offering solutions in infrastructure monitoring and asset management. Improved maintenance prioritization could lead to more efficient government spending on roads, potentially freeing up resources or signaling a more data-driven approach to infrastructure development.

Investors should monitor the specific implementation of this technology and the government's procurement processes. It remains to be seen how extensively this technology will be rolled out and its impact on the efficiency of road maintenance operations.
