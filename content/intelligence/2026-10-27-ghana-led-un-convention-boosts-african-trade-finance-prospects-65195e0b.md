The High Street Journal reports Ghana is leading 26 countries in signing a United Nations Cargo Documents Convention. This convention aims to allow businesses to use cargo moving by road, rail, and air as collateral for financing.

This policy development is significant for investors targeting trade and logistics across Africa. The Convention directly addresses a major barrier to trade by expanding access to financing. This could enhance liquidity and reduce risks for businesses involved in cross-border commerce, potentially boosting trade volumes and associated services in Ghana and other participating African nations.

Investors should monitor the implementation of this Convention in Ghana and other African signatory countries. The practical application of using cargo as collateral will be key to realizing its full potential in expanding trade finance and unlocking new opportunities across the continent.
