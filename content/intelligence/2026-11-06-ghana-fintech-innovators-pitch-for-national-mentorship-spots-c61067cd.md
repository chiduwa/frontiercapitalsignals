MyJoyOnline Business reports that 10 innovators from the Southern Zone of the 2026 MoMo Fintech Lab are set to pitch their solutions to a panel of industry experts. This event is scheduled for Friday, November 6, 2026, in Accra. The innovators are competing for five spots in the National Mentorship Programme.

This hackathon signals ongoing innovation and development within Ghana's fintech ecosystem, particularly at the early stage. For investors, events like this highlight emerging talent and potential startup opportunities. The selection for a National Mentorship Programme could indicate future growth prospects for the chosen fintech solutions.

Investors should monitor the outcomes of this pitching event to identify the five selected participants and gain insight into the types of fintech solutions advancing to the National Mentorship Programme. Further details on the mentorship program itself, once available, could offer clarity on the support and resources these emerging fintechs will receive, potentially influencing their trajectory and future funding needs.
