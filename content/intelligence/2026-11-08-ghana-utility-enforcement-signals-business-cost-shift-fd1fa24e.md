MyJoyOnline Business reports that the Electricity Company of Ghana (ECG) will launch a nationwide Mass Revenue Mobilisation Exercise starting Monday, November 9. This exercise, which is intended to recover outstanding electricity bills, will continue until Friday, November 27, 2026. The initiative targets all types of customers with arrears, including residential, commercial, and industrial entities, as well as Ministries, Departments, and Agencies (MDAs). ECG has issued warnings of disconnection for customers who have outstanding arrears.

This protracted revenue mobilization indicates a significant push by ECG to improve its financial health and enforce payment discipline across Ghana. For investors, this could mean increased operating costs or disrupted operations for commercial and industrial customers with significant arrears if disconnections occur. It also signals a potentially stricter regulatory environment regarding utility payments, impacting cash flow management for businesses operating in Ghana. The inclusion of MDAs suggests a broad government effort to streamline financial accountability within public services.

Investors should monitor the implementation and enforcement of this exercise, particularly its impact on the operational continuity and financial stability of commercial and industrial businesses. The duration of the exercise, extending to 2026, implies a sustained effort, suggesting a long-term shift in payment expectations. Observe the rate of disconnections and the speed of arrears recovery, as this will indicate the overall effectiveness and potential for wider economic impact.
