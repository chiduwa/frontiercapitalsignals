The High Street Journal reports that AGI Regional Chairman Dela Gbeve encouraged Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in Ghana's Eastern, Volta, and Oti regions. He urged them to use trade fairs, innovation, and partnerships to boost their growth.

This signals a focus on SME development and regional economic activity in Ghana. Increased SME engagement in trade and innovation could point to emerging market opportunities and potential for local partnerships or supply chain investments. Investors may find new entry points in these regions as local businesses expand.

Investors should monitor the actual uptake of these strategies by SMEs in the specified regions. Observing participation in trade fairs or new partnership formations will indicate if this push for growth materializes into tangible business expansion.
