TechCabal reports that Teraco is tapping wind energy to power its data centres. This development was highlighted in the first edition of TC Mzansi, which aims to cover technology developments in South Africa and the wider Southern African Development Community (SADC) region.

This initiative by Teraco indicates a growing focus on sustainable energy solutions for critical infrastructure, such as data centres, within the SADC region. For international investors in African markets, including SADC member Malawi, this points to potential investment opportunities in renewable energy generation, energy infrastructure, and eco-friendly data centre development. It underscores the increasing demand for reliable and green power solutions to support Africa's expanding digital economy.

Investors should closely monitor how similar renewable energy integration strategies are adopted for data centres and other energy-intensive operations across the SADC region and potentially beyond. Further developments in wind or other clean energy projects supporting digital infrastructure in countries like Malawi could present actionable investment signals.
