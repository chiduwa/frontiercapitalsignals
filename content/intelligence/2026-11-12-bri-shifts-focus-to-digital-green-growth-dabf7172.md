The Independent (Uganda) reports that a think tank report identifies digitalization and the green transition as twin drivers of Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) cooperation. The report, titled “Building an Open World Economy Through Belt and Road Cooperation,” was issued by the Xinhua Institute.

This intelligence suggests a strategic direction within BRI initiatives, prioritizing digital infrastructure, technological advancements, and sustainable environmental projects.
