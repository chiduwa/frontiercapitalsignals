A new citizen-led organization, the Platform for Mindset Change (PMC), launched in Malawi this March. It targets a national 'mindset crisis,' aiming to tackle corruption and dependency through a grassroots movement.

This movement signals a grassroots push for improved governance and reduced corruption. A genuine shift in public accountability can significantly enhance the business environment, attract foreign direct investment, and improve long-term economic stability by mitigating systemic risks.

Monitor the PMC's ability to gain traction and influence policy. Observe government and institutional responses, alongside any measurable impact on transparency indices or business confidence. The movement's longevity and effectiveness will signal Malawi's commitment to structural governance reforms.
