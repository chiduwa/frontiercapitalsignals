While these events occurred in South Africa, they affect broader regional investor sentiment. Such incidents can amplify perceived security risks across African frontier markets, including Malawi, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, and Uganda. International investors may apply a higher risk premium to the region, despite specific country-level stability.
