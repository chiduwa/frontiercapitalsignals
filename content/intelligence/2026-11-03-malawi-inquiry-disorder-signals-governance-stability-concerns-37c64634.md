Nyasa Times reports that a parliamentary inquiry into the plane crash that killed former Vice-President Saulos Chilima and eight others descended into chaos. The report indicates that independent campaigner Christy Lwara clashed with lawmakers, leading to accusations, insults, and repeated interventions from the committee chair.

This development, while not directly economic, may signal broader governance challenges or political instability in Malawi. The breakdown of decorum in a high-profile parliamentary inquiry could impact international investor perceptions of institutional effectiveness and the predictability of the political environment. Such situations can elevate political risk, making long-term planning more difficult for businesses.

Investors should monitor subsequent proceedings of the inquiry and broader political discourse. Continued public disagreements or a perception of dysfunction within key institutions could influence investor confidence and perceptions of the operating environment.
