Africa’s telecom infrastructure boom connected hundreds of millions. Crucially, this rapid expansion also created a new class of powerful entities and significant billionaire fortunes across the continent.

This matters because it highlights the immense wealth generation potential inherent in foundational infrastructure development. Telecom served as a key enabling sector, proving that investing in essential connectivity can yield outsized returns and build enduring economic powerhouses.

Investors should now watch how this proven model translates to adjacent or evolving infrastructure plays. Monitor the next generation of digital infrastructure, including data centers, fiber optic networks, and the critical backbone supporting the continent's rapidly expanding fintech and e-commerce ecosystems.
