Mozambique, long defined by its massive offshore gas discoveries and the substantial energy investments from TotalEnergies and Eni, is now emerging with a new narrative. The country is actively positioning itself as Southern Africa's next industrial platform, moving beyond its primary role as a major gas exporter. This indicates a strategic shift towards broader economic diversification.

This evolution is significant for investors. An industrial platform implies extensive development in related sectors, including manufacturing, logistics, infrastructure, and supporting services. It signals a move up the value chain, creating more resilient and varied investment opportunities that are less solely dependent on the global energy market.

Going forward, watch for concrete policy actions supporting industrial growth, new infrastructure projects, and diversification of foreign direct investment beyond the Rovuma Basin. Key indicators will be the development of local supply chains, regulatory reforms to attract industrial capital, and the growth of non-energy exports.
