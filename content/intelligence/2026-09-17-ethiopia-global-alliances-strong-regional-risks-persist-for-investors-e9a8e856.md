Abiy Ahmed has successfully cultivated a robust network of international strategic partners, including strong ties with Kenya, France, the UAE, and renewed relationships with major global powers like China and Europe. This demonstrates significant diplomatic success abroad, signaling Ethiopia's growing relevance on the world stage.

Strong international alliances signal potential for increased foreign investment, trade, and development financing, benefiting Ethiopia's long-term economic prospects. These relationships can de-risk certain investments by providing political backing and market access. However, persistent regional tensions with Eritrea, Sudan, and Egypt introduce significant geopolitical uncertainty and risk, potentially impacting regional stability and cross-border trade.

Investors should monitor Abiy's efforts to de-escalate tensions and build trust with neighboring countries. Any progress on regional diplomatic fronts, particularly with Eritrea and Sudan, would significantly reduce perceived risk. Conversely, continued deterioration of regional relations could offset the gains from international partnerships and limit Ethiopia's investment appeal.
