The Independent (Uganda) reports that the Bank of Uganda increased its benchmark interest rate by 2.25 percentage points, setting the new Central Bank Rate at 12%. Economist Dr. Fred Muhumuza has questioned this policy decision.

Dr. Muhumuza argues that government borrowing and ongoing fiscal deficits are distorting investment decisions and weakening productive sectors. For investors, this suggests potential challenges in capital allocation efficiency and the overall health of sectors crucial for foreign exchange generation. The raised interest rate could also impact borrowing costs and investment returns across the economy.

Investors should monitor the Bank of Uganda's future policy statements and any further discussions around fiscal policy and government borrowing. Observing how these factors influence investment flows and the performance of key productive sectors will be critical.
