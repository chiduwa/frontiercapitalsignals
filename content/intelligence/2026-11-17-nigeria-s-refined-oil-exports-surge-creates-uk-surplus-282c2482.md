Africa Briefing reports Nigeria sold £1.02 billion worth of refined oil to Britain in the year to June 2026. This more than doubled the value of its shipments to the UK. As a result, Nigeria recorded a £245 million refined oil trade surplus with the UK, with total UK imports of Nigerian refined oil exceeding £1 billion.

This surge in refined oil exports and the resulting trade surplus highlights a growing revenue stream for Nigeria's energy sector. The timing of this coincides with Dangote expansion and UK refinery closures, which could indicate a strategic market opportunity for Nigerian refined products. This development could positively impact foreign exchange earnings and potentially drive further investment into Nigeria's refining capacity.

Investors should monitor the sustainability of this export growth and its impact on Nigeria's overall trade balance. Further details on the operational capacity and future expansion plans of Nigerian refineries, particularly in light of international market shifts, will be important. Also, observe any policy developments supporting or hindering further energy sector exports.
