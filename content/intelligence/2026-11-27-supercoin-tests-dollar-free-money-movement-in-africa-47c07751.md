TechCabal reports that Supercoin, led by Gideon Greaves, sees a business opportunity in facilitating money movement without relying on the US dollar. The company's commercial viability is being tested on its ability to trade sufficient rand and shillings to offer payment companies better rates and faster delivery of funds compared to their current providers.

This initiative signals a potential shift in the efficiency and cost of cross-border payments within African markets, particularly involving local currency exchanges. Should Supercoin succeed, it could introduce significant competition to existing payment solutions, potentially lowering transaction costs and speeding up transfers. This could impact the profitability models of traditional payment processors and create new investment avenues in innovative fintech solutions.

Investors should closely monitor the outcomes of Supercoin's commercial test, focusing on its effectiveness in providing competitive rates and speed for payment companies. Its performance will offer crucial insights into the viability of dollar-free money movement models and their potential for broader adoption across other African currencies and regions.
