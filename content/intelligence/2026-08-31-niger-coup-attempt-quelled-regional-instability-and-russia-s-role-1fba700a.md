Niger's ruling junta, led by General Tiani, successfully suppressed an attempted coup last Friday. Junior officers targeted the presidency, airport, and national television. The junta quickly neutralized the threat, reportedly with assistance from Russia's Africa Corps.

This event reinforces the persistent political and security instability across the Sahel region. For investors, it signals heightened risk perception, potentially affecting capital flows and development projects even in neighboring, more stable markets. Russia's overt support to the junta further solidifies its growing security influence, a critical geopolitical factor for regional stability and resource plays.

Moving forward, watch for any escalations or retaliatory actions within Niger and broader regional responses from ECOWAS or the African Union. Continued monitoring of Russia's expanding footprint and its implications for governance and security partnerships across West Africa remains crucial for assessing long-term investment prospects.
