Nigeria's Super Eagles have commenced their qualification campaign for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations. This major continental tournament is set to be co-hosted by Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania, positioning East Africa as a focal point for regional sporting events.

This development signals significant infrastructure and tourism investment opportunities, particularly in Kenya and Uganda. Large sporting events catalyze capital flows into hospitality, transport, facility upgrades, and related services. Such events also boost local consumption and provide a substantial platform for brand visibility across the continent.

Investors should closely track infrastructure project announcements and execution in the host countries. Monitor progress on stadium renovations, accommodation development, and transport link enhancements. Watch for public-private partnership opportunities, especially within the hospitality, logistics, and media sectors.
