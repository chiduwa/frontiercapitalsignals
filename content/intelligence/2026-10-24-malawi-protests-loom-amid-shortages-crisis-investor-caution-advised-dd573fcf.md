Nyasa Times reports that Malawi's Human Rights Defenders Coalition (HRDC) has threatened to mobilise nationwide protests. This warning follows deepening shortages of essential services, specifically fuel, electricity, water, and vital medicines. The HRDC indicates citizens can no longer tolerate persistent failures in public service delivery.

These looming protests signal escalating social unrest driven by critical resource scarcity. For investors, shortages of fuel, electricity, and water directly impact operational costs and business continuity in Malawi. Potential nationwide demonstrations could disrupt economic activity, raise political risk, and affect supply chains across various sectors.

Investors should closely monitor the HRDC's next steps and any government response aimed at addressing the reported shortages. The scale and duration of any actual protests will be a key indicator for assessing the immediate and long-term implications for Malawi's business environment and overall political stability.
