At the recent UNGA, world leaders emphasized the critical need for sustained investment in global pandemic preparedness. A key call was to finalize negotiations on a WHO Pandemic Agreement annex establishing a Pathogen Access and Benefit-Sharing (PABS) system.

This development signals a significant push towards bolstering health infrastructure and biotech capabilities worldwide, especially in emerging markets. For investors, the PABS system, once ratified, creates a framework for technology transfer and local pharmaceutical manufacturing. It will likely unlock substantial funding and strategic partnerships, de-risking future health shocks and creating new opportunities in healthcare and life sciences across Africa.

Monitor the ongoing WHO Pandemic Agreement negotiations, particularly progress on the PABS annex. Watch for specific funding pledges and policy shifts from multilateral organizations and developed nations. We also need to see how African governments prepare to attract and integrate these investments into their national health security strategies.
