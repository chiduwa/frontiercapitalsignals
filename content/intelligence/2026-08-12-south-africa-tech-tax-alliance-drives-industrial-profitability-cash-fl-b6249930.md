Deloitte Africa and Refuel have formed a strategic alliance to simplify diesel rebate claims from the South African Revenue Service (SARS). This partnership combines Refuel’s fuel-tracking technology with Deloitte Africa’s tax and advisory expertise, creating a "tech plus tax" solution for businesses, particularly in mining and agriculture.

This initiative directly addresses operational efficiency and cash flow for key industrial sectors. For investors, it signals improved profitability and stronger balance sheets for South African companies in mining and agriculture, by ensuring they successfully claim eligible tax refunds. It de-risks operations by streamlining complex regulatory processes.

Monitor the adoption rate and measurable impact on corporate earnings and cash flow within targeted sectors. Watch for potential expansion of this "tech plus tax" model to other complex tax or rebate systems across other African markets, signaling broader regional opportunities for efficiency gains and investor returns.
