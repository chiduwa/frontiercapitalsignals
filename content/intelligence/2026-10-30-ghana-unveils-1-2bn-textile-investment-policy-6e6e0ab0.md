MyJoyOnline Business reports that Ghana's Cabinet has approved a new textile and garment policy, targeting $1.2 billion in investment and 150,000 direct and indirect jobs over the next decade. The policy also aims to expand the country's textile and garment industry from an estimated $400 million to about $2 billion.

This policy signals significant government support for the textile and garment sector, creating a potential opening for investors. The stated goal of reviving domestic cotton production across 50,000 hectares suggests opportunities in raw material supply and agricultural inputs, complementing the industrial expansion. This could foster a more integrated and resilient value chain.

Investors should monitor the formal launch of the policy by the Ministry of Trade, Agribusiness and Industry for further details on implementation and specific incentives. Tracking progress on cotton production targets and industry expansion will be crucial to assess the policy's impact and identify tangible investment opportunities.
