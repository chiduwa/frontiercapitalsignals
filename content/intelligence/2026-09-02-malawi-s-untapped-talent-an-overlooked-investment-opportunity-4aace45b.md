Malawi is reportedly overlooking its local artisan talent, pushing youth primarily towards white-collar careers. A prominent sign writer, Jai Msoma, exemplifies this challenge, arguing the country has not fully recognized the value of skilled tradespeople like himself, despite their successful businesses.

This points to a significant misallocation of human capital and an undervalued labor pool. For investors, it highlights potential for high-impact returns in vocational training, skill development programs, or businesses that can professionalize and integrate local artisan services. Tapping into this overlooked sector could offer competitive labor advantages and strengthen local supply chains.

Track any government policy shifts concerning vocational education, SME support, or trade promotion. Observe private sector responses, including new ventures or partnerships aiming to formalize and scale artisan businesses. Monitor youth employment data to identify emerging trends in both white-collar and skilled trade sectors.
