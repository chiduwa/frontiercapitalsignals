Kaspersky warned at its META Cyber Security Weekend about the severe risks of advertising data weaponization by cyberthreat actors. The digital advertising ecosystem, processing billions of daily requests, now presents a significant vulnerability. This data, initially used for targeted ads, is increasingly exploited for malicious purposes.

This development directly impacts digital-first businesses across African markets. Increased cyberthreats translate to higher operational costs, potential data breaches, reputational damage, and regulatory penalties. Companies relying heavily on digital advertising or holding substantial customer data, particularly in e-commerce, fintech, and media, face amplified security expenditures and investor scrutiny.

Monitor how African governments and regulatory bodies respond with new data protection laws or enforcement. Observe corporate investment in advanced cybersecurity solutions and privacy-enhancing technologies. Opportunities may emerge in the cybersecurity sector, while companies with weak data governance face increased risk and potential valuation pressure.
