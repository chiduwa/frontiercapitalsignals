The Africa Report indicates that after three years of economic shock therapy, Nigeria is now focused on turning stability into productive investment. The NESG chief executive, Tayo Aduloju, reports the central bank is preparing to rethink lending incentives. Additionally, an overhaul of state-owned infrastructure is noted as a potential defining legacy for Tinubu's administration.

These potential reforms could significantly reshape the investment landscape in Nigeria. A rethink of lending incentives by the central bank might influence capital allocation and financing costs for businesses across various sectors. The proposed overhaul of state-owned infrastructure could present substantial opportunities in areas such as privatization, upgrades, or new public-private partnerships.

Investors should closely monitor upcoming official announcements regarding the central bank's specific plans for modifying lending incentives. Further details on the scope, timeline, and implementation strategies for the state-owned infrastructure overhaul will also be crucial for identifying concrete investment prospects and understanding the government's policy direction.
