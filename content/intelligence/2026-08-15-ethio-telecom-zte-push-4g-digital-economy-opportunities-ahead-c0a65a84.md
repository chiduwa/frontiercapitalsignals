Ethio Telecom and Chinese vendor ZTE are accelerating a large-scale network modernisation program in Ethiopia. This initiative aims to significantly expand 4G mobile connectivity and boost the country's broader digital economy.

This signals substantial infrastructure investment in Ethiopia's digital backbone, opening opportunities for ancillary services, digital platforms, and tech-driven businesses. Increased high-speed connectivity will fuel consumer spending and enterprise adoption, driving growth across the broader tech and digital services sectors. It also underscores a firm commitment to economic digitisation, a key driver for long-term market expansion.
