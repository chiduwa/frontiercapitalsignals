TotalEnergies and BluEnergies are intensifying their assessment of Liberia's Harper Basin. They are conducting new seismic and seabed studies, pushing the nation closer to a potential deepwater drilling decision in its undrilled frontier.

While Liberia is not a focus country for Frontier Capital Signals, this development signals increasing exploration appetite across West Africa’s Atlantic margin. Success here would validate similar frontier plays in Ghana and Nigeria, potentially boosting valuations for companies with acreage in analogous geological settings. It also highlights the long-term capital commitment required for deepwater projects, attracting major international players.

Monitor announcements from TotalEnergies and BluEnergies regarding their drilling decision. Any confirmed drilling or positive study results will likely spur interest in other underexplored deepwater blocks across the region, including in our target markets.
