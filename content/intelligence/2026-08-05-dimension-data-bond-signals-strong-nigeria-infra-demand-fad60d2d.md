Dimension Data Limited successfully closed its Series 1 corporate bond, raising N4.05 billion (US$2.9 million). This marks the initial tranche of a larger N20 billion (US$14.7 million) bond program, with funds directed towards expanding its fibre infrastructure across Nigeria.

This oversubscribed bond signals strong investor appetite for Nigeria's digital infrastructure sector. It underscores robust demand for fibre connectivity, a foundational element for economic growth, and proves a viable funding route for critical infrastructure expansion in the market. Expect further similar debt-funded growth.
