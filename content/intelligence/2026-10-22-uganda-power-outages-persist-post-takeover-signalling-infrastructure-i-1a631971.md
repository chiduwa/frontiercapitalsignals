Nile Post reports that power outages continue to persist in Uganda despite a recent takeover. The UEDCL Boss is providing explanations for why these service interruptions remain.

For investors in Uganda, the ongoing persistence of power outages, even after a takeover, signals fundamental challenges within the electricity sector. This situation suggests that efforts to improve power supply have not yet yielded stable results, impacting business operations and increasing costs for companies reliant on consistent electricity.

Investors should watch for the detailed explanations from the UEDCL Boss to understand the specific causes and the scope of the underlying issues. Monitoring the effectiveness of the takeover and any future policy responses will be key indicators of progress in stabilizing Uganda's power infrastructure.
