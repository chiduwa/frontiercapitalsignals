Capital FM Business reports that PwC Africa saw its revenue increase by 3.4 percent to US$546 million, or Sh70.5 billion, for the financial year that ended in June 2026. This growth was reportedly supported by continued investment. The news item's title specifically links this uplift to AI and the firm's expansion in technology investment.

This revenue growth for a major professional services firm signals potential strength in Africa's corporate sector and growing demand for technology-driven services. Investors should note the reported role of AI and technology investment in driving this growth. This suggests that businesses embracing digital transformation are finding opportunities across the continent, potentially leading to increased efficiency and new service offerings.

Investors should monitor further reports on technology adoption and investment trends within professional services and other key sectors in Africa. The performance of firms like PwC can be an indicator of underlying economic activity and corporate spending. Observing how technology, particularly AI, continues to impact business models and revenue streams will be key for identifying future growth areas.
