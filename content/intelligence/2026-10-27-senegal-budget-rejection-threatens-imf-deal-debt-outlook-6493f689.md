Africa Briefing reports that lawmakers loyal to Ousmane Sonko have rejected Senegal's revised 2026 budget. This action stems from a political rivalry between President Bassirou Diomaye Faye and Sonko. The country's energy subsidies are pushing the deficit to 7.6 percent, and its $2.2bn IMF program remains unapproved.

For investors, this development signals increased fiscal instability and potential delays in critical external financing. Africa Briefing indicates Senegal faces a worsening debt crisis, which the budget rejection and unapproved IMF program could exacerbate. This situation could heighten perceived risk and impact sovereign creditworthiness.

Investors should monitor future developments regarding Senegal's revised budget approval and the status of the $2.2bn IMF program. The resolution of the political rivalry impacting economic policy will be key. Any further information on efforts to address the rising deficit and debt crisis will be important to watch.
