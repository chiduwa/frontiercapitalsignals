Ethanol Company Limited (EthCo) has committed K65.2 million to sponsor the Ladies Interclub Golf Tournament for the seventh consecutive year. This substantial investment highlights growing corporate engagement in local sports and community events, with the tournament taking place at Kasasa Golf Club.

This consistent and significant sponsorship by EthCo, a major industrial player, signals robust corporate social responsibility and marketing budgets within Malawi's economy. It points to a stable operating environment for established companies with disposable income and long-term brand building strategies, suggesting underlying economic confidence and potential for growth in consumer discretionary spending.
