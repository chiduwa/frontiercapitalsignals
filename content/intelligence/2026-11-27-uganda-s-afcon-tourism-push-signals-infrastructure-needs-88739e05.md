The Independent (Uganda) reports that the Uganda Tourism Board (UTB) has launched the 11th Pearl of Africa Tourism Expo (POATE 2027). This initiative sets the stage for a period where the country hopes to leverage next year’s football spotlight to attract more tourists and boost business.

Tourism players in Uganda are looking for the AFCON event to translate into longer visitor stays and increased commercial activity. This suggests potential growth opportunities within the tourism sector. However, the report also highlights that roads, accommodation facilities, and air links require development, indicating significant infrastructure investment needs to support the anticipated tourism influx.

Investors should monitor how Uganda addresses these identified infrastructure gaps. Any progress or specific plans in improving roads, rooms, and air links could indicate actionable investment opportunities tied to the country's tourism strategy and its bid to capitalize on the AFCON event.
