Nyasa Times reports that Malawi's Finance Minister, Joseph Mwanamveka, has called for closer coordination between fiscal and monetary authorities across the Common Market for Eastern and Southern Africa (COMESA). He emphasized that aligned policies are crucial for achieving macroeconomic stability amidst an uncertain global economic landscape. This call was made at the opening of the 2026 COMESA Committee of Governors of Central Banks Symposium in Lilongwe.

For international investors in COMESA markets, including Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda, this initiative signals a potential push towards more predictable and stable economic environments. Greater policy cohesion across the region could mitigate country-specific risks and foster a more attractive investment climate by reducing volatility and enhancing confidence in regional economic management.

Investors should monitor how COMESA member states respond to this call for coordination. The focus should be on any concrete actions or joint policies that emerge from such discussions, as these would indicate tangible steps towards improved macroeconomic stability and a more harmonized investment landscape within the COMESA region.
