The Independent (Uganda) reports that Uganda's Minister of Defence, Kiryowa Kiwanuka, speaking at the Tana Forum in Bahir Dar, Ethiopia, advocated for African nations to retain and process their wealth locally. He challenged conventional development metrics and warned against raw material exports, stating they deplete the continent.

This call for local value addition and processing signals potential policy shifts in Uganda and possibly other African nations towards greater domestic industrialization. Investors currently involved in raw material extraction or export may face future pressures or incentives to establish local processing operations. Conversely, opportunities could emerge in sectors supporting value addition, such as manufacturing, logistics, and processing technology.

Investors should monitor specific policy proposals or legislative changes in Uganda that align with this sentiment, particularly regarding export tariffs on raw materials or incentives for local processing. It remains unclear how quickly or broadly these sentiments will translate into actionable policy.
