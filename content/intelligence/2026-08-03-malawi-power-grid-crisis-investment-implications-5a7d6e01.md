An energy expert has issued a scathing assessment of Malawi's state-run power distributor, Escom, demanding an urgent overhaul of its maintenance and monitoring systems. The expert highlighted the crumbling power infrastructure as the direct cause of persistent, country-wide blackouts.

This situation directly impacts investor confidence and operational stability for businesses in Malawi. Persistent blackouts escalate operational costs through reliance on alternative power sources and hinder productivity across all sectors. A weak grid infrastructure is a significant impediment to economic growth and an FDI deterrent.

Investors should monitor Escom's concrete response and any government commitments to address these infrastructure deficiencies. Watch for potential tenders in grid modernization, renewable energy projects, or policy shifts inviting private sector participation in power generation and distribution. This criticism may catalyze necessary reforms.
