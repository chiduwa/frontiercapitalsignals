MyJoyOnline Business reports that Absa Bank Ghana LTD’s new headquarters, Absa Place, has achieved IFC EDGE Advanced certification. This recognition signifies the building's high standards of energy and water efficiency.

This development suggests a growing emphasis on sustainable construction and operational efficiency within Ghana's commercial real estate sector. For international investors, it flags potential opportunities in green building materials, energy and water efficiency technologies, and the development of eco-friendly properties.

Investors should monitor the broader adoption of green building standards across Ghana, particularly how these initiatives influence commercial property values and tenant demand. Watch for any emerging regulatory frameworks or incentives promoting sustainable infrastructure.
