Nyasa Times reports that Press Corporation Plc and its group of companies will continue investing in tree planting and conservation efforts. This commitment, aimed at tackling climate change, was affirmed by Puma Energy Managing Director Steveria Kadangwe on behalf of the group. The pledge was made at an awards ceremony for schools recognized for maintaining trees planted under the "Press for Nature" initiative, where schools also won borehole prizes.

This initiative signals Press Corporation Plc's sustained commitment to environmental, social, and governance (ESG) factors.
