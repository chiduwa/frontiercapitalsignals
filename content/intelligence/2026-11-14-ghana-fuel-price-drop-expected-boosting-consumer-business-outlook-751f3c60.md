MyJoyOnline Business reports that petrol prices in Ghana could fall by up to 8.23% starting November 16, 2026. Diesel prices are also expected to decline, according to the Chamber of Oil Marketing Companies (COMAC).

This anticipated reduction in fuel costs, if realized, could lower operational expenses for various businesses reliant on transport and logistics. It may also increase discretionary spending among consumers, potentially boosting sectors like retail and manufacturing.

Investors should monitor official announcements and market reactions closer to the November 2026 timeline. The actual percentage decrease and its sustained impact on overall economic indicators will be key to assess.
