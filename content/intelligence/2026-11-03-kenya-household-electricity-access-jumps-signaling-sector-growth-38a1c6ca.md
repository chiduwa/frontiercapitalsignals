Capital FM Business reports that the share of Kenyan households using electricity has risen significantly. This increased to 70 per cent from 58 per cent in 2022, according to a recent survey.

This development signals growing infrastructure penetration and potentially higher consumer purchasing power in Kenya. For investors, this indicates increased demand for electricity generation, transmission, and distribution services. It also suggests opportunities in related sectors, such as consumer electronics, appliances, and digital services, as more households gain access to a stable power supply. This trend supports long-term growth in the energy and consumer markets.
