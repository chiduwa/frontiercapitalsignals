Malawi24 reports that Malawi's fuel shortage has entered its second week, with motorists spending nights in queues and drivers struggling to find fuel. Uncertainty is growing over when supplies will return to normal. The Malawi Energy Regulatory Authority (MERA) and the Ministry of Energy have yet to provide a public update on when the situation is expected to improve.

This extended shortage implies significant operational disruptions for businesses relying on transportation, energy, and logistics across Malawi. The lack of public updates from MERA and the Ministry of Energy further exacerbates uncertainty regarding market stability and supply chain reliability for investors operating or considering the Malawian market.

Investors should monitor the duration of the shortage and any official communications from energy authorities. A prolonged disruption could impact business continuity, inflation, and overall economic activity, potentially affecting investment sentiment in Malawi.
