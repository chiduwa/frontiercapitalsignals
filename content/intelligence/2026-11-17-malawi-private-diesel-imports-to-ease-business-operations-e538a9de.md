Malawi24 reports that the Consumers Association of Malawi (CAMA) has welcomed a decision by the Malawi Energy Regulatory Authority (MERA). This decision allows companies and organizations to import their own diesel. CAMA Executive Director John Kapito stated that the move could ease fuel shortages and help businesses get back on their feet, also noting it could reduce something unspecified.

This policy shift by MERA, if fully implemented, has the potential to directly impact operating costs and supply chain stability for businesses in Malawi, particularly those reliant on diesel. The ability of private entities to secure their own fuel supply could reduce disruptions and support business continuity. This change might improve the operational environment for various sectors.
