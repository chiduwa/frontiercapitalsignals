AfCFTA has signed a 20-year, $3.1 billion concession with Nigeria's Bergmans Security Consultants and Supplies to build a continent-wide connected customs system. This aims to significantly reduce border delays, revenue losses, and fraud across Africa.

This initiative addresses critical friction points for cross-border trade. A more efficient, transparent customs environment will boost trade volumes, improve supply chain predictability, and unlock opportunities in logistics, e-commerce, and export-oriented sectors across our target markets.

Key details around national participation, repayment mechanisms, and data safeguards remain undisclosed. Future clarification on these terms will be crucial for assessing implementation risks and identifying specific entry points for private capital as the project progresses.
