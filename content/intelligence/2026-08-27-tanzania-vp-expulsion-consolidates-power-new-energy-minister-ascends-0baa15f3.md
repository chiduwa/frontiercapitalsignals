Tanzania's ruling CCM expelled outgoing Vice-President Emmanuel Nchimbi immediately following his resignation. The party unanimously backed Energy Minister Deogratius Ndejembi as his successor.

This swift action signals firm control by the CCM, suggesting either policy continuity or potential shifts, particularly in energy given Ndejembi's portfolio. For investors, it highlights the party's capacity for decisive internal changes, influencing the predictability of the business environment.
