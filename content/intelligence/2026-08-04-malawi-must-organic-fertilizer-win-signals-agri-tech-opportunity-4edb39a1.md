The Malawi University of Science and Technology (MUST) secured K78 million after winning the 2026 Innovation Fund Pitching Competition. Their organic fertilizer project was selected as the top initiative among 123 entries, receiving substantial funding to advance its development.

This victory signals strong institutional and government backing for agricultural innovation in Malawi. An effective local organic fertilizer can enhance food security, improve soil health, and reduce reliance on costly imported inputs. This creates tangible investment opportunities within sustainable agriculture, agri-tech, and local supply chain development.

Investors should monitor the project’s progress, focusing on pilot results and potential scaling partnerships with agribusinesses. Watch for broader policy support for sustainable farming and domestic input production, which will further unlock and derisk investments in Malawi's agricultural sector.
