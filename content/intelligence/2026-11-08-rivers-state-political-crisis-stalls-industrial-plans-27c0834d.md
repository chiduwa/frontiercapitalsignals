BusinessDay reports that a fierce political crisis in Rivers State has stalled plans for industrialisation. This crisis occurred after what was perceived as a bright industrial future dawning on the Rivers State economy when Sim Fubara assumed leadership. The Manufacturers Association of Nigeria (MAN) has reportedly recounted these developments.

This situation highlights the significant impact of political instability on economic development and investment prospects. Persistent political crises can deter both local and international capital, disrupting long-term industrialisation strategies and affecting projected returns in the Rivers State economy. Investors evaluating opportunities in Nigeria must factor in such governance risks.
