FMDQ Exchange has approved the quotation of CapitalSage Technology Limited's N45.12 billion Commercial Papers program. This includes the N4.29 billion Series 1 and N3.33 billion Series 2 tranches, marking a significant step for the company in accessing local capital markets for its funding needs.

This approval provides CapitalSage, a prominent technology firm, with vital short-term funding to enhance operational liquidity and drive potential expansion initiatives. For international investors, this presents a compelling fixed-income opportunity in Nigeria's dynamic tech sector, diversifying portfolios and signaling FMDQ's confidence in local growth companies.

Investors should monitor the successful issuance and subscription rates for these initial CP series, along with CapitalSage's strategic deployment of the raised capital. Watch for impacts on their technology product development, market penetration, and financial performance, as well as any future tranches from the overall N45.12 billion program.
