Malawi's Parliament is resuming public hearings into the June 10 Chikangawa plane crash, an incident that killed former Vice-President Saulos Chilima and eight others. The Ad-Hoc Committee continues its evidence gathering process.

This ongoing inquiry impacts Malawi's political risk profile. The transparency and integrity of the investigation are critical, directly influencing perceptions of institutional strength and governance. A credible resolution can stabilize political sentiment, while any perceived cover-up or mismanagement risks fueling public discontent and undermining investor confidence in the country's rule of law.

Investors should monitor the committee's findings and the government's response. The nature of any conclusions, especially regarding accountability and institutional failures, will signal the country's commitment to good governance. Observe any subsequent policy changes or personnel shifts, as these could reflect broader political adjustments.
