MyJoyOnline Business reports that Ghana Integrated Aluminium Development Corporation (GIADEC) and RUSAL have agreed to develop bauxite mining and processing operations in Ghana. RUSAL, described as one of the world's largest aluminium producers, has an obligation to build an alumina refinery with an annual capacity of 1.2 million tonnes as part of the key terms. GIADEC sources indicate the agreement was signed in Moscow during the Russia–Africa Forum.

This signals significant long-term capital investment and industrial development in Ghana's mining sector. The refinery project could create substantial supply chain opportunities, from construction and logistics to services supporting the new industrial operations. Such large-scale foreign direct investment potentially enhances Ghana's position in global aluminium production, impacting infrastructure and related industries.

The execution of this agreement could indicate broader government commitment to attracting large-scale industrial partnerships, potentially influencing other resource-based projects in Ghana.
