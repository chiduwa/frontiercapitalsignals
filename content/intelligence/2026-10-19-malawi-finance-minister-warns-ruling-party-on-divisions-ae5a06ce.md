Nyasa Times reports that Malawi's Finance Minister, Joseph Mwanamvekha, issued a warning to members of the Democratic Progressive Party (DPP). He urged ambitious figures within the party to cease internal power struggles and instead concentrate on assisting President Peter Mutharika in rebuilding the country. This message was delivered at the Mulhako wa Alhomwe Cultural Festival.

Political cohesion within the ruling party can significantly impact policy execution and government stability. Divisions, as highlighted by the Finance Minister, could potentially detract from the government's focus on national development and economic initiatives. For investors, internal political friction may introduce uncertainty regarding the consistency and implementation of long-term economic strategies.

Investors should monitor the extent to which these calls for unity are heeded within the DPP. Continued public or private signs of division could signal potential challenges to government effectiveness and policy stability. Conversely, greater internal alignment could reinforce confidence in the government's capacity to pursue its rebuilding agenda.
