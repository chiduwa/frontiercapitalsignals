Malawi24 reports that FCB Nyasa Big Bullets rejected a proposal from Masters FC to shift a Sunday football fixture to a night kick-off. The Bullets' supporters' General Secretary, Archibald Kasakura, stated a night kick-off could create problems for supporters. The club cited Malawi's electricity and fuel crisis as the reason for insisting on the approved 3pm kick-off time.
