The Independent (Uganda) reports that Uganda convened its diaspora community in Ethiopia for a two-day event in Addis Ababa. The forum, themed “Connecting, Celebrating and Contributing: Harnessing the Ugandan Diaspora for National Development,” aimed to strengthen ties between Ugandans living in Ethiopia and national development priorities, while also showcasing Ugandan culture.

This initiative signals Uganda's proactive strategy to attract diaspora capital, trade, and expertise. For international investors, this suggests a potential increase in investment flows from the diaspora, which can complement foreign direct investment or create new domestic business opportunities. It may also indicate government efforts to cultivate a more favorable investment environment to capitalize on these contributions.

Investors should monitor whether specific policy incentives or investment vehicles are announced to facilitate diaspora engagement in trade and investment. The effectiveness of this call to action and any subsequent follow-up measures by the Ugandan government will be key indicators of sustained investment opportunities.
