Reports of health hazards from expired food products are on the rise, prompting experts to highlight the critical need for consumers to meticulously check expiry dates before purchase. This signals growing public awareness and concern regarding product safety in the market.

This trend directly impacts Fast-Moving Consumer Goods, or FMCG, companies and retailers. Heightened consumer scrutiny will pressure firms to optimize supply chain efficiency, inventory management, and product rotation to minimize expired stock on shelves. Brands demonstrating superior quality control and transparency stand to gain market share, while those with lax practices face reputational damage and reduced sales.

Monitor for potential regulatory responses, including stricter food safety standards and enforcement mechanisms. Also, observe how key FMCG players adapt their production, distribution, and marketing strategies to address these evolving consumer expectations and reduce waste from expiring products.
