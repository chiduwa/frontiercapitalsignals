Mary's Meals Malawi has launched an Alumni Campaign, inviting former school feeding beneficiaries to contribute. This initiative aims to bridge a funding gap, seeking support from those who once benefited to feed 3.4 million children awaiting meals.

This campaign signals both the persistent socio-economic challenges in Malawi, with millions still reliant on aid for basic needs, and the growing potential of local giving. It underscores the vital role NGOs play in addressing systemic issues like food security and education, which directly impact future human capital development and market stability. A healthy, educated populace is foundational for sustained economic growth.

Monitor the campaign's success as an indicator of local philanthropic capacity and community engagement. Observe if the government or private sector steps up with complementary initiatives to reduce dependency on such appeals. The long-term trajectory of food security and education funding will directly influence Malawi's human capital development, a key factor for long-term investment attractiveness.
