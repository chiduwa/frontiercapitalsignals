Africa Briefing reports Morocco's proposed $239m sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) plant would need to import most of its raw materials. Imported waste oils would provide 60 percent of the feedstock. The EBRD loan for this project remains under review.

This reliance on imported waste oils raises questions about supply costs and how much value the country could retain from such an industry. Despite these challenges, Europe offers growing demand for sustainable aviation fuel, indicating a potential market for output. This scenario highlights the complex balance between developing green energy infrastructure and managing raw material sourcing.

Investors should monitor the outcome of the EBRD loan review and any further details on the plant's supply chain strategy. The cost implications of importing most raw materials will be critical to the project's overall economic viability and could offer insights for similar green energy initiatives across the continent.
