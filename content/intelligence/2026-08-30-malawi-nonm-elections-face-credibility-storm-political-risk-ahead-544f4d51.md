Malawi's upcoming NONM elections, scheduled for November 27, 2026, are already facing a significant credibility storm. This early emergence of concerns signals potential challenges to the electoral process.

For investors, electoral credibility issues can signal broader governance risks and potential political instability. Such challenges often introduce policy uncertainty, impacting the predictability of the regulatory environment and long term investment security. A contested outcome could lead to market volatility.

Closely monitor the specific nature of these credibility concerns and official responses from electoral authorities and the government. Observe civil society reactions and any international engagement. The resolution, or escalation, of these issues will be a critical indicator for Malawi's political risk profile leading into 2026.
