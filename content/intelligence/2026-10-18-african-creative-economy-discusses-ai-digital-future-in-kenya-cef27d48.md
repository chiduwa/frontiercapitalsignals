The Standard reports that Mombasa will host the four-day Afrotellers Conference. This event will address AI and digital power, along with the ownership of African stories and cultural representation. A key focus will be the future of the continent's creative economy.

This conference signals increased attention on the intersection of technology and creative industries in Africa. For investors, discussions around AI and digital power could highlight emerging business models, intellectual property trends, and potential investment areas within content creation, distribution, and digital platforms. The focus on the "future of the creative economy" suggests a sector ripe for innovation and growth.

Investors should monitor outcomes from such discussions for insights into regulatory shifts, funding initiatives, or collaborative ventures that could impact the creative and tech sectors. Observing how African creatives plan to leverage or address AI and digital tools will be crucial for understanding future market dynamics and identifying early-stage opportunities.
