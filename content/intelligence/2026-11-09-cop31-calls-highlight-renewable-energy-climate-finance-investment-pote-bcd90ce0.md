The High Street Journal reports that COP31 has opened with renewed calls for faster climate action, greater adaptation finance, and increased renewable energy investment. Governments are reportedly facing mounting pressure to translate their existing climate commitments into measurable results.

This global focus on climate action signals a potential shift in investment priorities. For investors in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda, these calls could translate into enhanced opportunities within the renewable energy sector and projects related to climate adaptation. Increased pressure on governments may also lead to new supportive policies or regulatory frameworks in these markets.

We recommend monitoring the outcomes of COP31 for specific pledges or funding mechanisms targeting renewable energy or adaptation finance in emerging economies. Any concrete initiatives or policy directives could directly impact the viability and attractiveness of relevant projects across our focus countries.
