The Independent (Uganda) reports that experts warn Uganda's ambition to achieve a $500 billion economy by 2040 could be jeopardized. These experts suggest that environmental degradation, weak accountability, and unsustainable business practices pose significant risks to this growth target. The warning emerged at an event hosted by the Institute of Certified Public Accountants.

For investors in Uganda, these warnings highlight potential long-term risks to economic stability and business operating environments. Concerns about environmental degradation and weak accountability could impact the sustainability of investment returns, particularly in sectors reliant on natural resources or those sensitive to regulatory oversight. Unsustainable business practices might also indicate future operational challenges or increased compliance costs.

Investors should monitor how the Ugandan government and private sector address these issues, especially regarding environmental policies and strengthening accountability frameworks. The active role suggested for accountants in shaping economic decisions could signal future changes in corporate governance or reporting requirements. This could influence due diligence and risk assessment for new and existing investments.
