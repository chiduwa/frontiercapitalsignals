South Africa's data center sector is undergoing rapid expansion, attracting substantial investment and now hosting nearly half of Africa's data center capacity. This positions the country as a strategic hub for the continent's digital economy, with its local market projected to grow significantly from US$2.16 billion.

This robust growth in South Africa signals a wider acceleration of digital infrastructure development across the entire African continent. As data processing and connectivity demands surge, markets like Ghana, Nigeria, and Kenya will increasingly require localized data storage, cloud services, and supporting network infrastructure. This presents direct investment opportunities beyond South Africa's borders.

Investors should monitor capital deployment into fibre optic networks, last-mile connectivity, and edge computing solutions in East and West Africa. Observe government policies on data localization and incentives for data center development in these emerging digital economies, as they will indicate prime areas for the next wave of infrastructure investment and growth.
