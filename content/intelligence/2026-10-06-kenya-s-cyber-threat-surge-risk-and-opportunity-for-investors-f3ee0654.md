Kenya recorded 11.12 billion cyber threat events in the year to June 2026, a 29% increase from the previous year. The Communications Authority of Kenya's latest report shows Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks more than doubled, rising 114.3%. This marks a significant escalation in the digital threat landscape.

This surge directly impacts investor interests, increasing operational costs and potential disruption for businesses reliant on digital infrastructure across finance, e-commerce, and logistics. It necessitates higher spending on robust cybersecurity measures, data protection, and resilience planning. For investors, this translates to heightened risk profiles for digitally exposed assets, but also presents a clear demand for cybersecurity solutions.

Investors should closely watch government and corporate responses, including new regulatory frameworks and increased cybersecurity investments. Opportunities will emerge in the local and international cybersecurity markets as demand for defensive technologies and expert services grows. Evaluate portfolio companies' exposure and their strategic plans to mitigate these evolving cyber risks.
