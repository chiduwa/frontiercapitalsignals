TechCabal reports that Nigeria's telecom regulator is considering offering 15-year spectrum licences. This proposal aims to encourage additional network investment from operators.

This initiative could offer telecom operators greater certainty, potentially de-risking long-term investment in Nigeria's broadband infrastructure. Longer licence terms can improve financial planning and return on capital for significant network build-outs.

The success of this proposal will depend on whether the incentives truly drive new broadband improvements or merely support existing network rollout plans. Investors should monitor the specifics of the proposed incentives and the regulatory framework's implementation.
