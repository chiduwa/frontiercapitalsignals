TechCabal reports that INFRAGORA plans to begin building two AI-ready data centers in Nigeria by Q2 2027. This initiative is backed by a proposed investment of $650 million to $1 billion and dedicated power infrastructure.

This potential $1 billion investment signals significant growth opportunities within Nigeria's digital economy, particularly for AI-related services and advanced computing infrastructure. It could also create upstream and downstream benefits for connectivity providers, cloud services, and skilled labor markets in the region.

Key execution hurdles include securing financing and ensuring reliable electricity. Investors should monitor developments regarding the proposed funding and the progress on dedicated power infrastructure ahead of the Q2 2027 building commencement.
