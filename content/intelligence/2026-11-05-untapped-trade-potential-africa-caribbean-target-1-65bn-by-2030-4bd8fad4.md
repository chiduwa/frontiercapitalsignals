Africa Briefing reports that annual trade between Africa and the Caribbean could reach $1.65 billion by 2030. A new international study indicates that around 80 percent of African goods export potential remains untapped, highlighting a significant commercial opportunity.

This development signals considerable growth potential for businesses involved in trade logistics, export facilitation, and financial services across Africa. The study identifies shipping costs and financing barriers as primary restrictions, indicating specific areas where investment could unlock this untapped potential.

Investors should monitor upcoming policy initiatives and private sector solutions aimed at reducing these shipping costs and improving trade financing mechanisms. Further details on the specific sectors or goods driving this export potential would provide more targeted investment insights.
