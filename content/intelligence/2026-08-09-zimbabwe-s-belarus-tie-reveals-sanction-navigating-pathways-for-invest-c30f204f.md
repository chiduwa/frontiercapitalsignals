This enterprise spans agriculture, mining, and diplomatic engagement. Significantly, Zingman has achieved this while successfully navigating Western sanctions.

This case illustrates a specific model for securing significant economic interests in African resource markets, even amidst Western sanctions. It signals that strong geopolitical alignments, here between Minsk and Harare, can unlock substantial opportunities in key sectors like mining and agriculture.
