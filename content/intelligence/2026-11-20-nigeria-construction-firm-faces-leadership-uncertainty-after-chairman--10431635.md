Premium Times reports that Festus Onuh, chairman of Ferotex Construction Company Ltd and APC campaign finance director, died in a road accident. The incident occurred while he was travelling along the Port Harcourt-Enugu Expressway to attend a party campaign event.

The death of a company chairman introduces leadership uncertainty for Ferotex Construction Company Ltd. Given his dual role, his passing might affect both the company's operational stability and its broader relationships, especially concerning public sector projects often influenced by political ties in Nigeria's infrastructure market. Investors should consider the potential for leadership transition risks.

Investors should monitor any announcements regarding the leadership succession at Ferotex Construction Company Ltd and assess the potential impact on its project pipeline and operational continuity.
