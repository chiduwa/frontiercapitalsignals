Malawi's Chief Justice, Rizine Mzikamanda, made an unusual public appeal, asking Malawians to maintain faith in the judiciary despite reported delays. This direct request highlights underlying concerns about the institution's perceived credibility.

For international investors, a credible and efficient judiciary is fundamental. Weak judicial trust or significant delays directly impact contract enforcement, dispute resolution, and property rights protection, increasing operational risk and discouraging long-term capital commitments in Malawi.

Investors should closely monitor any specific reforms initiated to address judicial delays and enhance public trust. Observe government support for judicial independence and concrete evidence of improved case resolution efficiency, as these will signal real progress in strengthening Malawi's rule of law.
