The High Street Journal reports that regional ministers will use the two-day Invest Ghana Summit to present investment opportunities. These opportunities are expected to originate from their respective regions.

This gathering offers a direct channel for international investors to explore potential investments across various regions of Ghana. The involvement of regional ministers suggests a focus on local development priorities and projects, which could open new avenues for capital deployment.

Investors should monitor any specific opportunities or policy incentives that may be highlighted by the ministers during the summit. Details regarding the nature of the investment opportunities presented and the level of regional government support will be key to assessing their viability.
