Pan-African fintech Moment just closed a $22 million Series A funding round, bringing its total raised capital to $55 million. AlphaCode Venture Partners led the round, with continued backing from General Catalyst, MultiChoice, and new investment from Canal+. This capital will accelerate development of its continental payments platform.

This significant funding injection into a three-year-old fintech validates the strong growth potential and investor appetite for scalable digital payment solutions across Africa. The involvement of established players like MultiChoice and Canal+ suggests strategic interest in integrating Moment's payment infrastructure, pointing to future ecosystem expansion and revenue opportunities.

Monitor Moment's execution in deploying this capital to expand its continental platform. Look for specific country rollouts, new partnerships, and adoption rates. Success will indicate further consolidation and innovation within Africa's payments landscape, presenting potential for M&A or strategic alliances.
