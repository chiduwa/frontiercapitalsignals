Kenya experienced a significant surge in cyber threats, with events reaching 11.12 billion in the year to June 2026, a 29% increase from the previous year. Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks more than doubled, rising by 114.3%, according to the Communications Authority of Kenya's latest report.

This escalating threat environment poses material risks to digital-first businesses and critical infrastructure across sectors, from financial services to e-commerce. It highlights the growing need for robust cybersecurity solutions and expertise, potentially increasing operational costs for companies and impacting valuations. Investors must assess their portfolio companies' resilience against such threats.

Monitor regulatory responses and potential policy shifts aimed at strengthening national cyber defenses. Look for emerging investment opportunities in cybersecurity firms, data protection services, and secure cloud infrastructure providers, all poised to benefit from this increased demand for digital resilience. Also, evaluate the long-term impact on Kenya's broader digital transformation agenda.
