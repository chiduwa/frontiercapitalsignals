Capital FM Business reports that new facilities, an ICT hub and a recording studio, are being established in Kilifi. These additional facilities are intended to benefit youth by creating opportunities for talent development and the acquisition of digital skills.

This development is significant for investors as it points to a strategic push for economic diversification and human capital development. The focus on digital skills and the creative economy signals potential for growth in sectors related to digital content, technology services, and entertainment, fostering a more skilled workforce.

Investors should monitor the implementation and impact of these new facilities on youth engagement and the actual expansion of the creative economy in the region. Tracking job creation, startup activity, and the uptake of digital skills could indicate emerging investment opportunities in related industries.
