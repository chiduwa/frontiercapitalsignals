The High Street Journal reports that Ghana's natural resource productivity relies on stronger research capacity and commercially viable technology. This initiative involves African scientists at the University of Ghana receiving training in digital tools and and exposure to solar-powered farming.

This focus on research and technology signals a potential shift in how Ghana approaches natural resource management. Investors should consider how enhanced research capabilities and technology adoption, particularly in areas like digital tools and solar farming, could improve productivity and create new market opportunities or efficiencies for businesses operating within or supporting Ghana's natural resource sectors.

Watch for further developments in the implementation and scaling of these training programs and the commercialization of new technologies. Investors should also monitor for specific policy support or funding aimed at bolstering research infrastructure and integrating these advanced skills into broader economic activities.
