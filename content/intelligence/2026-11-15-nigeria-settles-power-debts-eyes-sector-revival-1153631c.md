BusinessDay reports that President Bola Tinubu’s government has paid N1.23 trillion of legacy debts owed to power generators. This significant financial outlay aims to address long-standing arrears within the sector.

For investors, this payment signifies the government's declared intent to stabilize and potentially revive Nigeria’s power sector by clearing historical liabilities. Such a move could improve the financial health and operational consistency of power generation companies, potentially making the sector a more predictable environment for capital.

Moving forward, investors should monitor how effectively this debt payment translates into tangible improvements in power supply and sector performance. Observing further policy initiatives designed to ensure long-term sustainability and prevent the re-accumulation of arrears will be crucial in assessing future investment viability.
