Africa Briefing reports Citigroup expects African governments to sell more international bonds in 2027 than any previous year. This prediction is based on stronger investor demand anticipated to sustain a recovery in borrowing. Nine African countries have already raised $16.9 billion in Eurobonds this year.

This outlook suggests increased opportunities for international investors in African sovereign debt in the medium term. The anticipated record sovereign bond deals, exceeding 2026 levels, indicate a potential rebound in capital markets. Stronger investor demand points to improving market sentiment for African assets.

Investors should closely monitor developments around debt servicing and currency risks, as Africa Briefing notes these could complicate the recovery. The ability of African governments to manage these challenges will be crucial for the predicted bond boom to materialize.
