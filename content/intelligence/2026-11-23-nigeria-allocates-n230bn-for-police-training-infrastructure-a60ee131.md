Premium Times reports that Nigeria's National Economic Council (NEC) has approved an additional N130 billion for the rehabilitation of police training institutions. This decision brings the total funding allocated for this program to N230 billion.

This substantial government allocation signals a commitment to improving security infrastructure in Nigeria. For investors, this funding represents potential contract opportunities in construction, rehabilitation, and related services sectors. The increased budget suggests a more extensive scope for the program, indicating a significant pipeline of work.

Investors should monitor the tender process and contract awards associated with this N230 billion program. The execution timeline and transparency of procurement will be key indicators of the pace of infrastructure development and potential for private sector participation.
