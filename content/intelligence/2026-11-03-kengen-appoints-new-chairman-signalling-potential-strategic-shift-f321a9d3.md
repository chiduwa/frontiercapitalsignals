Capital FM Business reports that Kenya Electricity Generating Company, KenGen, has appointed John Kipngetich Mosonik as its new board chairman. He replaces the outgoing chair, Alfred Agoui, as the electricity generator's new leader.

This leadership change at a key electricity generator in Kenya could signal evolving strategic direction or a renewed focus on operational efficiency.
