The High Street Journal reports a tourism consultant is urging the 2027 Budget to focus on transforming tourism policies into measurable economic gains. The consultant specifically recommends prioritizing infrastructure, creative industry financing, and regional tourism development.

These proposals are intended to attract investment, create jobs, and enhance economic returns in the tourism sector. For investors, this signals a potential strategic government focus on areas like hospitality infrastructure, supporting creative industries, and promoting regional travel within Ghana.

Investors should watch for official government responses to these recommendations and any concrete budget allocations or policy initiatives unveiled in the run-up to the 2027 Budget. The actual implementation of these priorities will be key for identifying specific investment avenues.
