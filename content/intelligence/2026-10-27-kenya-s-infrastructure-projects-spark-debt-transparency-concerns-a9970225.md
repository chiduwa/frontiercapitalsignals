The Standard reports that President Ruto's mega infrastructure projects are currently facing scrutiny. This is attributed to rising costs, opaque financing mechanisms, and mounting public debt.

For investors, this situation highlights potential risks related to Kenya's fiscal stability and project transparency. Increased public debt can influence sovereign risk assessments and the overall cost of doing business. Opaque financing, in particular, may signal elevated operational and governance risks for involved or prospective investors in large-scale ventures.

Going forward, it will be crucial to observe how the government addresses these concerns, especially regarding transparency in project financing and managing debt levels. Further clarity on these issues could impact investor confidence in major infrastructure initiatives.
