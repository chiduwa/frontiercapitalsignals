Nile Post reports that Uganda's government, through the Ministry of Agriculture and the Ministry of Water and Environment, has unveiled a plan to boost agricultural production. This initiative involves rehabilitating existing irrigation schemes, harvesting water from rivers and streams, and extending supplies to farms.

This policy move aims to improve food security and climate resilience. For investors, it signals government commitment to supporting the agricultural sector, potentially creating demand for agricultural inputs, technology, and infrastructure development related to water management and irrigation systems. It could also de-risk agricultural investments by mitigating climate-related uncertainties.

Investors should monitor further details on the scope, funding mechanisms, and timelines for these irrigation projects. Observing the specific regions targeted and any potential public-private partnership opportunities will be key to identifying actionable investment avenues.
