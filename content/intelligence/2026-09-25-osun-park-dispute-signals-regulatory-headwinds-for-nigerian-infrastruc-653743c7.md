Osun State's ruling APC and the Osun Assembly are at odds over a proposed state-level park management system. The APC argues the constitution assigns motor park establishment, maintenance, and regulation to local government councils, challenging the Assembly's proposed centralized approach.

This jurisdictional clash signals potential regulatory instability for businesses operating or planning infrastructure investments within Nigeria, particularly those reliant on clear local government cooperation or consistent state-level policy. Such disputes can introduce operational delays, increased compliance costs, and uncertainty around permit approvals, impacting long-term project viability and investor confidence.

Monitor how this dispute resolves, whether through legislative compromise or judicial intervention. Its outcome will offer insights into the clarity and consistency of regulatory frameworks across different government tiers, influencing investment risk assessments for urban development, logistics, and public transport sectors nationwide.
