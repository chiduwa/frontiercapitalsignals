BusinessDay reports that the Federal Government has mandated the Equipment Leasing Registration Authority (ELRA) certificate for public procurement processes that involve equipment leasing.

This represents a regulatory tightening for firms engaged in equipment leasing or those looking to participate in government contracts requiring such services in Nigeria. It introduces a new compliance hurdle that could affect operational costs, market access, and the competitive landscape for businesses in this sector. Investors should note this as a potential barrier to entry or an opportunity for well-compliant entities.

Going forward, investors should monitor how swiftly the equipment leasing sector adapts to this new requirement. Key areas to watch include the practical implications for existing public procurement projects, the efficiency of ELRA certificate issuance, and any potential shifts in market share among compliant versus non-compliant leasing providers.
