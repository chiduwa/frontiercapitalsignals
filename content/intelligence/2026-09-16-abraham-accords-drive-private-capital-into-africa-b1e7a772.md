Jared Kushner, the US special envoy, is spearheading efforts to revitalize the Abraham Accords by injecting significant private capital. This initiative directly links to American recognition of Moroccan sovereignty over Western Sahara, aiming to provide substantial economic benefits to Rabat six years after the initial agreement.

This move signals a renewed US focus on economic diplomacy in Africa, using private investment as a key tool for geopolitical stability and development. For investors, it highlights a potential model where diplomatic frameworks unlock substantial capital flows, setting a precedent that could inform future investment strategies across the continent, including in sub-Saharan markets seeking similar partnerships. It also underscores a growing emphasis on infrastructure and development projects tied to strategic alliances.
