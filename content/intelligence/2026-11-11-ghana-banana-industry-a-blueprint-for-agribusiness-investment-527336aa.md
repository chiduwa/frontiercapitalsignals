MyJoyOnline Business reports Ghana's banana industry is showcasing the potential of large-scale irrigation and private-sector investment for agricultural transformation. Golden Exotics Limited, with its extensive commercial banana plantation around Kasunya and Asutsuare in the Shai-Osudoku District, is central to this development, creating jobs and strengthening non-traditional exports.

This highlights a successful blueprint for Ghana's agricultural sector, demonstrating how private capital can drive production growth. The model potentially de-risks future investments in large-scale agribusiness and export-oriented ventures across the country's non-traditional export portfolio.

Investors should monitor how this irrigation and private investment model can be replicated beyond bananas into other non-traditional export crops. Sustained success and broader government support for such agricultural initiatives will indicate further actionable investment opportunities.
