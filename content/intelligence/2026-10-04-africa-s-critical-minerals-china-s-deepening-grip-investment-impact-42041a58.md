Chinese firms now control over half of the Democratic Republic of Congo's critical mining projects, a significant increase from less than a third in 2023. This rapid expansion confirms Beijing's successful long-term strategy to secure key mineral resources.

This growing Chinese dominance in the DRC sets a precedent for critical mineral resource access across Africa. Investors should recognize the intensified competition for strategic assets, potentially driving up acquisition costs and influencing market dynamics for key inputs in EV batteries and renewable energy technologies continent-wide. African governments may face increased pressure to either align or diversify their partnerships.

Monitor how other African nations with significant critical mineral reserves, particularly our focus countries, adjust their investment policies. Watch for potential counter-strategies from Western powers aiming to secure their own supply chains, which could create new opportunities for diversified investment and local value addition in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda.
