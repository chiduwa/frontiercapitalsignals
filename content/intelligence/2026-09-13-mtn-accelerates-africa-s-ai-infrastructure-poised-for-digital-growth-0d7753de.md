MTN Group, through its MTN Digital Infrastructure unit, has announced a strategic partnership aimed at advancing Africa's AI-ready digital infrastructure ecosystem. This proactive move directly addresses the rapidly accelerating demand for resilient cloud computing, AI workloads, and data-intensive services across the continent.

This partnership signals MTN's calculated positioning within a high-growth, foundational sector. Reliable, locally hosted digital infrastructure is critical for AI adoption and economic diversification across our target markets. Investing in this underlying infrastructure now promises significant long-term returns as AI integration accelerates, driving demand across various industries and boosting data consumption. It de-risks future digital transformation for companies operating in Africa.

Investors should closely monitor the specifics of this partnership, including geographic rollout plans, initial infrastructure deployment timelines, and potential for further joint ventures. Key indicators will be data center capacity expansions, connectivity improvements, and the actual uptake by enterprises leveraging AI and cloud services. Observe any direct impact on MTN's enterprise segment revenue within Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda, and Malawi.
