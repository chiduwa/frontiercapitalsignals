The Standard reports that Kenya is adopting a new strategy to leverage its cultural heritage. This initiative aims to unlock opportunities within the creative economy by transforming indigenous skills into commercially viable businesses.

This development indicates a policy focus on formalizing and commercializing traditional crafts and cultural expressions. For international investors, this could open new avenues in sectors such as cultural tourism, artisanal production, and creative industries, potentially leading to new venture opportunities and growth in related markets. The emphasis on creating commercially viable businesses suggests government backing for enterprise development in this space.

Investors should closely monitor the specific implementation of this strategy, including any support mechanisms or incentives for businesses. Observing how indigenous skills are integrated into new commercial models, and the early success of initiatives stemming from this strategy, will be crucial for identifying tangible investment potential in Kenya's evolving creative economy.
