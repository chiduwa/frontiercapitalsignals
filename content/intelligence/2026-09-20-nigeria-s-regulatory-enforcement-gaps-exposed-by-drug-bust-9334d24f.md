Nigeria's NDLEA recently intercepted N3.4 billion worth of illicit drugs and arrested a key trafficker. The suspect consistently evaded checkpoints using a forged police ID, facilitating long-term illicit consignment transport.

This incident underscores persistent governance and regulatory enforcement challenges, including potential corruption within state security apparatuses. Such vulnerabilities can heighten operational risks, particularly for logistics and supply chain integrity, impacting investor confidence in the broader business environment and rule of law.

Investors should monitor the government's response to security sector corruption and any subsequent reforms targeting law enforcement integrity and customs controls. Concrete actions demonstrating improved oversight and accountability will be crucial indicators of progress in de-risking the operational landscape.
