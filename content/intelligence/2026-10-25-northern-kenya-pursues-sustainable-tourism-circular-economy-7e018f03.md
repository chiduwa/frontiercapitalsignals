Capital FM Business reports that Northern Kenya is developing a circular economy focused on sustainable tourism. This region experiences prolonged droughts that strain local communities and wildlife, intensifying competition for increasingly scarce water. The report highlights the survival of an orphaned elephant within this challenging environment.

This focus on sustainable tourism and a circular economy in a drought-prone area suggests a strategic approach to economic resilience. It may signal future opportunities in eco-tourism, conservation infrastructure, or ventures that align with environmental sustainability and community engagement, particularly in managing water resources and wildlife conflicts.
