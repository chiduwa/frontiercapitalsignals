Nigeria's Federal Capital Territory Minister, Nyesom Wike, is actively mobilizing a 'Rainbow Coalition' to strengthen support for President Tinubu's reelection. This tactic is generating significant political agitation, particularly causing concern within states controlled by the ruling APC party, highlighting internal party dynamics.

This aggressive political maneuvering, while aimed at bolstering the presidency, introduces an element of intra-party friction and broader political uncertainty. Investors should closely track how this evolving dynamic impacts policy consistency, regulatory stability, and the overall governance environment, especially in states experiencing this heightened political pressure.

Monitor the reactions of APC state governors and other key political stakeholders as Wike's influence expands. Observe any resulting policy shifts or localized political instability in affected regions. The Tinubu administration's ability to effectively manage these internal power plays will be critical for maintaining a predictable and stable business climate.
