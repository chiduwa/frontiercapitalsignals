Afriex, a cross-border payments infrastructure company processing over $600 million annually, has partnered with Global Innovations Bank (GIB). This strategic relationship strengthens Afriex's core banking infrastructure, specifically for its B2B PaymentsAPI, enabling enhanced settlement and regulatory support.

This development is significant for investors focused on high-growth African markets. Faster settlement times and expanded regulatory compliance reduce friction for businesses conducting international trade and investment. It improves Afriex's operational efficiency and reliability, making it a more robust partner for companies moving capital across Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda. This underpins smoother financial flows essential for economic expansion.

We will monitor Afriex's payment volume growth, particularly its penetration and impact within our target African markets. Observe any new regional partnerships or integrations that leverage this strengthened infrastructure. Key metrics to watch include any reported improvements in cross-border transaction speed and cost for businesses operating in these dynamic economies.
