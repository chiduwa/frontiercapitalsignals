TechCabal reports Afreximbank has committed $118 million to Nigeria's creative economy. This significant funding aims to help businesses in the sector scale within the country.

This investment suggests a growing focus on the creative sector as a potential area for growth in Nigeria. However, TechCabal also highlights that access to finance alone may not fully unlock Africa's creative potential. Investors should note that cross-border trade barriers, weak intellectual property protection, and limited routes to market remain major obstacles. These challenges could impact the scalability and profitability of creative ventures across the continent.

Investors should monitor developments related to policy efforts addressing these identified obstacles, particularly cross-border trade facilitation and intellectual property reforms. The effectiveness of this $118 million commitment will likely depend on progress in these broader areas.
