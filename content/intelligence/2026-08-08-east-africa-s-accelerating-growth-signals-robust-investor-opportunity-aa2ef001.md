East Africa is projected to be the continent's fastest-growing region in 2025, with real GDP growth accelerating to an estimated 6.6 percent. This marks a significant jump from 4.3 percent in 2024, according to the African Development Bank.

This substantial acceleration in East African growth signals robust underlying economic momentum, presenting compelling opportunities for international investors. Stronger regional GDP growth typically translates to improved consumer purchasing power, expanding markets, and potentially higher returns across diverse sectors in key economies like Kenya and Uganda.

While the regional outlook is strong, monitor how the estimated $119 billion annual financing gap is addressed. This represents both a potential constraint and an investment opportunity for private capital. We also need to track individual country performance, given eight economies within the broader African context remain below 5 percent growth.
