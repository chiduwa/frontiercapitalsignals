Nigeria's downstream oil sector is gripped by an escalating conflict. Dangote's mega-refinery is confronting significant hurdles, including adverse court rulings and challenges in securing crude oil. This intensifies the struggle against established fuel importers, who fear a potential monopoly.

This struggle is critical for investors, signaling potential regulatory instability and competition risks within Nigeria's vital energy market. It forces a reassessment of the government's commitment to equitable market access versus favoritism for domestic champions. This impacts market entry, pricing structures, and project viability across the sector.

Monitor closely government interventions, especially evolving crude allocation policies and any new regulatory frameworks impacting fuel imports and refining. Further court decisions will also be crucial. These developments will define the operational environment for major industrial projects in Nigeria.
