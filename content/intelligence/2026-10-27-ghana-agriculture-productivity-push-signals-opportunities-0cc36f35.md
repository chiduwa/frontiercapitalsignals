MyJoyOnline Business reports that ISSER's 2025 State of the Ghana Economy Report indicated an encouraging but uneven agricultural recovery in 2025. The report also highlighted an industrial slowdown, pointing to broader development issues. To improve agricultural productivity, ISSER recommended expanding effective irrigation, mechanisation, and extension services, along with greater attention to crop yields.

This signals potential government or private sector focus on agricultural infrastructure and technology in Ghana. Investors in agriculture, agricultural technology, and related services, including irrigation systems and farm machinery, may find emerging opportunities. The emphasis on productivity gains suggests a strategic shift towards improving fundamental inputs in the sector.

Investors should monitor specific policy announcements or budget allocations related to these proposed agricultural enhancements. Watch for government tenders or private sector initiatives in irrigation, mechanisation, and agricultural extension. Any concrete steps towards implementing ISSER's recommendations could indicate actionable investment avenues.
