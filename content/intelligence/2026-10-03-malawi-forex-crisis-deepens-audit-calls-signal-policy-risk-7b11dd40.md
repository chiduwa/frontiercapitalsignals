Malawi's UTM Party leader, Dalitso Kabambe, has publicly called for an independent audit of the country's severe forex allocation system. This comes as businesses struggle to import essential goods, and public institutions face critical shortages, underscoring the depth of Malawi's ongoing foreign exchange crisis.

The persistent and worsening forex shortage presents material operational risks for international investors. Companies reliant on imports will face supply chain disruptions, increased costs, and potential difficulties repatriating profits. Lack of transparency in allocation further amplifies these risks, signaling potential governance challenges and currency volatility.

Investors should monitor the government's response to these audit calls and any subsequent actions from the Reserve Bank of Malawi regarding forex allocation and availability. Observe policy adjustments aimed at stabilizing the currency and addressing the root causes of the shortage. The impact on key import-dependent sectors will be a critical indicator.
