Recent data reveals a paradox across Africa. While there's strong support for free trade and open borders, a significant portion of the population expresses wariness towards immigrants. This sentiment persists despite only 13% being aware of the African Continental Free Trade Area, the very initiative designed to foster such integration.

For investors, this creates friction for AfCFTA's ambitious goals. The fear of immigration could slow the vital free movement of people component, impacting cross-border labor markets, skills transfer, and the efficiency of regional supply chains. Industries reliant on seamless talent mobility may face unexpected regulatory or social hurdles.

Watch closely for how regional governments address this disconnect. Monitor policy responses aimed at reconciling open border rhetoric with public concerns. Key indicators will include official stances on immigration, public awareness campaigns for AfCFTA, and specific sector-level impacts on labor sourcing and workforce integration across member states.
