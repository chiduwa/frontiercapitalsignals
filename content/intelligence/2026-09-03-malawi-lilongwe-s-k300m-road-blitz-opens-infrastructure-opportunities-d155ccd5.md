Lilongwe City Council has committed K300 million to rehabilitate roads across all 12 constituencies, allocating K10 million to each under the Constituency Development Fund. This ambitious citywide program will be carried out in phases, as confirmed by CEO Clement Stambuli.

This initiative signals a clear government commitment to improving urban infrastructure in Malawi, creating direct opportunities for local and regional construction and civil engineering firms. Improved road networks will enhance logistics, reduce transport costs, and stimulate broader economic activity within Lilongwe, benefiting various sectors reliant on efficient movement of goods and people.

Investors should monitor the tender processes and contract awards as these rehabilitation phases commence. Watch for the timely completion and quality of these projects, and assess if this K300 million investment is a precursor to larger, sustained infrastructure spending in Malawi, particularly with potential for increased development partner funding.
