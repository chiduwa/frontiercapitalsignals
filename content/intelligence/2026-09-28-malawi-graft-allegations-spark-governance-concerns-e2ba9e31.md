Dr. Dalitso Kabambe has issued an open letter making serious, yet unevidenced, allegations of high-level corruption, money laundering, and human trafficking in Malawi. His claims implicate cabinet ministers and the judiciary, but he has not presented specific cases or proof. Local press is urging him to provide substantiation.

These unproven allegations inject significant uncertainty into Malawi's governance landscape. Even without evidence, such public accusations against key institutions signal potential for political instability, erode investor confidence, and suggest a challenging operating environment. This perceived risk directly impacts investment decisions and capital flow.

Monitor whether Kabambe follows through with evidence and how Malawian authorities, including the Anti-Corruption Bureau and judiciary, respond. Any official investigations, the government's stance on these claims, and broader anti-corruption reforms will be critical indicators for assessing future political and regulatory stability.
