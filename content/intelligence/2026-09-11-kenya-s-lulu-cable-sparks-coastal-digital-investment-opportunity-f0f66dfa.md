INDOI and its partners are developing the LuLu Coastal Cable, a 500km fibre-optic system connecting Mombasa and Lamu. This extends high-capacity digital infrastructure along Kenya's Indian Ocean coastline, targeting service by Q2 2028. It will include five strategic landing points.

This project signals significant growth in Kenya's digital economy. Enhanced coastal connectivity will boost e-commerce, remote work, and data centre opportunities. Locations like Vipingo, specifically highlighted, could become magnets for technology and logistics investments, driving real estate and related service sector demand.

Monitor the project's financing and regulatory approvals. Watch for specific announcements regarding data centre or tech park developments in the coastal regions, particularly around Vipingo. Investor interest in logistics, digital services, and real estate within these corridor cities will likely increase.
