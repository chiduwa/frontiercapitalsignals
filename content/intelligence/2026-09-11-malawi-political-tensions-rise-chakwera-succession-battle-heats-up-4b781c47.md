A new, shadowy pressure group named 'Mbembembe' has emerged within Malawi's opposition Congress Party (MCP). Their stated mission is to prevent former president Lazarus Chakwera from amending party rules to prolong his leadership, signaling a significant internal power struggle.

For investors, this internal party strife within a major political entity introduces a layer of political risk. It suggests potential for increased volatility and uncertainty regarding future policy direction or regulatory consistency, particularly as the country approaches its next election cycle. Political stability is crucial for sustained investor confidence and long-term planning.

We are watching the 'Mbembembe' group's influence and actions closely, as well as the MCP leadership's response to this challenge. Any signs of broader political fracturing or shifts in alliances within Malawi's political landscape will be key indicators for assessing national stability and its implications for the investment climate.
