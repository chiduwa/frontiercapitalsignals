TechCabal reports that Fintan Byrne, CEO of CR2, stated at AFIS 2026 in Luanda that African banks need to rethink their digital transformation approach. He suggested banks must move beyond simply launching digital apps and instead invest in stronger banking infrastructure.

This call for a strategic shift matters to investors as it highlights evolving priorities within the African financial sector. Strengthening core infrastructure is seen as crucial for banks to cut operational costs, significantly improve customer experience, and effectively compete with emerging fintech companies. This perspective could signal opportunities for technology providers focusing on back-end banking solutions.

Investors should monitor how African banks respond to this advice, particularly regarding their capital allocation for infrastructure projects versus consumer-facing apps. The emphasis on cost reduction and enhanced customer experience indicates potential shifts in market dynamics and competitive strategies across the continent's banking landscape.
