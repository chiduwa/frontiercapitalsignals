A parliamentary inquiry in Malawi has revealed critical deficiencies in state capacity. The search for a missing aircraft was severely hampered by insufficient police manpower to cover the vast Chikangawa forest, according to testimony from a former Deputy Commissioner of Police.

This incident underscores potential systemic weaknesses in Malawi's emergency response capabilities and resource allocation. For international investors, it flags concerns about the government's operational efficiency, crisis management preparedness, and the broader ability to support complex logistical or security needs crucial for large-scale projects, particularly in remote regions. It reflects potential governance gaps.
