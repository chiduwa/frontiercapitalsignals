Premium Times reports that Oando, an energy company, has announced the final terms for a N203.8 billion rights issue. The company is offering approximately 4.1 billion ordinary shares, each priced at N50. This offering is on a basis of one new ordinary share for every two ordinary shares held by eligible shareholders.

This rights issue presents a significant capital raise for Oando. For eligible shareholders, it offers an opportunity to maintain their proportional ownership. For other investors, the N50 per share price and the 1-for-2 basis provide clear terms for evaluating the company's current capital needs and valuation, potentially signaling future strategic moves.

Investors should monitor the market's reaction to these terms and the eventual uptake rate of the new shares. Premium Times' report does not specify the intended use of the capital or the timeline for the offering, which are key details to watch for when assessing the long-term impact on Oando's operational performance and shareholder value.
