BusinessDay reports that the Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC) has approved the disbursement of N30 billion. This amount represents the third tranche of the meter acquisition fund, which is allocated to Electricity Distribution Companies (DisCos).

This regulatory action indicates a continued commitment to addressing metering gaps within Nigeria's electricity sector. For investors in infrastructure development, power generation, or associated services, this signals ongoing opportunities and potential expansion in the market for metering solutions and related technologies. It could also contribute to improving revenue assurance for DisCos, potentially enhancing their financial viability.

Investors should closely monitor the effectiveness of this fund in driving actual meter deployment and its impact on the operational performance of DisCos. While the source is thin on prior outcomes, future tranches or related policy initiatives would confirm sustained opportunities and the long-term direction of regulatory support for sector improvement.
