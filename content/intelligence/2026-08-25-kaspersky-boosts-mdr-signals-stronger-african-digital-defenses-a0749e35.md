Kaspersky has rolled out a significant update to its Managed Detection and Response (MDR) service. This enhancement introduces stronger monitoring, threat detection, and investigation capabilities, providing security teams with deeper context for faster, more confident decision-making against cyber threats.

This development is crucial for investors in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda. Improved cybersecurity frameworks directly reduce operational risk for businesses operating in these rapidly digitizing economies. Robust protection for data and digital assets fosters greater confidence for investment in critical sectors like finance, e-commerce, and tech startups, accelerating digital transformation.
