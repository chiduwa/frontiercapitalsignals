Malawi's foreign exchange reserves registered an 11% increase, reaching $616.3 million by June 2026, as per the Reserve Bank of Malawi's latest review. This marks a modest rise from $596.5 million.

Despite this uptick, an economist warns the improvement is nowhere near enough to ease the persistent forex shortages plaguing businesses. This signals continued operational challenges for import-reliant sectors and potential hurdles for capital repatriation, dampening immediate investor confidence. The underlying structural issues driving these shortages have not been resolved.

Investors should closely monitor the RBM's policy responses and any significant external inflows, such as donor support or improved export performance. We require sustained, substantial increases in reserves to materially ease market pressure before considering the situation significantly improved for foreign investment.
