Omoyele Sowore has returned to Nigeria, announcing his intention to launch a 2027 presidential campaign. He is expected in Lagos before proceeding to Abuja to formalize his political re-entry.

This move signals an early start to the 2027 electoral cycle, potentially increasing political noise sooner than anticipated. Early and prolonged campaign periods in Nigeria can introduce uncertainty, impacting investor sentiment and policy focus. It suggests a potentially competitive landscape forming, requiring close monitoring.

Investors should monitor the nature and breadth of Sowore's support base, as well as reactions from established political parties. Early coalition-building or signs of significant opposition unity will be critical indicators of the election's potential impact on policy stability and economic direction.
