Trevor Manuel, South Africa's former finance minister, recently stated that the nation's democracy requires substantial repair work. He highlighted deep-seated political and governance issues affecting the continent's largest economy.

Manuel's candid assessment, coming from such an influential figure, directly flags escalating political and governance risks in South Africa. For investors, this translates into potential policy uncertainty, regulatory instability, and increased operational challenges, impacting long-term capital allocation decisions across the region.

Watch closely for policy responses from the ANC ahead of next year's elections, particularly any reforms addressing corruption or state-owned enterprise inefficiency. Assess the impact of these democratic challenges on broader investor sentiment and economic stability, looking for potential contagion effects in key African markets.
