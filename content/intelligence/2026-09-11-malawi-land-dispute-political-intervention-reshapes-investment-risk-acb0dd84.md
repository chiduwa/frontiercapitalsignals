President Mutharika has intervened in a land dispute in Thyolo, ordering that part of a contested estate be shared among 836 farmers rather than sold for development. This decision, announced by Minister of Lands Chimwemwe Chipungu, effectively saves hundreds of farmers from losing their livelihoods.

This presidential action significantly increases political risk for investors in Malawi, particularly those involved in agriculture, real estate, or any sector requiring large-scale land acquisition. It signals a willingness to override commercial land deals for social stability, creating uncertainty around land tenure security and potential for expropriation when local communities are impacted.

Investors should closely monitor the implementation of this directive and any subsequent government statements on land policy or investment guidelines. Future large-scale land transactions, especially those with perceived community conflict, will be key indicators of the ongoing risk landscape and the government's stance on balancing development with social equity.
