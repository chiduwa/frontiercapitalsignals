Jared Kushner is leading efforts to revive the Abraham Accords, proposing a significant injection of private capital to underpin new agreements. This initiative directly involves Morocco, which previously secured US recognition of its sovereignty over Western Sahara as part of the original accords. The current focus is on delivering tangible economic benefits to participants.

The proposed infusion of private capital into the Abraham Accords framework signals new investment opportunities and accelerated regional economic integration. While initially centered on the MENA region, this capital deployment could establish precedents for deal structures, sector focus, and risk appetite relevant to frontier markets across Africa. It indicates a clear push for stability via economic incentives.
