Zambian President Hakainde Hichilema, recently re-elected for his second term, pledges to expand existing government programs, particularly in mining and education. His focus also includes enhancing state legitimacy. However, his administration faces a deeply divided electorate and lingering suspicions of vote-rigging from the recent election.

For investors, Hichilema's commitment to program continuity, especially in the critical mining sector, signals policy stability and potential for sustained growth. This predictability can be attractive. Conversely, the political fragmentation and questions surrounding electoral integrity introduce elevated political risk, potentially impacting governance stability and the social license for investment.

Moving forward, closely monitor the practical implementation of key mining and education policies. Watch for any escalation of political tensions or challenges to Hichilema's mandate from opposition forces. Assess how the government actively addresses the legitimacy concerns, as this will be crucial for maintaining investor confidence and long-term stability.
