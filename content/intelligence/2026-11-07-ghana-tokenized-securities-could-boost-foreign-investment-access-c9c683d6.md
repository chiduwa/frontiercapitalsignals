MyJoyOnline Business reports that the CEO of WeWire, Ebenezer Ghanney, suggests tokenising Ghana's government securities to attract more foreign investors and expand government funding. He believes this approach could reduce traditional barriers for international investors seeking to access Ghana's financial market.

Tokenising government bonds and Treasury instruments could simplify foreign participation, potentially increasing liquidity and demand for Ghana's sovereign debt. This development signals a potential shift towards greater market accessibility, which could broaden the pool of investors for the government's financial instruments.

The WeWire CEO identified government securities as relatively easy to tokenise due to existing market structures and custody arrangements. Investors should monitor official discussions or initiatives related to implementing such digital asset strategies, as they could create new investment avenues and enhance market efficiency for Ghana.
