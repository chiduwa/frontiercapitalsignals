Zomba City South legislator James Mpunga warned Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) supporters against internal divisions, stating such conflicts could weaken the party and hinder development efforts in his constituency. He urged unity to strengthen the DPP and advance local development.

Persistent internal strife within a major political party, even in opposition, signals potential political instability. This can disrupt policy consensus, delay legislative processes, and undermine investor confidence by creating an uncertain operating environment. Investors prioritize stable political landscapes for long-term growth.

Monitor further rhetoric from DPP leadership and the party's cohesion ahead of future elections. Observe any spillover effects into broader political discourse or legislative actions. A unified political front, even in opposition, often signals a more predictable policy outlook.
