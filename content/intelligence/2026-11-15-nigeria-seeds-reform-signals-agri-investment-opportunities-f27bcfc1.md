Premium Times reports that Nigeria's seed council (NASC) and PIND have partnered to reform the country's seed system. This collaboration seeks to combine NASC’s regulatory expertise with PIND’s experience in market systems development.

This partnership is intended to expand access to certified seeds and strengthen local seed enterprises. For investors, this signals potential growth opportunities within Nigeria's agricultural sector, particularly for businesses involved in seed production, distribution, or agricultural inputs. The initiative also explicitly aims to attract investment, especially in the Niger Delta region, indicating a focused effort to channel capital into specific areas of the value chain and geography.

Investors should monitor the implementation details of this reform. Key indicators will be progress in expanding certified seed access and the tangible strengthening of local seed enterprises. Observing specific initiatives designed to attract investment in the Niger Delta will also be crucial for identifying actionable opportunities. The extent of regulatory and market systems improvements will determine the impact on the agricultural investment landscape.
