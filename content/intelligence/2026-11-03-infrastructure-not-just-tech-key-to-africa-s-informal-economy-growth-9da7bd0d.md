TechCabal reports that Africa Finance Corporation (AFC) CEO Samaila Zubairu recently stated at AFIS in Luanda that bringing informal businesses online has limited impact without reliable power, storage, and transport. He argued that technology alone cannot fix Africa's informal economy if foundational infrastructure is lacking.

This perspective highlights a critical investment theme for the region. It suggests that opportunities lie not just in digital solutions but also in the underlying physical infrastructure that enables broader economic participation and growth. Investors should note the emphasis on power, logistics, and storage as essential components for sustainable development.

Looking ahead, investors should monitor progress on major infrastructure initiatives, specifically the Lobito Corridor. TechCabal reports Zubairu sees this project as a chance to build industries focused on processing minerals and moving farm produce within the region. Developments in such corridors could unlock significant investment in associated real sector industries.
