MyJoyOnline Business reports that the Ghana Statistical Service (GSS) has released new figures in its "Ghana in Numbers, 2025" publication. These figures indicate that approximately 12 million Ghanaians will be aged between 15 and 35 years in 2025. This represents roughly one in every three people and marks the largest youth population in Ghana's history.

This significant demographic shift presents both opportunities and challenges for investors. A large youth cohort typically signals a growing consumer base and a potentially expanding labor pool. Businesses targeting younger demographics, such as education, technology, consumer goods, and entertainment, may find an increasing market. Conversely, the sheer size of this group could strain public services and infrastructure if not adequately managed.

Investors should monitor subsequent GSS publications for more detailed statistics on this demographic, particularly concerning labor force participation and consumption patterns. Understanding the specific skills and economic engagement of this youth population will be crucial for identifying key growth sectors and potential areas of investment.
