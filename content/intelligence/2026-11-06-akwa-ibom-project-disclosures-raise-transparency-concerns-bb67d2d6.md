Premium Times reports that Nigeria's President Tinubu is set to commission multiple multi-billion naira projects in Akwa Ibom. Despite repeated budgetary allocations running into billions of naira for these eight projects, the Akwa Ibom government has not publicly disclosed their contract sums or procurement records.

This situation highlights potential transparency issues in public infrastructure spending within Nigeria. For investors considering involvement in government-backed projects or public-private partnerships, the lack of public disclosure on contract details and procurement records could signal heightened operational and reputational risks. It may also complicate due diligence efforts for potential investments.

Investors should monitor for any future disclosures regarding these projects' financial details or any changes in the Akwa Ibom government's procurement transparency policies. Observing whether similar projects in other Nigerian states face comparable disclosure challenges will also be crucial for assessing the broader investment landscape.
