Malawi's Department of Disaster Management Affairs, DoDMA, has activated early drought action across 23 districts. This move follows updated weather forecasts indicating a high risk of dry conditions across the country.

This signals potential disruptions to agricultural output, especially for rain-fed staple crops like maize, which are vital for food security and a significant contributor to GDP. Investors should anticipate inflationary pressures on food prices and increased government expenditure on humanitarian aid. Firms in consumer goods, logistics, and financial sectors may face headwinds.

Going forward, closely track crop assessment reports, government fiscal responses, and inflation data in the coming months. Also, monitor international aid commitments, as these will influence forex reserves and Malawi's capacity to import essential goods.
