FCB Nyasa Big Bullets, a prominent Malawian football club, was fined MK3.35 million by the Super League of Malawi (SULOM) Disciplinary Committee for misconduct during a match on July 19, 2026. This disciplinary action follows an incident during their FDH Bank Premiership game against Ekhaya FC.

This incident, while in sports, signals an active regulatory environment. Strict enforcement by bodies like SULOM reflects a broader governmental push for compliance and rule adherence across sectors.
