Nyasa Times reports that Kenyan singer Bien performed at the fourth edition of Live at Kumbali on Saturday night, held at Kumbali Castle. The event in Lilongwe saw an audience, already engaged for hours, greet the Pan-African star with significant anticipation.

This event signals consumer demand for live entertainment and cultural experiences in Malawi, particularly for artists with Pan-African appeal. A successful event like this, attracting a large and engaged audience, indicates a potentially growing market for the entertainment and leisure sector. It also highlights the viability of established venues like Kumbali Castle for hosting such events.

Investors should observe trends in Malawi's live entertainment and hospitality sectors for sustained growth in consumer spending on cultural events. Future editions of Live at Kumbali and similar events could provide further indicators of market appetite and potential opportunities for investment in venues, event management, or related tourism infrastructure.
