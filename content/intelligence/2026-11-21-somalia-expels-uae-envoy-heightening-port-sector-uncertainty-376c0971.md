Africa Briefing reports that Somalia has ordered the United Arab Emirates ambassador to leave within 72 hours. Somalia accuses Abu Dhabi of meddling in its internal affairs and backing regional authorities that oppose the federal government. The United Arab Emirates has rejected these accusations of political interference.

This diplomatic dispute, which Africa Briefing notes centres on strategic ports and regional authority, creates uncertainty regarding the stability of governance and international relations in Somalia. For investors, such tensions can signal increased political risk and potential disruptions to infrastructure projects or trade flows tied to these strategic assets.

Investors should monitor how this dispute evolves, particularly regarding its impact on existing or planned port developments and regional political dynamics. The situation could influence the operating environment for businesses with interests in Somalia's maritime sector or those requiring stable regional governance.
