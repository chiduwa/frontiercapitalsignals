A Malawian court sentenced Juma Fabiano, 49, to three years in prison for selling dog meat in Kasungu. He was found guilty by the Chief Resident Magistrate's court despite admitting to the charge.

This event, while seemingly minor, highlights the Malawian judiciary's active enforcement of consumer protection and public health regulations. For investors, it underscores the importance of understanding local laws, cultural norms, and the government's capacity to prosecute breaches, even for less common offenses.
