The High Street Journal reports that Ghana’s GSA is repositioning its certification drive, shifting its focus towards business competitiveness. This new strategy aims to leverage certification to improve productivity, reduce operating costs, strengthen quality management, and expand market access for Ghanaian enterprises.

This policy development could signal a more favorable operational environment for businesses in Ghana. Investors may find opportunities in sectors where enterprises can significantly benefit from enhanced efficiency, lower costs, and broader market reach due to improved quality standards and certification. The push for expanded market access, particularly, could open new avenues for growth.

Investors should closely observe the execution and impact of this repositioned certification strategy. Monitoring which specific Ghanaian enterprises or sectors demonstrate tangible improvements in productivity, cost efficiency, and export capabilities will be crucial for identifying potential investment opportunities.
