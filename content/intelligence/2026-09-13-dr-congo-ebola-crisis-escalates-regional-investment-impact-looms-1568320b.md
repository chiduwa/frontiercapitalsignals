DR Congo's Ebola crisis has reached an unprecedented scale. Confirmed cases of the Bundibugyo virus now exceed 7,000, with over 3,300 fatalities, making this the deadliest outbreak on record. This represents a severe and ongoing public health emergency in the country.

While centered in DR Congo, this escalating epidemic carries regional investment implications. A prolonged health crisis destabilizes supply chains, impacts labour mobility, and drains resources from other sectors in the DRC, affecting cross-border trade and regional logistics. Neighboring economies, particularly Uganda, face potential spillover risks, impacting investor sentiment and operational continuity.

Investors should monitor containment progress within DR Congo and the efficacy of public health interventions. Watch for any signs of cross-border transmission into our focus markets, especially Uganda and Malawi given the source. Also, track the regional humanitarian and economic response, noting potential disruptions to trade corridors and resource allocation.
