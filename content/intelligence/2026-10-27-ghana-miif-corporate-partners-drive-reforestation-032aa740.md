MyJoyOnline Business reports that the Minerals Income Investment Fund (MIIF) has partnered with the Forestry Commission of Ghana to rehabilitate and restore 50 acres of degraded land. This initiative is located within the Subri River Forest Reserve. Zijin Golden Ridge Limited, Ghana International Bank, and three other financial institutions are providing support for this project, which forms part of their corporate social responsibility on Environmental, Social, and related aspects.

This partnership demonstrates a concerted effort by a state fund, private mining interests, and the financial sector towards environmental restoration and corporate social responsibility (CSR) in Ghana. Such initiatives can signal an increasing emphasis on Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) factors within the operating environment. Investors should note this collaborative approach, which may indicate a stable regulatory landscape that encourages corporate responsibility.
