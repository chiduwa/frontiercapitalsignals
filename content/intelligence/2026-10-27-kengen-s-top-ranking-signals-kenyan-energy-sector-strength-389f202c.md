Capital FM Business reports that Kenya Electricity Generating Company (KenGen) has been named among Africa’s top 25 energy companies. This recognition came in a 2026 ranking published by Pan African Voice.

This highlights KenGen's significant positioning within the African energy sector. For investors, it signals potential in Kenya's power generation market and underscores the operational capabilities of key players. This could enhance confidence in the country's broader energy infrastructure investment prospects.

Investors should monitor further insights into the specific criteria Pan African Voice used for its 2026 ranking. Also, watch for any subsequent announcements from KenGen or broader market developments that reinforce this positive assessment of Kenya’s energy sector.
