Capital FM Business reports that Kenya's National Infrastructure Fund (NIF) is in discussions with the Capital Markets Authority (CMA) and the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE). These talks aim to create a new investment.

This initiative suggests a potential new avenue for private investors to participate in infrastructure projects within Kenya. If successful, it could broaden the range of available investment products on the Nairobi Securities Exchange. The collaboration between the NIF, CMA, and NSE points towards efforts to deepen capital markets and channel domestic and international capital into key development areas.

Investors should monitor further announcements regarding the nature and structure of this proposed new investment. Details on its specific focus, asset class, and regulatory framework will be crucial for assessing its viability and potential returns. The progress of these discussions between NIF, CMA, and NSE bears watching for specific investment opportunities.
