The Independent (Uganda) reports that Qualcomm Technologies, Inc. awarded Qualikeeper Investments Ltd. the 2026 Qualcomm for Good Social Impact Fund. This recognition is for Qualikeeper's affordable edge-AIoT livestock intelligence platform, StocKeeper. The announcement occurred during the fourth annual Qualcomm Make in Africa Startup event's first in-person Finale.

This development signals investor interest in African livestock technology solutions, particularly those leveraging AIoT for intelligence platforms. Qualikeeper's win highlights a growing trend of venture capital and corporate support flowing into tech-driven agricultural innovations across the continent. Such funding can accelerate market penetration and validate business models in the underserved livestock sector.

Investors should watch for further details on Qualikeeper's operational footprint and expansion plans, which are not specified in the report. The success of StocKeeper could indicate broader opportunities for similar affordable edge-AIoT solutions in livestock management across Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda. It also points to the continued strategic involvement of global tech giants like Qualcomm in nurturing African startup ecosystems.
