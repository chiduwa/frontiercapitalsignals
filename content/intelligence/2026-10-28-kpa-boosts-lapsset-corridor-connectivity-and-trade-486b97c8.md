The Standard reports that the Kenya Ports Authority (KPA) is set to establish a dry port in Moyale. Concurrently, KPA also plans to launch a ferry service on Lake Turkana. These initiatives signal a strategic focus on improving regional logistics infrastructure.

These developments are aimed at enhancing connectivity within the region. They are also intended to open the Lamu Port South Sudan Ethiopia Transport (Lapsset) corridor to trade. For investors, this suggests potential for increased trade flows and opportunities in logistics, warehousing, and transport services along this vital economic pathway.

Investors should monitor the progress and specifics of these infrastructure projects. Details regarding operational timelines, freight volumes, and the broader impact on cross-border trade will be crucial for evaluating direct and indirect investment opportunities in the Lapsset corridor.
