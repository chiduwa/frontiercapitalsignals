Nigeria and Ghana's MTN operations were pivotal in the first half of 2026, delivering two-thirds of the cash repatriated to MTN Group. This strong performance allowed the telecoms giant to proceed with a $6.24 million share buyback, even as its South African division faced challenges.

This demonstrates the robust cash generation capabilities and strategic importance of West African markets for MTN. For investors, it underscores where the true resilience and value creation lie within MTN's diverse portfolio, offsetting weakness in more established regions. It signals that these markets are not just growth engines but significant contributors to group liquidity.

Monitor the sustained cash flow generation from Nigeria and Ghana, and MTN's subsequent capital allocation decisions. Investors should also watch for any regulatory or economic shifts in these key markets that could impact future repatriations and overall profitability. The telco sector's broader performance in West Africa bears close watching.
