Despite ongoing political and security disputes, particularly regarding ECOWAS membership and sovereignty, Côte d’Ivoire and the landlocked Alliance of Sahel States (AES) are maintaining vital trade relationships. Geographic necessity and commercial imperatives are forcing both sides to sustain crucial business activities.

This dynamic underscores the enduring resilience of trade routes and economic imperatives over political division in West Africa. For investors, it signals that critical supply chains and market access within the region remain active, reducing overall market fragmentation risk in the face of political volatility.

Watch for continued stability in cross-border trade volumes and any policy shifts impacting logistics or tariffs. Further, observe infrastructure developments aimed at strengthening these trade corridors, as these will directly influence efficiency and investment attractiveness in regional logistics and related sectors.
