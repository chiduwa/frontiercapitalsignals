Malawi's public hospitals are experiencing critical drug shortages. Stolen government medicines, including essential antibiotics, are being diverted and sold on the open market, with some even reaching Tanzania. The Parliamentary Committee on Health confirms this widespread theft.

For investors, this signals substantial governance and regulatory risks. Such large-scale theft exposes deep corruption and weak supply chain integrity within the public sector. It directly impacts healthcare stability, workforce productivity, and overall social confidence, posing a long-term drag on economic growth and foreign direct investment.

Monitor the Malawian government's response, including specific measures to secure drug supply chains and prosecute those responsible. Observe any broader anti-corruption drives or reforms in public procurement. These actions will indicate the government's commitment to addressing systemic issues impacting investor confidence.
