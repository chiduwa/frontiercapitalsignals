MyJoyOnline Business reports that Petra Master Trust's pension schemes recorded returns of up to 15.63%. Yvonne Buliga, Head of Portfolio Management and Operations, stated at the Petra Master Trust Scheme Annual General Meeting that the Petra Opportunity Pension Scheme returned 15.63%.

These reported returns exceeded inflation, indicating the schemes successfully preserved contributors’ purchasing power. Such performance can signal effective fund management or a robust local investment environment, offering a benchmark for other financial products in Ghana.

Investors should monitor broader trends in Ghanaian pension fund performance and how these returns compare to other asset classes. Further details on the specific assets driving these returns could provide additional insight into market opportunities.
