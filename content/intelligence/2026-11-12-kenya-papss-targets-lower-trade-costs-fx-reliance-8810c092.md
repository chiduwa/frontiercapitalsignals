Capital FM Business reports that PAPSS, described as the Pan-African Payment and Settlement initiative, is focusing on local currencies to unlock Kenya's Africa trade. This aims to help Kenyan businesses cut cross-border payment costs and reduce their reliance on foreign currencies when trading with other African markets.

This development suggests potential for improved operational efficiencies and reduced foreign exchange risks for Kenyan companies engaged in intra-African trade. For investors, this could translate into stronger financial performance for businesses that extensively trade regionally, as their cost structures may become more predictable.

Investors should observe the implementation and adoption of this payment system by Kenyan businesses and its actual impact on cross-border transaction costs. Further details on the scope and functionality of the Pan-African Payment and Settlement initiative will be crucial for assessing its full market implications.
