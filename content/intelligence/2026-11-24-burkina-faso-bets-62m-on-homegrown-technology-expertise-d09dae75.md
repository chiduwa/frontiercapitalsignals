Africa Briefing reports Burkina Faso has opened a CFA37.37bn ($62.3 million) technological academy. This significant investment aims to train domestic engineers and scientists, with President Ibrahim Traoré hoping to reduce the country's dependence on foreign technology.

This initiative involves eleven engineering disciplines, specifically targeting strategic industries. The investment complements Burkina Faso's broader ambitions in mineral processing. For investors, this signals a government commitment to developing local technical capacity and adding value to natural resources within the country.

Investors should monitor how effectively this academy develops a skilled workforce and its impact on innovation and efficiency in key sectors, particularly mineral processing. Observing the contribution of these trained professionals to reducing technological imports will be important.
