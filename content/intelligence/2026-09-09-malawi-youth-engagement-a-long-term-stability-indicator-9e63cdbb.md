UK-based strategist Rhodrick Junaid Kalumpha recently urged Malawian youth, community organisers, and traditional leaders to actively participate in nation-building. He emphasised that the country's future should not be left to others.

This call for increased youth civic engagement is a positive signal for long-term stability and economic predictability. Active youth involvement often translates to more robust policy development, reduced political risk, and a more engaged, skilled workforce over time. It can also foster an environment conducive to entrepreneurship and innovation, creating new market opportunities.

Investors should monitor specific government initiatives supporting youth participation in governance and economic sectors. Watch for new policy frameworks encouraging youth entrepreneurship, skill development programs, and evidence of youth-led community projects gaining traction. These developments signal a maturing political and economic landscape.
