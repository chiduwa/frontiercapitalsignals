MyJoyOnline Business reports a two-day investment summit is scheduled for November 16-17 at the Kempinski Hotel Gold Coast City in Accra. This event aims to bring together local and international stakeholders.

The summit's focus is on exploring investment opportunities, facilitating business partnerships, and connecting investors with projects across all of Ghana's 16 regions. This suggests a concerted effort to attract foreign capital and develop diverse sectors within the country. It signals potential for new deal flow and strategic alliances.

Investors should monitor official announcements regarding the specific sectors or projects highlighted at the summit. Details on the participating stakeholders and outcomes of any facilitated partnerships will provide further clarity on emerging investment trends in Ghana.
