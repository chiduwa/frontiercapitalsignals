TechCabal reports that Ericsson views enterprise customers as the future growth driver for 5G in South Africa. Specifically, Ericsson sees mining, ports, and utilities as crucial markets for private 5G networks. This shift reflects South African mobile operators' strategy to move beyond consumer data and generate new enterprise revenue.

This development signals a potential new revenue stream for telecommunications companies, diversifying their income beyond saturated consumer markets. The focus on private 5G networks in industrial sectors suggests significant infrastructure investment opportunities. While specific to South Africa, similar opportunities could emerge in other African markets like Ghana, Nigeria, and Kenya which also have substantial mining, port, and utility operations.

Investors should monitor the uptake and success of private 5G networks in these identified sectors within South Africa. Watch for any indications of similar strategies being adopted by mobile operators or technology providers in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda, particularly in their respective industrial and infrastructure segments. This trend could indicate broader investment potential in African telecom infrastructure and specialized technology services.
