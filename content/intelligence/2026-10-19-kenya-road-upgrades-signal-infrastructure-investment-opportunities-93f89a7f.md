Capital FM Business reports that President William Ruto pledged a 12-kilometre road upgrade in Kapsabet, Kenya. The report also states that plans were announced to expand the Eldoret-Kapsabet-Chavakali road into a four-lane highway.

These commitments signal potential opportunities within Kenya's infrastructure sector. Road development projects typically drive demand for construction services, materials, and related logistics. Enhanced connectivity could also impact real estate values and commercial activity along the upgraded and expanded routes.

Investors should monitor further details on funding allocation, project timelines, and tendering processes for both the Kapsabet road upgrades and the four-lane highway expansion. Specific government announcements regarding these projects will provide clarity on execution and potential entry points.
