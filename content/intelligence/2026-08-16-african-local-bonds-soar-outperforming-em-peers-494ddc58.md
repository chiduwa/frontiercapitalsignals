African local-currency government bonds have significantly outperformed major emerging market debt benchmarks. An MCB Investment Management index for these bonds gained 5.9 percent in dollar terms this year, driven by strong double-digit yields and favorable currency movements. Notably, Zambia delivered an impressive 32.3 percent dollar return.

This performance highlights a compelling opportunity for international investors seeking diversification and higher returns beyond traditional EM debt. The substantial domestic debt issuance, nearing $500 billion annually, indicates deep and increasingly liquid markets. Such strong, broad-based outperformance signals a maturing asset class with attractive yield-to-risk profiles.

Monitor central bank policies across key African economies, particularly actions related to interest rates and inflation management. Pay close attention to currency stability and any policy shifts impacting capital flows. Continued robust performance will depend on sustained economic reforms and a favorable global risk environment supporting investor confidence in these high-yielding markets.
