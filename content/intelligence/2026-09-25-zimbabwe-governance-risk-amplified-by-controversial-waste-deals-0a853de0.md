Dilesh Nguwaya, a Zimbabwean businessman, is again central to a major public contract, this time for Bulawayo’s waste management. This follows his controversial involvement in a multi-million-dollar Covid-19 medical supply scandal and previous Harare waste management deals.

His repeated engagement in significant public sector deals, despite a history of controversy, highlights persistent governance challenges in Zimbabwe’s procurement landscape. For investors, this signals heightened reputational and operational risks associated with public-private partnerships, particularly in infrastructure and services. Such patterns can erode trust, impact project stability, and potentially expose foreign entities to legal or political scrutiny.

Monitor the transparency and due diligence standards applied to Nguwaya’s Bulawayo waste deal, especially concerning financing structures and contract terms. Observe how the Zimbabwean government addresses public concerns regarding procurement practices, as this will indicate broader commitment to improving the investment climate.
