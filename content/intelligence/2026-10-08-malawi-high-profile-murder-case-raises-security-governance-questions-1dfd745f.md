Malawi authorities arrested a Fountain of Victory church pastor in connection with the alleged murder of an FDH Bank employee. The victim, Tathokoza Salimu, was found dead in a car with a neck wound. Police call logs reportedly place the pastor with Salimu on the day she died, leading to his detention in Dowa.

While a criminal matter, this incident touches on perceptions of security and rule of law in Malawi, critical factors for foreign investors. The involvement of a major local bank employee and a religious figure amplifies scrutiny, potentially raising concerns about personal safety for expatriate staff and local talent. It also presents a negative, albeit indirect, reputational overhang for FDH Bank.
