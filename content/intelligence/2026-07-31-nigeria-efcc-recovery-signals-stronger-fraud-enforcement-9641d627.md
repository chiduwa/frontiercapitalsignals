The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) has recovered an additional ₦42 million for a businessman defrauded in a cement supply deal. This builds on an earlier recovery of ₦158 million in a separate but similar cement fraud case, demonstrating the agency's active pursuit of financial crime.

This persistent recovery action by the EFCC is a positive signal for international investors. It underscores Nigeria's commitment to tackling commercial fraud and enhances confidence in the enforceability of contracts and the protection of assets, crucial for mitigating operational risks in key sectors like construction and trade.

Investors should monitor the EFCC's sustained recovery rates and the efficiency of legal processes for fraud resolution. Track broader government initiatives aimed at strengthening commercial law and improving judicial effectiveness, as these will further de-risk investments in the Nigerian market.
