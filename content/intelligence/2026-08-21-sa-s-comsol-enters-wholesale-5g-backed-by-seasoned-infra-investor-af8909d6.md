Comsol, a long-standing South African fixed wireless and private network operator, is pivoting into the wholesale 5G home broadband market. This strategic expansion is supported by new shareholders, including Platform Investment Partners, known for its extensive fibre investments across four geographies.

This signals a significant confidence boost for the African telecom infrastructure sector. Platform Investment Partners' backing, given their track record in fibre, validates the potential for high-growth wholesale models in 5G across the continent. Investors should see this as a strong indicator of escalating demand for last-mile connectivity and digital infrastructure plays.

Monitor Comsol's execution and market penetration in South Africa. More broadly, look for similar infrastructure-focused private equity plays entering other key African markets, particularly in Ghana, Nigeria, and Kenya, as demand for reliable and high-speed broadband intensifies. Early movers securing wholesale infrastructure assets will gain significant advantage.
