Malawi's National Statistical Office (NSO) and Communications Regulatory Authority (MACRA) face public scrutiny over the recruitment process for ICT survey supervisors and enumerators. Social media users are questioning the transparency and fairness of the hiring.

For investors, this signals potential governance weaknesses and execution risks in public sector projects. Lack of transparency in such processes can erode public trust, delay critical data collection, and create regulatory uncertainty, impacting investment climate perceptions.

Watch for official responses from NSO and MACRA, any subsequent investigations, and the ultimate impact on the survey's credibility. This situation could prompt broader calls for accountability in government procurement and project management.
