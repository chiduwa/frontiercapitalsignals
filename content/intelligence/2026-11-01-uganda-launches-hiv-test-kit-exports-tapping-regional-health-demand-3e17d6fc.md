The Independent (Uganda) reports the launch of Uganda’s first local manufacturing facility for 'OraQuick' HIV self-test kits in Mukono. This factory has already exported its first consignment to Kenya. Health workers have welcomed the facility, noting its potential to increase awareness of HIV status and contribute to Uganda's goal of ending the AIDS epidemic by 2030.

This development signifies a nascent but potentially growing sector for local health manufacturing in Uganda, with immediate export capacity to a key regional market like Kenya. The production of essential medical supplies domestically, and their cross-border distribution, could indicate emerging opportunities for supply chain investments or partnerships in the East African healthcare market. Such initiatives align with regional public health objectives and address local demand.

Investors should monitor the factory's production scaling, market penetration, and potential for further regional export agreements. The sustained demand for essential health products, combined with growing local manufacturing capabilities, could point towards the viability of similar ventures across the region. Regulatory support for local production and regional trade of medical devices will be key indicators.
