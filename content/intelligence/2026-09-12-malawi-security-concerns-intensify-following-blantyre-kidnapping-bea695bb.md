A Malawian auditor, Nusrat Osman Jiwa, was abducted in daylight near Mount Pleasant, Blantyre. She was reportedly bundled into a grey/silver Nissan Dualis, registration number 6956. This incident has prompted a police search and left her family and employers seeking answers.

This incident elevates concerns about personal security and rule of law in Malawi, critical factors for foreign direct investment. Such occurrences introduce operational risks, increase security costs, and can deter international talent and capital. Perceived instability directly impacts investor confidence and long-term planning.
