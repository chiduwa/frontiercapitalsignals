Hakainde Hichilema has secured a second term as Zambia's president in a closely fought election. This outcome extends his United Party for National Development's, UPND, mandate.

Hichilema's reelection signals policy continuity, generally favorable for investor confidence in Zambia and the broader Southern African region. His first term focused on economic reforms, debt restructuring, and combating corruption. This stability could attract further foreign direct investment into Zambia, potentially benefiting regional trade and supply chains that include countries like Malawi.

Investors should monitor the implementation of ongoing economic reforms, particularly progress on debt restructuring negotiations. Watch for any cabinet reshuffles and the government's renewed focus on key sectors like mining and agriculture. Regional trade agreements and infrastructure projects will also be critical indicators for broader market sentiment across Southern Africa.
