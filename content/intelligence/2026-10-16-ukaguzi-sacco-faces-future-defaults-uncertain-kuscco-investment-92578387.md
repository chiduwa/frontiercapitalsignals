This development suggests potential challenges within Kenya's Sacco sector. Anticipated loan defaults in 2025 could signal underlying economic pressures affecting borrower repayment capacity. The uncertain recovery of the KUSCCO investment highlights inter-institutional financial risks that investors tracking the stability of financial cooperatives should consider.
