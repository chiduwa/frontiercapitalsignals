Nigeria’s entrepreneurial ecosystem is experiencing a significant resurgence. This growth is driven by a rapidly expanding informal economy and technology's role in lowering barriers to entry for new businesses across the country.

This resurgence signals robust grassroots economic activity and widespread innovation. Investors should see opportunities in sectors that serve or leverage this dynamism, particularly fintech, logistics, and platforms empowering micro, small, and medium enterprises. This represents a fertile ground for high-growth, impact-driven investments.

Next, we must watch for evolving regulatory frameworks and government policies designed to integrate or support these expanding informal and tech-enabled sectors. Monitor infrastructure development and digital inclusion rates, as these will be critical for scaling operations and unlocking further investment potential.
