CDH Investment Bank reported a significant K41 billion Profit After Tax for the first half of 2026. This represents a robust 124% increase over the K18.3 billion recorded in the same period last year. CEO Thoko Mkavea highlighted the bank’s inherent strength as a key driver of these results.

This exceptional performance signals robust health within Malawi's financial sector and potentially broader economic resilience. For investors, CDH's accelerated growth points to strong operational execution and a favorable banking environment, suggesting attractive opportunities in the country's financial services space.
