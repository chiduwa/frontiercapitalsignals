Former Inspector General Merlyne Yolamu revealed inadequate resources severely hampered the Malawi Police Service's response to the Chikangawa plane crash search. This lack of equipment and funding significantly contributed to the search failure, beyond challenging weather and terrain.

This incident highlights systemic underinvestment in critical public services, posing indirect risks to foreign direct investment. Deficient emergency response capabilities can impact business continuity, investor confidence, and perceptions of state capacity, particularly concerning infrastructure reliability and security protocols.

Investors should monitor parliamentary reactions and any government initiatives to address resource gaps in key state institutions. Watch for upcoming budget allocations, procurement reforms, or public statements indicating a commitment to improving emergency services and national infrastructure resilience.
