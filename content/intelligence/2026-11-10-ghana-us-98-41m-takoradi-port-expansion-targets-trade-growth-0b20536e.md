The High Street Journal reports that Mahama is set to cut the sod for a US$98.41 million Takoradi Port berth expansion. This 300-metre facility will be entirely funded by GPHA, the authority responsible for the project.

This initiative reflects GPHA's aim to expand Ghana's port capacity and enhance its contribution to the trade sector. For investors, this suggests potential for increased efficiency in trade logistics, possibly benefiting businesses involved in import/export, shipping, and supply chain operations within or through Ghana.

Investors should monitor the progress of this infrastructure project and its impact on trade volumes and logistics costs. The successful completion could signal broader government commitment to improving trade infrastructure, potentially leading to further opportunities in related sectors.
