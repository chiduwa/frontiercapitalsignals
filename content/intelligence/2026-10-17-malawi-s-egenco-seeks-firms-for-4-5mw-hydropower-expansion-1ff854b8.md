Malawi24 reports that the Electricity Generation Company (EGENCO) is seeking an engineering firm to oversee the development of a 4.5-megawatt hydropower extension project in Karonga District. The project aims to increase generation capacity at the existing Wovwe Hydropower Station and boost electricity supply in Malawi. EGENCO has invited international consulting firms to submit proposals for Owner's supervision.

This development signals a tangible commitment to bolstering Malawi's electricity infrastructure. It creates direct business opportunities for engineering and consulting firms, and potential ripple effects for other service providers in the energy sector.

Investors should closely track the progress of this tender and any further announcements regarding EGENCO's project pipeline or broader national energy development strategies.
