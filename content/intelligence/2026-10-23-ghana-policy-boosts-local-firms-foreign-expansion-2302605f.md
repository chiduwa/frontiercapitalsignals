MyJoyOnline Business reports that Ghana's government is seeking to help local businesses expand into foreign markets. This initiative comes as competition within the African Continental Free Trade Area intensifies.

This policy indicates a strategic shift aimed at strengthening Ghanaian firms' competitiveness beyond national borders. For investors, this could signal increased cross-border activity by Ghanaian companies, potentially creating new partnerships or acquisition targets. It also highlights the growing competitive pressure within the AfCFTA, which may affect business performance across the continent.

Investors should monitor the specific mechanisms or programs the Ghanaian government introduces to facilitate this expansion. Details on targeted sectors, financial support, or market entry assistance would provide clearer signals. Understanding which Ghanaian firms are best positioned to leverage this support will be key.
