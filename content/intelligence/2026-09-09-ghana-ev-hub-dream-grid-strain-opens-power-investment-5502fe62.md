Ghana aims to become a West African EV manufacturing and adoption hub. This ambitious plan includes new EV factories, supporting mines for battery minerals, and increased EV charging infrastructure. However, these initiatives, alongside other growing power demands like data centers, are converging on an already constrained national power grid.

This presents a dual-edged opportunity. Investors backing Ghana's EV value chain must factor in potential power supply bottlenecks and costs. Conversely, the significant gap in energy infrastructure creates substantial opportunities for private sector investment in power generation, transmission, and smart grid solutions. Reliable power is a fundamental enabler for all industrial growth.

Monitor Ghana's energy sector policy and regulatory developments, specifically new incentives for private power generation and grid upgrades. Watch for tenders or public-private partnership announcements in renewable energy projects and grid modernization. The government's execution on power sector reforms will dictate the pace and viability of its broader industrialization agenda, including the EV revolution.
