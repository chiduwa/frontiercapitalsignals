Ethio Telecom reported a significant jump in annual earnings, driven by mobile data and internet services now overtaking voice calls as its primary revenue source. This represents a historic business model shift for the national operator, underscoring Ethiopia's rapid digital adoption.

This development signals a critical monetization point for digital transformation in a major African economy. For investors, it highlights robust and accelerating consumer demand for data services, indicating a strong foundation for sustained growth in the broader African digital economy. It validates the investment thesis in digital infrastructure and services across frontier markets.

We should monitor Ethio Telecom's continued data-driven growth strategies and average revenue per user (ARPU) trends. Watch how this shift influences competitive landscapes and policy reforms in other regional telecom markets. Further policy support for digital infrastructure and innovation will unlock additional investment opportunities.
