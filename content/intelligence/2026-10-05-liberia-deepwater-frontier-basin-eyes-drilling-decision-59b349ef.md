TotalEnergies and BluEnergies are deepening their studies in Liberia's undrilled Harper Basin, conducting new seismic and seabed analyses. This intensive work moves them closer to a crucial deepwater drilling decision in this frontier offshore region.

For investors, this signifies serious movement in a high-potential, underexplored basin. A confirmed discovery could attract substantial upstream capital, drive infrastructure development, and generate significant state revenues, potentially reshaping Liberia's economy and influencing broader West African energy exploration.

The critical next step is the actual drilling decision. Watch for official announcements from TotalEnergies and BluEnergies regarding their study outcomes and exploration timelines. A firm commitment to drilling will be the key trigger for elevated investor interest in Liberia's nascent energy sector.
