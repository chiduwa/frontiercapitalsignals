Nyasa Times reports that CDH Investment Bank (CDHIB) has been named Malawi’s Investment Bank of the Year 2026 by London-based Global Banking and Finance Review Magazine. This marks the 13th time CDHIB has received this award. The report also notes that the bank's after-tax profit is climbing towards K41 billion in the first half of the year.

This consistent recognition for CDHIB, coupled with strong profit growth, signals potential stability and leadership within Malawi's financial sector. For international investors, it highlights a potentially robust and well-regarded institution in a frontier market. The climbing after-tax profit could indicate healthy underlying economic activity or strong performance from the bank's operations.
