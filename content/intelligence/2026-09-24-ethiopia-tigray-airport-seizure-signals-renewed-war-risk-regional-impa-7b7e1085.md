Tigrayan forces have taken control of Mekelle airport. This move follows the recent alignment of former battlefield adversaries against Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed’s government, signaling a significant escalation in Ethiopia’s political landscape.

This development heightens political risk and the potential for renewed conflict in Ethiopia. While not directly within our core focus markets, instability in a regional economic anchor like Ethiopia can ripple through East Africa, impacting broader investor sentiment and potentially disrupting regional trade routes and supply chains that touch Kenya, Uganda, or even beyond. Investors should note the increased risk profile across the wider region.

Monitor the federal government's response and any further military movements. Watch for international and regional diplomatic efforts to de-escalate tensions. The trajectory of this conflict will be a key determinant of broader East African stability and investor confidence in the coming months.
