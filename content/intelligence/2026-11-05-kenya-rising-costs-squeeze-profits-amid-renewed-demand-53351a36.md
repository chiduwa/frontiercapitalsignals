The Standard reports that Kenyan businesses are experiencing renewed demand. However, this positive trend is being challenged by rising fuel, transport, and other input costs. These increasing expenses are reportedly squeezing profits for companies and reducing consumers' purchasing power.

This situation presents a mixed signal for investors. While renewed demand suggests underlying economic resilience, the simultaneous pressure on profit margins from rising operational costs could impact corporate earnings and valuations. Additionally, the squeeze on consumers' purchasing power signals potential headwinds for consumer-facing businesses, as discretionary spending may decrease.

Investors should closely monitor the trajectory of fuel, transport, and other input costs in Kenya. Assessing how businesses adapt to these pressures, potentially through pricing strategies or cost efficiencies, will be key. Further observation of consumer spending trends will also be important to gauge the overall market health.
