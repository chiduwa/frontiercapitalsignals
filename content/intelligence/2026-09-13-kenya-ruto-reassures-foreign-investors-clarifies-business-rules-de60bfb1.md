President Ruto has clarified Kenya's position on foreign businesses and employment, stepping back from an earlier crackdown on small traders. He now reassures lawful foreign nationals that they are welcome to operate and work, provided they comply with local laws and regularize their status within 90 days.

This policy reversal significantly de-risks the investment landscape for foreign capital. The initial crackdown had sparked concerns over regulatory unpredictability and Kenya's commitment to international trade agreements, including EAC protocols. Ruto's clarification restores confidence, signaling a more stable and predictable operating environment for foreign investors.

Investors should closely monitor the practical implementation of this 90-day regularization period. Watch for any subsequent legislative changes or new enforcement measures that could impact foreign enterprise. Also, observe how this clarification influences Kenya's trade relations and standing within the East African Community.
