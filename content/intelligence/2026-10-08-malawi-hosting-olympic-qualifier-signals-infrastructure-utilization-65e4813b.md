Malawi is set to host Zimbabwe for a 2028 Olympic Games qualifier, with the first leg taking place at Lilongwe's Bingu National Stadium. This marks a significant international sporting event utilizing key national infrastructure.

This event underscores Malawi's capability to host large-scale international competitions, leveraging significant public infrastructure like the Bingu National Stadium. Such gatherings generate short-term economic activity in hospitality, transport, and retail, signaling the country's readiness for major event logistics.

Investors should observe Malawi's sustained commitment to attracting and executing international events.
