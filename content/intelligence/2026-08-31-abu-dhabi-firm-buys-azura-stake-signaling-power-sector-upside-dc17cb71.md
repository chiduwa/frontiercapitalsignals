ePointZero, an Abu Dhabi-based energy infrastructure platform, has acquired a 90 percent stake in Azura Power Holdings Ltd. This significant acquisition positions Azura to further capitalize on Africa’s growing demand for reliable power, with the new ownership explicitly aiming to capture the continent's power-hungry markets.

This deal is a clear signal of robust foreign direct investment interest in African energy infrastructure. It underscores a strong belief in the long-term value and growth prospects within the continent's power generation sector. Investors should view this as validation of the investment thesis around essential, income-generating infrastructure assets addressing critical supply gaps in key markets.

Investors should now watch ePointZero’s broader expansion strategy across Africa, particularly how it leverages Azura’s established presence. Monitor for subsequent consolidation within the power sector and new project developments, especially those backed by significant foreign capital, as these indicate continued market maturity and opportunity.
