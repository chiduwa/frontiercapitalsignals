BusinessDay reports that mesh-grids are emerging as an alternative for bringing electricity to rural communities in Nigeria. These communities are described as being too dispersed for conventional mini-grids to be effective.

This development signals a distinct opportunity within Nigeria's off-grid energy sector. Investors focusing on rural electrification or innovative infrastructure may find new avenues in deploying or financing mesh-grid solutions, addressing a market gap where current alternatives are less viable. The emergence of this model suggests a potential expansion of the addressable market for energy providers.

Investors should monitor the pace of adoption and scaling of these mesh-grid solutions. Observing policy support, potential pilot projects, and initial private sector engagement will be key to understanding the viability and growth trajectory of this emerging segment in Nigeria's energy landscape.
