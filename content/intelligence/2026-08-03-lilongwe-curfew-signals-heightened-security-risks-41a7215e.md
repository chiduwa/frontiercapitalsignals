Lilongwe police have imposed an 8 pm curfew following the murder of seven motorcycle taxi operators, known as Kabaza riders, in the past week. This drastic measure comes amidst a targeted crime wave causing significant insecurity in the city.

This curfew directly impacts the night-time economy and critical last-mile logistics in Lilongwe, particularly for businesses relying on efficient movement of goods and people after hours. Increased security risks can elevate operational costs, deter investment, and negatively affect consumer confidence and overall economic activity in the capital.
