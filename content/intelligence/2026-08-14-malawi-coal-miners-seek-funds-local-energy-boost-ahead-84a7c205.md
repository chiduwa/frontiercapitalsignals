This development signals a clear domestic drive for import substitution in a critical energy sector. For investors, it points to potential opportunities in mining operations, supply chain infrastructure, or financing local producers. Government backing, if secured, could de-risk investments and create a more favorable operating environment for increasing local energy independence.
