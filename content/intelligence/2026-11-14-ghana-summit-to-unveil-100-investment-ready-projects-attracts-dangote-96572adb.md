The High Street Journal reports that Ghana is hosting its maiden Invest Ghana Summit from Monday, November 16-17, in Accra. This event is expected to attract global investors, including Aliko Dangote, and will present a pipeline of approximately 100 investment-ready projects.

This summit signals a concentrated effort by Ghana to draw foreign direct investment. The presence of global investors and a prominent figure like Aliko Dangote suggests a high level of interest and potential for significant capital deployment. The availability of 100 "investment-ready" projects indicates specific opportunities for investors. The High Street Journal highlights that major deals are expected, implying tangible outcomes from these discussions.

Investors should monitor any reported outcomes or announcements from the summit, particularly regarding the types of deals closed or the sectors that attract the most interest. The High Street Journal did not specify the sectors of these projects, so clarity on this will be important for targeting opportunities.
