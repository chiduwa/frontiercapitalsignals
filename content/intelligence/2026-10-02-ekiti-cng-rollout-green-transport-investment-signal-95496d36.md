Ekiti State has deployed 15 Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) buses, simultaneously cutting public transport fares by 50%. This move, initiated by Governor Biodun Oyebanji, directly aligns with President Tinubu's directive to alleviate transport costs across the nation.

For investors, this represents a tangible commitment to alternative fuel adoption at the state level in Nigeria, particularly in the wake of fuel subsidy removals. It creates immediate demand for CNG vehicles, conversion kits, and crucially, the development of robust CNG refueling infrastructure, opening avenues for public-private partnerships in green transport.

Watch for similar initiatives from other Nigerian states and the federal government's broader strategy for CNG infrastructure rollout. Specific areas of interest include tenders for vehicle supply, conversion services, and the licensing or development of new CNG distribution and refueling stations.
