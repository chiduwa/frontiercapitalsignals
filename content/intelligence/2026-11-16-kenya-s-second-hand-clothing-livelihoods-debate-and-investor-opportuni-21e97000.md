Capital FM Business reports that Kenya’s second-hand clothing trade supports millions of livelihoods. This trade also extends the useful life of garments, positioning it centrally in current discussions.

For investors, the significant role of the second-hand clothing sector in Kenya highlights a robust, informal economy. This sector demonstrates strong consumer demand for affordable goods and contributes substantially to employment. Its position in a broader debate suggests potential for policy shifts or increased formalization efforts in this sizable market.

Investors should monitor developments in the ongoing debate surrounding the second-hand clothing trade. Any policy changes or regulatory proposals affecting this sector could either create new investment opportunities, particularly in formalizing supply chains, or introduce new operational considerations for existing businesses.
