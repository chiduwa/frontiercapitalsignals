Premium Times reports that Mr. Cardoso stated a strengthened partnership between the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and NNPC is expected to improve transparency in oil-sector foreign exchange markets. This collaboration specifically targets how NNPC's FX inflows integrate into the central bank and, by implication, federal government finances.

Greater transparency in these critical FX flows could enhance investor confidence and market predictability. For international investors, clearer insights into NNPC's foreign exchange contributions may signal more stable FX liquidity management and improved fiscal health, crucial factors for capital allocation and potential repatriation.

Going forward, investors should closely observe the practical implementation and any tangible outcomes of this partnership on FX market dynamics and overall liquidity. The actual impact on market stability and transparency is yet to be demonstrated.
