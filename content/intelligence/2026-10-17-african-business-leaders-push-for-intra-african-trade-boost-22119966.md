The Standard reports that African business leaders are urging governments to dismantle trade barriers and enhance infrastructure across the continent. This initiative aims to significantly boost intra-African trade, primarily through the AfCFTA framework.

This concerted private sector push signals a strong desire for accelerated regional economic integration. For investors, reduced trade barriers could translate into easier market access, lower operational costs, and expanded consumer bases. Improved infrastructure, if realized, would directly support supply chains and logistics, potentially boosting profitability and attracting further investment into sectors like manufacturing, logistics, and retail.

Investors should closely monitor governmental responses to these calls, looking for concrete policy changes concerning trade tariffs, customs procedures, and commitments to infrastructure development. The pace and scope of these changes will be key indicators for potential market expansion and operational efficiency improvements across the region.
