Malawians are pushing for an end to data bundle expiry, mirroring a legal challenge already underway in South Africa. There, Vodacom and MTN are contesting ICASA's stricter regulations, which aim to protect consumers from losing unused data and incurring unexpected out-of-bundle charges.

This trend highlights growing regulatory pressure on mobile network operators across Africa to reform data pricing and expiry policies. For investors, this signifies potential shifts in telco revenue models, increased compliance costs, and the growing influence of consumer protection bodies on market operations.

Monitor the South African court's decision, as it will likely set a precedent for similar demands and regulatory actions in other African markets, including Malawi. Watch for any legislative or regulatory moves in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, and Uganda addressing data expiry or out-of-bundle charges.
