MyJoyOnline Business reports that an agribusiness expert advises young people in agribusiness to start independently and small, rather than immediately partnering with friends without clear agreements on responsibilities, loss management, and investment accounting. This guidance comes from the Executive Director of the Centre for Climate Change & Food Security (CCCFS).

This expert advice highlights a potential systemic risk in how new agribusinesses are formed locally. International investors considering partnerships or investments in smaller, emerging agribusiness ventures may face challenges related to informal business structures and poorly defined roles. Understanding these foundational issues can inform due diligence processes and the need for robust governance frameworks when engaging with local entities.

Investors should monitor how common these informal partnership structures are among potential local partners. Scrutiny of internal governance and partnership agreements will be crucial to mitigate risks stemming from these identified challenges in nascent agribusiness ventures.
