BusinessDay reports that the Vios Energy CEO foresees Nigerian customers potentially abandoning the national grid. This assessment is based on the continued decline of solar battery prices, a trend that could lead to a "Pakistan-style grid exit" in the country.

This projection signals a potential major disruption in Nigeria's energy market. If battery prices continue to fall and grid reliability remains a concern, it suggests a growing market for distributed and off-grid solar solutions. Investors should evaluate the implications for traditional utility infrastructure and the emerging opportunities in renewable energy and battery storage.

Going forward, investors should closely monitor the trajectory of solar battery costs and the consistent reliability of Nigeria's national grid. Observe any government policy shifts or incentives that might accelerate or mitigate this trend, as it presents both risks to incumbent energy providers and significant growth potential for alternative power solutions.
