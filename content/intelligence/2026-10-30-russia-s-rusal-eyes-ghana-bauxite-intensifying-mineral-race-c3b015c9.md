Africa Briefing reports that Russian aluminium giant Rusal is targeting Ghana's vast bauxite deposits, aiming for 5 million tonnes annually. This move marks Rusal's intention to gain a foothold in Ghana's mining industry with a project expected to dramatically increase production. Russia's entry adds to the growing competition for Ghana's mineral resources.

This development signals intensifying international interest in Ghana's bauxite reserves. For investors, Rusal's ambition to significantly boost production suggests a high-potential mining opportunity within the country. However, Africa Briefing highlights that local processing remains Ghana's biggest challenge, which could impact the value chain and investor returns if not addressed.

Investors should monitor specific project details and timelines as Rusal establishes its presence. It will be critical to observe how Ghana plans to overcome its local processing challenges, as this could influence the long-term viability and broader economic impact of such large-scale mining operations. Further competition in the mineral sector is also likely.
