Capital FM Business reports a proposed Sh100 billion road project for the Nairobi–Thika–Juja–Kenol–Sagana–Marua highway. Motorists could begin paying toll charges under this project. Financial close for this initiative is expected in the 2028/29 period.

This development signals substantial infrastructure investment opportunities in Kenya. The proposed toll charges indicate a potential shift towards user-funded infrastructure, which could attract private sector participation through concessions or other financing models. A project valued at Sh100 billion presents significant prospects for construction companies, financing institutions, and related service providers.

Investors should closely monitor the project's financing structure and implementation details as they emerge. Key aspects to watch include the specific framework for toll collection and any tenders for construction or operation, especially as the 2028/29 financial close date approaches.
