AfCFTA's true commercial power for African movie production remains largely unrealized by governments. This signals a significant, overlooked economic opportunity for the continent's creative industries, ripe for immediate capitalization.

The AfCFTA framework inherently reduces barriers to cross-border collaboration, financing, and distribution for film projects. This vastly expands the addressable market beyond individual national borders, unlocking substantial revenue potential for production houses, studios, talent, and related tech platforms. Investors entering now can secure early mover advantage in a sector poised for exponential growth.

Monitor for government policy shifts or specific incentives aimed at fostering regional film production and distribution under AfCFTA. Pay attention to new co-production deals, increasing private equity investment in studio infrastructure, and the emergence of pan-African streaming services or content aggregators as key indicators.
