The Standard reports that a Malindi court has barred Mining CS Joho from awarding manganese mining rights at Lali Hills in Tana River County. This decision follows a petition filed by members of the Orma pastoralist community, effectively halting the tender process.

This court intervention highlights regulatory and community engagement risks inherent in Kenya's mining sector. Delays in tender processes, even after initial stages, can impact project timelines and investor confidence in resource extraction. Investors should consider the potential for community resistance and judicial review in similar ventures.

Further developments in this court case will be important to monitor, particularly regarding its resolution and any precedents it might set for future resource tenders in Kenya. The long-term implications for the stability and predictability of mining investments in the region will depend on how such disputes are managed.
