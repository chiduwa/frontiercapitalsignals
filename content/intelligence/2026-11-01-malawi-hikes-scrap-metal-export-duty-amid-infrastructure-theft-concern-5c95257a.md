Malawi24 reports that the Malawi Revenue Authority (MRA) has increased the export duty on scrap metal by 40 percent. This policy adjustment aims to deter the theft of public infrastructure components, specifically mentioning water and electricity equipment.

This regulatory change could impact businesses involved in scrap metal collection, processing, and export in Malawi, potentially increasing operational costs or reducing profitability for some. For investors in key public services, the measure signals government efforts to protect essential infrastructure like water and electricity networks, which could improve service reliability and reduce operational losses for relevant utilities.

Investors should monitor the effectiveness of this policy in curbing infrastructure theft and its broader impact on the scrap metal industry. Observing whether the measure leads to a noticeable improvement in the security of public utilities could offer insights into the operating environment for infrastructure-related investments.
