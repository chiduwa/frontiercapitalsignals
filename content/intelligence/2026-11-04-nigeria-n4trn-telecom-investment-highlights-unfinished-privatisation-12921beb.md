BusinessDay reports that Nigeria's telecommunications industry was liberalised twenty-five years ago, leading to private operators replacing the state-controlled Nigerian Telecommunications Limited (NITEL). This development comes as the sector has seen a reported N4trn telecom investment, which BusinessDay suggests exposes "unfinished business of privatisation".

For investors, the mention of "unfinished business of privatisation" signals potential complexities in Nigeria's telecom sector despite significant capital inflows. This could imply lingering regulatory hurdles, unresolved government stakes, or policy inconsistencies that may affect the operating environment or future growth trajectories for private telecom entities and their partners.

Fund managers should monitor how Nigerian authorities address these reported "unfinished" aspects. Clarity on the remaining privatisation issues could either de-risk existing investments or unlock new opportunities in the sector by fostering a more predictable and fully liberalised market for infrastructure, services, and related digital ventures.
