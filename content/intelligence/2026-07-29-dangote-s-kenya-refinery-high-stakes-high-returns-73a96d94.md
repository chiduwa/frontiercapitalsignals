Dangote's proposed $17bn refinery project in Kenya aims to establish the country as East Africa's refining hub.

This massive infrastructure play could reshape regional energy dynamics and logistics, creating substantial ancillary investment opportunities. However, its success hinges on navigating significant financial, regulatory, and market challenges, which will dictate long-term returns and stability.

Investors should closely monitor progress on financing commitments, government approvals, and Dangote's operational track record. Key indicators will be detailed project timelines, off-take agreements, and mitigation strategies for existing regional refining overcapacity concerns.
