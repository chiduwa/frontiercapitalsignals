Oil prices have declined for six straight sessions, driven by renewed US-Iran discussions. These talks are signaling a potential easing of global oil supply concerns, pushing crude benchmarks lower.

For investors in Nigeria, this trend directly impacts government revenue and currency stability. Lower oil prices can strain fiscal budgets, potentially influencing infrastructure spending and subsidy reforms. Conversely, oil-importing nations like Kenya and Ghana could see a slight relief in import bills, though the broader macro impact leans towards producers.

Watch closely for developments in US-Iran negotiations and any corresponding statements from OPEC+. Any concrete agreement or shift in production policy could significantly alter the current price trajectory, impacting energy sector valuations across the continent.
