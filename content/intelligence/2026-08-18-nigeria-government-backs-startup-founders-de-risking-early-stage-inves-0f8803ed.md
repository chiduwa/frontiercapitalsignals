The Nigerian Federal Ministry of Youth Development, via its Nigerian Youth Academy (NiYA), has partnered with entrepreneurship platform Cascador. This collaboration launches the NiYA × Cascador Founders Program, a pilot initiative. It will provide 20 young Nigerian entrepreneurs with critical business training, mentorship, and access to non-dilutive funding.

This partnership signals direct government support for Nigeria's burgeoning startup ecosystem. By offering training, mentorship, and non-dilutive capital, the program aims to de-risk early-stage ventures, enhancing their maturity and investor readiness. It promises to cultivate a stronger pipeline of high-potential, better-prepared founders, making the Nigerian tech landscape more attractive for local and international capital.
