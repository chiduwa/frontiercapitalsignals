Nordica's Fibroid Care Centre in Nigeria has successfully completed 1,000 High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound (HIFU) treatments for fibroids. This achievement marks five years of strategic investment and innovation in advanced women's health technology within the region.

This development signifies growing demand for sophisticated, non-invasive medical procedures in the Nigerian healthcare market, particularly in women's health. The successful adoption of high-tech solutions like HIFU demonstrates market readiness for specialized medical services and strong potential for scaling innovative healthcare delivery models across Africa. It outlines a viable investment pathway in medical technology and specialized clinics.
