An Op-Ed explores three seminal visions of Yoruba power through Awolowo, Tinubu, and Obasanjo. Awolowo focused on federalism and ethnic development. Tinubu built influence via alliances and succession. Obasanjo prioritized Nigerian nationalism over ethnic politics. These philosophies shape the moral architecture of leadership and political confidence within Nigeria.

For investors, these distinct political philosophies are crucial indicators of future governance and policy stability in Nigeria. Awolowo's legacy suggests a focus on regional development and resource control. Tinubu's alliance-building approach signals pragmatic deal-making and potential for continuity. Obasanjo's nationalist stance points to federal unity and broad national initiatives impacting resource allocation and inter-state relations. Understanding these influences provides insight into potential policy predictability.

Moving forward, monitor how these foundational political ideas manifest in current policy debates, particularly within economic planning, federal-state dynamics, and infrastructure allocation. With Tinubu's presidency, his own political philosophy, alongside the enduring legacies of Awolowo and Obasanjo, will significantly shape Nigeria's investment environment and long-term development strategy.
