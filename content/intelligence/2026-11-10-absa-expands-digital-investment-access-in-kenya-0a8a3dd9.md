Capital FM Business reports that Absa Bank Kenya has expanded its Absa Next digital banking platform. This expansion now includes investment products, allowing customers to access five unit trust funds. These funds are available with minimum investments starting from Sh1,000.

This development suggests a potential shift towards broader accessibility for investment products in Kenya. By integrating unit trust funds into a digital platform and setting a low minimum investment threshold, Absa Bank Kenya may aim to attract a wider range of investors. This could indicate a trend of increased competition and innovation in digital financial services, potentially boosting participation in the local investment market.

Investors should monitor the adoption rates of these new digital investment products and their impact on Absa Bank Kenya's market position. Observing how other financial institutions respond with similar offerings will be key to understanding the evolving landscape of retail investment in Kenya.
