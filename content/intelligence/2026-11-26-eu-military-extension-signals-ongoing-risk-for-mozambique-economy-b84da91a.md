Africa Briefing reports the European Union has extended its military assistance mission in Mozambique until December 2028, allocating €17.17 million. This extension addresses an Islamist insurgency in Mozambique's gas-rich northern province.

The continued EU military presence indicates persistent security challenges in a key economic region. Mozambique seeks greater military self-reliance, yet the ongoing external support highlights the severe threat the insurgency poses to the country's economic recovery. This situation signals elevated risk for investments dependent on stability in the gas-rich areas.

Investors should monitor the effectiveness of the extended military mission in containing the insurgency and its impact on the region's security. The pace of Mozambique's progress towards military self-reliance and the broader implications for economic recovery remain critical indicators.
