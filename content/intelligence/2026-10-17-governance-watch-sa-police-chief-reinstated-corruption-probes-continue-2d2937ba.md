Africa Briefing reports that South African President Cyril Ramaphosa reinstated national police commissioner Fannie Masemola. This follows the end of a six-month suspension and the withdrawal of charges by prosecutors. These charges were linked to a controversial R228m police healthcare contract.

This development matters to investors as it highlights the ongoing scrutiny of governance and public sector contracting in Africa. The reinstatement, despite previous charges related to a significant public tender, may be viewed with caution regarding the transparency and accountability mechanisms for large government contracts. Continued wider police corruption investigations, as reported by Africa Briefing, underscore persistent risks in the regulatory environment.

Investors should continue to monitor the broader police corruption investigations mentioned by Africa Briefing. The handling and outcomes of these probes will offer further insights into the commitment to combating graft and upholding the rule of law, which are crucial factors for investor confidence in the region.
