The Standard reports the World Bank warns of escalating highway construction costs in Kenya. Specifically, the World Bank estimates that restricted access across six active road contracts in Wajir and Mandera is generating approximately Sh1.2 billion every month in additional project expenses.

This situation highlights significant operational risks and potential cost overruns for infrastructure investments in the region. Such additional expenses can erode project profitability and introduce uncertainty for contractors, lenders, and investors involved in road development and related supply chains. It also indicates potential challenges in project execution and stakeholder engagement.

Investors should closely monitor developments regarding the restricted access issues in Wajir and Mandera, as their resolution will impact project timelines and overall financial viability. The approach to absorbing or financing these substantial additional costs could also signal future trends for large-scale infrastructure projects across East Africa.
