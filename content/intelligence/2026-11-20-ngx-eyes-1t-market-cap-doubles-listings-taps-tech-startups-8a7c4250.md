TechCabal reports that the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) currently lists 146 companies. The bourse aims to double this number as part of its strategy to achieve a $1 trillion market capitalization within the next decade. This ambition, according to the news item, needs the participation of its tech startups.

This signals a significant growth trajectory for Nigeria's public markets, presenting potential opportunities for investors seeking exposure to the region. The focus on doubling listings, particularly with an implied reliance on tech startups, suggests new avenues for capital deployment and liquidity growth. Investors should note the long-term nature of this ambition.

Investors should monitor the NGX's initiatives and policy changes aimed at attracting new listings, especially from the tech sector. Watch for regulatory adjustments, incentive programs, and the actual pipeline of companies considering going public. These will provide early indicators of the NGX's progress towards its stated goals.
