MyJoyOnline Business reports that Germany is urging African countries to prioritize value addition in critical minerals. This includes leveraging technology, innovation, and strategic partnerships to develop local value chains and unlock greater economic benefits from these natural resources. Germany's Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy also emphasized that stronger cooperation between German and African businesses will be critical.

This German initiative signals a potential shift towards more localized processing and manufacturing within African critical mineral sectors. Investors in technology, innovation, and infrastructure supporting mineral processing could find new opportunities. The emphasis on stronger cooperation between German and African businesses suggests potential for joint ventures and foreign direct investment in this space.

Investors should monitor specific policy responses from African governments regarding value addition in critical minerals. The practical implementation of technology and innovation-driven partnerships, particularly with German firms, will be key to identifying tangible investment avenues. Details on proposed strategic partnerships and their scope will clarify specific opportunities.
