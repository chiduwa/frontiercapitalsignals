The Standard reports that Gulf Energy's first-phase production schedule remains on track. Road transport of oil to Mombasa is due to begin from the December 1 target date.

This development signifies concrete progress towards Kenya's initial commercial oil output. For investors, it indicates the country's advancement towards becoming an oil producer, potentially creating opportunities in related logistics, infrastructure, and energy services within the region.

Investors should observe the successful commencement and consistency of this initial road transport. Future updates on production and export logistics will be important for assessing the long-term outlook for Kenya's commercial oil sector.
