Premium Times reports experts are urging Nigeria to develop local manufacturing, expand skills development, and strengthen regulation in its growing solar energy market. This is aimed at moving beyond importing solar technologies.

This focus on local content could unlock the country's $8.5 billion solar potential and maximise economic benefits. Investors may find opportunities in domestic solar technology manufacturing, vocational training for the sector, and regulatory compliance services.

The development of specific policies or incentives supporting local production, skills training, or regulatory frameworks in Nigeria's solar sector will be key to watch. This will indicate how the country plans to leverage its reported $8.5 billion market.
