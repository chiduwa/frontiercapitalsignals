Yoco, a leading South African fintech, has aggressively cut transaction rates by up to 40%. This strategic move aims to save small businesses over R250 million annually, marking a significant play for market share and customer acquisition.

This signals Yoco's intent to evolve beyond basic payments, establishing itself as a comprehensive operating system for small businesses. Its deep push into lending, software, and AI strengthens its ecosystem, promising higher customer stickiness and a wider revenue moat. This strategy, while currently in South Africa, holds significant implications for fintech development across our focus markets.

Investors should monitor Yoco's market share growth, the profitability impact of these cuts, and the uptake of its new value-added services. Also, watch for similar competitive plays or potential expansion strategies across other key African economies, as this model could be replicated.
