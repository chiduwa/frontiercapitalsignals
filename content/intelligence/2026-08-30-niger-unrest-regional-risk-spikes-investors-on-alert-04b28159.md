Gunfire and explosions were reported overnight August 28-29 in Niger's capital, Niamey. Activity was concentrated around the military base adjacent to the international airport. The precise nature of the events remains unconfirmed, but points to significant internal unrest.

This incident signals elevated political risk in the broader Sahel region. While Niger is not a core market for Frontier Capital Signals, instability in a neighboring West African state can trigger broader risk aversion. Investors in frontier markets should monitor potential contagion effects, especially concerning regional security and investor sentiment towards West African assets.

We are watching for official statements, military clarification, and reactions from regional bodies like ECOWAS. Any confirmed coup or prolonged instability will likely impact regional capital flows and project development. Assess the potential for increased security costs or delays for any investments across the broader West African region.
