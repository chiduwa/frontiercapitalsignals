Major mining groups in the Democratic Republic of Congo, including Glencore and CMOC, are actively pivoting operations from cobalt to copper. This strategic shift is a direct response to rising global copper prices and ongoing restrictions impacting cobalt exports from the country. Copper's far greater global demand profile is now eclipsing cobalt in the DRC's critical minerals race.

This reorientation signals a significant long-term shift in the DRC's mining sector. Investors should evaluate portfolios for companies with strong copper exposure in the region, anticipating increased investment in exploration, extraction, and infrastructure for the red metal. Copper offers more stable and robust growth potential compared to the volatile cobalt market, potentially de-risking supply chain considerations.

Monitor government policies and incentives specifically targeting copper production and export within the DRC. Watch for new infrastructure projects supporting copper logistics and processing. Keep an eye on global demand trends for electrification and green energy, which heavily rely on copper, further cementing its strategic importance for the country.
