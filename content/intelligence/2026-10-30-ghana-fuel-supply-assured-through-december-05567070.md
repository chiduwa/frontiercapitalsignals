MyJoyOnline Business reports that the Chief Executive Officer of the Ghana Chamber of Bulk Oil Distributors (CBOD), Dr Patrick Kwaku Ofori, has assured that Ghana will not experience fuel queues and that fuel supply is assured through December.

This assurance regarding stable fuel supply through December is a positive signal for overall economic stability in Ghana. Predictable fuel availability can reduce operational risks for businesses reliant on transportation and energy, potentially supporting consistent economic activity and consumer spending.

Investors should monitor the actual implementation of this assurance and any subsequent statements regarding fuel supply stability beyond December. Consistent energy access is a key factor for long-term operational planning and investor confidence in the Ghanaian market.
