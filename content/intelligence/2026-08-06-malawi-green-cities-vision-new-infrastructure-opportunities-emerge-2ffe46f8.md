Malawi's Minister of Lands, Chimwemwe Chipungu, has announced plans to implement Morocco's green, sustainable urban planning model across the country. This strategic decision follows recent talks with the Malawi Housing Corporation, indicating a significant overhaul of Malawi's traditional approach to housing development.

This initiative presents substantial investment opportunities within Malawi's infrastructure sector. Investors should recognize the potential for large-scale projects in green building technologies, sustainable urban planning, renewable energy integration, and waste management. It reflects a clear government commitment to modernizing the housing sector with an environmentally conscious framework.
