Nigeria’s hospitality industry is again lauded for its exceptional resilience, consistently weathering economic downturns and currency fluctuations. Despite its robust performance during challenging periods, the sector's long-term growth is reportedly constrained by inadequate infrastructure.

This resilience signals a stable defensive play, offering consistent performance even amid broader economic volatility. The identified infrastructure deficit presents a clear opportunity: targeted investments in supporting infrastructure, such as power, transportation, and digital connectivity, could unlock substantial growth and valuation upside for hospitality assets.

Investors should monitor government policy announcements and private sector partnerships focused on infrastructure development, particularly in key business and tourism centers. Concrete progress on these infrastructure projects will be the primary catalyst for significant expansion within the hospitality sector.
