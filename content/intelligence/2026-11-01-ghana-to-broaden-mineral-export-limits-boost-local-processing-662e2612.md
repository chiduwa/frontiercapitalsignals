The High Street Journal reports that Ghana plans to extend limits on raw mineral exports. These restrictions would cover bauxite, manganese, iron ore, and lithium. The timeline for these extended limits is by 2030.

This policy initiative aims to help Ghana retain more foreign exchange and expand domestic processing. For investors, this signals potential opportunities in mineral processing infrastructure and related industries within Ghana. Mining companies currently exporting raw materials may need to adapt their strategies.

Investors should monitor the specific details and implementation roadmap of these planned export limits. The High Street Journal indicates the policy aims to expand domestic processing, so watch for government incentives or regulatory changes supporting this objective.
