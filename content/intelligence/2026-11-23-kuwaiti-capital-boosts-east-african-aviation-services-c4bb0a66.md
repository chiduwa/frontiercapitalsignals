The Independent (Uganda) reports that capital originating from Kuwait is building an aviation empire in East Africa. These investments are reshaping critical airport services in the region, including ground handling and fuel logistics. Menzies Aviation is identified as assisting with aircraft turnaround and baggage handling at Entebbe International Airport.

This development indicates significant foreign investment in East African aviation infrastructure and services. It highlights the growing influence of Gulf state capital in key regional sectors, which could lead to improved operational efficiencies and service standards in airport operations. Investors should note the increasing presence of specialized international operators like Menzies Aviation.

Investors should monitor the continued expansion of these Kuwaiti-originated investments across the region's airports. The extent to which these investments extend beyond Uganda into other East African markets will be key.
