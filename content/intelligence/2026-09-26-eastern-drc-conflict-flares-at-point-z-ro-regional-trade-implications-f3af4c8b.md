Fierce fighting has intensified for weeks around Point Zéro, a village in Eastern DRC's highlands. This location is a critical choke point, positioned at the intersection of several strategic routes, making its control central to the ongoing conflict.

Point Zéro's strategic position means its control directly impacts regional trade and supply chain stability. Prolonged conflict here threatens the movement of goods and resources, including critical minerals, across the wider East African region. This significantly escalates perceived risk for any investments reliant on regional stability and transport infrastructure.
