Veteran politician Nevers Mumba has allied with President Hakainde Hichilema ahead of Zambia's 13 August elections. Mumba, a former Vice President, maintains his presidential ambitions but is currently backing the incumbent's electoral efforts.

This strategic political alliance could signal a consolidation of power, potentially reducing fragmentation in the political landscape. For investors, such developments often translate to a more predictable policy environment and enhanced governance stability, which generally improves confidence and reduces perceived political risk.

Investors should monitor how effectively Hichilema leverages this expanded support and the alliance's impact on overall election dynamics and outcomes. Watch for signals regarding policy continuity and whether this partnership contributes to sustained political stability beyond the immediate election cycle.
