Nile Post reports that Uganda's Ministry of Agriculture, in partnership with private entities, has launched a coffee roasting masterclass.

This initiative, though minimally detailed, appears to signal a strategic focus on enhancing domestic coffee value addition capabilities. For investors, this could imply government support for skills development and a push towards producing higher-value processed coffee products, potentially creating new market opportunities within the agricultural sector.

Further details on the specific objectives and scope of this masterclass, as well as the identities of the private partners involved, are not yet available. Investors should monitor for subsequent announcements to understand the full implications for Uganda's coffee industry.
