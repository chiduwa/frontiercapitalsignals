PLAC highlights procurement transparency, not funding, as INEC's primary challenge for Nigeria's 2027 general elections. They emphasize the need for public scrutiny of electoral spending.

This signals a persistent governance challenge within Nigeria's electoral process, specifically around public procurement. Transparency and accountability in critical national processes are key indicators for investors assessing political risk and institutional strength. Weak procurement can lead to inefficiencies, corruption, and erode public trust, potentially contributing to post-election disputes and instability.

Monitor INEC's response to these transparency calls and any concrete steps taken to reform its procurement processes ahead of 2027. Look for legislative changes, public audits, or new oversight mechanisms. Investor confidence will hinge on visible progress toward robust, accountable electoral institutions.
