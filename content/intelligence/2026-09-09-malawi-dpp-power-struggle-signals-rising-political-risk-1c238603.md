Malawi's ruling Democratic Progressive Party, DPP, is grappling with a significant internal power struggle. A social media commentator warns of 'vultures circling' as friction intensifies between key senior figures like Norman Chisale and Shadric Namalomba. This public disagreement signals deeper internal disunity within the party leadership.

This internal strife introduces heightened political uncertainty, directly impacting Malawi’s investment climate. Investor confidence relies on predictable governance and policy stability, which internal party conflicts can undermine. Such instability raises regulatory risks and could delay or disrupt ongoing projects, particularly those requiring government support or approval.

Investors should closely monitor how the DPP leadership addresses this power struggle. Key indicators include official party statements, any high-profile resignations or dismissals, and potential implications for government policy direction. Assess whether this internal conflict escalates to affect broader national stability or upcoming political cycles.
