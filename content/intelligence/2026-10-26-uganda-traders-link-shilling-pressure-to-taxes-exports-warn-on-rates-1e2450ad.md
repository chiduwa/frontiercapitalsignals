Nile Post reports that Uganda's traders' association chairperson, Isa Sekitto, attributes the shilling's pressure to high taxes and weak exports.

Sekitto suggests that lowering business costs, reviving regional trade, and boosting exports are crucial to enhance foreign currency inflows. This signals potential policy areas that could impact operational expenses and revenue for businesses. He also warns that higher interest rates risk constraining business investment, which could affect overall economic growth.

Investors should monitor government actions regarding tax policies and initiatives to improve the export environment and regional trade relations. The direction of interest rates and their potential effect on private sector investment will be a key indicator for future market conditions.
