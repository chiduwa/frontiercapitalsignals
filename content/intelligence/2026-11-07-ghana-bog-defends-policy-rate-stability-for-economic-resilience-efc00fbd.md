MyJoyOnline Business reports that the Bank of Ghana Governor, Dr. Johnson Asiama, has strongly defended the Monetary Policy Committee's decision to hold the policy rate at 14% between May and September 2026.

The Governor stated this action was taken to preserve economic stability, despite facing unfavorable external conditions including rising global oil prices, and domestic price pressures. For investors, a commitment to a stable policy rate can signal the central bank's intent to manage inflation and provide a predictable monetary environment, which influences borrowing costs and asset valuations.

Investors should closely monitor the trajectory of global oil prices and domestic price pressures. The sustained stability of the policy rate will likely depend on how effectively the central bank can mitigate these external and internal challenges to maintain economic equilibrium.
