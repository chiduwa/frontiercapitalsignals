The Standard reports that President William Ruto stated the Kenyan government intends to offer concessionary loans to manufacturers. This initiative is aimed at enabling interested manufacturers to participate in industrialisation efforts without facing financing hurdles.

For investors, this policy signals a potential reduction in capital costs for the manufacturing sector, making investments more attractive. It indicates a government focus on industrial growth and a willingness to support local production through financial incentives.

Investors should monitor for further details regarding the scope, eligibility criteria, and implementation timeline of these concessionary loans. Understanding the specific industries targeted and the scale of financing available will be crucial for identifying actionable opportunities.
