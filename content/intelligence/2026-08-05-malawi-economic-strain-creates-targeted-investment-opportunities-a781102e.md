High-pressure environments often signal unique value plays for shrewd investors. Identified sectors like agriculture, energy, and digital tech frequently represent essential services or growth drivers, potentially offering strong returns as the economy stabilizes or policies prioritize these areas.

Track government policy shifts and specific regulatory incentives targeting agriculture, energy, and digital tech. Observe if this macroeconomic pressure translates into tangible reforms or project pipeline acceleration within these identified sectors.
