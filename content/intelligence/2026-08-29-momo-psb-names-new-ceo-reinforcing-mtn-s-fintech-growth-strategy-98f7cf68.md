MoMo Payment Service Bank, MTN Nigeria's fintech subsidiary, has named Bode Abifarin as its new Managing Director and CEO, effective September 1, 2026. MoMo PSB holds a Central Bank of Nigeria license to offer digital financial services, including money transfers and bill payments via mobile.

This appointment signals MTN's serious intent to accelerate growth and strengthen leadership within its fintech subsidiary. Abifarin's expected expertise will be crucial for navigating Nigeria's dynamic digital payments landscape, driving innovation, and expanding market share. For investors, this move underscores MTN's commitment to revenue diversification and scaling its high-potential financial services segment.

Investors should monitor Abifarin's strategic direction and operational execution starting in late 2026. Key indicators to watch include new product development, user acquisition rates, transaction volumes, and overall financial performance. Also, observe any regulatory shifts affecting the payment service bank model in Nigeria.
