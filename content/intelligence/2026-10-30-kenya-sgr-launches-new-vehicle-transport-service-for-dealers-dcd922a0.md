Capital FM Business reports that Kenya Railways has launched a new vehicle transport service on the Standard Gauge Railway (SGR). This service is designed to allow car dealers, importers, and other businesses to move vehicles.

This initiative expands the freight services offered by the SGR. For investors in Kenya's logistics and automotive sectors, this indicates an evolution in domestic transport options, potentially impacting supply chain efficiency for vehicle movement. It also suggests Kenya Railways is seeking to diversify its service offerings and target specific business segments.

Investors should monitor the adoption rate of this new SGR service by car dealers, importers, and other businesses. Observing how this service integrates into existing logistics frameworks will be key to understanding its broader economic impact on vehicle distribution and related industries in Kenya.
