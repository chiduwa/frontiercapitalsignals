Ghana's government has promised to invest $10bn over four years across agriculture, manufacturing, and other productive sectors. Africa Briefing reports that $5bn of this amount is proposed for agriculture, awaiting detailed allocations. The government also targets annual investment equivalent to 1.5 percent of GDP, with separate capital expenditure projections totaling GH¢215.93bn ($18.52bn).

This initiative signals government intent to stimulate key productive sectors. The scale of the promised investment could create significant opportunities for businesses and investors operating in these areas. However, Africa Briefing highlights that the government's fiscal strategy raises budget questions, implying uncertainty regarding funding or execution that investors should monitor.

Investors should watch for further details on how the $10bn investment will be financed and allocated, particularly for agriculture, as the 2027 Budget approaches. Monitoring how the government addresses the raised budget questions will provide insight into the plan's feasibility and potential impact.
