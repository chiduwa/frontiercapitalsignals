Malawi's Mighty Wanderers secured a tough CAF Champions League draw against Tanzania's Simba SC. Coach Bob Mpinganjira frames this challenge as a significant opportunity for the club to showcase its continental progress, indicating high expectations for their performance in the 2026/27 preliminary round.

Major continental football exposure, even from a preliminary round, drives local and regional media viewership, advertising spend, and potential sports tourism. This engagement translates into increased consumer activity, brand visibility for domestic sponsors, and can indirectly highlight Malawi's growing sports and entertainment sector to regional investors.
