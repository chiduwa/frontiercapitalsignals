Deloitte Africa and Refuel have launched a strategic partnership to simplify the process of claiming diesel rebates from the South African Revenue Service (SARS). This alliance merges Refuel's specialized fuel-tracking technology with Deloitte Africa's extensive tax and advisory capabilities, creating a tech-enabled solution for sectors like mining and agriculture.

While focused on South Africa, this collaboration highlights a critical theme for African investors: leveraging technology to improve regulatory compliance and operational efficiency. Streamlined rebate claims directly enhance cash flow and profitability for fuel-intensive businesses, which are prevalent in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda. Such innovations can unlock significant value in core industries across the continent.

Monitor the adoption and success of this 'tech plus tax' model within South Africa. Its performance will indicate the viability of similar solutions expanding into other African markets facing high fuel costs and complex tax regimes. Look for opportunities in companies providing efficiency-boosting technologies or in sectors poised to benefit from improved regulatory frameworks and cash flow.
