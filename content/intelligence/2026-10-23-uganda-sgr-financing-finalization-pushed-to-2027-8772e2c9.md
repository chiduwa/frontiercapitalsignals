The Independent (Uganda) reports a delay in concluding financing arrangements for Uganda's Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) project. The finalization has been pushed from November 2026 to January 2027, which consequently delays the start of the 48-month construction timeline. Preliminary works by the contractor are reportedly already underway.

This postponement impacts the timeline for a major infrastructure initiative, which requires approximately €2.7 billion in financing. While preliminary activities continue, the full-scale economic stimulus from the SGR's extensive construction phase, including supplier contracts and direct employment, will be realized later than initially anticipated. Investors focused on construction, logistics, and related sectors in Uganda should factor this revised schedule into their outlook.

Investors should monitor further developments regarding the government's financing efforts for the SGR. The precise timing of the main construction commencement remains a key indicator for future project-related opportunities.
