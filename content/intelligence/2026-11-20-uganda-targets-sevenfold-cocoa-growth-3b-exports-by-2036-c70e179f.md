The Independent (Uganda) reports that Uganda has launched its first national strategy for the cocoa sector. This strategy aims to multiply cocoa production nearly sevenfold over the next decade, targeting 500,000 tonnes and $3 billion in annual exports by 2036. The plan also seeks to shift more processing and profit into the country.

This new strategy signals Uganda's commitment to significantly expanding its cocoa industry. The ambitious targets for production and exports, coupled with a focus on domestic processing, suggest potential investment opportunities in cocoa farming, processing infrastructure, and related value chain services. Investors should note the sector's long-term growth potential.

The Independent (Uganda) indicates that the delivery of these targets will hinge on investment and farm-level change. Therefore, monitoring policy implementation, specific investment incentives, and progress in attracting both local and international capital will be crucial. Observing the initial steps taken to foster farm-level improvements will also be important for assessing viability.
