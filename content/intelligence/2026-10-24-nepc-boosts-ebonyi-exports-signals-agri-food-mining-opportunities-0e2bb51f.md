BusinessDay reports the NEPC is promoting various products from Nigeria's Ebonyi State for global markets. These include rice, cassava, palm oil, processed foods, and solid minerals. The agency is also urging producers to obtain quality certification for these goods.

This initiative signals potential government support for export-oriented ventures within Ebonyi State, particularly in its agricultural, food processing, and mining sectors. The emphasis on quality certification could enhance market access and global competitiveness for these products, potentially improving their value for international investors.

Investors should monitor the practical implementation of these quality certification efforts and their subsequent impact on export volumes. Also, watch for specific policy incentives or support programs that may emerge from the NEPC or Ebonyi State government to accelerate this export drive.
