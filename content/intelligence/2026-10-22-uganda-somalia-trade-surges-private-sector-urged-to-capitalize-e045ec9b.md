The Independent (Uganda) reports Uganda's exports to Somalia more than doubled in a year. Exports increased from sh11.4 billion ($2.75 million) in 2023 to sh28 billion ($6.78 million) in 2024. Uganda and Somalia government officials state businesses are currently utilizing only a small portion of this market.

This significant growth in export value highlights an expanding trade corridor between Uganda and Somalia. The explicit call from officials for private sector involvement suggests governmental support for expanding commercial activities. Investors could find opportunities in sectors facilitating this bilateral trade, or in businesses looking to enter or expand within the Somali market from a Ugandan base.

Investors should observe any further official guidance or policy developments that aim to facilitate greater private sector engagement. Tracking the specific sectors experiencing this growth could also identify targeted opportunities within this expanding trade corridor.
