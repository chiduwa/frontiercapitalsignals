The Independent (Uganda) reports that Finance Minister Henry Musasizi has challenged the Uganda Registration Services Bureau (URSB) to make business formalization more attractive. Musasizi urged URSB to link formal registration to tangible economic benefits.

This policy intent suggests a strategic move to incentivize formal sector participation by directly connecting registration with access to finance, public procurement, commercial partnerships, and wider markets. For investors, this indicates a potential improvement in the operating environment for formally registered businesses in Uganda, potentially enhancing transparency and reducing informal market risks.

Investors should closely observe how the URSB translates this challenge into actionable policies and specific mechanisms. The implementation details of tying formal registration to these economic advantages will be crucial for understanding the practical impact on Uganda's business landscape and the ease of doing business for formal enterprises.
