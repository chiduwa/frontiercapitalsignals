A recent report on top countries for families relocating in 2026 highlights key criteria: schools, childcare, healthcare, safety, and overall quality of life. These factors are becoming paramount for families deciding where to live abroad.

This trend signals growing demand for robust social infrastructure in attractive investment destinations. Countries demonstrating progress in these areas can better attract and retain skilled expatriate talent and a thriving middle class, bolstering economic stability and long-term growth. Investment opportunities emerge in real estate, education, healthcare services, and urban development catering to these essential family needs.

Monitor Ghana, Kenya, and Nigeria for government initiatives and private sector investments focused on improving education standards, modernizing healthcare facilities, enhancing safety, and creating livable urban environments. Such developments will increasingly become key differentiators for attracting significant foreign direct investment and human capital.
