Borno State has experienced two school abductions within 45 days. Following the first incident in Mussa, officials reassessed security in Lassa and ordered “Safe Schools” measures. However, gunmen attacked a school in Lassa just 35 days later, indicating a significant breakdown in the implementation or effectiveness of these promised security protocols.

For international investors, this recurring pattern is a critical red flag. It erodes confidence in the government's capacity to protect assets, secure personnel, and ensure operational continuity. Such insecurity directly impacts supply chain reliability, human capital retention, and increases overall business risk, highlighting the execution challenges of government initiatives.

Monitor closely for concrete, measurable improvements in security infrastructure and genuine accountability from authorities, rather than just policy announcements. Persistent insecurity in Borno could prompt a broader reassessment of regional investment exposure and operational viability across various sectors.
