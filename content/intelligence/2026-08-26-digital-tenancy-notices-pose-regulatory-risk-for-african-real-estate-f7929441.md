WhatsApp has become a primary communication channel for landlords and tenants, extending to critical notices like evictions. This trend is questioning the legal standing and enforceability of digitally delivered vacating orders, particularly when tenants default on rent or refuse to leave.

For real estate investors, this situation highlights emerging regulatory and operational risks. Legal ambiguities surrounding digital communication validity can lead to protracted eviction processes, increased legal expenses, and unpredictable rental yields. It underscores the necessity for robust lease agreements and a clear understanding of local tenancy laws across the target markets.

Investors should closely track judicial rulings and legislative developments pertaining to digital communication in landlord-tenant relationships. Observing how property management companies adapt their protocols and contract clauses to address these legal uncertainties will be crucial for assessing investment stability.
