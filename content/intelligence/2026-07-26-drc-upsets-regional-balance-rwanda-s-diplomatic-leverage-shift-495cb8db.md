Kinshasa has deployed a high-ranking diplomat to Europe, presenting a memo purportedly signed by the FDLR. This move seeks to undermine Kigali’s standing with Western partners, particularly given the stalled Washington Peace Accords.

This significantly alters the diplomatic calculus in the Great Lakes region. For investors, it indicates a shift in political leverage and potentially increased volatility in regional relations, impacting perceived country risk and the stability of cross-border investments.

Monitor Western capitals' reactions to this diplomatic gambit and Kigali's counter-strategy. Any further escalation or de-escalation will heavily influence regional stability, affecting aid, trade, and investment confidence.
