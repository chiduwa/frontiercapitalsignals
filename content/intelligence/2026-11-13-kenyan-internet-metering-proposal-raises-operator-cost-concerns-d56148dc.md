The Standard reports that Telkom Kenya has urged Parliament to reconsider proposed internet metering regulations. Telkom warns that these planned changes could increase operational costs for service providers in the country.

This development signals potential regulatory shifts that could impact Kenya's telecommunications sector. Increased operational costs could compress profit margins for internet service providers, making investment less attractive or necessitating price adjustments for consumers. Investors in Kenyan telecom infrastructure or service providers should note the potential for higher expenses.

Investors should monitor Parliament's deliberations on these proposed internet metering regulations. The final decision will indicate the regulatory environment for service providers and could influence sector profitability. Pay attention to how the government balances consumer access with provider operational viability.
