The US House of Representatives is poised to extend the African Growth and Opportunity Act, AGOA, through 2028. This follows the Senate's lead, signaling strong bipartisan support for renewing the preferential trade program as Congress returns from its summer break.

This extension provides crucial policy certainty for investors in eligible African nations, including Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda. It guarantees continued duty-free access to the lucrative US market for thousands of products, particularly in textiles, agriculture, and manufacturing. Such predictability de-risks long-term investment decisions and encourages expansion in export-oriented sectors.

Investors should monitor the final Congressional approval and Presidential assent, which now appears highly probable. Beyond the immediate extension, watch for signals on subsequent US-Africa trade policy evolution and specific sectorial growth in beneficiary countries that leverage this sustained market access.
