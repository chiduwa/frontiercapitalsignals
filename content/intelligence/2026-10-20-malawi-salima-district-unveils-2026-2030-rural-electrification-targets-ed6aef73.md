Malawi24 reports that Salima District has identified 63 rural sites for electricity connections under the Malawi Rural Electrification Programme (MAREP). This includes 13 new sites added to the draft plan, which forms part of the draft Rural Electrification Masterplan for 2026-2030. The plan was presented during a validation exercise in Salima.

This initiative signals a clear government commitment to expanding energy access in Malawi, outlining a potential medium-term project pipeline through 2030. For investors, this could indicate opportunities in power generation, transmission, distribution, and associated services, especially those catering to rural and off-grid solutions. The identification of specific sites suggests future tender or partnership prospects.

Investors should monitor the finalization and approval of the 2026-2030 Masterplan, as well as details on funding, implementation timelines, and specific project tenders. Further information on the newly added 13 sites may also provide insight into evolving priorities or specific regional focus areas.
