Nile Post reports that Uganda's Finance Minister is seeking an integrated business registration system. This initiative aims for URSB, URA, the Uganda Investment Authority, and local governments to link their databases. The goal is to allow businesses to access services without repeatedly submitting the same information, which is intended to cut costs.

This proposed policy change signals a potential improvement in Uganda's business environment. For international investors, a streamlined and less bureaucratic registration process could reduce operational friction and administrative overheads, making the market more attractive. It suggests a government focus on improving efficiency for businesses.

Investors should monitor progress on this integration initiative. The successful implementation and actual linkage of these various government databases will be key. Any further details on the timeline or specific agencies involved in the integration efforts will provide more clarity on the potential impact for businesses operating or looking to enter Uganda.
