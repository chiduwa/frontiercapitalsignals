Africa Briefing reports that Chinese President Xi Jinping endorsed Africa’s right to control its mineral resources. China also pledged to help the continent capture more value from mining. This occurred during talks between President Xi and Botswana’s President Duma Boko, where a green mining accord was signed between the two countries.

This policy backing could signal a significant shift towards increased local processing and value addition across African mining sectors. Investors should anticipate potential new regulatory frameworks encouraging in-country beneficiation, which may impact existing mining operations and supply chains. The green mining accord also suggests a growing focus on sustainable practices, potentially creating new investment opportunities or compliance requirements.

Going forward, the crucial test will be the actual implementation of local processing initiatives and policies designed to capture more value from minerals. Investors should closely monitor how African governments, potentially with Chinese support, develop and enforce these strategies. Observing the details and broader applicability of the China-Botswana green mining accord will also provide insights into future environmental standards and investment trends in the region.
