Premium Times reports that Bayelsa Governor Douye Diri has rejected former Cross River Governor Donald Duke's assertion that the Lagos-Calabar Coastal Highway is unbuildable. Governor Diri cited roads and bridges successfully constructed in Bayelsa under similar swampy Niger Delta conditions, disputing the claim.

This development highlights ongoing discussions about the technical and practical feasibility of major infrastructure projects in challenging terrains. For investors, Diri's perspective, backed by local examples, could suggest that complex engineering projects, even in difficult environments, might be more viable than previously assumed. This influences the risk assessment and potential for investment in Nigeria's construction and infrastructure sectors.

Investors should monitor further details on the proposed highway's technical plans and any subsequent official statements or feasibility studies. Observing the progress and outcomes of projects in similar conditions, particularly those cited by Governor Diri, will be key to understanding the realisable potential for such large-scale ventures.
