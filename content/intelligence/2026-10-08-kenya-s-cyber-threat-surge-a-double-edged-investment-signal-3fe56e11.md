Kenya's communications regulator reports a significant surge in cyber threats, reaching 11.12 billion events by June 2026. This marks a 29% increase year-on-year, primarily driven by a 114.3% rise in Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks.

This escalation signals heightened operational risks and potential revenue disruption for companies with significant digital footprints in Kenya. For investors, this translates to increased expenditure on cybersecurity infrastructure and services across various sectors. The trend underlines the critical importance of evaluating a company's digital resilience as part of due diligence.

We are monitoring how Kenyan regulators and businesses respond. Look for new national cybersecurity policies, increased investment in protective infrastructure, and the emergence of stronger local and international cybersecurity service providers. This development will shape the digital investment landscape and define which companies can effectively mitigate these growing risks.
