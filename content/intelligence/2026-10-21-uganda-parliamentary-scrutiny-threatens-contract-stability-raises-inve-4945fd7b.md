The Independent (Uganda) reports that Uganda's push to attract investment is confronting a challenge beyond financing and infrastructure. This challenge is uncertainty over whether government contracts will survive parliamentary scrutiny without disruption, costly disputes, or demands for cancellation. Parliament has raised important questions across enquiries into expressway operations, vehicle inspections, and digital number plates.

This situation matters to investors as it risks raising Uganda’s investment costs. The potential for government contracts to face disruption, costly disputes, or demands for cancellation introduces significant operational and financial uncertainty. Such instability could deter new foreign direct investment and complicate existing projects reliant on government agreements.

Investors should monitor the ongoing parliamentary scrutiny of government contracts and its impact on project stability. Observe any outcomes or resolutions from the mentioned enquiries into expressway operations, vehicle inspections, and digital number plates to gauge the direction of policy risk.
