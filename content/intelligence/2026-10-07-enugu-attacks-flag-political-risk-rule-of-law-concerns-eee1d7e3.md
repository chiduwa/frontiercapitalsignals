Hoodlums violently attacked and disrupted political meetings of the NDC and PDP in Nkanu West LGA, Enugu State. A man who filmed these incidents has since been remanded by the court.

This incident highlights escalating political tensions and security risks, particularly ahead of elections. Such disruptions raise concerns about the rule of law and the stability of local operating environments, directly impacting investor confidence and business continuity.

Investors should closely monitor the broader political climate, government responses to these security breaches, and judicial outcomes. The integrity and management of future electoral processes will be key indicators of stability.
