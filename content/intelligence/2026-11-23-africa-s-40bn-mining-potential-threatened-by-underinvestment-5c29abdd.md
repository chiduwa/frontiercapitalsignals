Africa Briefing reports that McKinsey estimates Africa's mining industry could unlock $40 billion in additional economic value by 2035. This significant potential, however, is currently at risk due to chronic underinvestment, exploration shortages, and costly infrastructure across the continent.

This signals a substantial long-term opportunity for investors in the African mining ecosystem, but also highlights critical barriers to entry or growth. Investors should note the need for solutions addressing exploration and infrastructure, which could create related investment opportunities. The emphasis on shared facilities and domestic processing suggests a shift towards retaining more value on the continent, potentially impacting supply chain and processing investments.

Investors should watch for initiatives or policy changes aimed at promoting shared facilities and increasing domestic processing within the mining sector. Also, monitor developments in addressing infrastructure financing gaps and incentivizing exploration activities across Africa, as these will be key to unlocking the reported value.
