Nigeria's persistent hydrocarbon metering and calibration deficiencies are costing the state billions in lost crude revenues, with an estimated $1 billion yearly figure highlighted. This critical issue will be a top agenda item at the upcoming NiHMEC 2026 conference.

Resolving these metering gaps could significantly boost Nigeria's fiscal position, enhancing the government's capacity for development spending and debt servicing. This improved revenue outlook directly impacts the stability and attractiveness of the Nigerian investment landscape, particularly for oil and gas sector participants and related service providers.

Investors should monitor the outcomes and specific policy commitments from NiHMEC 2026. Key indicators will include new regulatory frameworks, investment opportunities in metering technology and services, and the implementation timeline for proposed solutions to stem these losses.
