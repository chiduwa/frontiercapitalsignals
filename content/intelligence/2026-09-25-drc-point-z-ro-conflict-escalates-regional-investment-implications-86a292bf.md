Fierce fighting continues around Point Zéro in Eastern DRC. This village, strategically located in the highlands, controls critical regional routes.

Prolonged instability in Eastern DRC, particularly around key transport choke points like Point Zéro, elevates regional supply chain risk. This impacts commodity flows, trade routes, and overall investor confidence across East and Central Africa.

Monitor conflict resolution efforts and the security status of regional trade arteries. Watch for any displacement of populations or disruption to critical resource extraction and logistics, which could affect regional input costs or market access.
