The Independent (Uganda) reports that TotalEnergies anticipates global oil demand will peak between 2035 and 2040. The French energy company, which operates Uganda's Tilenga oil project, also maintains that petroleum production will remain necessary for decades.

This outlook raises questions about the long-term commercial prospects of Uganda's multibillion-dollar oil investments. For investors, this creates uncertainty regarding the returns and viability of projects tied to future oil demand beyond the projected peak.

Investors should monitor further statements from TotalEnergies and other key players regarding their long-term strategies in the region. Observing how the Ugandan government responds to these demand forecasts, particularly concerning project timelines and diversification efforts, will be critical.
