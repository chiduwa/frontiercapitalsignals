Africa Briefing reports that Guinea plans to have five alumina refineries operating by 2030. Three of these facilities are reportedly already under construction. This initiative reflects the country's efforts to process its mineral wealth domestically rather than exporting raw materials.

This development in Guinea highlights a growing trend across Africa towards value addition in the mining sector. For investors, this signals potential opportunities in infrastructure development, power generation, and industrial processing within the continent. It suggests an increasing focus by African governments on retaining more economic benefit from their natural resources.

Investors should monitor the progress of these refinery projects in Guinea and observe how the country addresses the crucial need for power supply to support these industrial ambitions. This model of domestic processing could influence policy and investment decisions in other resource-rich African nations, indicating future opportunities or competitive landscapes.
