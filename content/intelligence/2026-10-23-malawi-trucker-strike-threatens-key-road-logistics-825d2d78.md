Malawi24 reports that the Truck Drivers Union of Malawi has asked the government to urgently fix a dangerous stretch of the Mzuzu-Karonga Road at Chiweta Hills. The union warns of a potential suspension of truck operations on this route if the road's worsening condition, which is putting lives at risk, is not addressed.

A shutdown of truck operations on this part of the Mzuzu-Karonga Road could disrupt logistics and supply chains within Malawi. Investors with interests in Malawian businesses relying on road transport, especially those operating in or through the northern regions, should monitor this development closely. It highlights ongoing infrastructure challenges that can impact operational efficiency and cost.

The immediate watch is whether the government responds to the union's request to fix the Mzuzu-Karonga Road at Chiweta Hills, and whether the truck drivers proceed with their threatened suspension of operations. The duration and scope of any potential shutdown would determine the extent of its impact on local and regional trade.
