Nyasa Times reports that an advocacy group for refugees in Malawi, INUA Advocacy, has urged the government to fast-track a new Refugee Bill. The group, which promotes refugee rights, called on the Ministry of Homeland Security and the Ministry of Justice to expedite bringing the bill to Parliament.

The proposed legislation is significant for investors because it would permit refugees to work and trade in Malawi. This change could expand the available labor pool, stimulate local commerce, and potentially create new consumer demand, offering fresh market opportunities for various sectors.

Investors should monitor developments regarding the Refugee Bill's progress through Parliament. The eventual passage and implementation of this law could signal a more inclusive economic environment, impacting labor supply, consumer markets, and overall economic growth in Malawi.
