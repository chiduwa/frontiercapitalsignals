Africa Briefing reports that Kenya's economy is projected at $151.15 billion in 2026, making it East Africa's largest economy in dollar terms this year. This is $24 billion more than Ethiopia's projected GDP of $127.15 billion for the same year, according to the IMF's October 2026 World Economic Outlook.

For investors, this data highlights Kenya's current economic scale and stability as a dominant player in the East African market. However, Ethiopia's growth forecast, which is more than twice Kenya's, indicates a rapidly expanding market with potentially higher returns for long-term investors willing to navigate its unique landscape.

Moving forward, investors should monitor how these growth trajectories unfold. While Kenya offers a larger established market, Ethiopia's accelerated growth rate could present significant opportunities for market entry and expansion in the coming years.
