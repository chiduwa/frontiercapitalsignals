MyJoyOnline Business reports that Hubtel CEO Alex Bram has urged the Ghanaian government to complement its planned investments in artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure with funding for specialized training. He specifically called for investments in training for AI engineers and data scientists.

The CEO warned that Ghana's current shortage of skilled professionals could hinder efforts to develop a competitive AI industry. For international investors, this highlights a potential bottleneck in scaling AI-driven operations within Ghana, signaling that talent acquisition might become a significant operational challenge if not addressed. Conversely, it points to a clear opportunity for education and training providers in the tech sector.

Investors should monitor how the Ghanaian government responds to this call for enhanced training funding. Any policy shifts towards greater human capital development in AI could mitigate future operational risks for tech companies and potentially open new avenues for investment in the education and vocational training sectors focused on emerging technologies.
