Africa Briefing reports that Gabon's long-delayed Mabounié mining project has progressed, with an Emirati-backed company submitting an updated feasibility study. This proposed investment exceeds $2bn and targets an annual production of 16,000 tonnes of niobium.

This development signals significant foreign direct investment potential in African mining, particularly for strategic minerals. Investors tracking regional commodity supply chains or major capital projects in Africa may find this relevant. The project's scale underscores substantial interest in the continent's resource sector.

Africa Briefing indicates that financing for the Mabounié project remains unclear, despite a 2027 construction target. Investors should monitor future announcements regarding funding and project commencement. Clarity on financing will be a critical indicator for the project's realization.
