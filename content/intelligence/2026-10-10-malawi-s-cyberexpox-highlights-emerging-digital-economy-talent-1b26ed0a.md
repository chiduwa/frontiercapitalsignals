Malawi24 reports that the CyberExpoX event, scheduled for December 5, will showcase Malawian game developers, esports athletes, and digital artists in Blantyre. The event also offers scholarship opportunities for some local creators.

This initiative matters to investors as CyberExpoX aims to connect these local innovators with opportunities in the global digital economy, while also helping them protect their creative work. This suggests a burgeoning digital talent pool and potential for growth in Malawi's tech and creative sectors, potentially opening new avenues for investment or partnerships.

Investors should monitor the impact of CyberExpoX on global connections for Malawian digital talent and the uptake of scholarship opportunities. Observing how these initiatives foster local innovation and intellectual property protection will be key for understanding future sector development.
