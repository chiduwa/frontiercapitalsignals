Global data centers are experiencing unprecedented power demand. AFCOM's latest report shows average rack density surged from 16 kW to 27 kW in one year, driven by intensive AI workloads. Operators universally expect this trend to accelerate, requiring significantly more robust infrastructure.

This global shift creates urgent demand for advanced data center infrastructure, sustainable power generation, and efficient cooling solutions across Africa. As digital economies expand in Ghana, Nigeria, and Kenya, local and international players must invest heavily to meet these new energy and cooling requirements. Opportunities exist in new data center builds, grid upgrades, and renewable energy projects.
