UBA has opened the subscription channel for the Dangote Refinery IPO, making this landmark public offer accessible to both Nigerian and eligible international investors. This move signals a significant step in democratizing access to one of Africa's most anticipated industrial projects.

This represents a unique opportunity to gain exposure to a critical piece of Nigeria's, and Africa's, industrial future. The refinery is a strategic asset positioned to transform regional energy markets, reduce import reliance, and generate significant revenue streams, offering substantial long-term growth potential.

Monitor subscription rates and international investor uptake to gauge market demand. Key areas to watch include the final valuation metrics, the refinery's operational ramp-up, and its impact on Nigeria's forex stability. Post-listing performance will indicate broader investor confidence in large-scale African industrial projects.
