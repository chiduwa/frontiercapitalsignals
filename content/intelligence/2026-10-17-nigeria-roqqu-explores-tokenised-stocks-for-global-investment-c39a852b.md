TechCabal reports that Roqqu intends to make Nigerian stocks available to global investors. This plan involves using blockchain-based tokens.

This initiative could significantly expand foreign participation in Nigeria's stock market. Increased foreign capital could enhance market liquidity and valuations.

Key areas to monitor include the resolution of questions around shareholder rights, regulatory frameworks, asset custody, and liquidity for these blockchain-based tokens. These aspects will determine the viability and attractiveness of such an offering.
