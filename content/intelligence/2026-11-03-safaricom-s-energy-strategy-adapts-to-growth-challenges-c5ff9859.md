TechCabal reports that Safaricom's cleaner energy push is struggling to keep pace with its growth. To address this, the company is combining solar installations with lithium-ion batteries and implementing more efficient power systems. Safaricom is also establishing additional grid connections and utilizing remote monitoring capabilities.

This situation highlights a significant challenge for infrastructure providers in rapidly expanding markets: ensuring scalable and sustainable power solutions amid swift growth. It underscores a critical demand for advanced energy technologies and efficient management systems. Investors could find opportunities in companies supplying robust, scalable power solutions to mitigate these growth-related energy deficits.

Investors should monitor the effectiveness of these specific energy solutions in addressing Safaricom's growth-related power demands. Observing how other rapidly expanding operators in the region tackle similar energy-growth imbalances, and the policy responses to such infrastructure challenges, will be important for future insights.
