Africa Briefing reports that older Africans are facing growing challenges in accessing essential healthcare and support as they age. A recent World Health Organisation (WHO) report warns of widening healthcare gaps for this demographic.

This situation presents a clear signal for investors, as Africa's older population is projected to triple by 2050, indicating a substantial and growing market for age-specific healthcare services. The WHO's call for stronger investment in long-term care suggests potential for policy initiatives and funding directed towards this sector. Furthermore, only a small fraction of WHO African Region countries reported adequate programme funding in 2023, underscoring significant unmet demand.

Investors should closely track policy developments and investment programs stemming from the WHO's recommendations for long-term care. This could reveal opportunities in healthcare infrastructure, specialized geriatric services, and health technology solutions designed for an aging demographic.
