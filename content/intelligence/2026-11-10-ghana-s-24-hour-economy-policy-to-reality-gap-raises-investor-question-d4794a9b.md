The High Street Journal reports that Economist Dr. Adu Owusu Sarkodie has raised concerns regarding Ghana’s 24-Hour Economy. He argues that there are significant differences between its manifesto promise, policy document, and actual implementation, with job creation commitments reportedly lacking verifiable evidence and clear accountability.

This reported divergence highlights potential inconsistencies in government policy execution, which can introduce uncertainty for investors assessing long-term operational environments. The stated lack of verifiable evidence and clear accountability for job creation commitments suggests challenges in achieving stated economic goals, potentially impacting labor market dynamics and consumer spending.

Investors should monitor future reports on the practical rollout of the 24-Hour Economy initiative. Observing any government response to these criticisms, particularly regarding policy adjustments or clearer accountability measures, will be key for understanding the stability and predictability of Ghana's economic policy landscape.
