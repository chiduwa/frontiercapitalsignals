BusinessDay reports that Africa's growing creative industry needs improved access to financing, stronger copyright protection, and better infrastructure. These elements are crucial for the sector to effectively leverage its expanding talent base.

This outlook outlines key opportunities and challenges for investors. The demand for better financing points to potential in financial services tailored for creative businesses or direct investment into promising ventures. Calls for stronger copyright protection and improved infrastructure highlight regulatory and development areas that, if addressed, could unlock substantial value and mitigate risks within the sector.

Investors should monitor policy developments concerning intellectual property rights and observe government or private initiatives aimed at enhancing sector-specific infrastructure. Progress in these areas would signal increasing maturity and improved investability for the creative industry across the continent.
