The Africa Report indicates that significant quantities of cocaine are being trafficked through Ghanaian ports, with the drugs subsequently appearing in Europe. Customs officers involved are facing prosecution. However, uncertainty remains regarding how these large shipments bypassed detection and who facilitated their passage.

This situation signals potential weaknesses in Ghana's port security and regulatory oversight. For investors, systemic vulnerabilities in port operations or customs controls could introduce unforeseen risks to supply chains, trade logistics, and the overall business environment. Concerns about governance and the rule of law may also arise.

Investors should monitor ongoing investigations and any subsequent policy responses by the Ghanaian government. Potential reforms in port management, customs procedures, and enforcement efforts will be key indicators of how authorities address these issues, which could impact the ease of doing business and trade reliability in Ghana.
