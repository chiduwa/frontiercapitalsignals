Malawi24 reports that Malawians face another squeeze on electricity supply as the Electricity Generation Company (Malawi) Limited (EGENCO) prepares to remove 32.4 megawatts from the national grid for four days. This is due to the shutdown of Unit 4 at Kapichira Power Station, starting at 22:00 hours. ESCOM has also announced power cuts in parts of the country on Sunday.

This disruption in power supply signals ongoing challenges within Malawi's energy infrastructure. Investors in sectors reliant on consistent power, such as manufacturing, retail, or services, should anticipate potential operational disruptions and increased costs from alternative power sources. The repeated nature of such "squeezes" points to systemic issues that impact business continuity.

Investors should monitor the duration and frequency of these power supply disruptions as an indicator of broader infrastructure stability. Any further announcements from EGENCO or ESCOM regarding grid improvements or alternative energy solutions will be crucial for assessing long-term operational risks and opportunities in the country.
