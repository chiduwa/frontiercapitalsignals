TechCabal reports a global Microsoft outage disrupted operations for several South African businesses and public institutions. Affected entities included Eskom, Absa, and FlySafair. This incident highlighted the country's growing reliance on Big Tech's cloud infrastructure.

This event underscores the operational risks inherent in deep integration with global cloud services for businesses across Africa. For investors targeting Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, Malawi, and Uganda, the South African outage signals the potential for similar disruptions impacting business continuity and critical services. Companies with significant reliance on external cloud providers could face notable downtime, potentially affecting earnings and stability.

Investors should monitor how businesses and governments in the region respond to these single-point-of-failure risks. Watch for potential shifts towards diversified cloud strategies, increased investment in local data center infrastructure, or enhanced disaster recovery planning in response to such vulnerabilities. This incident may prompt broader discussions on digital sovereignty and infrastructure resilience across the continent.
