Tunde Obileye, an infrastructure maintenance expert, underscored the significant financial consequences of neglecting preventive maintenance across building systems and equipment. Ignoring proactive upkeep ultimately leads to higher operational costs and premature asset failure.

This signals critical investment implications. Poorly maintained assets, whether in real estate portfolios or public infrastructure, face accelerated depreciation and unexpected capital outlays, eroding returns. Conversely, it creates opportunities for specialized maintenance service providers and asset management firms addressing this market gap.

Investors should monitor government infrastructure spending for dedicated maintenance budgets and the growth of professional facility management services. Watch for real estate developers and PPPs integrating lifecycle cost analysis and robust preventive maintenance clauses. This indicates a maturing approach to asset longevity and value creation.
