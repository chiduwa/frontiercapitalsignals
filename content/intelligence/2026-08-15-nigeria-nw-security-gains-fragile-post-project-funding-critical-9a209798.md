An EU-funded peacebuilding project in Nigeria's Katsina and Zamfara states is 87% complete, showing significant progress in an 18-month intervention. However, the International Organization for Migration, IOM, warns that a lack of sustained funding threatens the long-term viability of these gains after the project concludes.

This matters directly to regional investment outlooks. Sustained peace is foundational for economic activity, talent retention, and supply chain integrity. A return to instability in these states would increase operational risks for businesses, potentially impacting local assets and discouraging new capital inflow into key sectors reliant on regional stability.

Investors should monitor forthcoming announcements regarding follow-on funding from international donors or the Nigerian government. Pay close attention to security incident reports from Katsina and Zamfara after the project's official end date. Any deterioration would signal heightened risk, while new public or private initiatives to maintain stability would present a positive signal.
