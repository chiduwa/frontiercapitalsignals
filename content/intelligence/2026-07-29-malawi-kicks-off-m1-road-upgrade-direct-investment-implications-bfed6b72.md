Malawi's Minister of Transport, Jappie Mhango, has officially launched the rehabilitation of the M1 Road. This critical project spans from the Bunda Turnoff in Lilongwe to the Kameza Roundabout in Blantyre, marking a significant infrastructure investment.

This major upgrade directly improves logistics and reduces transport costs between Malawi's two largest cities. Investors should note the positive implications for agricultural exports, manufacturing supply chains, and domestic trade efficiency, creating ancillary opportunities in logistics, warehousing, and service industries along the corridor. It signals government commitment to economic enablers.

Monitor project timelines, progress, and the eventual impact on regional trade volumes. Pay attention to any new tenders for related services or businesses looking to leverage the improved connectivity, especially in industrial and agricultural zones along the M1.
