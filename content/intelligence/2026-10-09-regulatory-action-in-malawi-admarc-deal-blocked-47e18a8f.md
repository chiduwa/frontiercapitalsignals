Malawi24 reports the Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB) has blocked ADMARC's K3 billion tarpaulin procurement deal. This action was taken amid suspected corruption, with taxpayers reportedly questioning the K30 million cost for a single tarpaulin.

This development indicates a continued effort by authorities to address corruption within public sector procurement in Malawi. For investors, such regulatory intervention can signal a commitment to improving transparency and accountability in government tenders, potentially enhancing the overall investment climate.

Investors should monitor the progress and outcome of the ACB's investigation into the ADMARC deal. Observing future public procurement processes will be key to gauging sustained anti-corruption efforts and their implications for doing business in Malawi.
