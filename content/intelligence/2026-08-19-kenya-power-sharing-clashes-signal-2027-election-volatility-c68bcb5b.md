Cracks are appearing in Kenya's broad-based government as the 2027 election approaches. Legislators from President Ruto’s UDA and Oburu Oginga’s ODM are clashing over an unformulated power-sharing agreement. This signals growing internal political friction within the ruling coalition.

Political stability is paramount for sustained investor confidence and policy predictability. These internal power struggles introduce significant uncertainty, potentially impacting regulatory consistency, delaying key reforms, and diverting government focus from economic priorities. Such friction can cool foreign direct investment interest.

Monitor the progress, or lack thereof, in formalizing the power-sharing deal. The ability of the coalition to resolve these disputes amicably will indicate future policy stability. Early positioning for the 2027 elections and any shifts in the government’s economic agenda warrant close observation.
