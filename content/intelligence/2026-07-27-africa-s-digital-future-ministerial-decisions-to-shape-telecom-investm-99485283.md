African ICT Ministers opened the African Telecommunications Union's (ATU) CPL-26 conference in Abuja, Nigeria, on July 23-24, 2026. This session brings together governments to decide the future direction and operational mandate of the ATU, focusing on telecommunications, information and communication technologies, and the digital economy across the continent.

This conference will shape policy and regulatory frameworks influencing the digital landscape in key markets like Nigeria, Kenya, and Ghana. Decisions made here regarding spectrum allocation, infrastructure development, and digital services can unlock significant growth for telecom operators, tech companies, and digital service providers. Expect clarity on cross-border digital economy initiatives and potential for harmonized regulations, reducing operational risks and expanding market access.

Monitor the official communique from CPL-26 for specific resolutions on policy harmonization, infrastructure investment plans, and any new mandates for the ATU. Key indicators will be commitments to regional digital integration and clear timelines for implementing new frameworks. Watch for announcements on public-private partnerships or specific initiatives targeting broadband expansion and digital inclusion.
