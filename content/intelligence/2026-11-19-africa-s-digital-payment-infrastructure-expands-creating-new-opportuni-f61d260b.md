Capital FM Business reports that 89 per cent of African adults reside in countries with access to instant payment systems. This highlights the rapid expansion of digital financial infrastructure across the continent. The report also indicates a surge in African instant payments.

The widespread availability of instant payment systems signals significant growth in digital financial services, creating opportunities for fintech investments and digital commerce. Investors can explore companies facilitating these payments or leveraging this infrastructure for new services. The rapid expansion suggests an increasingly digitized consumer base and economy.

While instant payments are surging, the source mentions trust remains a hurdle, though specific details are not provided. Investors should monitor developments in payment security and regulatory frameworks that could build trust and further accelerate adoption. Opportunities may arise in solutions addressing this trust deficit.
